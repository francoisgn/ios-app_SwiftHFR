### Problem
ezzz's legacy fix `5c0f10db` (build 39): HFR answers a member profile **403 without the `md_id` cookie**. An account that lacks it (imported from legacy, whose `MultisManager updateCookies:` replaced the account's cookies and dropped it; or a login past 30 days, since only the login sets it) gets 403 on every profile while the rest of the forum works, and SwiftHFR showed « accès refusé, ta session a peut-être expiré », which a session check cannot fix.

### Cause
HFR gates profiles on the **presence** of `md_id`, not on the session; only the login sets it and no page refreshes it. SwiftHFR's login already gets it (both field sets do, so ezzz's login-field change is not a change of HFR and is not ported) and its jar already merges answers (#383, legacy fix 3), but a session without `md_id` was sent anyway and its 403 read as an expired session.

### Change (why this wide and no wider)
- `HFRSession.memberIDCookieName` / `holdsMemberID(now:)` (HFRNetwork): a live, non-empty `md_id` other than `deleted`.
- `LiveUserProfileSource`: `ProfileLoginState` gains `hasMemberID`; without it no request leaves and the state is `profileReloginRequired` (legacy `reloginRequired`, `UserProfileLoadingError.swift:30-32` @ `5c0f10db`, in « tu », pointing to « Se reconnecter » since SwiftHFR re-logs an account in place where legacy asks to remove and re-add it); a 403 is `profileReloginRequired` when the session lost `md_id` meanwhile and stays `profileAccessDenied` with it (legacy's check after the answer, `UserProfileService.swift:43-47`).
- `LiveMemberAvatarLookup` (#714): no lookup without `md_id`, and the new state is a refusal (suspended until a login).
- One rule for « a live `md_id` »: `StoredCookie.isLiveMemberID(at:)` (HFRModels: present, not empty, not `deleted`, not expired), used by the session, the sync merge and the adoption (review finding 2).
- Account sync (LOT-173, review finding 1): `AccountSyncMerge` keeps this device's live `md_id` when the copy that wins (later `credentialsAt`) holds none and is the same login (same `md_user` and `md_passs`, #1385's identity).
