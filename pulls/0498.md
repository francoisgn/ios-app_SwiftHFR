Direct-PR: operator decision 2026-11-06 on #368 ("progress-model slide rework", an exception to FREEZE 2 limited to this slide), tooling only, self-merged when CI is green and the adversarial review says APPROVE.

The problem: on the progress-model slide (#494) a lot being worked on showed as `macOS` or `ready`, and `macOS` counted every `host: mac` lot although the cloud now codes the App lots of M2.

What changed (`tooling/roadmap/weekly/progress.py` only, plus tests and the re-rendered PNG):
- new bucket `en cours`, right after `done`: a lot that is claimed (`G.CLAIMED`) or named by an open PR (`G.PRS`), whatever its host;
- `macOS` keeps what only the Mac can do: a lot waiting on a capture, or a `host: mac` lot that is not an App lot of M2; a `host: mac` App lot of M2 goes to `ready` (rule of 2026-11-05);
- captures moved from `human` to `macOS`; `human` keeps decisions, keys, settings, triage;
- footnote, legend and donut sizes updated so the slide still fits 1920×1080.

Choices to know: the stated order puts a lot claimed by a `mac/*` session in `en cours`, not in `macOS`; the Mac checks owed are not traceable per lot, the footnote says so.

Tests: `test_a_lot_counts_in_exactly_one_bucket_by_priority` rewritten for the new order (claimed, open PR, capture, M2 App rule, done outranks all); 40 roadmap tests pass.
