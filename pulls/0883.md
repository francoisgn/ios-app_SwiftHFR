# PR #883: [SwiftHFR] Success confirmations dismiss themselves after 4 s (not under VoiceOver)

Closes #864
Claim: mac/M1

**Problem.** The "réponse postée avec succès" confirmation stays on screen until the cross is tapped. The same happens with the banners after an edit, a delete, a new topic or a new MP.

**Cause** (hypothesis, not reproduced on a device). The 4 s dismissal was a `Task.sleep` in each view's `.task(id:)`. For the reader, `ReaderReplyFlow` sits at the root of `TopicReaderView` (`TopicReaderView.swift:97`), so covering or rebuilding the view cancels the sleep, and the guard then returns without clearing. A timer owned by the model fixes it whatever the trigger, since it no longer depends on the view lifecycle.

**Change.**
- One shared `ConfirmationAutoDismiss`:
  - a 4 s delay, with an injectable sleep;
  - generation-guarded, so an old timer never clears a newer sentence.
- It is owned by `TopicReaderModel`, `TopicListModel` and `MessageListModel`. It is armed when a success sentence is set and cancelled when the sentence is cleared.
- The three view timers are removed.
- Success sentences only: errors and unconfirmed messages never reach it.
- The cross is unchanged.
- Under VoiceOver nothing is armed, so the sentence stays until the cross. The issue asks that the auto-dismiss never cut the announcement short, and the system gives no reading-time value to wait for.
- Legacy at 87fe0d1c has no auto-dismissing banner. The 4 s is a choice within the 3–5 s the issue allows.

**Tests.**
- `ConfirmationAutoDismissTests` (8). They run on a manual sleeper, with no wall-clock wait and with bounded waits. They cover:
  - the delay is within range;
  - the reply, topic and MP sentences clear after the delay;
  - the cross wins over a stale timer;
  - an old timer does not clear a newer confirmation;
  - an unconfirmed reply arms nothing;
  - with VoiceOver on, nothing is armed; with it off, the timer is armed.
- They fail with the timer or the VoiceOver guard disabled, shown red on iPhone and iPad.
- Full gate green on iPhone and iPad (1483 tests).

**Not verified.** The cause above, and the behaviour under VoiceOver on a device.

**Known low points (review round 2, not blocking).** Three tests read the host's real VoiceOver state: they assume it is off, as in CI. VoiceOver is read only when the timer is armed.
