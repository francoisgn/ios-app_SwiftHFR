3. **Test 22 vs `donnees.md`**: the page contains `https://www.cnil.fr`; the test allows that one `<a href>` (never a resource).

6. **Stats cache** is invalidated after an accepted send (else the 10th respondent would not see results); otherwise 1 minute.
7. `fermer`/`effacer` refuse before `UX_FERMETURE`; `export --sortie` is a prefix (date and extension appended); invalid `Content-Type` gives 400 (not 415) to stay within the §7 table.
