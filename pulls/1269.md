**Problem.** The smiley detail and the GIF confirmation each opened a third sheet over the composer: composer, then panel, then detail.
- The smiley detail was a bare `VStack` with no title and no close button, so a swipe was the only way out. VoiceOver and Switch Control users cannot rely on that.
- HIG › Sheets: "Display only one sheet at a time from the main interface".
- HIG › Accessibility: "if you use a swipe gesture to dismiss a view, also make a button available".
- HIG › Modality: "Make it easy to identify a modal view's task".
- Legacy had two layers: the smiley detail was an in-panel card with an `xmark` « Fermer » (`HFRswift/Swift/AnswerView.swift:2496-2509` @ `87fe0d1c`).

**Change.**
- `SmileyPickerView`: `.navigationDestination(item: $model.presented)` inside the panel's existing `ThemedNavigationStack`.
  - The detail gets the smiley's code as an inline title, and the system back button.
  - It keeps the app background and the panel's notice (« Erreur :/ » on a failed favourite included).
  - « Insérer » still closes the panel, through `model.chosen`.
- `GifPickerView`: `.navigationDestination(isPresented:)`, bound to `model.selected`. Going back clears the selection. (`GiphyGIF` is not `Hashable`, so `item:` is not available for it.)
  - The GIF shown is kept while the view pops.
  - `GifConfirmationView` loses its own stack and its « Annuler », since the back button replaces it. Its title « Insérer ce GIF ? » and the app background stay.
- The picker models are unchanged apart from one comment.
- Docs: LOT-104, LOT-105 and LOT-155 now describe the push. The old "sheet instead of an overlay card" lot decision is replaced.

**Tests** (`App/SwiftHFRTests/Smileys/PickerLayersStructureTests.swift`, app-hosted; not run here, since Linux cannot build the app):
- `theSmileyDetailIsPushedNotPresented` and `theGifConfirmationIsPushedNotPresented` check that neither panel presents a sheet for these views, and that the confirmation has no stack of its own.
- Both are red before the change.
- They are structure checks: they do not prove the push happens. A hosted test (as in `ComposerPlacementTests`) is feasible on a Mac session. The device check below is the proof.

SwiftLint 0.65.1 `--strict` clean.

**Device check** (#893; `host: mac-helps`; needs a simulator or device, iPhone and iPad):
- Smiley detail:
  - It opens with at most two layers, a back button and a title.
  - VoiceOver reaches « Retour ».
  - « Insérer » inserts and closes the panel.
  - With a dark or OLED theme, the background is themed.
  - A favourite added or removed shows the notice. Check where it sits: the review expects it over the top of the smiley preview rather than under the navigation bar. If that looks wrong, give the detail a full-size frame before the overlay.
  - From a search result: back returns to the results, with the search still active.
  - A keyword tap in the detail goes back, then searches.
- GIF confirmation:
  - Same layers, back button and title.
  - The GIF stays visible while going back.
  - The background is themed.
- Check alongside #909 (the picker closing on the first tap), which is in the same flow.
