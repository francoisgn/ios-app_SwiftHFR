## What and why
**Problem.** On a slow network, "+ › Editer" then "Répondre" while "Chargement de l'édition…" shows: the reply composer opened, then the edit composer was set too when the form arrived (`MessageWriteModel.openEditor` guarded only its own state and set `editor` after the await without looking at the reader). Symmetric with "Citer" in flight then "Editer". The reply button ignored an edit in flight (LESSONS 11).

**Change.**
- `TopicReaderModel.composerSlotIsFree` (`+Composer`): no composer open, none being prepared (`isLoadingQuote`), no reply handed to the screen and not yet shown. The one rule every way to open a composer asks first.
- `isLoadingQuote` now means "a composer is being prepared": the quotes set it as before, and **"Editer" sets it for its form read**. The reply button (disabled by `isLoadingQuote`), the quotes and the MP are then refused while the edit form loads.
- `openEditor` is refused unless the slot is free; after its await, a composer that opened meanwhile drops the late edit with "Edition abandonnée : un autre message est en cours d'écriture.".
- `requestReply`, `quoteMessage`, `quoteSelection` ask the slot first and hand their request through `hand(_:)`, which drops it with a feedback line if a composer opened during their awaits.
- The author menu's MP (menu and profile paths, `AuthorMenuFlow.openMessage`) asks the same slot.
- `docs/lots/LOT-086-quote-composer.md`: the flag's new meaning.

Not wider: only the views report a composer open or closed, as on the base. A model-side report was tried and reverted (it could freeze the reader after a root swap). An existing hole, a composer open at a root swap never reporting its close, is #1242.
