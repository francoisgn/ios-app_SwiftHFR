### Problem
Re-selecting the tab the tab bar already shows did nothing. Legacy reloads the list with a haptic: the tab bar posts `.rootTabReselected` (`MainWindow.swift:2444-2494` @ 87fe0d1c) and each list listens (Catégories' forum list `:229-238`, its topic list `:893-900`, Favoris `Favorites.swift:785-794`, Messages `MPListView.swift:658-667`); GAP-007 "not planned". UXD-04 (decisions of 2026-11-02) is "A: reselecting a tab refreshes it, with a haptic" (LOT-170 spec); it says nothing about scrolling to the top first, so none is added.

### Cause
SwiftUI's `TabView` does not call its selection binding for a tap on the selected tab (and `RootModel.selectedTab` ignores an unchanged value), so nothing observed it. The sidebar `List` selection of the split root had the same blind spot.

### Change
- `RootModel.reselect(_:)`: a light haptic through `HapticFeedback` (injected, `.live` by default, so « Retours haptiques » governs it, GAP-149), then Catégories reloads its forum list and the topic list it shows, Favoris its list only (as `Favorites.swift:785-794`; a category pushed over it listens to the Catégories tab, `MainWindow.swift:893-900`), Messages its page. Plus and Recherche do nothing; the reader is untouched (GAP-008, UXD-02).
- One haptic, at the start. Legacy plays two, at the start and at the end of the refresh (`Common.swift:108-114`); the pull to refresh of #1207 plays one, so does this.
- iPhone tabs and the Duo tab-bar root: `TabBarReselectionObserver`, a port of legacy's UIKit observer (`MainWindow.swift:2597-2790`, `:1613-1625`). It keeps the `UITabBarController` delegate SwiftUI installed, asks it first for `shouldSelectTab` / `didSelectTab` and forwards every other delegate call to it (`responds(to:)`, `forwardingTarget(for:)`); it holds that delegate weakly and takes the controller back when SwiftUI puts another delegate on it; when the delegate found is another observer it adopts that one's previous delegate, never chaining observers; it reports a tap on the selected index, and a UIKit selection back to `model.selectedTab` (legacy `handleUIKitTabSelection`, HFR-092). `RootTab.atBarIndex` maps the index to the tab (Recherche before Plus).
- iPad and unfolded Duo split root: `RootModel.selectFromSidebar(_:)` treats a tap on the destination already selected (`selectedDestination`, so Plus shown as Réglages is a selection) as a re-selection.

No new dependency, no engine change, no `.sensoryFeedback`.
