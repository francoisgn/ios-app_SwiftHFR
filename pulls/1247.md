### Change (deletions; no behaviour change)
- `PlusEntry.owningLot` / `isBuilt` (`RootTab.swift`): always nil / always true, no caller. `RootModelTests` kept the one real fact (the seven entries, in order).
- `ExperimentalFeature.isListed` (`{ true }`) and its guards in `ExperimentalSettings.isOn`/`set` and `ExperimentalTheme.listedFeatures`. The three `isListed` expectations of the feature suites (`SearchTabTests`, `DenseRowsTests`, `MPDarkTopicTests`) go, and `ExperimentalSettingsTests` now checks that every option sits in a section (`Set(listed) == Set(allCases)`, `groups.flatMap(\.features) == allCases`) instead of comparing `allCases.filter(\.isListed)` with itself.
- `ComposerToolStyle.plain` / `current` and the `style:` parameters: the operator's device check of 2026-11-16 kept variant D (chips) ([#895 comment](https://github.com/ezzz/HFRnow/issues/895#issuecomment-6913715068): « variant D (chips) kept as the default »), so the choice is recorded and the bare-glyph variant is deleted. What stays is the chip geometry, in the enum now named `ComposerChip` (`chipInset`, `chipDiameter`); `ComposerToolSize`, `ComposerHeader.buttonSize` and `ComposerControlRow.toolSize` lose their `style:` argument. `ComposerToolSizeTests` lose the `plain` rows and keep what is real (shared size, the 320 pt fit, the cap, the chip in the card and the whole cell outside it).
- `RootModel.favoritesStartupSource`: used by test suites only (the app passes `TopicSource(mode: options.mode)` at `RootModel.swift:209`). The nine suites that read it now pass `TopicSource(mode: .flagsAndFavorites)`, what the app builds in its default mode; the test that compared the constant with itself goes. `categoriesStartupSource`, which the app uses, stays.
- `HapticFeedback.notification(_:)` and its `Kind.notification` / `play` branch (no caller; only `HapticFeedbackTests` used it). The two haptics tests now play impacts only.
- Docs: `LOT-123` (haptics) and `LOT-174` (variant D) no longer name the deleted APIs.
