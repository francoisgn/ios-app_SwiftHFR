### Problem
On iPad regular width, a sub-forum chosen in the « Sous-forum » picker shows its name over an empty list: nothing is requested.

### Cause
`CategoriesSidebarColumn` (`RootSidebarView.swift`, `navigationDestination(item: $navigation.selectedSource)`) builds its `TopicListView` with no `.id(source)`. The picker replaces `selectedSource` while the list is on screen, so SwiftUI keeps the same view and the load `.task` is not run for the new model. The compact `TopicsColumn` has `.id(source)`. Same pattern in the Favoris stack's `openedCategory` destination (`RootTabsView.swift`), where `scope.select` replaces `openedCategory`.

### Change
`.id(source)` / `.id(category)` on those two `TopicListView`s; nothing in `TopicListView` itself (#1414 replaces its load modifier, no overlap). Messages, Plus (already `.id(entry)`) and Recherche do not swap models in place: unchanged.

### Tests
`TopicFilterSidebarHostedTests.aSourceChosenInThePickerLoads(chosen:)` (hosted, 1366 pt regular width, through `scope(for:).select`, for a `.forum` and a `.subforum` source; asserts the FakeDataSource received the request). Red before the fix, green after. Also green: TopicFilterListHostedTests, SidebarDetailHostedTests, RegularWidthListStyleTests, RootModelTests, TabReselectHostedTests (iPad Pro 13" iOS 27.2 simulator, deleted after).
