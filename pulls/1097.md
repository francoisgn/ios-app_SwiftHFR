### Problem
Réglages › Notifications MP had no way to turn MP notifications off while keeping the badge. It also said nothing when iOS's own settings refused notifications or badges: SwiftHFR missing from iOS › Notifications went unnoticed on the device (#1060). Operator decision of 2026-11-18 (scope 3, [comment](https://github.com/ezzz/HFRnow/issues/1060#issuecomment-6969598588)).

### Cause
Legacy has only the badge switch (`AppSettingsView.swift:527-531` @ `87fe0d1c`). LOT-140 added the interval, the last check and a button to iOS's settings, but no notifications switch and no read of `notificationSettings()`.

### Change
- **`SettingKey.mpNotificationsEnabled`** (`mp_notifications_enabled`, HFRStorage): default **on**, so an absent key (every earlier version) keeps today's behaviour. Device scope, in the catalogue.
- **`MPBackgroundCheck`**: with the switch off, no notification is shown or withdrawn. The news is still recorded (turning the switch back on never shows old news late), and the badge still follows « Badge messages non lus ».
- **`NotificationPermission` / `NotificationPermissionReading`** (app): what iOS allows (authorization, badges), read from `UNUserNotificationCenter` by `LiveNotificationPermission`. Tests use a fake.
- **`MPNotificationSettingsModel`**: the switch and the alert.
  - « Notifications » on while iOS refuses → « Notifications désactivées dans iOS » / « Autorisez SwiftHFR dans Réglages › Notifications pour recevoir les messages privés. »
  - Badge on while iOS shows no badge (once iOS has asked) → same title, « …pour voir la pastille sur l’icône. »
  - Buttons « Ouvrir les réglages » (`openNotificationSettingsURLString`) and « Annuler ».
  - iOS is read each time Réglages appears and **every time the app is active again**. Each **kind** of alert is shown at most once per **opening of Réglages, the life of its `SettingsView`** (a pop back from Thème, Icône or Listes is not a new opening; review r2), never over another alert (an alert already pending counts as shown): back from iOS's settings with nothing changed, no loop; notifications allowed there but badges still off, the badge alert. Refused notifications take the badge with them: with « Notifications » on, their alert alone is shown, never a badge alert after it.
  - Turning a switch on checks it again, even after its alert this opening (a user action, not a loop). « Notifications » turned on while iOS never asked (`notDetermined`) shows the system prompt, no alert.
- **Where the checks live** (review r1): `SettingsView` owns the model. The opening check, the scene-phase re-read and the alert are a modifier on its `Form` (`mpNotificationPermissionChecks`), so they run with the section scrolled out of sight. Each toggle's `onChange` is on that toggle. `MPNotificationSettingsSection` now takes the model; its legacy pointer is updated.
- LOT-140 spec: UX evolutions, step 7, Settings, D10, Tests.

Why no wider: `prepare()` still asks for permission at launch (the badge needs it), and the badge path is untouched.
