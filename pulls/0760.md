## What and why
**Problem.** ADR 0013 §6 (Q12): accounts stay device-only by default; the opt-in « Synchroniser mes comptes via le trousseau iCloud » and an optional Face ID / code lock were not built.

**Change.**
- Spec `docs/lots/LOT-173-keychain-sync-lock.md`: the ADR rules as one reconcile table, the second-order criteria of #312, decisions D1–D9 (proposed defaults, for the maintainer to confirm).
- Engine `HFRStorage/AccountSync/` (Foundation-only):
  - `AccountSyncEnvelope` (full / tombstone, v1), `AccountSyncState` (device-only), `AccountSyncMerge` (per-account: newest credentials, consent markers), `AccountSync` (switch-on, switch-off, reconcile, local change).
  - `KeychainDataItem` + `KeychainItemDescriptor`: the synchronizable copy is `hfr.accounts.sync.v1`, `AfterFirstUnlock`, `kSecAttrSynchronizable`; the state is `hfr.accounts.sync-state.v1`, `AfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly`; both in the data-protection Keychain. LOT-065's item is unchanged.
- App:
  - `AccountsModel` reconciles at launch, before the restore, and at each foreground, and reports every real store change (login, refresh that changed something, avatar, removal).
  - Credentials replaced by another device are adopted by the existing `HFRSession` (no navigation rebuild).
  - `AppLockModel` + `AppLockCover`: one window per scene at `.alert + 1` (covers sheets), key while covered, per-scene phase, `ContinuousClock` grace, no animation.
  - Settings sections « Comptes » and « Sécurité »; `NSFaceIDUsageDescription`.
- Settings catalogue: `app_lock_enabled` and `app_lock_grace_seconds`, device scope.

**Why this wide.** The issue's acceptance criteria. Not covered here: the LOT-157 wipe (PR #745, not merged) must report its removals through `AccountSync.localChange(.removal)`; this is noted on #745. Three Low points from the review are in #765.

### Mac checks (two devices, one Apple ID, iCloud Keychain on)
1. A logs in and switches on; B (fresh install) launches → accounts appear, switch on.
2. B logs in again → A gets the cookies at its next foreground, and its screens are not rebuilt.
3. A switches off → B switches off at its next foreground and keeps its accounts; B logged out of every account is never opted in again.
4. Lock with « Après 1 minute »:
   - away 2 min → cover and Face ID;
   - cancelling keeps the cover;
   - the app switcher shows the cover (two windows on iPad);
   - VoiceOver reads only « Déverrouiller »;
   - an open composer gets no keystroke.
5. Grace « Immédiatement »: unlocking with the device code does not relock in a loop.
6. `xcodebuild build test` on iPhone 18 Pro and an iPad simulator (iOS 27.0); `HFR_KEYCHAIN_TESTS=1` on a signed host; `SecItemAdd` of the synchronizable item does not return −34018.

### For the maintainer (developer account)
Expected: **nothing to enable**. A synchronizable generic-password item in the app's default access group needs iCloud Keychain on the device, not a capability. No entitlement file was added. Mac check 6 confirms this.

### Decisions needed
D1–D9 in the spec. In short:
- D1: per-account merge on equal `enabledAt`.
- D2: consent markers.
- D3: the main account stays per device.
- D4: lock delay 0 / 1 / 5 min, device-only.
- D5: a raced envelope is treated as a tombstone.
- D6: a device whose passcode was removed unlocks.
- D7: confirm the ADR rules.
- D8: a login during the provisional window does not end it (departs from ADR §6, to meet #312's acceptance criterion).
- D9: ADR 0013 appendix E row for the two lock keys.
- The French texts (switch explanation, messages) are to be reviewed by the operator before merge.
