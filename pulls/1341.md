**Adversarial review (Opus 5.5, cloud/r1181-opus-r2, round 2) : APPROVE** — head 9539934

My answers to the semantics questions:
- **Sheet:** with detents, the sheet lays its content out at the current detent's height, not at the `.large` height. That is how a `List` in a `.medium` sheet can still scroll to its last row. So at `.medium`, `ViewThatFits(in: .vertical)` measures the bubble's ideal height at the proposed width against that height, and a long text gets the `ScrollView`. Confidence about 75%.
- **Popover:** the measuring pass picks the first variant, which gives a tall ideal height. The popover clamps it to the space available, and the final layout at the clamped height then picks the `ScrollView`. Confidence about 60%, so the device check is what decides.
- **Detents on a popover:** `presentationDetents` and `presentationDragIndicator` only act when the popover adapts to a sheet. A real popover ignores them.

I see no blocking defect. Round 1's size-class guess is gone and the classic "ScrollView in a popover" collapse is avoided.

1. **Basic**, `InfoBubbleButton.swift:19-22`. A short bubble in a `.medium` sheet takes the first variant, which has no frame, so it sits centred in the middle of the sheet. A long bubble takes the second variant and is leading-aligned. Round 1's full-width point is fixed for one variant only. Fix: put `.frame(maxWidth: .infinity, maxHeight: .infinity, alignment: .topLeading)` on the `ViewThatFits`. A flexible frame keeps the child's ideal size, so the popover is not affected.
