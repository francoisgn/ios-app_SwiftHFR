### Problem
Logged out, the author menus (reader, filtered posts sheet) and the profile sheet offered no way to block: "Blacklist" is hidden because the blocklist is per account (LOT-118), and nothing told the reader why. Operator decision D3 of LOT-159 (2026-11-24): show « Connectez-vous pour bloquer », which opens the login.

### Cause
`PseudoListActionHandler` and `UserProfileModel.offers(.blocklist)` simply return false without an account; there was no stand-in entry.

### Change
- New action kind `signInToBlock` (title « Connectez-vous pour bloquer », priority of the blocklist, author menu only) and `SignInToBlockActionHandler`, available only while `PseudoListManager.isAvailable(.blocklist)` is false. `PseudoListActionHandler.all(...)` registers it next to Blacklist/Whitelist; the reader (`TopicReaderView`) and the filtered posts sheet (`FilteredPostsMenuModel.wantsSignIn`) both use it and present `LoginView` through `signInToBlockSheet`.
- Profile sheet: `UserProfileModel.offersSignInToBlock` / `requestSignIn()` / `wantsSignIn`, a button with a VoiceOver hint, same sheet.
- Unchanged: Blacklist/Whitelist logged in, Whitelist logged out (global list, LOT-118), "Alerter", own-post rules.
- LOT-159 spec: D3 moved from open decisions to decisions.
- Scope settled on #1297: only Blacklist is replaced. Whitelist stays offered logged out (global list, LOT-118); the reader's message « + » menu never carried list entries (`MessageMenuPolicy`) and is untouched; the author menu is the reader surface.
- The reader's list handlers come from one function the view calls (`TopicReaderModel.pseudoListHandlers`), so a test sees what the reader registers.
