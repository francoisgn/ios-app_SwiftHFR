Closes #839
Claim: mac/M1

**Problem.** `ReaderSettingsWatchTests` flaked: the watch delivered a settings change after `stop()`.

**Cause.** A change that resumed the watch task is queued on the main actor. `stop()` cancels the task, but the queued loop body still ran `read()` and `changed()` afterwards.

**Change.** The loop body now returns when its task is cancelled (`guard let self, !Task.isCancelled`). `stop()` and the body both run on the main actor, so `stop()` cancels first and the body sees it. Nothing else changes.

**Tests.**
- `aChangeQueuedBeforeStopIsNeverDelivered` forces the interleaving: the task is parked, a change is posted, then `stop()` runs with no suspension in between, over 20 iterations.
- The count is snapshotted once the task is parked, because `didChangeNotification` is process-wide and another test may post.
- Without the guard it fails on every expectation, on iPhone and iPad.
- Full suite: iPhone green (1476 tests). iPad green except the known flake #564 (`TopicRowActionsTests.aRemovalInFlightIsNotSentTwice`).
