### Problem
#1073 asks every tab to look like Catégories. Measured on current `dev-swift` (`1df2121a`), iOS 27.0 simulators, fixture data, logged out (no account):

- **iPhone 18 Pro Max, landscape** (the scene really rotated, 956 × 440 pt): after #955 every tab is a stack, and every list is the same inset-grouped block. The cards span x = 78–878 pt in Catégories, Favoris, Messages, Plus, Recherche and Réglages. The « énormes marges » of #648/#1073 (Messages in the old landscape split) no longer reproduce.
- **iPad Pro 13, landscape** (1376 × 1032 pt window; the iPad scene refuses a programmatic rotation, `UISceneErrorDomain` 101): Catégories and Messages show the grouped grey column with white blocks and the title card. **Favoris is the odd one out**: a plain white list, no block, the large title on a separator line. The same goes for a category's topic list pushed in the Catégories column.

### Cause
`TopicListView` (Favoris, Drapeaux, a category's topics) leaves the list style automatic. `ForumListView` (`.listStyle(.insetGrouped)`, #427) and `MessageListView` (#576, #803) set it explicitly. On an iPhone the automatic style is the same inset-grouped block, so #808 (#803) saw no difference there. In an iPad split column the automatic style is a plain list (measured here), so Favoris differs from the other two.

Legacy sets no list style either: the lists at `Favorites.swift:666`, `MainWindow.swift:663` and `MPListView.swift:580` @ `87fe0d1c` have none. Only `AnswerView.swift:1911` and `PollView.swift:209` set one. Favoris and the topic list also hide the system background and paint the theme palette behind the list: `.scrollContentBackground(.hidden)` plus `.background(themePalette.listBackgroundColor)` (`Favorites.swift:744-745`, `MainWindow.swift:789-790`). Legacy's iPad look is **not measured**, because `87fe0d1c` does not build (AGENTS.md §2).

### Change
- `Navigation/TopicListView.swift`: `.listStyle(.insetGrouped)`, explicit, as Catégories and Messages. The legacy pointer now cites legacy's list lines and says its iPad look is not measured. On an iPhone nothing changes, because the automatic style was already this block (Pro Max cards unchanged at 78–878 pt; whole target green).
