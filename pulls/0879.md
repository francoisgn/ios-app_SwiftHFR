**Problem.** The reader's top "…" menu listed Filtrer and the page items before Hors ligne and Rechercher. Legacy puts Rechercher first and the page items last (`MessagesView.swift:5575-5604` @ 87fe0d1c).

**Cause.** The four secondary items came from three separate `.toolbar` modifiers, and the system orders those outermost first.

**Change.**
- One `ToolbarContent` (`ReaderOverflowToolbar`) declares the order: Rechercher, Hors ligne, Filtrer, then the page items under a separator.
- `ReaderSearchFlow` and `ReaderOfflineSave` no longer add toolbar items, and the conditions under which they are offered are unchanged.
- The offline items live in `ReaderOfflineMenuItems`, so saving a page does not re-run the reader's body.
- Répondre is not in the overflow: it stays a bottom-bar button, unchanged.
- A pure builder (`ReaderOverflow.entries`) pins the order.

**Scope.** #645 ships alone: the triage put it in Group R, and mac/M1 decided not to take the whole group. #820 and #821 build on `ReaderOverflowToolbar`.

**Tests.** `ReaderOverflowTests`: 2 tests on the pure builder (the full order, and missing entries keep the order). Full gate green on iPhone and iPad.

**Mac check wanted:** the "…" order on iPhone and iPad (Rechercher, Hors ligne, Filtrer, separator, page items).

No `### Ledger` section: this restores the legacy order and changes no GAP row.
