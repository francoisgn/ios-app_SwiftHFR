## Problem

The survey did not ask which experimental options (#903) members want by default, and D1 listed the sidebar sections as text only, although the cards exist since #914.

## Change

- **X1**: new multiple-choice question, wording and ids as proposed in #919, on its own screen after D2 and before the cards, no condition (iPhone profiles see it too). `aucune` is exclusive (checking it unchecks the others and the reverse).
- New question type `choix-multiple` (the only architecture addition, needed because every existing choice is single): stored as a list of codes in `questions.md` order; the question's `exclusive` field names the exclusive code. Validation (also used by `--corriger`), stats (each code counted over the respondents of the question, so shares do not sum to 100), analysis export (one column, codes joined by a space) and the end-screen comparison handle it.
- `questions.md` (new « Choix multiple » line and reading rule), `cadrage-construction.md` §2 (row, budgets 6 min 55 s / 4 min 55 s) and `formulaire-spec.md` (type list) updated. `donnees.md` unchanged: only answer ids are stored, no new kind of data.
- **D1 cards**: the 9 cards are copied to `web/visuels/D1-cartes/` (embedded in the binary), referenced by each D1 section in `questionnaire.json` with the alt text of `inventaire.md`, and shown next to each section's state. Sort and order behaviour unchanged; one image per section is moved, not recreated, on re-sort. CSP unchanged (`img-src 'self'`).

## Tests
