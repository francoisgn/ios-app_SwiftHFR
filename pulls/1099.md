## What and why

**Problem.** #246 asks that everything follow the selected account: drafts, quotes, MPs and their badge, the background MP check, MPStorage, lists; and that the session's state be shown plainly.

**Cause / audit** (spec `docs/lots/LOT-156-multi-account.md`, legacy @ `87fe0d1c`). Most of it already followed the account, by construction of LOT-081 (the root is rebuilt on a new session object, one `HFRSession` per account): lists, drafts (per topic and account), the multi-quote selection (each account's own `quotes*` cookies), MPStorage (one model per account), the Messages tab badge (count per session generation), the background check (current account, per-account ledger), Q-8. Two gaps remained:
- the **app icon badge** kept the previous account's count after a switch or a logout: a root shows `nil` ("leave the icon alone") until a page gives a count, and logged out no page ever does. Legacy resets `nb_mp` to 0 at every switch and logout (`MultisManager.m:107-123`, `:155-166`; `HFRplusAppDelegate.m:704-706`, `:788-793`), and the icon follows it (`MainWindow.swift:1564-1567`, `:1779-1784`);
- **Comptes** said nothing about when a session lapses (legacy neither, `AccountMenuViews.swift:11-26`).

**Change.**
- `RootModel.clearsIconBadgeBeforeCount(accountName:initial:)`: a root logged out, or rebuilt on a new session (`initial` set), makes `appIconBadge` 0 until its first count. A window's first root with an account still leaves the icon alone (GAP-192: the background check set it for that account).
- `AccountRow.sessionExpiry`: the earliest expiry of the stored `md_user`/`md_passs` (HFR sets both for 30 days at login and on the logged-in home page, followed list and profile page, HFR-PROTOCOL §6b `W03`, `R74`). Shown only for a `valid` session and a date still ahead: « Session valable jusqu’au 18 décembre »; « Session expirée : reconnectez-vous »; a footer explains the 30 days, renewed whenever the app opens the forum with the account, and that drafts, lists and settings stay.
- No wider: nothing else needed a change; the audit table records where each item is handled and tested.
