**Problem.** In a continuation (75 % opacity) the ordinary quote header, the signature, the trailer and its count bubble are `--secondary` faded with their surface: 2.8 to 4.1:1 (quote header on the allowlist or moderation tint down to 2.8; signature, trailer and bubble 3.15 light / 3.61 dark on the page).

**Change** (`reader.css`, no other theme touched): `.message.continuation { --secondary-faded: color-mix(in srgb, var(--secondary) 30%, var(--text)) }`; `b.s1`, `.signature`, `.trailer`, `.image-link`, `.deleted-note`, `.blocked-summary` of a continuation use it as colour, the count bubble as background. 70 % toward the page text gives ≥ 5.03:1 in light and ≥ 5.67 in dark on the worst surface (bubble 5.67 / 6.12). A dedicated variable, since a custom property cannot refer to itself. The folded quote header keeps its #1358 rules (higher specificity). The last three selectors share colour and surface with the tested ones; no hosted test draws them.

**Tests.** `ReaderAllowlistContrastWebTests` (app-hosted, macOS CI): the probe also returns the ordinary quote header, signature, trailer and bubble; 12 new tests (plain, allowlisted, moderation continuation × modern/classic × light/dark); each fails without the change on the computed pre-fix values.
