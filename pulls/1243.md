## What and why
**Problem.** On an image-heavy page (6 images at a time, the others waiting), leaving the reader before they finish (a tab switch) runs `ReaderWebController.stopLoading()`. A ready document is kept, and its image tasks, running and waiting, are cancelled. On return `show` skips the same document, so those images were never asked again: blank, or a text link (`reader.js` turns an `error` into a link), until « Actualiser ».

**Change.**
- `ReaderWebController`: `inFlightSources` keeps the source of every image request the policy let through, running or waiting; it is cleared on completion, on `stop(task)` and on a new document. `stopLoading()` copies them to `cutImageSources` for a ready document, before `webView.stopLoading()`. The next `show` of that same ready document calls `retryCutImages()`: one `hfrReader.retryImages(sources)`. A process death clears the list, since its reload asks for every picture again.
- `reader.js`:
  - `replaceWithLink` returns how to put the picture back (link node, avatar with its `has-image` wrap). `load`'s and the video thumbnail's `error` handlers record that in `failedLoads` by source.
  - `retryImages(sources)` restarts the loads of those sources that have no pixels yet (`complete && naturalWidth > 0` is left alone, since a cut load may end with neither picture nor `error`), then puts back and refetches the ones that failed.
  - Nothing outside the list is touched, the page does not reload and does not move.

The reload was tried first and dropped in review: it could not keep the place on pages whose pictures carry no dimensions, and it refetched everything.

## How it was verified
Read, not run here (hosted app tests; macOS CI must show them green):
- `ReaderPresenceWebTests.aShownPageStoppedWithImagesInFlightShowsThemWhenItComesBack`: a transport holds the page's picture; ready, `stopLoading()`, `resume()` + `show`, release. The picture ends loaded, there is no `.image-link`, and `ready` fired once (no reload). On the base it stays blank or a link.
- `ReaderPresenceWebTests.aCutPictureThatFailedIntoALinkIsPutBackAndFetchedAgain`: the first answer is a 404, so the page shows a real link; after `retryImages` the picture is put back, loaded, and fetched again (request count grows). On the base `retryImages` does not exist.
- SwiftLint 0.65.1 `--strict`: clean; `node --check reader.js`: passes.
- Device check owed (a real tab switch on an image-heavy page): to be listed on #893.
