## Problem
`Message.quotes` (`MessageQuote`: author, depth, cited numreponse) had no producer. The quote engine (LOT-049) and "posts quoting me" need it.

## Cause
Legacy only read the quote author, inline, for its own/blacklist policy (`ParseMessagesOperation.m:191-226`). It never computed the cited number, depth or anonymous quotes.

## What changed, and why this wide
- `MessageQuoteAnalyzer.quotes(inContentHTML:)` (public) and `quotes(in: Element)` (internal), for raw `contentHTML` and LOT-034 `MessageContent.html`. No policy, no rewriting, no URL followed.
- Iterative walk: SwiftSoup's `parents()` crashed the test thread at 500 nested tables.
- Spec `docs/lots/LOT-032-quote-analysis.md`, HFR-PROTOCOL §6c "LOT-032", LESSONS 52. Constructing `Message.quotes` is left to the `TopicPage` orchestration.

## Oracle (decided on #59)
The LOT-003 oracle has no per-quote field. The legacy-derivable subset (authors, anonymous count) is recomputed from `dicoHTML` with the legacy rule. Cited numbers and depth are new facts, checked on the captures by an independent regex reader.

## Tests (all fail without the change: the type did not exist; mutations checked)
- `MessageQuoteOracleTests`: authors and anonymous count equal legacy on 20 cases; cited numbers and quote count match the captures (135 total); nested case R28/R32a/R32b; same result on LOT-034 output.
- `MessageQuoteAnalyzerTests`: nesting and order, own-header scoping, anonymous, `oldcitation` (inline and the R33b capture), spoiler not a quote, text normalization, fragment rejection, whole class tokens, 2 000 nested tables.
- Mutations: dropping `oldcitation` or flattening depth fails 2 and 4 tests.

## Pending capture
Nested authored quote; quote of a deleted/unknown account; header fragment not `t<n>`; `oldcitation` on an oracle topic page.
