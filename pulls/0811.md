**Problem.** Xcode shows 5 warnings from 3 sites: the unused `Bool` of the private `NotificationLedgerStore.update` (`recordNews`, `markCheckedIfDue`; twice because HFRStorage is built twice) and a non-Sendable closure converted to `@Sendable` in `ForumScopeBar`.

**Change.**
- `recordNews` and `markCheckedIfDue` use `_ = try update(…)`: their result travels through the captured `news` / `due`, so the `Bool` means nothing to them. `@discardableResult` was not used, so a future caller that needs the `Bool` still gets the warning.
- `ForumScope.select` is `@MainActor (TopicSource) -> Void`: its only producer (`NavigationModel.scope(for:)`) is `@MainActor` and writes main-actor state; a main-actor closure converts to `Binding`'s `@Sendable` isolated(any) setter.

No behaviour change.

**Tests.** `swift test` in `Packages/HFRKit`: 1940 tests, 228 suites, all passed (the NotificationLedger tests cover the store; no test fails without the change, since it is warning-only).
