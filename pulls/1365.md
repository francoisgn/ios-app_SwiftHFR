## Problem
The operator's Xcode build of dev-swift shows 7 « main actor-isolated … from a nonisolated context » warnings in five app files (table on #1364).

## Cause
- `RowTint`, `ComposerHeader`, `MessageRowView` are `View`s, so their statics are main-actor by inference, yet pure value-type helpers (`ListLook.selectedStrength`, `MinimumHitRegion.overhang`, `MessageRowInfoLine.make`) call them.
- `PagerToolbar` is not a `View`: its builders are nonisolated but build main-actor views (`PagerPageMenuPill.init`).
- `TabBarReselectionObserver.Coordinator` was `@MainActor` while its `NSObject` overrides `responds(to:)` / `forwardingTarget(for:)` are nonisolated and read `previousDelegate`.

## Change
- `nonisolated` on the pure `RowTint.strength`, the constant `ComposerHeader.hitSize` and `MessageRowView.avatarSubject` (no main-actor state touched).
- `@MainActor` on the view-building members of `PagerToolbar` (`toolbarContent`, `strip`, `stripRow`, `classicRow`, `statusView`), not on the struct: its memberwise init and `pageActions` are used from nonisolated tests (`PagerVisibilityTests`), which stay unchanged.
- `Coordinator` is `nonisolated` as a type; its UIKit-touching members (`attach`, the two delegate methods, the static helpers) are `@MainActor` one by one; the two NSObject lookups only read the weak reference. No `@preconcurrency`, no `nonisolated(unsafe)`, no `assumeIsolated`. No behaviour change, no test edited.
