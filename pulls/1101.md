### Problem
In legacy at `87fe0d1c`, the author can add a poll from the composer ("Ajouter un sondage", `AnswerView.swift:873-906`, `TopicPollDraft.swift`). SwiftHFR had no way to do it.

### Cause
Never ported: legacy added it after `2440d79` (GAP-099).

### Change
- **Engine** (`HFRClient`):
  - `PollDraft`: legacy's draft and validation, plus the one-line and BMP rules the other fields already have.
  - `ForumForm.offersPollCreation`: legacy's test, an unchecked `have_sondage` box and a `textreponse0` input (`ReplyService.swift:279-281`).
  - `PostingFlow.edit(…, poll:)`:
    - refuses a poll on a form that does not offer one (`pollNotOffered`), and an incomplete poll (`invalidPoll`);
    - checks each poll field against the form fetched at send;
    - then sends `have_sondage=1`, the question, the answers packed from slot 1, `max_votes`, `allowvisitor` and the closing date in the Gregorian calendar. Every unused answer slot and date input goes out empty.
  - The write is sent once and confirmed by the edit sentence (`W41`).
- **App**:
  - `ComposerPoll` state, a poll row with legacy's texts, and `PollCreationSheet`.
  - The row shows on a new topic and on the edit of a public first post.
  - Send stays off while the poll has a problem.
  - The poll is sent through `LiveReplyPoster.edit`.
  - When a poll is set and the form fetched at send cannot take it, the message says to remove the poll.
- **Why no wider**:
  - A poll with a **new topic** stays off, exactly as in legacy: no new-topic form has `have_sondage` (`R45a`, `R170a`).
  - Posting through `nouveau_sondage.htm` needs its answer captured first: #1100. That issue also covers the answer to a closing date, which is sent today on legacy's format alone.
  - Spec completed in `docs/lots/LOT-100-poll-creation.md`.
  - Fable's non-blocking round 3 findings are in #1104.

### Tests
- `PollCreationTests` (HFRKit, Linux, green):
  - the body reproduces `W41`'s field for field (names and values; boxes sent as a browser sends them), and the edit is confirmed;
  - `offersPollCreation` on 6 captured forms and on `R170a`;
  - nothing is posted on `R62a`, on a form whose `have_sondage` box is already checked, for 10 invalid polls, or for 4 forms that do not serve a poll field the way `R63b` does;
  - unused inputs go out empty;
  - 10 answers, packing, visitors and the closing date;
  - the closing date is written in a fixed time zone, and the year stays Gregorian on a device using the Buddhist calendar.
- Mutations, each making the tests fail:
  - removing the `offersPollCreation` guard;
  - removing `have_sondage=1`;
  - removing the per-field checks;
  - removing the emptying of unused inputs;
  - removing the Gregorian calendar.
- `PollComposerTests` (app; Compile check green on 4373bc8):
  - the poll is sent with an otherwise untouched first-post edit;
  - removing the poll;
  - an incomplete poll blocks Send;
  - no poll row on a later post or a private thread;
  - on a new topic the row is shown but off;
  - a failed form read, then a retry;
  - `pollNotOffered` at send keeps the poll on screen and blocks Send;
  - the poll-specific message when the form cannot take the poll;
  - the messages.
- HFRKit `swift test` is green; `swiftlint --strict` reports nothing on the changed files.
