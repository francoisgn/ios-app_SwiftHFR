### Problem
Réglages › Stockage MP offered, behind the experimental option « Stockage MP détaillé » (#859), one switch per function, each with an ⓘ popover. The operator wants legacy's single switch kept in Réglages, only « MP multiples → dark topic » moved to Réglages › Expérimental as its own option with a description under the toggle and no ⓘ, and the storage-message creation question to stay (« Oui » preselected, a confirmation when it is deselected). Decision of 2026-11-17 on #1014 (point F and the comment of 09:53).

### Cause
`experimental.mpStorageSwitches` gated per-function switches (`MPStorageFunctionRow`, `MPStorageFunctionInfo`, the per-account `disabled` list). The creation question was a Créer / Annuler alert.

### Change
- **Réglages › Stockage MP**: the single « Activer le stockage MP » switch, as legacy. The per-function rows, their ⓘ popovers and the section footer are removed.
- **Réglages › Expérimental**: `ExperimentalFeature.mpDarkTopic` (`experimental.mpDarkTopic`, off by default, device-only) replaces `mpStorageSwitches`; its description sits under the switch (`DescribedFeatureRow`), no ⓘ, no popover. Code in `App/SwiftHFR/Experimental/MPDarkTopic/`, markers `// EXPERIMENTAL(mpDarkTopic, #1014)`. `MPStorageSyncModel.isEnabled(.mpFlags)` also needs the option; the blacklist and bookmarks follow legacy's single switch.
- **Migration, tolerant** (`LegacySettingsNormalizer`, run at launch, before `ExperimentalSettings` is created: `SwiftHFRApp.init` builds it after the normalizer since round 1): `experimental.mpStorageSwitches` true → `experimental.mpDarkTopic` true (never over a choice already made on the new key); false, unreadable or absent writes nothing; the stale key is removed only after it was read. #859's per-account `disabled` list is no longer read; a member who had switched `mpFlags` off for an account keeps it off: when the model gets its environment the option goes off and that account's `mpFlags` entry is removed so it never blocks the option again (nothing deleted on the forum; the record type and its decoding are unchanged). The option is device-wide, so one account's entry turns it off for every account (conservative); the rule is written in LOT-130.
- **Known behaviour change**: a member who never turned #859's option on had the dark topic effectively on (every function was on while the option was off); it is off now, as the operator asked (« off by default »).
- **Creation question** (operator comment): `MPStorageCreationChoice` (pure state) and `MPStorageCreationQuestionView` replace the Créer / Annuler alerts (Réglages and the first-blocklist-write one): « Oui » preselected; « Valider » on « Oui » creates; deselecting « Oui » raises a popup « Sans stockage MP » listing what is lost (shared blacklist, message bookmarks, « MP multiples → dark topic », worded from `MPStorageFunctionInfo`); « Confirmer » records `declined` for the account, « Revenir » restores « Oui ».
