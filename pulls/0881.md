Closes #565
Claim: mac/M1

**Problem.** On iPhone, the page pager of a paged list (topic lists, Favoris, MP list, search results) sits inside the floating tab bar's frame and cannot be used (LOT-185 follow-up).

**Cause.** `PagerToolbar` used the system `.bottomBar` placement, which the iPhone TabView puts inside the floating tab bar's frame. The frames measured on #565 show it: pager at y=803..841, tab bar at y=791..874.

**Change.**
- `PagerPlacement(rootHasTabBar:)` and a `.pagerBar` modifier replace the toolbar content at the three call sites.
- `RootNavigationView.rootHasTabBar(idiom:sizeClass:)` is the single decision between the sidebar and tab bar roots, and it feeds the `\.rootHasTabBar` environment value.
- Placement follows the root, not the size class: a Plus or Pro Max iPhone in landscape is regular width and still has the tab bar.
- Tab root (every iPhone, compact iPad): the controls are a `safeAreaBar` glass strip above the tab bar. `ViewThatFits` drops the first and last buttons when the strip is too narrow. The strip is hidden while the search field is focused.
  - The bar stays attached even when there is no pager, with zero height. Attaching it only when a pager exists would change the content's identity, and search would lose focus and scroll position.
- Sidebar root (iPad, regular width): unchanged, system bottom bar items, no `safeAreaBar` attached.
- `RootNavigationView.usesSidebarRoot` is now a static function; no behaviour change.

**Tests.** `PagerPlacementTests` has 8 tests, including "a phone at regular width has the tab bar". Inverting `rootHasTabBar` fails 5 of them (output recorded). Full suite green on iPhone 18 Pro and iPad Pro 11 (1483 tests).

**Not covered.** The tests cover the placement decision, not the layout itself. Still wanted, on a device or in the simulator:
- the strip visible and tappable above the tab bar on iPhone 18 Pro, in light, dark, Dynamic Type XL and Reduce Transparency;
- a Pro Max in landscape;
- Slide Over on iPad;
- the bottom edge of an unpaged list (empty `safeAreaBar`).

Known nit left (round 3, to avoid a 4th round): `PagerToolbar.swift:5` starts with `///` instead of `//`, so part of the port header shows in Quick Help.

No `### Ledger` section: GAP-221 and GAP-024 stay accurate.
