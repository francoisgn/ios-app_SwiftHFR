### Problem
With the modern composer (Réglages › Expérimental), the reader's docked card slid behind the keyboard. It happened past about 12 lines at 100 % (#1286), and after a smiley was inserted from the picker (#1285). It came back only when the keyboard was lowered.

### Cause (measured)
On the iPhone 18 Pro simulator, iOS 27.2, with the real software keyboard (328 pt), I hosted the **real `TopicReaderView`** (fixture page, its own reply path, no login, no network). The measurements and tables are on #1285.
- `ReaderStateOverlay` stacked the bottom pull-to-refresh icon beside the page with `.padding(.bottom, 96)` (#1207, `de5ccd99`, merged 2026-11-19, the day before the report). That gave the reader's page a **minimum height of about 135 pt**.
- The docked bar is a `safeAreaInset(.bottom)` of that page. Once the bar plus 135 pt is taller than the space between the navigation bar and the keyboard, the stack overflows **both ways**: the card grows upwards and its bottom slides behind the keyboard, and the page runs under both bars. At 100 %, the card's bottom was 5 pt behind the keyboard at 8 lines and 37 pt behind at the cap. At 130 %, it was 11 pt behind from 4 lines.
- Over a page that can shrink (a plain `ScrollView`), the same text keeps the card 8 pt above the keyboard at every line. The cap (`ComposerCardLayout`, `ComposerKeyboardRoom`) is not the cause.
- #1285: the picker hides the keyboard, and an insert shows it again within 0.5 s. The bar comes back exactly where it was: 8 pt above with 3 lines, 15 pt behind with 10 lines, before and after the insert alike. The panel only revealed a reply already past the overflow line.
- Also: the reader's inline navigation bar measures **54 pt**, not the 44 pt `ComposerCardLayout.navigationBarHeight` assumed (#1136 asked for a measure), so the room was 10 pt too tall.

### Change
- **Source** (`ReaderStateOverlay`): the two pull icons and the state content are overlays of a `Color.clear` base. The base always exists (over a shown page the content builds no view), fills the space with no minimum height, and takes no touch. The icons never size the page, and they are drawn where they were: the pulled screenshots are pixel-identical to the `ZStack` before this PR. Under a docked composer, the page now ends exactly at both bars.
- **Backstop** (`ReaderComposerPresenter`): the page gets exactly what the bar leaves it (`.frame(minHeight: 0, maxHeight: .infinity)` on the content, always attached, so the page's identity never changes). Any other page with a minimum height overflows under the bars, never pushing the bar. This covers every docked composer (reply, edit, the author menu's MP) and every panel.
- `ComposerCardLayout.navigationBarHeight` 44 → 54 (measured). The cap now lands at 12 lines at 100 % on this screen, as the operator observed.
- Not changed: the keyboard room model, the panels, #853's interactive keyboard drag-down (`keyboardDismissMode`, untouched; its test `underADockedComposerDraggingThePageDownPullsTheKeyboardDown` passes), the legacy composer (`isDocked` only).
