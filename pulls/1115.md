### Problem
The unfolded iPhone Duo (a `.phone` that reports regular width and regular height) showed the tab bar with stretched iPhone stacks. The rotation setting also locked it. Legacy at the reference gives it the split root (sidebar, list and topic), shows the topic full width in portrait, does not lock its rotation, and keeps the topic read across a fold or an unfold (GAP-210, GAP-213, GAP-215, GAP-217).

### Cause
`RootNavigationView.usesSidebarRoot` was the `2440d79` rule (`idiom == .pad && regular width`). `OrientationPolicy` had no unfolded case.

### What changed, and why this wide
- `RootNavigationView.usesSidebarRoot(idiom:horizontal:vertical:)`: the legacy rule (`MainWindow.swift:1380-1388` @ 87fe0d1c). The root switch moves into `RootLayout` so a hosted test can drive it over a given `RootModel`. Its `rootLayoutIdiom` override lets the test pin the idiom.
- `RootSidebarView` + `DuoSplitColumns`: legacy `prefersDetailOnlyTopic`. On a `.phone` in portrait, an open topic takes the whole width. Landscape, or no topic, brings the list back. Every other case keeps the user's choice. The list-back half applies to the iPad too, as legacy's `ensureVisibleIPadListIfNeeded` (`:2281-2290`): an iPad whose user hid the columns gets list and topic back when the topic goes. The orientation is measured in `RootLayout`, which outlives a fold, and seeds the split's visibility, so an unfold in portrait with a topic open starts with the topic alone.
- Fold handoff: both roots already read one `RootModel` (LOT-170), so the topic and its reader/page carry over. A collapsed tab split with its topic selected shows the reader by itself, measured for Favoris, Catégories (three columns) and Messages; an explicit start column was tried and dropped. `RootModel.rootSwapped()` closes the readers that links pushed, because legacy carries only the root reader. This also applies to an iPad window crossing regular width (Slide Over), as in legacy. Intentional difference: each tab keeps its own topic, not only the selected tab's (see spec).
- `OrientationPolicy`: unfolded → `.allButUpsideDown` whatever `landscape_mode`; `refresh()` does not ask an unfolded scene to rotate; a height-class change (fold) refreshes it (`Common.swift:187-200, 216-242`).
- Spec: legacy counterpart, behaviour, differences, and what is left.
Not touched: the FEAT-016 design, the cover-screen thumb actions, inline titles (#681: no hang), the Plus carry.
