**Problem**: with the modern composer (Réglages › Expérimental), a new private message opens at partial height over the MP list; its close and send buttons are clipped at the top and its tool row sits half under the suggestion bar (operator screenshot, 2026-11-18). Partial height keeps the page behind visible, which helps when answering but not for new content.

**Cause**: every composer opened at partial height with a toggle (#653), whatever it writes; `ComposerHeight.shown` / `offersToggle` knew only the device and the text size.

- The glass buttons (close and send; drafts only for a topic's reply) are already the first row of the full-height composer, above the Destinataire/Sujet fields, at the shared size of #1129 (`ComposerToolSize`); no placement code changes. A hosted check pins that they sit in the top band under the safe area, above the fields, inside the screen's edges.
- Folded in from the last review of #1129: `.allowsHitTesting(false)` on the legacy bar's title overlay, so at large text sizes the edge of the drafts button under it keeps its tap. No test covers it (a hit test on the overlay needs a device, listed on #893 (comment of 2026-11-19)); the first version had a constant-only test, dropped after review.
- Docs that said « every composer opens at partial height » follow (`ComposerHeight`, `ComposerPlacement`, `ReplyComposerView`, LOT-174's height section); the test `aReplyOrAnEditOpensAtPartialHeightOnlyOnAnIPhone` is renamed.

**Tests**:
