### Problem
In Favoris the first scroll stutters, while the large title leaves and the tab bar minimises. It stutters again on the way back up. The stutter grows with the number of sections and rows.

### Cause (measured in a hosted probe, simulator, no logged-in session)
I counted body evaluations and layout passes in a hosted Favoris tab: `TabView` with `.tabBarMinimizeBehavior(.onScrollDown)`, `NavigationSplitView`, `TopicsColumn`, and 10 categories × 8 topics. The scroll was driven 5 pt per frame. I also stepped the top and bottom safe area to stand in for the bars moving.

| Build | List body runs | Row closures | Header builds | Row layout passes (size/place) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before #1017 (the body reads the title visibility, as `@State titleIsVisible` did) | 1 per crossing | 25 | 10 (every section) | 24 / 36 |
| `dev-swift` today (after #1017) | 0 | 6 (only new rows entering) | 0 | 6 / 9 |

- Before #1017, each crossing of the large title re-ran `TopicListView.body`. That run recomputed `displayedGroups`, rebuilt every section header and re-applied every realised row's swipe, menu and accessibility modifiers. It also laid the rows out again. The cost is O(sections + visible rows), it hits at both crossings, and it lands on the same frames as the tab-bar transition, which fits the operator's report.
- Since #1017 (merged 2026-11-17 11:38 UTC, "Refs #1015"), `TitleVisibility` is read only by `PillSlot`. A crossing redraws the pill and nothing else.
- On today's head, scrolling and the safe-area steps (bottom and top) trigger no body run and no row layout in the list, header, menu or pager. The pager modifier, the header margins and the Favoris options menu were all checked.
- So the cause the code shows is already fixed on `dev-swift`. The operator's report may predate #1017 (LESSONS 46). Check that the device build includes `f53cb5df`.

Not reproducible here: a programmatic scroll does not trigger the real tab-bar minimise. Whether the real minimise moves the content's safe area on a device is not measured. If the stutter remains on a build that includes #1017, the next step is an Instruments trace on the device.

### What changed, and why this wide and no wider
- `TopicListView` with its new `TopicListView+RenderSeam.swift` (kept in its own file to stay under the 400-line `file_length` limit): a test seam. `counting(_:_:)` passes its value through and, in debug builds, counts one build of the list body, of a section header (by group id) or of a row (by topic id), per model. It also keeps the list's `TitleVisibility` so the test can check that the crossings really happen.
  - The calls sit in existing expressions (`let loader = …`, `let isSuper = …`, and the header's returned view), so no `let _ =` is needed in a view builder, which `redundant_discardable_let` forbids. `.swiftlint.yml` is unchanged.
  - Keys are never removed, and an identifier can be reused once its model is freed. The test therefore reads deltas from a baseline.
  - Release builds keep only the pass-through.
- `TitleVisibility.report(_:)`: the large title's `onScrollVisibilityChange` goes through it, and debug builds count the reports.
- `FavorisScrollHostedTests`: the regression guard.
- No behaviour change. No change to the large title, the pill or the tab-bar minimise.
- The other suspects in the code study were left alone because the probe found no cost from them: rows not `Equatable`, no `.listStyle`, `DensityHeaderMargin`, `PagerBarModifier`.

### Tests
`FavorisScrollHostedTests/scrollLeavesTheListAlone()` hosts the app's `FavoritesTabView` (with a fake `ReaderServices`) under a `TabView` that minimises on scroll. Left out: the other tabs and the root's environment.
- **Down leg:** 300 pt past the large title, frame by frame. The title must report it left. Expected: no list body run, no section header build, and no rebuild of any row that was on screen at landing.
- **Safe-area stand-in:** the top and bottom safe area are stepped. Expected: no build at all.
- **Up leg:** back up until the title reports it is back (at least 2 reports). Expected: no list body run and no header build. Rows coming back on screen are built legitimately.
- **Positive controls:** the seam counted the body, the headers and the rows on screen at landing, and the title's reports (at least 1 down, at least 2 round trip).
- **Covered:** the large-title crossings in both directions, and the safe-area stand-in. **Not covered:** the real Liquid Glass tab-bar minimise. A programmatic scroll does not trigger it, and the stand-in is not shown to be what it does. That part is the device check.
- **Mutation runs** (a per-frame read of `titleVisibility.isHeaderVisible`). Each one fails:
  - In the list body: the body runs once going down and twice over the round trip. Every header is rebuilt, and the rows on screen are rebuilt.
  - In `header(_:title:)`: every header is rebuilt at each crossing, and the rows are rebuilt too.
  - In a row (`rows(_:)`): every row on screen is rebuilt at the crossing.
- The suite passed 3 times out of 3 on the head `1fc0500b`. The mutations and the whole `SwiftHFRTests` target ran on `bb0620ee` (1896 tests in 241 suites passed, iPhone 18 Pro simulator, iOS 27.0); `1fc0500b` only turns a test helper's triple into a struct for SwiftLint.
