### Problem
- The operator wants a new slide order for the weekly deck (overview, KPIs, milestones M1–M5, one feature-card slide each for M1–M4, the daily app, M5, today), with the existing style kept and only light iOS-style touches.

- `weekly/build.py`: the 10 slides (order in the README). The tracker is fetched first and the state is written to `OUTDIR/state.json`. `generate.py` reads it through the new `ROADMAP_STATE` env var, so the committed snapshot is never used or rewritten. `--state FILE` reuses a saved state and refuses one older than 6 h. - `weekly/progress.py`: the charts are drawn at 1:1 with text of 20 px or more. They are split into `charts` and `donuts` for the KPI slide.
- `weekly/shot.js`: fails a slide that overflows the page or its box, has overlapping body blocks, has text under 20 px, or contains the word « legacy ».
- `tooling/roadmap/README.md`: the slide order and the format and counting rules.
- No `state.json` or `milestones.json` is committed. This is a Linux-only tooling change: no `App/` or `Packages/` file is touched.
