### Problem
"Alerter" was offered by LOT-087's policy but had no handler, so the entry stayed hidden: a member could not report a message to the moderators (legacy parity, App Review 1.2 report content). The engine side (LOT-050, #395) was merged without its screen.

### Cause
LOT-087 left the alert handler, its sheet and the mail fallback to this lot (`docs/lots/LOT-087-message-actions.md:54`).

### Change
Spec: `docs/lots/LOT-135-moderator-alert-screen.md` (legacy counterpart at `87fe0d1c`, `MessagesView.swift` `ModerationAlertComposerView` and friends).
- `ModeratorAlertModel` (`@Observable @MainActor`): the `.alert` handler. With an alert link and an account it reads the alert page through `ModeratorAlertFlow.prepare` (one at a time, "Chargement de l’alerte…"): already alerted → HFR's sentence as the reader's toast (legacy "Alerte déjà envoyée." when HFR gives none); form → the sheet; error → "Alerte impossible", saying nothing was sent.
- `ModeratorAlertComposerModel` + `ModeratorAlertComposerView`: legacy's sheet "Alerte Modération" (warning, placeholder, Annuler / Envoyer). One send at a time. HFR's confirmation (`W46`) closes it with HFR's sentence. A refusal where nothing was sent (`rejected`, `emptyReason`, `unsupportedCharacters`, `network`) keeps the sheet and the reason. `unconfirmed` closes the sheet with "Alerte non confirmée", and the sheet can never send again (`isFinished`). A form that is no longer usable (`notLoggedIn`, `sessionChanged`, …) closes the sheet.
- Mail fallback (legacy `:4568-4585`): without an alert link (or without an account), and only when the build carries `MODERATOR_ALERT_MAIL_ADDRESS` → Info.plist `ModeratorAlertMailAddress` (Q-10, ADR 0007: the address stays out of source; empty by default, so the fallback is off). Legacy's confirmation is shown first, then `mailto:` with legacy's subject and body, strictly encoded. A malformed or unexpanded address turns the fallback off.
- `LiveModeratorAlerter` wraps the engine flow behind an app protocol (`ModeratorAlerting`), so the tests script it without a session and nothing can reach HFR. It is built in `AppEnvironment` for a current account only. The offline reader has none.
- `ReaderAlertFlow` presents the progress, the sheet and the two alerts. The mail alert hands its `presenting` value to the button (SwiftUI clears the state first, learnings LOT-087).
- A menu reinstall rebuilds the alert model (fresh account services) unless an alert is in progress (`isIdle`).
- `installMenuActions` moved into an extension of `TopicReaderView` (`type_body_length`, review round 1).
- `ReaderServices` moved to its own file: `TopicReaderView.swift` would otherwise pass SwiftLint's 400-line `file_length`. The move changes no code.

No HFR fact is new: links, pages, form and answers are LOT-050's.
