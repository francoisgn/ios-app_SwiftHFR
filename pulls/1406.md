## What and why
**Problem.** With the account sync on, every valid session check rewrote the synchronizable Keychain item, and every other device reported the account `replaced` at its next foreground (audit #1388 A1, confirmed at runtime). That is the engine-side echo behind the #1276 loop; #1385 only guards the app's take-over. Also (issue comments): a copy dated ahead of this device's clock froze the device's renewed expiries and made its re-login lose.

**Cause.** HFR re-sends `md_user` / `md_passs` with a new +30-day expiry on every logged-in answer (`R74`), so `persistSession` always reports `.refresh`; the engine stamped `credentialsAt = now`, the merge kept the later stamp and compared whole `Account` values, dates included.

**Change** (engine, `HFRStorage/AccountSync`; spec LOT-173 "Merge" and "Local change"):
- **Same login** = same `md_user` and `md_passs` values, and `md_id` when both sides hold it (`R74` does not renew it). For the same login, the item keeps its entry, dates and main flag; the device keeps its own account. No write, no `replaced`.
- **Renewal margin (7 days).** The copy takes this device's account when its login-cookie expiry is more than 7 days after the copy's, so a device that bootstraps never imports expired cookies; mirror: a device more than 7 days behind the copy takes the copy's account (keeping its own token and avatar when the copy has none) and reports it `replaced`. ⚠️ **Operator decision:** this relaxes the issue's "writes nothing" to "at most one item write per account and week" (`AccountSyncMerge.renewalMargin`).
- **Main flag.** Every entry kept from the copy keeps the copy's flag; this device's flag replaces it only when its main is an entry of its own side. One main in the copy across all devices' merges.
- **Clamp.** While on, a login, refresh or removal is stamped strictly after the dates the copy and the device hold for that key (like `enabledAt` at a switch-on): a device whose clock is behind still wins with new credentials or a removal. The copy is read once in `localChange` and the push compares against those bytes. Stamping lives in `AccountSync+LocalChange.swift`.

No app change: #1385's guard stays. No legacy counterpart (LOT-173 is SwiftHFR-only), no ledger.
Spin-offs: #1413 (account order rewrites the copy, pre-existing), #1416 (refresh push vs a copy far ahead; unreachable with +30-day cookies).
