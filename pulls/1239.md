### Problem
On the iPhone Duo cover screen the system bars are one vertical strip on the right edge, and only its bottom is within thumb reach (beta feedback on legacy #372). Legacy at `87fe0d1c` (`ea531a2a`) answers it: « Actualiser » of the lists goes to the bottom of that column, and a pushed topic list or topic gets a « Retour » button there. SwiftHFR had neither the cover-screen detection nor the placement (GAP-713).

### Cause
`RootLayout` knew the orientation only; nothing in the app told the cover screen (compact width, tab bar root) from a regular iPhone. And iOS 27.2 itself draws a list's `.bottomBar` items at the top of the strip when the trailing slot is empty: that is legacy's measured defect on Catégories and Messages (Mac check of `87fe0d1c` on #372, checks 2b, 2e), reproduced here and measured (below).
