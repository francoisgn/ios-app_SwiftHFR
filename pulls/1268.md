With one flex item, `align-items` changes nothing, and R353a has such trailers (« Message cité 1 fois » with no edit note). Until then, owe it on #893.
3. **Low**, `reader.css:165`. The 44 pt hit area is short on width: min-width 1.6×0.765em ≈ 20.8 px, plus 22 px, gives about 42.8 px at default size, and less at smaller text sizes. Its bottom 12 px also overlaps by about 4 px the top of the next message's "+" button (`.menu-button`, 44 px tall with margin -12px). The `::before` is positioned and the button is not, so the bubble takes the tap there. Your reply is never shown, and the screen scrolls to the last post that quotes the message. Fix: after a write, switch to `.topic(page.topicID)`.
