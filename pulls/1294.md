## Problem
The « page x / y » pill is a `Menu` label with 12/6 pt of padding and no minimum size. Hosted, it measured 89 x 29 pt at xSmall and 101 x 32 pt at the default size (44 x 32 for the loading spinner): under the 44 x 44 pt of HIG > Buttons. On the iPad sidebar root the same pill also drew its own `glassEffect` inside the `.bottomBar` item group's glass. The strip's ‹ › arrows measured about 35 x 29 to 37 x 31 pt.

## Cause
`PagerPageMenuPill.pill(_:)` had no `frame(minHeight:)` / `contentShape` (the title pill has both, `ScreenTitle.swift:164-166`), one view served both placements, and the strip's arrows had no minimum size.

## Change
- `PagerPageMenu.swift`: the visible capsule is `PagerPillLabel` (text + padding + glass); the pill puts `.frame(minWidth: 44, minHeight: 44)` and `.contentShape(.rect)` around it. `drawsGlass` parameter.
- `PagerToolbar.swift`: `pillDrawsGlass(in: PagerPlacement)` (false in `.bottomBar`, whose item group draws the glass; true in the `.inset` strip) is read by both placements. `stripArrow(...)` gives the strip's ‹ ›, first and last buttons a glass capsule of exactly 44 pt (inner minimum 20 x 30: the glass style adds about 24 x 14 pt, measured on iOS 27.2).
- Same technique as #1292's `minimumHitRegion()`; the two can be unified once #1292 lands.

## What visibly changes (rendered on the simulator, light and dark)
- Tab-root strip (iPhone, compact iPad): pill and arrows are now all 44 pt tall capsules (operator decision 2026-11-24, #1155). Before: pill about 34 pt, arrows about 31 to 36 pt. The pill capsule growing to 44 pt is the cause below, not a choice; the arrows are enlarged on purpose.
- iPad bottom bar: unchanged look, one bar-drawn capsule around the pill (no nesting), 44 pt like the bar's arrows.
- Why the pill capsule is 44 pt and not 34: probed on the simulator, `glassEffect` on a `Menu` label fills the Menu's whole bounds, i.e. the 44 pt frame, whatever the modifier order; it is not the strip's `.glass` button style (the Menu's own `.buttonStyle(.plain)` overrides that: with `drawsGlass: false` inside the glass style no capsule is drawn, and a Menu pill with glass outside the glass style is 44 pt too). A hit region larger than the capsule without moving the layout was not found.
- Screenshots (not committed): ~/agent-work/2026-11-19-ezzz-hfrnow-mac-mbp/1155/shots/fix2-*.

## Tests
`PagerPillTouchTargetTests` (hosted, real views, `onGeometryChange`): pill >= 44 x 44 at xSmall and large, with and without glass; loading state; accessibility3 guard (already above 44 from its text); the label's own capsule hugs its text; `pillDrawsGlass(in:)` per placement; the strip arrow is 44 pt in the glass style. The arrow test was red before the arrow frame (35 x 29 and 37 x 31, 2 issues), then green; whole run 25 tests green on the iPhone 18 Pro simulator with PagerPageMenuTests and PagerPlacementTests. The `pillDrawsGlass` test checks the shared function both placements call, not a rendered result; the renders above cover that.

## Not covered
- Accessibility Inspector hit-area audit (pill and arrows, tab-root strip and iPad bottom bar, default and AX sizes).
- Reduce Transparency rendering (the setting could not be enabled in the simulator runs).
