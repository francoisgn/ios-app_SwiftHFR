## Problem
The « page x / y » pill is a `Menu` label with 12/6 pt of padding and no minimum size. Laid out and hosted it measured 89 x 29 pt at xSmall and 101 x 32 pt at the default size (44 x 32 for the loading spinner): under the 44 x 44 pt of HIG > Buttons. On the iPad sidebar root the same pill also drew its own `glassEffect` capsule inside the `.bottomBar` item group's glass (the second point of the issue).

## Cause
`PagerPageMenuPill.pill(_:)` had no `frame(minHeight:)` / `contentShape` (the title pill has both, `ScreenTitle.swift:164-166`), and the same view was used in both placements.

## Change
- `PagerPageMenu.swift`: `.frame(minWidth: 44, minHeight: 44)` + `.contentShape(.capsule)` inside the label, so the look (glass capsule hugging the text) is unchanged; the frame is the hit region. New `drawsGlass` parameter (default true).
- `PagerToolbar.swift`: the `.bottomBar` placement passes `drawsGlass: false` so the toolbar supplies the glass; the tab-root strip keeps its glass capsule.
- Same technique as #1292's `minimumHitRegion()`; the two can be unified once #1292 lands (not depended on here).
