### Problem
On iPhone the composer looks heavy (operator, 2026-11-12, reference: the Claude iOS app's composer): the control row ("Vider", undo, redo | smileys, GIF, image, "Aa") is two glass capsules under the text card, and the top bar has three large round buttons around the title.

### Cause
The #653 layout ported legacy's `composerHeader` and `composerToolbar` (`AnswerView.swift:768-819`, `:940-1054` @ 87fe0d1c) as they are: a full-size bar on top and the row as its own band under the card.

### Change
iPhone only (`ComposerChrome(isPhone:)`); the iPad composer is unchanged.
- **Controls inside the card** (`ComposerEditorCard.controls`, `ComposerControlRow.inCard`, `FormattingToolbar.inCard`): the row (and the "Aa" bar above it when shown) is the text card's bottom edge, its buttons straight on the card's glass (no glass capsules stacked on glass; the "Aa" bar under a hairline instead of its `.bar` band). The card's content is clipped to its rounded shape, so the bar's transition stays inside. Same buttons, states, labels and two-line `ViewThatFits` rule.
- **Card height** (`ComposerCardLayout.textCardHeight`): the text card is chips + editor + controls; the editor stops at its cap (six lines), so the text grows the card without ever covering the controls. The detent sum (`cardHeight`) is the same parts, now written as "above the card + the text card + gaps". Only `ComposerCardLayout` changes in `ComposerHeight.swift`; no detent or presentation code is touched (#853 owns it; a 3-way merge with `fix/853-composer-page-scroll` is clean).
- **Floating buttons** (`ComposerHeader.floats`): close, drafts and send are small glass capsules (the system's `glassEffect`, interactive, tinted for the prominent state; glyph sized with Dynamic Type), each inside a **44 pt hit area** (`ComposerHeader.hitSize`, padded `contentShape`): the operator decided (2026-11-13) that the capsules stay visually small, as the Claude app's (~30 pt), and the tap target keeps the HIG's 44 pt. The header is an `HStack` (buttons, truncating title, send), so the title never sits under a button at any text size; the title is a light caption. The iPad bar is dev-swift's, unchanged. Same actions, states (send greyed when empty, prominent with text, spinner while sending; close and drafts disabled while sending) and VoiceOver labels; the states are now pure (`ComposerHeader.SendState`, `closeIsEnabled`, `draftsAreEnabled`) so they are tested.
- `// Port of … Differences:` pointers updated in the four views.
