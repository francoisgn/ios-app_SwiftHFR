## Problem
The orchestrator is unavailable for 3 days, and the maintainer's quota runs out in 90-120 minutes. Contributors who lend tokens (Bybifok/moust4ki, Ezzz, others) had no portable brief. Nothing told them what they may take, how to claim without an orchestrator, how to get a review by a different model, or who merges.

## Change
- **`docs/agents/EXTERNAL-AGENTS.md`** (new): one file for any vendor's agent to read. It covers:
  - roles (core / external / maintainer) and reading order;
  - the non-negotiables and a **core-model guard**: a change to `HFRModels` public types, an ADR or the plan becomes a `BLOCKED:` for the core;
  - the two queues: issues labelled `external: ready`, and PRs with a `REVIEW WANTED: <model>` comment;
  - the claim protocol (re-read the comments before and after claiming);
  - the work loop;
  - the **self-spawned cross-model review**, with a fixed reviewer brief, and the cases where a **cross-contributor** review is mandatory (state-changing flows, sessions, auth, cookies, tokens, security findings, `HFRModels`, ADRs);
  - the **model matrix**, October 2026, with its limits: the reviewer is never the author model, the reviewer's tier is at least the lot's tier, and an Anthropic tier-A review is required whenever another vendor's model authored or reviewed the PR;
  - the PR rules, the **5-PR limit** with its alert message and handoff, and the **merge policy**.
- **Merge policy** (operator decision): the author merges a merge-ready PR (criteria plus an adversarial APPROVE on the head), and a merge-ready PR is never left open. Risky flows are merged by the core orchestrator or the maintainer. This is recorded in AGENTS.md §8. §0 and step 4 of the session start, CLAUDE.md and the README agent prompt now say the same instead of "merging is the operator's call".
- **`.github/labels.yml`**: new label `external: ready`.
- **README**: a "Lend your agent" section with the one line to paste.
