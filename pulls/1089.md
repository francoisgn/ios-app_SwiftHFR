## Problem
- **#685.** On a regular-width iPad, Bookmarks and Alertes Qualitay were two-column Plus entries: their list sat in the detail column, so a row would open its topic in that same column. Legacy @ `87fe0d1c` lists them in the content column and opens the topic in the detail column, as Favoris (`IPadSidebarDestination.usesTopicList`, `HFRswift/Swift/MainWindow.swift:1326-1333`; `iPadSidebarRoot` `:1849-1873`; same at `2fe43da3` `:1307-1313, 1796-1851`). Legacy also titles the sidebar sections "Forum", "Outils", "Plus" and the sidebar "Hardware.fr" (`:1878-1901`); SwiftHFR had untitled sections and "HFR".
- **#512** (legacy bug on iPad: after a link pushed a reader in the detail column and an edit was sent from it, tapped rows stayed hidden under the pushed reader). The requested SwiftHFR check, as a hosted test of the real `RootSidebarView`, **holds on `dev-swift`** for a row tap in Favoris and in Catégories, with and without the sent edit. Two neighbouring paths do **not** hold:
  1. a row menu page (LOT-125, "Première page"…) of the topic on screen while a link's reader is pushed: the new page opens **under** the pushed reader (Favoris and Catégories);
  2. a second topic opened in the new Bookmarks / AQ detail column after a link pushed a reader there (same shape).

## Cause
`ReaderColumn` gives its `NavigationStack` an identity from the selection (`.id("\(topic.id)#\(readerGeneration)")`). Measured in `SidebarDetailHostedTests`: when only that identity changes, the split's detail column keeps the view controller a link pushed, and the new reader becomes the root under it (stack `["Sujet 1 p2", "Sujet 1 p4"]`). A row tap escapes it because the content list's selection changes too, and that resets the column; a row menu page of the same topic keeps the selection, and the Bookmarks / AQ column has no list selection at all.

## Change
- `PlusEntry.usesTopicList` (Bookmarks, Alertes Qualitay), legacy's placeholder strings (`detailPlaceholder`, `MainWindow.swift:2048-2089`), titled `SidebarSection`s and the "Hardware.fr" title.
- `RootSidebarView`: those two entries take the three-column split, the entry in the content column (own stack), `PlusDetailColumn` in the detail column: legacy's placeholder, or the reader a row opened. Réglages, Rechercher, Crédits, Charte, Supprimer mon compte keep two columns. Compact width is unchanged.
- `RootModel.detailReaders` / `openInDetail(_:for:)`: one reader per entry, as legacy keeps `bookmarksTopicTarget` / `qualityAlertsTopicTarget` (`:2195-2213`). Lists reach it through the `openTopicInDetail` environment action (nil outside the split, where a list pushes on its own stack).
- `TopicReaderModel.closeLinkedReaders()`, called before a reader is replaced. In `NavigationModel` one `dropReader()` does it for every path that drops the selection or replaces the reader: `selectedTopic` set to another topic or to nil, a source change, a row menu page (`open(_:at:)`) and `reader(for:)` building a new model; re-selecting the same topic keeps its reader. A covered root view now runs its leave and stop path too. `RootModel.openInDetail` calls it for the Bookmarks / AQ column.
- Round 1 (Fable, finding 1): a Favoris section title naming the Catégories forum already open dropped the topic but kept its reader, so tapping the row again showed the page a link had pushed over it; fixed by the drop above.
- `PlusListColumn`: the Bookmarks / AQ content column (own stack, the `openTopicInDetail` action for every screen of it, pushed ones included). `docs/lots/LOT-170-tab-bar.md` points to #685 for the three-column entries.

**Why no wider.** The Bookmarks screen does not exist yet (LOT-132, #222: still a placeholder) and the Alertes Qualitay list is in draft PR #1001 (LOT-133), which pushes its reader in the Plus stack. This PR gives them the column and the action; **wiring their rows is theirs**: in the split, a row calls `@Environment(\.openTopicInDetail)` when it is non-nil, else pushes as today. #1001 also edits `RootSidebarView` (badge line) and `PlusEntryView`: a small merge, flagged for coordination. The size-class handoff legacy does on a fold (`handleRootSwap`, clearing the split targets) is LOT-147's (GAP-217) and is not touched: like Favoris, a detail reader stays across a size-class change.
