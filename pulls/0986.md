## Problem
A tester cannot paste the build, commit, OS and device model into a bug report: the Version row does nothing on tap.

`SettingsView` and `AboutView` show a plain `LabeledContent`; no build records its commit.

- `VersionRow` (both screens): a tap copies the report, shows « Copié » for 2 s and posts a VoiceOver announcement.
- Not changed: project settings, no build phase, no dependency.
