- `/hfr/<cat>/<sub>/sujet_<post>_<page>.htm` (empty slug, with/without `#bas`): **routed** as `.topic`. The title slug and its hyphen are optional in the canonical pattern; R121e shows it as the title link (`title="Sujet n°39106"`) in the cat 22 list.
