## Problem
Legacy had a Christmas mode (red accent, snow in the navigation bar, a one-time alert) hard-coded to 24/12/2022–09/01/2023 and forced off at every launch (`Classes/HFRplusAppDelegate.m:103, 365, 627-677` @ 87fe0d1c), so SwiftHFR has nothing for December. The operator wants Noël soft on by default on 01/12 (THEMES.md §5), in the TestFlight build before then.

## Cause
No seasonal engine: nothing knows the windows, nothing saves and restores the user's theme, no settings for it.
