### Problem

### Cause

- Look: the control now sits inside the list margins (+4 pt) like the category filter, instead of `.padding(.horizontal)` from the screen edges: slightly narrower, aligned with the conversation block.
- `LOT-185-liquid-glass-surfaces.md`: the surface row says the bar is superseded by #1013.
- Overlap: PR #1017 edits two lines of the same file (title tracker) and conflicts around the removed `.safeAreaBar`: a trivial conflict, resolved after the first merge.
