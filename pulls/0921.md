## What and why

### Problem
The operator wants the modern options kept behind **Réglages › Expérimental** switches, so that M4 keeps the legacy behaviour (ezzz's 1:1 non-regression) without losing the modern code; offline save is to be hidden, not removed (#822). This foundation has to land before #855, #817, #859, #895 and #902, which build the legacy side next to each option.

### Change
- **Store** — five device keys in the `SettingKey` catalogue (`experimental.<option>`), added to ADR 0013 matrix E as Device-only. All default to off except `modernComposer`, which stays on until #855.
- **`ExperimentalFeature` and `ExperimentalSettings`** (`App/SwiftHFR/Experimental/`):
  - one `@Observable` store owned by `SwiftHFRApp` and passed through the environment;
  - views read a flag only through `ExperimentalSettings.isOn(_:in:)`;
  - with no settings in the environment (previews, hosted tests), each option takes its default.
- **Réglages › Expérimental**, at the end of the list:
  - one sub-menu per theme that has an option to show, and the spec's footer sentence;
  - each switch has an ⓘ that shows its explanation, and VoiceOver reads the explanation as the switch's hint.
- **No dead switch**: an option is listed only once both of its paths exist.
  - Today the only one is « Composer moderne ».
  - `searchTab`, `mpStorageSwitches` and `imageLoadingChoice` are hidden until their own PRs land.
  - `flagIconRows` is hidden until #817 builds its switch-off path; until then both paths look the same.
  - A hidden option reads as its default, whatever the store holds.
- **Modern composer moved, not rewritten** (`Experimental/ModernComposer/`):
  - `ComposerHeight.swift` and `ComposerLayoutProbe.swift` move whole (`git mv`);
  - `ComposerChrome` moves out of `ComposerHeader.swift`;
  - `ReplyComposerView` makes the choice in one place, `usesModernComposer`, at its marker: an iPhone with the switch on;
  - switch off: an iPhone gets what an iPad has (full height, legacy's bar, the control row under the card). #855 builds the legacy full-screen cover on that side and flips the default.
- **Topic-row marks moved** (`Experimental/FlagIconRows/`): the unread dot and the flag icons, unchanged. The super-favourite mark and the unread-pages capsule stay in `TopicRowView`.
- **Offline save hidden** (#822): `OfflineSaveAvailability.isReachable == false`.
  - The single source: `AppEnvironment` gives the screens no offline services (Fable round 1).
  - The screens also guard on their own: the reader's « … » items, the reader's saver (so no progress banner), and Réglages › Hors ligne (`SettingsView.offlineEntry(in:)`).
  - The code, the eviction at launch and the pages already saved stay. Each place is marked `DISABLED(offline, #822)`.
