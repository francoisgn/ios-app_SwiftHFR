**Problem.** The Plus tab did not cross a fold. In the split root (unfolded Duo), a Bookmarks or Alertes Qualitay row opened its topic in the detail column (`RootModel.detailReaders`); on the cover screen the same row pushed a reader kept in the list view's own `@State`. So a fold with a topic open beside the list showed the list alone on the cover (`["Plus", "Bookmarks"]`), a topic opened on the cover was lost by an unfold, and an unfold brought back the detail reader of before the fold even when the cover had closed it. Legacy carries the Plus topic both ways (`handleRootSwap`, `MainWindow.swift:2157-2172` @ 87fe0d1c: `requestCompactPush(tab: .more, …)` consumed by the Plus root's `foldHandoffDestination()`, `:1836-1839`; on unfold `selectTopic(_, for:)` on the Bookmarks / Alertes Qualitay destination, or nothing read → every target cleared).

**Cause.** Two readers for one topic: the entry's detail reader in the model, the pushed one in the view (`BookmarksView.pushed`, `QualityAlertsView.opened` + `QualityAlertReaderScreen`). The tabs' topics already live in the shared model (#1115), the Plus entries' did not.

**Change.**
- `PlusStackReader` (`PlusView.swift`, applied by `PlusEntryView` to Bookmarks and Alertes Qualitay): on a plain stack (the Plus tab of the tab bar) it gives the list the same `openTopicInDetail` action as the split's content column, marked `besideTheList: false`, and pushes `model.detailReaders[entry]` over the list; back calls `closeDetail`. Beside a detail column (`PlusListColumn` set the action) it is inert.
- `OpenTopicInDetail.besideTheList` (default true) and `RootSidebarView.detailOpener(…, besideTheList:)`: the Bookmarks row stays highlighted only beside the list.
- `BookmarksView`, `QualityAlertsView`: call the action only; their own pushed reader and `QualityAlertReaderScreen` go.
- `RootModel.closeDetail(for: .bookmarks)` drops the bookmark selection with the reader (review round 1, finding 2): back on the cover screen left the row highlighted beside the placeholder after an unfold; legacy's one `bookmarksTopicTarget` drives the highlight and the detail (`MainWindow.swift:1978`, cleared by `clearAllTopicTargets`, `:2186-2193`). Alertes Qualitay has no selection to clear.
- Spec: the Plus tab paragraph of "Fold handoff", the two "left" items (the Plus tab done; the topic-stable Plus id of #1115 round 2 detail 2 settled as an intentional difference, as that review proposed: no code).

Wide as the two lists, the one modifier and the one line of `closeDetail`; no change to `rootSwapped`.

Differences from legacy, in the spec: the topic sits over the entry's list, not over the Plus menu (SwiftHFR keeps the entry open across a fold); unfolded, the entry it came from shows it whatever the sidebar showed before the fold (legacy carries it only towards the current Bookmarks / Alertes Qualitay destination, else Réglages opens empty).
