### Problem
Body links are drawn in `--action`, the accent (fill colour: S 0.65 / B 1 by day). As text it is 1.52:1 on white at the default hue, 1.07 at 60 degrees, 2.24 in dark at 240 degrees.

### Cause
`a { color: var(--action) }` in `reader.css` reuses the accent for text; the accent was designed for fills and the app tint.

### Change
- `ThemeAppearance+LinkColor.swift` (pure, Swift): `linkColor(dark:)` moves the accent toward black (light) or white (dark) by the smallest share that reaches 4.7:1 (4.5 plus margin for CSS-composited tints) on every link surface (page, placeholder, `--love`, `--modo`, quotes nested to about 15 % text, 15 % accent tint), and also after a continuation's 75 % opacity fade. An accent that already passes is untouched. Sent as `--app-link-light` / `--app-link-dark` (Legacy only).
- `reader.css`: `--link` defaults to those variables (fallback `--action`); `a` and `[code]` blue keyword spans (`span[style="color:blue"]`, the other accent-coloured text) use it. Underline and weight unchanged. The accent still paints fills, controls and the app tint.
- Gallery themes: unchanged, they send no link variable and keep their own `link` token (already held to 4.5:1 on their surfaces by `ThemeDefinition`'s contrast checks).
- Legacy look decision (for the operator): legacy draws body links and the `[code]` keyword in the accent too (`style-liste-light.css:339-341` `.message .content .right a { color: var(--color-action) }`, `:631-633` `.olcode .span`, `--color-action` = `ThemeColors.tintColor`, `MessagesView.swift:2833,2841`, `ThemeColors.m:618-619`, @ `87fe0d1c`), so this deliberately changes a legacy behaviour (1.5:1 at the default hue). The hue stays recognisable (test: within 6 degrees), only lightness moves; at the default hue the light link is a dark teal. Ledger below.
