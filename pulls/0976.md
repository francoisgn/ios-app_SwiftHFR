**Problem.** With Réglages › Expérimental › « Composer moderne » on, the floating composer drew a « Répondre » (« Edition », « Nouveau message »…) title between its buttons, on an opaque strip over the page, and the bare glass capsules faded into what was behind them.

**Cause.**
- `ComposerHeader.floatingBar` put the title in the row.
- The docked bar's opaque fill (`DockedComposerBar`) covered the row.
- The capsules were glass alone, so their glyph contrast depended on what was under them.

**Change.**
- New `Experimental/ModernComposer/FloatingComposerButtons.swift`: close and drafts on the left, send on the right, a `Spacer` between them, no title.
  - The marker `EXPERIMENTAL(modernComposer, #904)` sits in `ComposerHeader.body`.
  - The title stays as the row's VoiceOver label (`accessibilityElement(children: .contain)`).
- **No strip.** At partial height the docked bar's fill starts under the button row, and the page shows around the buttons.
  - The row reports its height to the bar through `ComposerButtonsHeightKey`; `DockedComposerBar.fillStart` places the fill.
  - Banners and the MP recipient and subject fields stay on the fill.
  - A tap between the buttons still stops at the bar (`contentShape`).
  - At full height the bar is filled from its top, as before.
  - `ReplyComposerView` is untouched.
- **Visibility.**
  - Non-prominent capsules: a near-opaque fill (85 % white in light, 85 % `#2C2C2E` in dark), a 0.5 pt hairline, under the interactive glass.
  - Glyph: `#1C1C1E` in light, `#F2F2F7` in dark, semibold.
  - Prominent capsules keep the accent glass.
  - A disabled button dims only its glyph (40 %).
- **Unchanged:** the 44 pt hit areas and the `FloatingSpacing.gap` insets.
- **Legacy bar** (option off, iPad): unchanged. It only gains a `.composerProbe(.title)`, which does nothing outside tests.

**Tests.**
- `FloatingButtonPaletteTests`:
  - glyph against the capsule laid over a white and a black page: ≥ 4.5:1 in light and dark (17.0 / 12.0 light, 7.7 / 13.6 dark);
  - the same against the composer's own background;
  - fill alpha ≥ 0.8;
  - `fillStart`;
  - the compositing arithmetic.
- `ComposerLayoutHostedTests.theFloatingButtonsSitAboveTheCardWithNoTitle` (replaces `theTitleNeverSitsUnderAFloatingButton`), at `.large` and `.accessibility3`:
  - no `.title` frame;
  - three buttons ≥ 44 pt, above the card, in one row;
  - close and drafts in the left half, send in the right half;
  - at `.large`, the part above the card is exactly the row.

  It fails before the change: the title probe reported a frame.
- `ComposerLayoutHostedTests.theDockedBarsFillStartsUnderTheButtonsRow` (`placement: .docked`): the fill starts at the row's bottom edge and covers the card. It fails without the preference, because the fill then starts at 0.
- `ModernComposerSwitchHostedTests.theTitleFollowsTheSwitch`: option on, no title; option off (and on iPad), legacy's title stays above the card.
- `theFloatingButtonsGlyphIsTheCardToolsSize` now measures against the semibold symbol.

**Device check (general review)**
- [ ] Reply, edit and MP in the reader: no strip, and the page is visible around Fermer + Brouillons (left) and Envoyer (right), directly above the card.
- [ ] Buttons readable over a light page and a dark page, in light and dark mode and on a gallery theme (including send's accent).
