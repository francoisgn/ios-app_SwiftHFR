## What and why
**Problem.** Far down a long page, the app goes to the background and iOS kills the WebContent process. `ReaderWebController.contentProcessTerminated` reloaded the ready document with `loadPage(document)` and nothing saved, so `documentIsReady` landed on `document.anchor` (the flag message) or at the top. With tap-to-load on, the third-party images already tapped came back as placeholders: `decisions(for:)` built `ImageDecision` without `tapped:` (the scheme handler already passed it, `startImage`).

**Change.**
- `ReaderWebController`: `lastReportedPost`, the last `.position(post:)` the **ready** page reported (a reloading page's own top-of-page report is not kept); `contentProcessTerminated` copies it to `relandingPost`; `documentIsReady` lands there (`scroll(to:landing:)`, the anchor's landing path) before the anchor or a saved reading top; `show()` of a document clears both. When the page reported nothing, the landing is the anchor or the top, as before. The `.position` bookkeeping moved to `positionReported` to keep `handle` within `cyclomatic_complexity`.
- `decisions(for:)`: `tapped: consented.contains(text)`. Consent stays per document (`show()` clears it).
- `// swiftlint:disable file_length` with its reason: the file was 399 lines; the scheme handler shares the controller's private state, so moving it out would widen access. Same precedent as `AccountsModel.swift:10`.
- `docs/lots/LOT-083-topic-reader.md`: the huge-page bullet says where the reload lands and that tapped images stay allowed.

Known narrow windows (review nits, accepted): two process deaths inside the landing window; a death then `stopLoading()` before the reload is ready.

## How it was verified
Read, not run here (hosted app tests need the simulator; Mac/CI run them):
- `ReaderInsetsWebTests.aReloadAfterProcessTerminationLandsOnTheLastReportedPost`: tall document, anchor 1, landing done, the page reports post 2, `webViewWebContentProcessDidTerminate`: after the second ready the scroll view shows message 2 under the bar. On the base it lands on 1.
- `ReaderInsetsWebTests.aTappedImageIsLoadedAgainAfterAContentProcessReload`: an imgur image is `tap`, tapped, process killed: `load`; another document: `tap` again. On the base the second check gives `tap`.
- SwiftLint 0.65.1 `--strict` on the changed files: clean.
