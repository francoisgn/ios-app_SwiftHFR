## Problem
A swipe-left removal in Favoris showed « Session non confirmée : rien n'a été envoyé. Reconnectez-vous puis réessayez. » on a session whose lists load. Every non-valid result of the LOT-044 session check (`SessionHealth`: 2 `expired` reasons and 3 `unconfirmed` ones) mapped to that **one** text. So the screenshot cannot tell which check failed, and « Reconnectez-vous » is wrong advice when the check merely proved nothing.

## What the Mac showed (2026-11-17, `dev-swift` 1df2121a, capture account user2 only, read-only)
- A fresh user2 session (logged in through `tooling/fixtures/flows.py`), run through `SessionRefreshFlow.refresh` (what `TopicFlagFlow.confirm` calls): **`valid`**.
- The probe request by hand (`/user/editprofil.php`, `config` + `page`, no redirect followed): **200, served at exactly the requested URL**, no `Location`, `Set-Cookie` present, not a `.hop`, the profile page parses, and the token is present.
- So HFR's profile page and the rules of `accountToken` hold. The non-valid answer on the operator's device comes from **the app-side state of that account's session**. Leads, not proven:
  - the account came from the legacy import, which keeps each cookie's own domain (`KeychainLegacyAccountSource.swift:89`), so the jar may hold two `md_user` and `userKey` takes the first non-empty one (`.unconfirmed(.identityNotConfirmed)`);
  - a login generation or identity change while the check ran (`.unconfirmed(.sessionChanged)`);
  - or a real `.expired`.

  The lists and the flag writer share one `HFRSession` (`AppEnvironment.swift:44, 58`), so a stale copy is ruled out.

## Change
- `TopicFlagActions.session(_:)`: each case and reason has its own text, and every one keeps « rien n'a été envoyé ».
  - Only `.expired` says « Reconnectez-vous »: « Session expirée … » and « HFR refuse les identifiants de ce compte … ».
  - `.unconfirmed` says « Session non vérifiée (page de profil inattendue | identité non confirmée par HFR) … Réessayez dans quelques instants. »
  - `.sessionChanged`: « Le compte connecté a changé pendant la vérification … Réessayez. »
- `TopicFlagOutcome.retryable`: a write refused by an `.unconfirmed` check, or by the network before it left, offers « Réessayer » in the list's alert. The row is captured when the alert is built, because the alert clears its state before a button runs (LOT-087). A write that may have left (`.unconfirmed` answer), an expired session, a refusal or a missing token are never offered again.
- The health case is logged (`os.Logger`, subsystem `SwiftHFR`, category `session`, case and reason only, no cookie, no token).
- Polls already split their texts (`PollMessages.swift`). MP deletion and the group roster do not map `sessionNotConfirmed` in the app today, so nothing changes there.

Why no wider: no change to the engine's check. Its rules hold on a live session, and the cause on the device is not yet known.
