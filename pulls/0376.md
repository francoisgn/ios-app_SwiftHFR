## Problem
In #370 the capture references of a lot were restricted to open issues (so that a PR or an unknown `#N` never blocks). A capture issue closed as `not_planned` or `duplicate` then stopped blocking its lot, although the plan wants such a closure to go through human triage (`sync_state.py` treats those closures as not done).

## Cause
`known_open` filtered on `state == open`, which also drops the closed-but-not-done captures.

## What changed
Known issue numbers (real issues, no PRs) plus the existing `not in closed` check: a PR or unknown reference still never blocks, a capture closed not planned or duplicate blocks again.

## Tests
`test_capture_closed_not_planned_or_duplicate_still_blocks_for_triage` (both reasons); the PR/unknown test of #370 still passes. Suite: 51 tests OK.
