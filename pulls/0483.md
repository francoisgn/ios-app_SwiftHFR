**Finding.** The captures confirm the policy, no behaviour change (so no CHANGELOG-LEGACY row). LOT-087 and LOT-031 specs ("Pending capture") and HFR-PROTOCOL §6c updated.

**Tests, shown to fail** (temporary source edits, reverted): dropping `favorite` fails the own-post and other-post tests; offering "Alerter" on one's own post fails the own-post test; offering the lists on one's own post fails the author-menu test.
