## What and why
**Problem.** The Plus entry "Supprimer mon compte" was a placeholder. Legacy (`PlusTab.swift:55-63, 79-110, 139-174` @ `87fe0d1c`) opened a mail composer with a recipient address hard-coded in source (Q-10), and kept every other piece of the account's data on the device. App Store 5.1.1(v) expects the app to explain how to delete the account and to remove its own data.

**Change.** Spec: `docs/lots/LOT-157-account-deletion.md`.
- `AccountDeletionView` (iPhone row and iPad sidebar entry): explains the account belongs to HFR; a `Link` opens HFR's own procedure, `https://www.hardware.fr/html/donnees_personnelles/` (capture `R98a`, footer of `R01`, HFR-PROTOCOL §6b "Account deletion"), in the browser. No mail composer, no address in source, nothing sent to the forum.
- Per-account wipe, behind a confirmation: `AccountsModel.remove` (Keychain entry + session), then `removeStoreItemIfEmpty()` deletes the Keychain item itself when no account is left, then `AccountDataEraser` empties the account's drafts, blocklist, reading positions, super-favourites and app smiley favourites. Every store is tried; failures are named, "Réessayer" erases only those. Other accounts' entries and the shared stores (allowlist, histories, offline topics, caches, settings) are untouched.
- Leftovers: account keys that still have data in a per-account store but are no longer stored accounts ("Retirer", a partial wipe, a late draft save) are read back from the stores and offered for erasing, each behind a confirmation; never a stored account's key, and none while the account store cannot be read. Engine reads added to HFRStorage for this: `DraftStore.accountKeys()`, `PseudoListStore.blocklistAccountKeys()`, and `AccountKey` made public (no `HFRModels` change). Positions are erased under every spelling of the account's key; an unreadable positions file is reported, not skipped.
- `AccountDeletionModel` is app-wide (`SwiftHFRApp`): erasing the current account rebuilds the root navigation, the result stays on screen.
- `PlusEntry.deleteAccount` is built (`owningLot` nil); `AccountsModel.store` is internal (read by `AccountsModel+Erase.swift`, to keep `AccountsModel.swift` under the 400-line lint limit).

Not wired in the app yet, so nothing to erase today: iCloud KVS lists (LOT-172 has no app caller), MPStorage local copy (LOT-130), notification ledger (LOT-091). The lot that wires a per-account store adds it to `AccountDataEraser`.
