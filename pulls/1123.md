### Problem
The top bar sits on a heavy dark band (operator feedback, 2026-11-18, dark mode).

### Cause (measured, not guessed)
Nothing of ours draws it. On the simulator (hosted iPhone root, content scrolled under the bar) the layers behind the bar are the system's: `ScrollEdgeEffectView` (h=156), `BackdropView`, `_UIBarBackground` (h=116). The automatic style is a frosted band with a hard edge at the bar's bottom, near-black over our dark lists. The empty top `safeAreaBar` (`TopicListView.swift:70`) adds a second scroll-pocket collector but its removal changed no pixel, so it stays.

### Operator decision (2026-11-18)
Option (b): `scrollEdgeEffectStyle(.soft, for: .top)` on every main screen (an Apple API, no custom blur or material, #1070).

### Change
- `ThemeSettingsModel.swift`: `AppScreenBackground` applies `.scrollEdgeEffectStyle(.soft, for: .top)` around its existing switch. Every screen root already carries `appScreenBackground()` (Catégories, topic lists, Favoris, Messages, Recherche, Plus, Réglages, reader), so it is set once.
- `ReaderWebController.swift` and `StaticPageView.swift` (« Charte du forum », « Crédits », own `WKWebView`): `webView.scrollView.topEdgeEffect.style = .soft` (a UIKit scroll view does not read the SwiftUI modifier). Round 1 (Opus) found the static page.
- Left alone: `VideoPlayerSheet` (a sheet) and `ZoomScrollView` (`ImageViewerView.swift:125`, shown only in a `fullScreenCover`): no main screen's top bar sits over them. No other `WKWebView`/`UIScrollView` shown by a main screen is built in `App/SwiftHFR`.

### Tests
`TopBarEdgeEffectTests` (4 tests): every main list's `topEdgeEffect.style == .soft` (hosted root: Catégories, Favoris, Messages, Plus), the reader web view, the static page web view, Réglages. All 4 seen failing without the source change (7 issues, `runs-1120/red-TopBarEdgeEffectTests.log`) and passing with it (`green-TopBarEdgeEffectTests.log`). Not covered by a test: the iPad sidebar and columns (same modifier on each column root, not hosted).
Whole `SwiftHFRTests` target: 2094 tests, only `DuoLayoutHostedTests/aFoldShowsTheTopicOpenInTheTab(tab: .messages)` fails (tracked in #1124), and it fails identically on `dev-swift` without this change (checked).

### Device-check points (#893)
- Apple category list, Favoris, Messages, reader, light and dark: content fades toward the bar, no hard line at the bar's bottom.
- Simulator renders: before = hard frosted band; after = gradient fade (Apple list and Favoris, dark and light, kept by the author; Catégories has two fixture rows, so it cannot scroll under the bar and its screenshots were dropped; the reader is pinned by test, not screenshotted).
