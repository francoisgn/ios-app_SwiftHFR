# PR #679: Ledger test: fold the real bodies into the pre-batch state

Direct-PR: one-test fix that unblocks the first ledger batch (#674); follow-up to #621 (#620).

**Problem.** `RealBodiesTest.test_real_bodies_fold_or_are_reported` (in `scripts/tests/test_docs_ledger.py`) copies the live `docs/CHANGELOG-LEGACY.md` and GAP file, then folds the merged bodies of #590, #604 and #607 into them. Ledger PR #674 already folds those PRs. On its head the rows exist, so #607's new `GAP-596` is refused and the "Fixture tooling tests" check fails: `[604] != [604, 607]`.

**Change.** Before folding, the test removes the #604 and #607 changelog rows and the `GAP-596` row from its copies. The copies then match the state before the batch, whatever the checkout. #604's in-place edit is applied again and reported as "already applied".

**Tests.** `python3 -m unittest discover -s scripts/tests` passes, with and without `GITHUB_ACTIONS=true`, both on dev-swift and with #674's two ledger files checked out. Before the change it failed on the second state.
