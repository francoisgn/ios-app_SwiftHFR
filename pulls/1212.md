### Problem
Operator feedback of 2026-11-18: in the Legacy theme the standard dark style sits on pure black (the same as OLED) instead of legacy's grey, and even messages carry an alternating tint that legacy does not have.

### Cause
- `ThemeAppearance.readerDarkSurfaces(.standard)` gave `0x000000`, and `reader.css` fell back to `#000000`. Legacy: `body.ios7 { background: rgba(46, 47, 51, 1) }` (`style-liste-dark.css:563-564` @ 87fe0d1c) and `.message .content { background: rgba(46, 47, 51, 1) }` (`:262-266`), i.e. #2E2F33.
- #568 added `--surface-alt` and its even-message rules; legacy's dark CSS has no alternating background.

### Change
- **Standard dark = #2E2F33** (`readerDarkSurfaces`): page `0x2E2F33`; quote surface `0x26282F` (another legacy dark surface, `rgba(38, 40, 47)`: `#actualiserbtn` `:330`, `#toolbarpage` `:602`; not read by the reader, kept for the settings tests); placeholder `0x35363B` and quiet separator `0x4A4B50`, lifted to stay apart from the grey page (the placeholder is the darkest grey that keeps the secondary text at 4.5:1: 4.69:1). « Plus sombre » (#0B0B0C) and OLED (#000000) are unchanged. `reader.css`'s dark fallbacks follow (`#2e2f33`, `#35363b`, `#4a4b50`). The web view's layer, the settings preview and the theme gallery read the same surfaces.
- **The grey has no headroom, so every standard surface but the separator (page, quote surface, placeholder) is capped at 100 % of the brightness slider** (as the darker and OLED pages already were): secondary text is 5.2:1 on the grey, 3.6:1 at 150 %. The slider still darkens them and still scales the separator.
- **Contrast fixes the grey needed** (rounds 1 and 2, recomputed with the WCAG formula):
  - a continuation message (75 % opacity) had its date and note at 3.45:1: in dark, `.message.continuation .date` and `.continuation-note` use the page text colour (5.9:1 or more). This rule is in the dark block, so it also applies to « plus sombre » and OLED, where it only raises contrast;
  - the classic header (15 % accent over the page) had its date at 3.75:1: in dark, `.style-classic .message .date` uses the page text colour (8.6:1); same for `.me` headers;

- **No alternating tint, every theme and style** (`reader.css`): `--alt-strength` (every definition and the Increase Contrast strengths), `--surface-alt`, `article.message:nth-of-type(even)`, and the classic-header and quote-tint variants written for even messages are removed. The message rule (`--separator-strong`) stays. The settings preview's sample text no longer mentions the alternation; `docs/THEMES.md`, `FEAT-010`, `LOT-122`, `LOT-188` are updated.
- **Native screens: not changed.** Legacy's native screens do not use #2E2F33: `AppThemePalette` (`HFRswift/Swift/Common.swift:813-900` @ 87fe0d1c) takes the system grouped backgrounds adjusted by the dark brightness (`listBackgroundColor`: `systemGroupedBackground`; rows: `secondarySystemGroupedBackground`, minimum white level 12); only the static pages use `rgb(30, 31, 33)` and the classic header `rgb(30, 31, 34)`.
