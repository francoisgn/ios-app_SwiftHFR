**Problem.** The GIF picker's clear-search ✕ (`GifPickerView.searchBar`) was a bare `.plain` button around a 16.7 pt (xSmall) to 19.7 pt (large) glyph: a hit region far under 44 pt (HIG, Buttons), the same defect #1292 fixed for the chip ✕ and the poll ⊖ (#1156).

**Cause.** No minimum hit region on the label.

**Change.** New `GifSearchClearButton` (same file), the technique of `PollAnswerRemoveButton`: `.minimumHitRegion()` on the label, then a negative padding of the overhang (`PollAnswerRemoveButton.overhang(of:)`), so the glyph and the text field keep their size and place in the search bar (the region overflows its neighbours). `regionProbe` is a test seam reporting the label's laid-out region. No change to `ComposerTouchTargetTests`.

**Review round 1** (r1328, Opus 5.5, APPROVE on 3f8d1de2; findings 1, 2, 4 fixed in bbd53bae, finding 3 is #1329): the probe reports the glyph's frame and the test asserts its centre equals the layout's; the leading overhang is capped at the explicit stack spacing (`GifSearchClearButton.spacing` = 8) and the remaining 4 to 6 pt go to the trailing side (the capsule's 10 pt padding plus the 12 pt outer margin: the region ends at most about 9 pt past the capsule's edge, nothing clipped), so it never reaches the field; `overhang(of:)` now lives in `MinimumHitRegion` and both buttons use it (`PollAnswerRemoveButton.overhang` forwards to it, its tests untouched).

**Tests** (`GifSearchClearTouchTargetTests`, hosted, iPhone 18 Pro iOS 27.2, `appTypography` wrapped inside the dynamic-type environment as in #1307):

- `theClearCrossKeepsTheLayoutOfItsGlyph` (same sizes): the button lays out with the bare glyph's size and origin (the look and the bar do not move). Red under the same mutation (the overhang then collapses the layout to 0 x 0); green with the fix.

The first fix attempt (cap only) moved the glyph 5.7 pt off centre, caught by the new centre test; the margins are now applied as padding inside the label.
