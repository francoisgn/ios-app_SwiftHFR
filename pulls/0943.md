**Problem.** The reader's default look was not the legacy one:
- the body ran the full width;
- the text was larger;
- quotes were thick and filled;
- vertical spacing was large;
- a coloured monogram showed when a member had no avatar.

**Cause.** `reader.css` was a modern rewrite (LOT-083), not a port of the legacy stylesheet values.

**Change.** Only `reader.css` changes, plus comments in `TopicHTMLBuilder`. It ports the legacy values at `87fe0d1c`. The paths cited in the issue are `SuperHFRplus/Assets/HTML/…` at that commit.
- **Body indent.** The modern style indents the body under the pseudo; the classic style stays flat (`MessagesView.swift:1827`).
- **Text size.** 15/17 of the Dynamic Type body, times the in-app scale (legacy already scales 15 pt by the picker factor through `UIFontMetrics(.body)`: `MessagesView.swift:3376-3381`, `Common.swift:314-322`; so 15/17 is legacy's own mapping).
- **Quotes.** 2 px left border, no fill, 4 px before the text and 5 px below (`style-liste.css:329-334`, `style-liste-light.css:104-111`).
- **Spacing.** No paragraph margin (`style-liste.css:39-47`), 10 px under the body (`style-liste-light.css:504-508`; its 10 px right padding is the message's own 1em right padding here).
- **No avatar.** A grey silhouette drawn in CSS only, since the CSP allows no `data:` image (`style-liste-light.css:203-205`).
- **Kept.** A gallery theme still brings its own quote surface back. The visibility tints and the folded-quote padding stay. The header, the date size and "⋯" are unchanged.
- **Not ported.** Legacy's `.content br` rule (`style-liste-light.css:514-519`), which had no visible effect.
- **Settings preview.** The dark-style preview now draws a border-only quote like the reader; `--app-dark-quote-background` is still delivered but unused by the default look.

**Open for the operator.**
- The body is indented by 2.7 rem × scale (aligned under the pseudo), not by legacy's fixed 52 px.
- The body keeps the message's 1em right padding (the operator asked for wider margins): about 7 px wider than legacy's 10 px.
- The side-by-side capture against legacy (light and dark, the gap between a quote header and its text) is owed to a device check by the operator; only a hosted-WebKit capture was done here.

**Tests.**
- `ReaderLegacyStyleWebTests`: 2 hosted `WKWebView` tests pin the computed values. They failed before the CSS change, on 9 expectations.
- `aQuoteNestedInAnOpenedBlockedQuoteKeepsThePlainPadding`: a quote inside an opened blocklisted quote keeps the plain 4 px.
- `ReaderTextScaleTests`, `ReaderSeparationWebTests` and `ReaderThemeTests` are updated to the new look.
- Full gate green on iPhone and iPad (1556 tests).
- Not checked: a device, or a real thread.
