### Problem
Lists, banners, toasts and pagers outside the reader use opaque `.bar` fills and hand-built bottom strips: no Liquid Glass, and the bars cannot move to the side on the iPhone Duo cover screen. The legacy app already ships glass.

### What changed (view-only, models untouched)
- Pagers of the forum list, MP list and search are system `.bottomBar` toolbar items (new `PagerToolbar`).
- Top strips (store problem, offline save, MP segment switch, filtered-posts banner) and the smiley search bar use `safeAreaBar` without the `.bar` fill. Topic-list sent/failure banners stay content-layer cards in a `safeAreaInset`.
- Floating toasts and banners (image viewer toast, smiley notice, filtered-posts progress banner) use `glassEffect`.
- "Résultats suivants" stays a bottom bar (`safeAreaBar`, `.glassProminent`): it carries a state line, a hint and Cancel, which a toolbar item cannot hold.
- `ellipsis.circle` becomes `ellipsis` (image viewer, Favoris menu); image viewer `toolbarBackground` removed.
- Kept (HIG: no glass in the content layer): notice cards, content-layer buttons, `FormattingToolbar` (LOT-174).
- Every site, decision, reason and HIG URL: `docs/lots/LOT-185-liquid-glass-surfaces.md`. CHANGELOG-LEGACY row 87.

### Tests
View-only: no model logic changed, existing app tests are the guard; CI app build + tests green (`.glassProminent` compiles).

### Mac check needed
- Light, dark, Dynamic Type XL, Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast.
- iPhone 18 Pro, iPad, iPhone Duo poses.
- iPhone, Catégories and Favoris: open a topic from a paged list, and a search result in Plus → Recherche. The list's bottom pager must not stay on the reader (own bottom inset, `TopicReaderView.swift:54`) and must come back on return.
- Look at: pagers over the tab bar, "Page N" contrast (optional: `.sharedBackgroundVisibility(.hidden)` if it looks like a button), 4-button search pager at XL, smiley search bar above the keyboard, glass banner contrast over the reader.
