Not a lot: capture batch for LOT-048 (#74) and LOT-036 (#63), part B of the operator-approved public-write capture (part A: #258). What is left on #265: the delete answer of a public first post (not authorised, stays pending capture).

**Problem.** The public first-post edit form (subject field, delete label) could only come from a topic an operator account started. Its topic id, message ids, subject, text and dates would tie `user1` to the real account (anyone can open the topic and read its author).

**What changed.**
- Anonymizer: new `own_topics` key in the git-ignored local config. On a page of such a topic it replaces:
  - the topic id with `9900001`;
  - the message ids with `9910000001`…, using one table per publish batch;
  - the subject and slug;
  - message bodies, the form review and the description;
  - each line of the edit text, keeping the line breaks;
  - form-encoded request bodies;
  - the "Sujets relatifs" links;
  - every date, and the meta date.

  An independent verifier (`verify_own_topic`, its own patterns) reports any of these positions left real on a page or meta naming a fake own topic id. It runs in `check --corpus`. `check --config` is mandatory for such a batch, as documented in the kit README and FIXTURES.md.
- Corpus: `R63a`, `R63e` (the topic page before and after), `R63b` (first-post edit form: action `/bdd.php`, visible `sujet` maxlength 70, `subcat` select, poll fields, "Effacer l'intégralité du sujet"), `W34` (edit answer), `W35` (restore answer: the stored text and subject were then byte-identical to the original). Also MANIFEST rows; R63c and R63d are captured but not published (verification reads only).
- LOT-004 oracles for R63b (form, edit), W34 and W35 (response).
- HFR-PROTOCOL §3.1 and the LOT-036 edit-form rows now cover the public first post. In LOT-036 and LOT-048 the pending first-post edit form is settled. The engine still asks for whole-topic confirmation on public deletes; trusting the signs on public forms is a separate decision.

**Anonymization.** `check --corpus` 240/240 and `check --config` clean. Independent checks over the raw files found no leak:
- one check covers names, tokens, cookies and ids;
- an own-topic check covers the topic and message ids, dates, times, subject and slug, and words of the sale text and replies.

Corpus reviewed by Fable: APPROVE, and its three low findings are fixed (unquoted JS ids, documented `check --config`, R63c/R63d recorded).

**Tests.**
- `tooling/fixtures/tests/test_own_topic.py`: one test per position. Each checks that the verifier reports the raw value and that the anonymizer replaces it; removing a rule fails its test.
- `HopFormOracleTests` with R63b, `HopResponseParserTests` with W34 and W35 (differential tests and the corpus sweep), and `PostingBodyOracleTests` with R63b.
- Kit tests and `swift test` are green.
