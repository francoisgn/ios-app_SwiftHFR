### Problem
The weekly deck had 10 slides centred on milestones that are already done. The operator wants a shorter report, sent Monday and Thursday, focused on the M4 finish, where test feedback piles up, and what M5 holds (#892).

### Change
- `weekly/build.py` now builds 6 slides:
  1. Où en est SwiftHFR (unchanged).
  2. Indicateurs (unchanged).
  3. Aujourd'hui: M1–M5 bars, plus a new **Hors jalon (issues)** bar. It shows done / total, the issues done since the baseline and the issues created since.
  4. M1 · M2 · M3: one pile of tiles per milestone, with one tile per main feature. The « terminés » title only appears once all three milestones are done. M3 is at 40/43 today, so the title currently reads « ce qui est livré ».
  5. M4 en détail:
     - the M4 lots (done / in progress / to do) and the M4 issues outside lots;
     - the lots closed since the baseline;
     - the test feedback: every `beta-test finding` issue (never the credit label), the same count as slide 1, by status (ouvert / réclamé / fait / écarté), with the issues added since;
     - a radar of the feedback (all and open);
     - bottom right, the end-of-M4 review tiles #774, #775, #836, #326 and #866, each with its goal and the count of issues carrying its `m4-review:` label.
  6. M5 en détail: tiles by category (past six categories, the smallest share the last tile, so none is dropped), a category radar, and a bottom band listing the open issues outside every milestone, by kind. The band is hidden when there are none.
- Dropped: Jalons, the M1–M4 feature slides, L'app au quotidien, and the helpers only they used (`card_pill`, `left_card`, `levers`, `milestone_view`, `dependents`, `m5_cards`).
- Mah-jong tiles (`.tile`, `.pile`): solid blue → violet gradients with a visible edge drawn by offset shadows and a slight overlap. No third-party artwork, icon or trademark.
- `weekly/counts.py` holds the new pure rules:
  - `axis_of` / `AXES` / `AXIS_RULES`: the radar axis of an issue, from its title.
  - `issue_milestone`: GitHub milestones are not used on this repo, so the milestone is taken from, in order: the issue's lot, `m5.FOLLOWUPS`, the end-of-M4 review trackers (M4), a `FEAT-0xx` intake issue (M5, except FEAT-016 in M4, per `docs/ROADMAP.md`), a title tag (`M4:`, `(M4)`, `(UXD-03, M2)`), a lot named in the title, then the labels (`beta-test finding` and `m4-review:*` mean M4, `experimental` means M5). Today this leaves 79 open issues outside every milestone.
  - `is_swift_issue`, `issue_status`, `feedback`.
- `weekly/shot.js` gains checks for:
  - tile overflow;
  - an icon covering tile text;
  - **tile text lying under another tile of its pile** (ellipsis-clipped text is clipped to its visible box first);
  - a tile running past the page margins.
- `tooling/roadmap/README.md`: the new slide list, the milestone and axis rules, and the tile style.
