**Problem.** The « Vider » toolbar button of the archived-drafts sheet deleted every archived draft of the topic at once, with no confirmation and no undo.

**Cause.** The button called `discardAllArchived()` directly. Legacy (`AnswerView.swift:1936-1941` @ 87fe0d1c) did the same; this is a deliberate difference (HIG: a destructive action is confirmed and its button names the action).

**Change.** « Vider » only calls `ArchivedClearConfirmation.request()`; a `confirmationDialog` attached to the button (« Vider les brouillons ? ») offers « Supprimer les brouillons » (destructive) and « Annuler », with a message counting what will be deleted. `confirm(discardingIn:)` empties the archive and does nothing without a prior request; it decides on its own `isRequested` flag, because SwiftUI clears the dialog's presenting flag before a button's action runs (LOT-087, caught in review round 2). Wording lives in `ArchivedDraftsFormat`. `ReplyComposerModel` and `ArchivedDraftsTests.swift` are untouched (#1250 edits them). Known and accepted: a draft move that starts while the dialog is open makes the confirm a silent no-op (the « Vider » button is disabled while a move runs).

**Tests.** `ArchivedDraftsClearConfirmationTests` (red before `ArchivedClearConfirmation` existed): `askingDeletesNothingUntilItIsConfirmed`, `confirmingDeletesEveryArchivedDraftAndCloses`, `confirmingStillDeletesWhenTheDialogAlreadyClearedItsFlag` (replays the dialog's order: request, flag cleared, confirm; fails against the earlier `guard isAsking`, which returned early and left 2 drafts), `aConfirmAfterACancelDeletesNothing`, `cancellingKeepsTheDraftsAndCloses`, `aConfirmWithoutARequestDeletesNothing`, and the wording.
