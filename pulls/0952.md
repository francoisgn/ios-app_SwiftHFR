**Problem.** The page bar « ⏮ ‹ Page N › » of the paged lists sat above the tab bar from the top of the list, where one rarely changes page.

**Cause.** `.pagerBar` always attached the pager. Nothing looked at where the list was scrolled.

**Change.**
- `.pagerBar` tracks the list's scroll geometry (`onScrollGeometryChange`).
- The rule is the pure `PagerVisibility`: the bar shows within 24 pt of the end, or when the list is shorter than the screen (its end is visible), and hides otherwise.
- It appears with a short animation, with none under Reduce Motion.
- Unchanged:
  - the three call sites: topic lists (including Catégories and Favoris), Messages and search results;
  - #881's placement rules: tab-root strip versus sidebar bottom bar, hiding while search has focus, `ViewThatFits`.
- Round 1 fixes (Opus):
  - The bar's own bottom inset (about 52 pt) used to push the distance past the tolerance and hide it again (flicker at the end). The rule now has hysteresis: it appears within 24 pt of the end and hides only beyond 24 pt + the bar's measured height (round 2: the strip is measured with `onGeometryChange` under the tab root; under the sidebar bottom bar it is the extra bottom inset seen while shown against the last hidden inset; no constant).
  - The list's accessibility actions are one stable `.accessibilityActions` (no `AnyView`), so the list identity (scroll, selection, search focus) does not change with the page steps.
  - The visible bar always stays under VoiceOver. That visible bar is what keeps the pages reachable; rotor exposure of the list-level actions is **not verified**.
- There is no setting, per the common ground of 2026-11-14.
- The additions of 2026-11-15 belong to the reader (#820) and are not in this PR.

**Tests.** `PagerVisibilityTests` (11 tests): hidden while scrolling away from the end; shown at the end and hidden again when scrolling up; a short list shows the bar at once; the bottom inset does not move the end; **the bar appearing does not hide it again** (offset 1200, inset 0 to 52, still shown); always shown under VoiceOver; no animation under Reduce Motion; the page actions list only the enabled steps.
- Red run for the flicker test: with the hysteresis term removed from `isShown`, `theBarAppearingDoesNotHideItAgain` fails on both iPhone and iPad (`isShown(distanceFromEnd: after, wasShown: true …)` expectation failed; log `out/f898-red.txt`); green with it.
- Round 2 red run: `aTallerBarThanAnyConstantStaysShownToo` (90 pt bar, offset unchanged, still shown) fails on iPhone and iPad with the old 64 pt constant (log `out/f898-red2.txt`), green with the measured height. `theBarHeightIsMeasuredFromTheStripOrTheInsetItAdds` covers the measurement rule. The H = 52 test is kept.
- Round 3: the hidden-inset baseline is recorded only while the bar is really hidden, before the height is computed, inside the pure `PagerVisibility.step`. `theSidebarBottomBarSettlesOnANearlyFittingList` replays the `.bottomBar` first-show sequence on a near-fitting list and checks it settles; red with the baseline written after the decision (iPhone and iPad, log `out/f898-red3.txt`), green now.
- Full suite green on iPhone 18 Pro and iPad Pro 11-inch (M5).

**Seen in the simulator** (a throwaway hosted test, not committed: a 60-row `List` and a 4-row `List` under `.pagerBar`, scrolled by `setContentOffset`; screenshots in `out/f898/shots/`, iPhone and iPad): the long list opens with no bar, from its initial geometry and without any scroll; at the end the bar is there and still there 1.5 s later (no flicker); after scrolling up it is gone; the 4-row list shows the bar at once.

**Not checked:** the real topic list, page 1 to 2 and the search field focus in the running app (no offline data to launch it; the harness above is not the app); the feel of the transition on a device; VoiceOver rotor exposure of the list actions.

Each change rebuilds the whole list: scroll position, selection, and the search `TextField` with its focus (#881's identity rule). Fix: one stable `.accessibilityActions { if … { Button(…) } }`, no `AnyView`.
2. **high** (not run) — `PagerToolbar.swift:121-125, 159, 169, 173-176`: the bar flickers at the end of the list. When it appears, the bottom inset grows by about 52 pt without moving `contentOffset`, so the distance from the end exceeds 24 pt, the bar hides, and the cycle repeats. `theBottomInsetDoesNotMoveTheEnd` tests the wrong case. Fix: hysteresis (hide only beyond tolerance + bar height), or keep the bar's height out of the distance; add a test for that case.
3. **medium** — `:162, 188`: the list-level accessibility actions probably are not offered by VoiceOver while it reads rows. Check on a device, or say on the PR that the always-visible bar is what keeps the pages reachable.
4. **low** — `PagerVisibilityTests.swift`: only the pure helpers are tested, and no red run is shown. State what was seen red; add the flicker case.
