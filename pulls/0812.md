### Problem
The round-2 review of #794 listed four Low gaps in the signature and mood redaction of `tooling/fixtures/anonymize.py`:
1. A member printing HFR's next element inside the block (forged boundary) ended the redaction and the `check` state early.
2. With no boundary, the redactor fell back to the depth count, and `check` ignored comments, declarations, processing instructions and end-tag attributes.
3. An unknown HFR element before the boundary was cut silently.
4. Three conditions of #794 had no test that failed without them: the XML exemption of the CDATA rule, the footer-table attributes and the bare `br`.

### Change
- **Redactor** (`_personal_block_end`): from a block's last `</span>`, the text must be HFR's own tail, in the one shape the whole corpus prints (2026-11-11, `PERSONAL_TAILS`):
  - a signature closes `</span></div></td></tr></table>`, then at most a `<br />` or the footer's layout-only `<table>`, then its boundary;
  - a mood is followed by an optional `<br/>` + transactions link, then the avatar, then `</td>` and one message cell per message. The link `href`/`onclick` and the avatar `div`/`img` are pinned attribute by attribute.

  Anything else raises `AnonymizeError` and nothing is written. A page with no boundary after a block also raises. A page here means an `html`/`body` tag or a message table; bare fragments (only unit tests use them) keep the depth count.
- **`check`** walks the DOM on its own (LESSONS 33–34) and refuses:
  - a boundary printed while the block's cell is still open;
  - a second `td.messCase2` in the message after a mood;
  - after a mood, anything but HFR's author-cell tail (bare `br`, then one `span.TransactionsReportsLink` holding one pinned `a` with the text `Transactions (N)`, then one `div.avatar_center` holding one pinned `img`, in that order);
  - comments, declarations, processing instructions, marked sections and end-tag attributes before the boundary;
  - a page that ends with no boundary after a block.
- README: what is refused, and the two shapes left outside the model.

**Outside the model**
- **Unknown HFR element right before a known tail (#813).** One example is #797's invented `<span class="MDStatus">…</span>` right before `</td>`. It is still redacted with the block, because byte for byte it is the same as a member's unbalanced `</span>` followed by a classed BBCode span, which #794 redacts on purpose. Nothing leaks; only HFR content is lost. Followed by anything else, it raises (tested). Whether to raise on every disagreement between the depth count and the boundary is an operator decision, tracked in #813.
- **Forged whole message.** A member who can print HFR's whole closing sequence and a full next message cannot be told apart from HFR by markup alone.
- **`div.messagetabledel` (deleted message).** The shape is legacy and the capture is pending: it now raises, and #123 names where to extend.
