**Problem.** HFR's batch 2 previews (`R443a`-`R443n`, PR #741) contradict several defaults of the LOT-097 renderer.

**Cause.** Those defaults were chosen before any capture (spec "Pending capture" 1-9).

**Change** (`HFRParsing/BBCode`, one fact at a time):
1. Syntax colours for `cpp` and `code` (captured keywords, `//` comments with the line's CR); `[c]` `[java]` `[php]` `[html]` stay text.
2. Raw blocks: `[b]` rendered in `code`; `[fixed]` around a block loses its tags; `[code]` in `[cpp]` and `[fixed]` in `[code]`/`[cpp]` render inside the item, content escaped and coloured again.
3. `[b][/b]` is text; a `[b]` pair holding a block loses its tags.
4. Quotes: two levels at most.
5. Smileys: substituted in URLs too (a `[url]` made text), except the `:/` of `://`; bare `http(s)` URLs are linked up to the smiley; a `)` after a typed `"` or `&` gets a space.
6. Colour: the open pairs with the first colour close, any name or case; 3-digit hex.
7. Whitespace: a single leading space of a code line stays plain. The message start is **not** a line start: `R443g` bytes are ` &nbsp; ` (the issue's wording is contradicted; the renderer was already right, now asserted).
8. Bullets: consecutive `[*]` lines are one list; the CR after a consumed break stays plain.
9. URLs: `www.` now gets `http://`; `[img]` takes only `http://`, `https://`, `ftp://`; a typed `"` in a URL attribute is written `&amp;#034;` as HFR does; the sanitizer keeps its own rule (intentional difference 2).

Spec: defaults replaced with the captured behaviour and its capture; pending 1-9 removed; what no capture shows is listed as pending 11 instead of guessed (keyword set, bare-URL terminators, `i`/`u`/`strike`, nested-block escaping, …). That is why the pending list is not only item 10. Follow-ups: #796 (quote shape `oldquote` vs `quote`, `[quotemsg]`, `[email]`, codes `:p:` / `:D:` missing from the catalogue, damaged `request_body` of `R443c`/`R443f`).
