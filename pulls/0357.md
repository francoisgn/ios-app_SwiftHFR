LOT-072
Closes #96
Claim: ext/ezzz-opus-1

**Problem.** HFRStorage compared pseudos with three private copies and two rules: the blocklist (`PseudoNormalizer`) and drafts (`DraftNameNormalizer`) removed U+200B and lower-cased; accounts (`AccountBook.normalized` + `samePseudo`) removed every format character, trimmed white space and lower-cased. A pseudo copied with a U+FEFF or a trailing space was one member for the accounts and another for the blocklist.

**Cause.** LOT-061, 063 and 065 each kept a private copy so as not to depend on each other (LOT-063 spec said so).

**Change.**
- `PseudoNormalizer` (moved to `HFRStorage/Pseudos/`) holds the one rule: `cleaned` (format characters removed, white space trimmed, case kept), `comparisonForm` (`cleaned`, lower-cased), `matches`. `DraftNameNormalizer` and `AccountBook`'s copies are gone; `PseudoNormalizer.normalize` (no caller outside HFRStorage) is removed.
- **Stored keys do not change** (ADR 0013 §7): blocklists and drafts are keyed by `AccountKey.make`, which `normalize` equalled, and `AccountKey` itself is untouched (comment only). Entries and MP recipients are compared at run time, so old data is still found under the wider rule.
- `unblock` / `disallow` remove every match: a list stored under the narrower rule can hold two spellings now merged.
- Blank pseudos (`" "`, U+FEFF alone) are refused by both lists. Legacy behaviour change recorded in CHANGELOG-LEGACY #49; LOT-061 and LOT-063 specs point to LOT-072.
- Not touched: `HFRText` (HFRParsing, cannot be imported) and `MPStorageBlocklistEntry.storageKey` (HFRModels, MPStorage merge key); ADR 0013 §7's sentence "today this coincides with `PseudoNormalizer.normalize`" is left as history (ADR not edited).

**Tests.** `PseudoNormalizerTests` (U+200B, U+FEFF, U+2060, U+200C/D, U+00AD, tabs/newlines, U+00A0, inner space, composed/decomposed accents, empty; account key = comparison form on a cleaned pseudo), `PseudoListStoreTests` (both lists, blank pseudos, a document stored before this lot found and fully unblocked, keys stored in the frozen form), `DraftContextTests`, `DraftStoreTests` (a draft stored before this lot found, frozen account keys), existing `AccountBookTests`. Eight mutations, each failing named tests: the old rule, format characters kept, no trim, `unblock` removing the first match only, blocklists or drafts keyed by the comparison form, recipients keyed by the account key, `AccountBook` comparing exactly. Full HFRKit suite green locally, SwiftLint strict clean, `check-lot-deps` 0 violations.
