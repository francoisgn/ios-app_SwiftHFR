### Problem
Composer controls had hit regions under the HIG's 44 x 44 pt: the image chip's ✕ (about 21 pt), the poll answer's ⊖ (about 20 pt), and the control-row / formatting cells outside the modern card (40 pt in compact density, 33.6 pt at 70 % text size regular, 28 pt compact at 70 %).

### Cause
The two icon buttons had no frame or `contentShape`; `ComposerControlRow.cellSize` clamped to 44 pt only `inCard`.

### Change
- `View.minimumHitRegion()` (`frame(minWidth: 44, minHeight: 44).contentShape(.rect)`): the glyphs keep their size, only the region grows.
- `ComposerImageChipCross` (glyph `@ScaledMetric`, capped at 28 pt) and `PollAnswerRemoveButton` extracted so the region can be measured. The chip's 44 pt box is offset so the glyph stays where it was (1 pt lower: the top overhang stops at the row's 10 pt margin); each chip is drawn over the next thumbnail (`zIndex`), so the ✕ box (11 pt past the thumbnail, gap 8) is never covered.
- `ComposerControlRow.hitCell(_:)` = `max(cell, 44)` is the region of the control row's and formatting toolbar's cells in both chromes. `cellSize(_:inCard:)` is unchanged, so the header's drawn circles (close, drafts, send) and the Experimental code (`ComposerToolSize`) read what they read before.
- The poll ⊖ reads its own glyph size and pulls the region back by `-max(0, 44 - glyph) / 2` a side (`PollAnswerRemoveButton.overhang(of:)`), at any text size: the ⊖ and its text field keep their place and width.
- Nothing under `App/SwiftHFR/Experimental/` is edited. No legacy behaviour is ported or changed on purpose: no Ledger section.

### What visibly changes
Only outside the modern card: the control row's and the formatting toolbar's buttons are 44 pt apart at least, so at compact density or a 70 % text size the glyphs sit further apart and the glass capsules are wider (legacy's row was 34 pt per cell, the issue accepts this). Outside the card at compact density or a 70 % text size, the row and the toolbar also get taller, and at 320 pt and 70 % the control row (356 pt) no longer fits one line and wraps to two (`ViewThatFits`). Nothing else moves, but the chip ✕ (1 pt lower).

### Tests (`ComposerTouchTargetTests`)
- `theImageChipCrossHasA44PointHitRegion(size:)` (xSmall, large, accessibility5) and `thePollAnswerRemoveButtonHasA44PointHitRegion`: hosted, measured frame. Red without the change: 18.3 / 21.3 pt and 19.7 pt; 44 after.
- `thePollAnswerRemoveButtonKeepsItsLayoutAndHasA44PointRegion(size:percent:)` (large, accessibility5, xSmall, xxxLarge, 70 %): the button's layout size equals its glyph's, and glyph plus overhang is 44 pt. Red first: layout was 44 against 19.7 (14 at 70 %).
- `theChipCrossGlyphIsCappedAtAccessibilitySizes`: the cross stays 44 pt at accessibility5.
- `theControlRowCellIsAtLeast44PointsInBothChromes`: `hitCell` of the cell is 44 at compact 100 %, regular 70 %, compact 70 % (40 / 33.6 / 28 before), card and not.
- `theHeaderCirclesOutsideTheCardKeepLegacysSize`: `ComposerToolSize(inCard: false)` stays the unclamped cell (it fails at the first head of this PR, where `cellSize` was clamped; not re-run red).
- Not covered, and why: that `contentShape(.rect)` makes the transparent margin take taps, and the poll row's negative padding. A hit test cannot show it in a unit-test host: probed on the simulator, the hosting view answers every window point with itself, with or without the shape. It needs an XCUITest tap (#893).
- Run: the whole Composer, Experimental and Polls test folders plus `DiscardConfirmationTests` (51 suites, 405 tests) pass on an iPhone 18 Pro, iOS 27.2.

For the #893 device check: at 70 % text size in compact density a List row's minimum height is 28 pt, so the row may be under 44 pt and cut the ⊖'s overflowing region. Check a tap at the ⊖'s top and bottom margins there.

Not checked: a tap on the ⊖ and the ✕ margins on a device (#893).
