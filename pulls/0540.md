## Problem
The reader shipped with the tab bar visible (LOT-083 / LOT-170). Legacy hides it while a topic is open (GAP-008, UXD-02 option A).

## Cause
No modifier on the reader.

## Change
`.toolbar(.hidden, for: .tabBar)` in `TopicReaderView`, the `// Port of … @ 2440d79` comments of `TopicReaderView` and `RootTabsView` now consistent with it (GAP-008, UXD-02), and the GAP-008 row and LOT-083 note updated. A followed link pushes another `TopicReaderView` (LOT-137), so it inherits the modifier; list screens do not have it, so the bar returns on the way back. Nothing else changes.

Legacy @ 2440d79: `.toolbar(.hidden, for: .tabBar)` at `HFRswift/Swift/Favorites.swift:807`, `HFRswift/Swift/MessagesView.swift:3256` and `:3909` (also `Common.swift:3198`).

## Tests

## Mac check needed
