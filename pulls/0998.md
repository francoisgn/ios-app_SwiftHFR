**Problem.** Favoris, Messages and Réglages open with a small inline title (`.navigationBarTitleDisplayMode(.inline)`), unlike Catégories and the current app; the inline title is a plain strip, not like the Liquid Glass buttons around it.

**Legacy (@ 87fe0d1c).** The main screens use the system large title: `navigationTitle("Messages")` (`MPListView.swift:636`), `("Favoris")` (`Favorites.swift:758`), `("Réglages")` (`AppSettingsView.swift:598`), `("Categories")` (`MainWindow.swift:195`), with `.expandedPhoneInlineNavigationTitle()` (`Common.swift:377-387`) forcing `.inline` only on the Duo inner display. So the default is `.automatic` (large), as asked.

**Design: one shared component** (`App/SwiftHFR/Navigation/ScreenTitle/ScreenTitle.swift`).
- `screenTitle(_:style:headerVisible:)` keeps the navigation title (back button, VoiceOver), removes the system inline title (`toolbar(removing: .title)`) and puts a **`TitlePill`** in the bar's `.principal` slot, with `sharedBackgroundVisibility(.hidden)` since the pill draws its own `glassEffect(.regular.interactive(), in: .capsule)`, 44 pt high like the buttons.
- `.largeThenPill` (main screens: Catégories, Favoris, Messages, Réglages, Plus): `ScreenLargeTitle`, the first row of the list/form (in its own `Section`, so the first real row keeps its rounded corners), shows the large title and reports its visibility (`onScrollVisibilityChange`); the pill appears once that row has scrolled away.
- `.pill` (pushed lists: a category's topic list, and the Recherche screen): the pill from the start (operator decision on #978).
- Pill: one line, « … » truncation, width from the free space between the buttons (the system's principal slot). **Tap** shows the full title in a popover anchored on the pill (a 2-3 line expansion inside the bar's height would be clipped); it folds on the next tap, a tap outside, or after 4 s. **Long press** copies the title (« Copié » in the pill, VoiceOver announcement, « Copier le titre » custom action). VoiceOver: one header element, hint explaining both gestures.
- The scroll-to-top gesture is the system status-bar tap, untouched by this code.
- #865 part: no title strip is kept at the top any more (the large title is content, the collapsed title is a floating pill over the content, the bar's buttons float as before).

**Wired in:** `ForumListView`, `TopicListView` (new `titleStyle`, default `.pill`; `TopicsColumn` gives `.largeThenPill` to Favoris only), `MessageListView` (minimal: 3 lines, since #964 edits this file), `SettingsView`, `PlusView`, `ForumSearchView` (pill).
