Problem: a smiley picked in the picker was inserted at the end of the message, not at the caret or over the selection.

Cause (plausible, not reproduced): `openSmileys()` captured the editor's live `TextSelection`, unreliable once the toolbar button or sheet takes focus; a nil range fell back to appending. Device check owed to say whether it was nil or a multi-selection.

Change: the model saves the range when a sheet opens (`ComposerFormattingModel.beginPick()`), and `insert(smiley:)` / `insertPicked(_:)` insert there, replacing a selection and moving the caret after the insert. Smiley, GIF and image sheets share it (the view's `rangeImage` and the GIF panel's `caret` are gone). The editor card's selection sync is extracted as `syncedRange` (nil keeps the current range). Formatting tags are unchanged: they still wrap a selection. The modern composer is hosted in `ReplyComposerView`, so it is covered with no change at its #904 markers.

Tests (`SmileyInsertionTests`, `PickedRangeTests`; each failed against the old shape, seen by capturing at insert time / returning the end for a nil selection):
- caret 5 → beginPick → caret moves to 9 → insert lands at 5 (smiley and image/GIF snippet)
- selection replaced, end append, UTF-16 caret, stale range clamped, punctuation, tag still wraps after a smiley
- `syncedRange`: nil keeps current, caret, multi-selection gives its first range
