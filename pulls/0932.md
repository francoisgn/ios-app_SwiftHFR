## What and why
Problem: the reader has no end-of-page state. Legacy promotes › at the end of a page that is not the last, and shows a refresh at the end of the last one. The operator also wants three things (#820, 2026-11-15 and 2026-11-16):
- the bubble and a bottom refresh at the end of the page;
- a pull to refresh with a discreet hint;
- one tight gap above the bottom bar.

| Part | Change | Legacy @ `87fe0d1c` |
|---|---|---|
| End of the page | `reader.js` reports it as a typed `edge` bridge message, on a change only: `scrollY + innerHeight` within 2 points of the bottom, legacy's formula. The visual viewport is read only under a pinch zoom. | `MessagesView.swift:1013-1035` (`notifyScrollState`) |
| Bar | Mid-page the bar shows ‹ › and reply. At the end of a page it adds the "N / M" bubble and the **same** `ReaderRefreshButton` as the top bar, with one `ReaderRefresh.run` path. › is `glassProminent` at the end of a page that is not the last, the refresh at the end of the last one, with a 0.22 s ease-out. VoiceOver keeps the bubble everywhere. Topic and MP readers alike. | `:4972-4978`, `:5639-5690`, `:5789-5790`, `:7372-7386` |
| Pull to refresh | A release past 72 pt beyond the end plays a light impact, then runs the same refresh, keeping the position. A shorter release does nothing. | `Common.swift:108-110` (`refreshStarted`), `:5051-5058` |
