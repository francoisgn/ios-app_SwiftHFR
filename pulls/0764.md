**Problem.** #755 wants HFR's dashes in front of a signature dropped, without touching dashes a member types. That needs HFR's exact markup. No capture showed it, because the anonymizer replaced the whole `span.signature` content with "Signature removed.".

**What was captured.** One public topic page, read anonymously on 2026-11-10 (`R267a`). It is `R20`'s topic, fetched at its canonical URL after HFR's two 301 hops, which are not published. All 9 signatures have the same shape:

```html
<span class="signature"> ---------------
			<br />…the member's signature…<div style="clear: both;"> </div></span>
```

- The 15 dashes are HFR's own: they open every signature span.
- The page has no other run of 15 dashes.
- Legacy deletes every `---------------` in the message HTML (`HFRswift/Wrapped/LinkItem.m:74` @ `87fe0d1c`), so it also deleted any in a message body.

**What changed.**
- `anonymize.py`: a signature span keeps HFR's head and HFR's closing clear div. Only what lies between them becomes "Signature removed.".
  - The head must match exactly: optional whitespace, exactly 15 dashes, whitespace, `<br />`.
  - Anything else is replaced as before: 16 dashes, dashes not followed by `<br />`, or anything after the head.
- `check` accepts only `(15 dashes)? Signature removed.` in a signature.
- The other changed files:
  - `R267a` and its meta, `corpus.json`, MANIFEST (Run 20 and a row);
  - `tooling/fixtures/README.md`;
  - HFR-PROTOCOL §6c LOT-034, new "Signature separator" bullet.

**Tests.**
- New unit tests in `tooling/fixtures/tests/test_anonymize.py`:
  - `test_signature_keeps_hfrs_separator_and_clear_div`;
  - `test_signature_dashes_typed_by_the_member_are_not_kept`;
  - `test_signature_check_accepts_only_the_separator_before_the_placeholder`.
- The first and last fail on the old anonymizer.
- The fixture tooling suite (339) passes, and `swift test` passes on every target.

**Leak check.** An independent check, separate from the redactor, passed for the batch. It also passed for the new rule: 24 crafted inputs, and no member text survived the head/tail logic. Locally, `check --corpus` (586 of 586) and `check --config` are clean.

**Not covered here.** The page has no message body with a line of dashes. #755's "a body line made of dashes is kept" case needs a unit fragment in its own tests.
