**Problem.** For an account whose pseudo `md_user` URL-encodes (e.g. "user two", key `user%20two`), the reader never classified its own posts (`.own`, class `me`) nor quotes of itself (`.ofMe`).

**Cause.** `VisibilityPolicy.init(ownPseudo:)` built `ownKey` from `PseudoNormalizer.comparisonForm` of the raw account key, which is the URL-encoded `md_user` value (HFR-PROTOCOL §5, `W03`); pages print the decoded pseudo.

**Change.** `ownKey` becomes `ownKeys`, built from the raw and percent-decoded forms of the account key. The pure rule moves to `HFRText.accountKeyForms(of:)` (HFRParsing: usable by HFRClient and the app; `PseudoListManager.keyForms` delegates to it). `FilteredPostsCriteria.keeps` uses the same forms for own posts, quotes and mentions (review round 1, finding 1). Accented pseudos stay pending capture (#140): a key that does not decode as UTF-8 is compared raw only, no guessed encoding. The `+` read as a space is a tolerance only (no capture shows it in `md_user`).

**Tests.** Red before, green after:
- HFRKit `FilteredPostsCriteriaTests.encodedAccountKey` (`user%20two`): 3 issues before the change, green after; `accountKeyForms` pinned by its own test; full `swift test` in Packages/HFRKit passes (585 tests).
- App (iOS 27.2 simulator, unsigned): `VisibilityPolicyTests` (`anEncodedAccountKeyMatchesItsOwnPostsAndQuotes`, red 4 issues before), `VisibilityWebTests` including the rendered `me` class (`anEncodedAccountKeyGivesItsPostsTheMeClass`, red before), `PseudoListManagerTests`: 40 tests green.

**Survey of own-pseudo comparisons that take the raw account key**
- Fixed: `VisibilityPolicy`, `FilteredPostsCriteria` (above); `PseudoListManager.isOwn` already (#1290).
- Not fixed, not the same helper: `QualityAlertActionModel.swift:120, 179, 217` (`held.initiator` and the `pseudo` sent to the AQ service are the raw key). Legacy (`MessagesView.swift:354, 441-442` @ 87fe0d1c) sends `currentAccountIdentity().displayNameLowercased` (the account's display name, not the stored key) and compares nothing locally. The fix is to feed the account's display name (`AccountRow.displayName`) to `LiveQualityAlertsSource.services`, which `AppEnvironment.make` does not receive today; the service's stored form for accented names is not captured. Left for a follow-up issue (text in the PR comment).
- `ForumVisibility.hidesCategory` (review account name, no encoded character) and `LivePrivateMessageSource.locatedStoragePost(of:)` / `MPCheckForum` (store key, no page comparison): unaffected.
