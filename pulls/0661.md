**Problem.** On the MP list, a read conversation's title stayed in the primary colour, so read and unread rows differed only by weight, dot and tint. Legacy greys read titles.

**Cause.** `MessageRowView` set the weight from the read state but never the colour. Legacy's shared `TopicListRowView` draws the title `.foregroundStyle(isVisited ? .secondary : .primary)` (`Common.swift:3118-3120` @ `2440d79`), and `MPRowView` passes `isVisited: isViewed` (`MPListView.swift:464-470`).

**Change.**
- `MessageTitleTone` (new, `App/SwiftHFR/Messages/`): unread → `.primary`, semibold; read → `ReadTitleStyle`, regular. `MessageRowView` uses it. The "non lu" capsule, the dot and the row tint stay, as the operator asked on #651.
- `ReadTitleStyle` is the system's secondary label (measured on the iOS 27 simulator: SwiftUI's `.secondary` resolves to `secondaryLabel`), **except in light**, where it keeps its Increase Contrast strength (alpha 0.8 instead of 0.6). The standard light one, legacy's colour, measures **3.4:1** on the white card; #651 asks for 4.5:1. It is now 6:1.
- **On the iPad's accent selection** (a prominent background) a read title uses the primary style, which the system turns white, as for an unread title. The hierarchical `.secondary` measured 1.7 to 2.3:1 there (review round 1).
- Spec: LOT-126 (`Title (#651)`, `Title contrast`, D5, the iPad measurements). GAP-227 edited in place, changelog fragment `651.md`.

**Themes.** The MP list has light and dark, each with and without Increase Contrast. The accent hue changes only the unread tint (covered by the worst case), the dark brightness only the reader. LOT-122's darker dark and OLED black are not in the app yet (#213), and classic/modern are reader styles.

| Theme | Read title on the card | Unread title, untinted / worst tint |
|---|---|---|
| Light | 6.0:1 (legacy 3.4:1) | 21 / 16.7 |
| Light, Increase Contrast | 6.0:1 | 21 / 13.8 |
| Dark | 6.0:1 | 17 / 10.3 |
| Dark, Increase Contrast | 7.1:1 | 15.5 / 7.0 |

**Mac check (iPad Pro 13-inch (M5) simulator, iOS 27.0).** A probe test hosted the real `MessageRowView` in `List(selection:)`, in `MessagesRootView`'s two-column split and the iPad root's three-column one, then read the window's pixels in all four themes.
- Unselected read rows: 5.97 / 5.93 / 7.11:1 on the content column's card, the same as the unit test. On the two-column sidebar's grey row (216,219,223 in light): 4.80 to 6.38:1.
- **Selected row: the system's accent fill. Its own white title is 3.9:1 in light and 2.7:1 in dark with the default blue, so no title colour reaches 4.5:1 there.** That fill is the system highlight that #570/#576 chose to keep. Changing it is a separate decision, not part of this PR.

**Not in scope, left to its own issue.** Topic lists also grey visited titles in legacy (`Favorites.swift:1010-1013`, `MainWindow.swift:652-657`, `ForumSearchView.swift:346-348`), but SwiftHFR's `TopicRowView` does not. Those rows carry flag tints, so a read title can sit on a tinted row. Recorded in LOT-126.

**Tests** (`MessageTitleToneTests`, Swift Testing, iOS 27 simulator). Each one fails when its part of the change is reverted (mutation runs):
- `rowDrawsTheTitleTone(dark:)`: renders `MessageRowView` with `ImageRenderer` and reads a pixel inside the title. It fails if the row loses `.foregroundStyle(titleTone.style)` or ignores the read state.
- `drawnColour(dark:)`: the tone's style paints the read colour and the primary colour (solid swatches).
- `prominentBackgroundUsesThePrimaryStyle(dark:)`: on increased prominence, a read title paints like an unread one. It fails with the hierarchical `.secondary`.
- `toneFollowsTheMembersReadState`: read → secondary and regular, unread → primary and semibold.
- `readTitleIsNotPrimary`, `readTitleContrast`, `unreadTitleContrast` (×4 themes): WCAG ratios on the system colours resolved per trait collection, including the tinted card at `RowTint`'s strength with a black or white tint (the worst any accent can do).
- `darkIsTheSystemSecondary`, `lightUsesTheIncreasedContrastSecondary`: pin the rule, and that the standard light secondary is under 4.5:1. The legacy colour in light fails `readTitleContrast` and this test.

Local: targeted suites on the iOS 27 simulator (three clean runs), plus the full app suite on the first head. 13 offline/draft tests failed while the Mac's disk was full, then all 61 passed when re-run alone. `swiftlint lint --strict` clean, `fold.py --check` ok, markdownlint clean.
