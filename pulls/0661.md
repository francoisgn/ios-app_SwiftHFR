**Problem.** On the MP list, a read conversation's title stayed in the primary colour, so read and unread rows differed only by weight, dot and tint. Legacy greys read titles.

**Cause.** `MessageRowView` set the weight from the read state but never the colour. Legacy's shared `TopicListRowView` draws the title `.foregroundStyle(isVisited ? .secondary : .primary)` (`Common.swift:3119-3120` @ `2440d79`), and `MPRowView` passes `isVisited: isViewed` (`MPListView.swift:464-470`).

**Change.**
- `MessageTitleTone` (new, `App/SwiftHFR/Messages/`): unread → `.primary`, semibold; read → `ReadTitleStyle`, regular. `MessageRowView` uses it. The "non lu" capsule, the dot and the row tint stay, as the operator asked on #651.
- `ReadTitleStyle` is the system's secondary label (measured on the iOS 27 simulator: SwiftUI's `.secondary` resolves to `secondaryLabel`), **except in light**, where it keeps its Increase Contrast strength (alpha 0.8 instead of 0.6). The standard light one, legacy's colour, measures **3.4:1** on the white card; #651 asks for 4.5:1. It is now 6:1. On a prominent background (the iPad's accent selection) it falls back to the hierarchical `.secondary`, which the system adapts.
- Spec: LOT-126 (`Title (#651)`, `Title contrast` rows, D5, a hand check of the iPad selection). GAP-227 edited in place, changelog fragment `651.md`.
- Nothing else: topic lists also grey read titles in legacy (`Favorites.swift:1010`, `MainWindow.swift:653`, `ForumSearchView.swift:346`) and SwiftHFR's `TopicRowView` does not, but those rows carry flag tints; that is a separate issue.

**Themes.** The MP list has light and dark, each with and without Increase Contrast. The accent hue changes only the unread tint (covered by the worst case), the dark brightness only the reader. LOT-122's darker dark and OLED black are not in the app yet (#213), and classic/modern are reader styles.

| Theme | Read title on the card | Unread title, untinted / worst tint |
|---|---|---|
| Light | 6.0:1 (legacy 3.4:1) | 21 / 16.7 |
| Light, Increase Contrast | 6.0:1 | 21 / 13.8 |
| Dark | 6.0:1 | 17 / 10.3 |
| Dark, Increase Contrast | 7.1:1 | 15.5 / 7.0 |

**Tests** (`MessageTitleToneTests`, Swift Testing, iOS 27 simulator). Each one fails when the change is reverted (mutation runs):
- `toneFollowsTheMembersReadState`: read → secondary and regular, unread → primary and semibold (fails if the read tone is `.primary`).
- `drawnColour(dark:)`: renders the tone's style with `ImageRenderer` and reads the pixel. A read title painted in the primary colour fails it.
- `readTitleIsNotPrimary`, `readTitleContrast`, `unreadTitleContrast` (×4 themes): WCAG ratios on the system colours resolved per trait collection, including the tinted card at `RowTint`'s strength with a black or white tint (the worst any accent can do).
- `darkIsTheSystemSecondary`, `lightUsesTheIncreasedContrastSecondary`: pins the rule and that the standard light secondary is under 4.5:1. The legacy colour in light fails `readTitleContrast` and this test.
