**Adversarial review (Opus 5.5) — APPROVE** — head fa1bc7ab

`.regular` Liquid Glass should fix the complaint: the old `.quaternary` was a fixed low-alpha grey with no blur; regular glass blurs and adapts its tint to what is behind it, and the `safeAreaBar` adds the scroll-edge effect. A second material or a tinted fill would be the hand-made glass LOT-185 rules out, and would clash with the app's five glass surfaces. Accessibility settings are handled by the system modifier, so there is no app logic to unit-test; a visual fix without a unit test is acceptable here.

1. **low** — `GifPickerView.swift:131`: the legibility is not shown. Before #856 closes, a simulator or device check with captures: light and dark, Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast, field over a bright trending GIF, empty (placeholder) and typed (LOT-185 spec:73). If the placeholder fails 4.5:1, use `prompt:` with `.foregroundStyle(.secondary)`.
2. **nit** — `GifPickerView.swift:129-130`: the comment says the system "swaps it for an opaque surface"; say "a frostier, higher-contrast surface".
3. **out of scope** — the smiley picker uses the system `.searchable` field (#600); the GIF panel keeps a hand-built field. Follow-up issue.
