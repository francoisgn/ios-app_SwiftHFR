**Problem**: in the floating (partial-height) composer the text card stops growing after six lines whatever the room above it; the operator wants it to grow until the top of the buttons nearly reaches the first line of the page's last message (device screenshot, 2026-11-18).

**Regression or cap?** The cap, as far as the code shows (investigation on #1136): growth below six lines was intact and pinned (`ComposerPresentationTests`, hosted test), and the measuring path had no recent change. A device-only regression cannot be excluded from a Linux session; the new geometry test pins growth on 12 and 40 lines.

**Cause**: `ComposerCardLayout.editorHeight` capped the text at `maximumLines = 6` lines of the body font, a number chosen when the card was first drawn (#653), with no relation to the screen.

**Change**:
- `ComposerCardLayout.room(window:topInset:keyboard:line:)`: the tallest card the screen allows = window height − top safe-area inset − the navigation bar (44 pt, HIG) − the keyboard − a strip of the page above (`pageStripLines` = 3 lines of the body font, enough for the header of the page's last message). The system lifts the sheet over the keyboard, so the keyboard counts here and not in the card's own height.
- `ComposerCardLayout.availableHeight` (nil until measured) and `textCap`: the editor's text grows up to what is left of the room after the buttons, banners, chips, controls, gaps and the text view's insets; never under six lines (today's cap, also the cap until the screen is measured, so a failed measure keeps today's behaviour). Past it the editor scrolls inside the card.
- `ComposerScreen` (new, `Experimental/ModernComposer`): the key window's height and top inset, and the keyboard's overlap from `keyboardWillChangeFrameNotification`; the `ComposerRoomUpdates` view modifier (`ComposerScreen.swift`) feeds `measured.availableHeight` for the modern chrome (`.docked`) only. The sheet's detent is already the card's measured height, so it follows; the legacy composer, the full height and landscape are unchanged.

- Review round 1 (posted on the PR): the room has no value until the keyboard has shown once (`ComposerKeyboardRoom`: six lines, so a long draft never sizes the card over the whole screen then shrinks it), and a hidden keyboard keeps the last room (the card does not rise over a page the user scrolled to read). Pure test `theRoomFollowsTheKeyboardOnlyWhileItIsUp`.

**Value on an iPhone 18 Pro (402 × 874 pt)**, illustrative until measured on the device (#893): top inset 62 pt, navigation bar 44 pt, keyboard about 336 pt with its suggestion bar, strip 3 × 20 pt: room = 874 − 62 − 44 − 336 − 60 = 372 pt for the whole card, about 250 pt for the editor's text (about 12 lines at the default size) against 120 pt today.

**Tests**: `ComposerCardGrowthTests` (pure): editor height for 1, 6, 12 and 40 lines on a 600 pt room (2 lines, 6, 12, then the room), the card never above the room, the unmeasured cap is six lines, a tight screen never goes under six lines, and the room formula (window, safe area, navigation bar, keyboard, strip). They fail on the base: `availableHeight` and `room` do not exist (compile failure), and 12 lines would be capped at 6. No Mac here, nothing was run; CI is the first run.

**Not verified (cloud)**: the room's real value and where the card stops on the device (the three-line strip and the 44 pt bar are the operator's to tune on #893), the keyboard notification across rotation and the floating-keyboard iPad (not a partial-height case), and the reader's docked bar (the same window-based room applies to it).

**Overlap**: #1217 (#1137, new MP and topic full screen) touches `ComposerHeight.shown/offersToggle`, not the card layout; no conflict expected. #1171 (Reduce Motion) touches the formatting bar's transition in `ReplyComposerView.swift`.
