- **« Doux » (proposed values, for the operator to validate on the screenshot):** page `#F5F4EF` (off-white), placeholder `#E9E7DE`, quiet separator `#D6D3C8`, secondary text `#636366` (a notch darker than #6C6C70, which measured 4.22:1 on the warm placeholder and 4.30:1 on the folded quote's 5 % tint). Contrast, recomputed with the WCAG formula: text `#1C1C1E` 15.5:1 on the page and 13.7:1 on the placeholder; the new secondary text 5.4:1 on the page, 4.8:1 on the placeholder, 4.9:1 on the folded quote tint, 5.1:1 on a classic header (15 % of the day accent) and 5.4:1 on a modern one. **« Quotes a step darker » is not drawn:** the default quote has no fill (#819, legacy `style-liste.css:329-334`), so no quote surface is sent.

The native list **rows** stay white (« a step lighter » than the Doux page); tinting them through the `ThemedList` tokens (#842) is follow-up #1221, for the operator's call.
- **Where it applies.** The reader (`--app-light-*` set by `readerScript`), the web view's own layer (`readerBackground`), the native screens (`ScreenFill.softLight`: Doux's page in light, the dark style's fill or the system's own background in dark, resolved with the fill's own trait collection so sheets keep their elevated black; one dynamic colour like a theme's), and the settings preview. A gallery theme ignores the light style, like the dark one. In dark, « Doux » also hides the list and split-view backgrounds (« Blanc » leaves them to the system), so an iPad sidebar loses its own material, as under any theme: added to the #893 device check.
- **The screen follows « Thème »** (`ThemeSettingsModel.Mode.controls`): Clair → « Style du thème clair » (Blanc / Doux) + a light preview; Sombre → the dark style, its preview and the brightness; Système → both groups, each titled « Thème clair » / « Thème sombre ». The accent hue shows in every case. `docs/THEMES.md` describes it.
- **« Thème » as three tiles** (`ThemeModeTiles`, comment of 2026-11-18): Clair, Sombre, Système (the dark card clipped to the lower-right half of the light one), each a rounded card with two text lines and a dot, drawn with SwiftUI shapes from `ThemeAppearance.tileLook(dark:)`: the light style's page or the dark style's page at the chosen brightness (a gallery theme's tokens under a theme), and the day or night accent for the dot, so it follows the hue slider live. Each tile is a plain button labelled « Thème clair » / « Thème sombre » / « Thème du système » carrying the selected trait when chosen; the chosen tile has an accent outline and label. They sit in an adaptive grid whose cell size is a `@ScaledMetric`, so they wrap instead of clipping at Dynamic Type XL. The old menu's `Mode.title` is removed (no user left).
