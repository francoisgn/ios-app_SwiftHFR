## Problem
`status: blocked` carries two meanings: a nameable dependency, and a decision (M5 not open, an operator's call). The live tracker has 63 open `status: blocked` issues. Many have every cited blocker closed (mostly M5 lots held by `M5_OPEN`), or name no readable blocker. A label that encodes a policy cannot be cleared by a script.

## Cause
- SwiftHFR's status axis has no state for "held by a decision".
- Nothing requires a blocked issue to name its blocker.
- `hygiene.py` unblocked lots from `lots.json` alone, and kept M5 lots `blocked` by a hard-coded rule.
