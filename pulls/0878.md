# PR #878: [SwiftHFR] Reader: give the web view the real top and bottom insets (last lines no longer under the bottom controls)

Closes #852
Refs #641
Claim: mac/M1

**Problem.** In the reader, the last lines of a page stay hidden behind the floating bottom controls and cannot be scrolled above them. The top edge (#641) shows the same symptom.

**Cause.** `TopicReaderView` applied `.ignoresSafeArea(edges: .vertical)` outside the `GeometryReader` that reads `safeAreaInsets`. Under that modifier the `GeometryReader` reports zero insets, so the web view got no top or bottom inset.

**Change.**
- New `ReaderUnderBars` reads the safe area first, then lets its content run under the bars; `TopicReaderView` uses it.
- It covers both edges. The web controller's inset handling was already correct and is unchanged.
- The keyboard region is ignored on the wrapper, so an open composer does not move the insets (not unit-testable: device check).

**Tests.**
- `ReaderUnderBarsTests.theBarsOfTheSafeAreaReachTheContentAtBothEdges` hosts the wrapper with a known safe area (top 100, bottom 80) and expects the content to receive at least that.
- It failed with the old structure (top 0.0, bottom 0.0) and passes after the change.
- Full suite green on iPhone 18 Pro and iPad Pro 11-inch (M5).

**Not verified**: on a device, scrolling to the end of a page (iPhone and iPad, portrait and landscape), #641 on a one-post page, and the page not moving under an open composer with its keyboard.

**Gate**: the iPad full suite hit the known flake #564 once (`TopicRowActionsTests.aRemovalInFlightIsNotSentTwice`); its rerun passed on both devices.
