**LOT-031**: per-message action links. Spec: `docs/lots/LOT-031-message-actions.md`. Sets the LOT-031 row of `docs/LOTS.md` to `done (#28)`.

**Problem.** The topic page needs, for each message, the quote, edit, alert, profile, new-MP and favourite links and the multi-quote arguments, typed into LOT-011's `MessageActions`. Nothing read them yet.

**Cause.** LOT-030 extracts the blocks and their `div.toolbar`, but the per-message links of `ParseMessagesOperation.m:371-390` and the URL handling of `ObjCMessageActionsBuilder` were not ported yet.

**What changed.**
- `MessageActionsParser.actions(in:)` (public) returns one `MessageActions` per block, in `MessageBlockParser.blocks(in:)` order. `actions(of:)` (internal) is the entry point for the future `TopicPage` builder, which works on the same `Document`.
- Lookups follow the legacy selectors (`alt="answer"`, `alt="edit"`, `alt="profil"`, `modo.php`, `addflag`, `quoter('hardwarefr'`), with these differences, all documented in the spec:
  - **Scope:** they run inside the block's `div.toolbar` only. Legacy searched the whole message row, so a link a member posts in a message could become an action.
  - **Origin:** every URL must be `https://forum.hardware.fr` (`HFRURL.forumRelativePath`, LESSONS 37/43). The app follows these links with the session, and `addflag` is a state-changing GET.
  - **Undecodable class:** gives nil, where legacy built a URL from the class name.
  - **MP link:** recognised by its parameters (`cat=prive`, non-empty `dest`), not by the literal legacy prefix.
  - **Multi-quote:** becomes a `QuoteSelectionToken`, valid only for the block's own `numreponse`.
- Out of scope on purpose: `delflag`, `delete` and policy flags.
  - The `delflag` link is page-level (header) and LOT-033 decision 5 leaves it out.
  - Delete is the edit form with `delete=1` (LOT-048).
  - `canDelete`, `isOwnMessage` and the other flags are app policy (LOT-011 decision 5).
- Docs:
  - HFR-PROTOCOL §6c `### LOT-031` (the toolbar link shapes).
  - A capture backlog row: own post on a public topic, and a quote/edit link printed in clear.
  - An AGENTS §11 learning.

**Tests.**
- `MessageActionsOracleTests.matchesOracle` (20 cases, 358 messages): every URL equals the legacy `result.actions[]` value, and the multi-quote token gives the same cookie name and id as legacy's `quoteJS` handling.
- `corpusCoversEveryAction` checks that every action appears in the oracles, so the comparison is never vacuous.
- `MessageActionsParserTests` covers:
  - `R21`, `R20`, `RA4`, `R22a` specifics.
  - Page-level `delflag`/`modo` links ignored.
  - `RA5`/`R04` give `[]`.
  - Links posted in the body are ignored.
  - Off-origin links (6 forms) give nil.
  - http upgrade and relative links resolve on the forum.
  - Obfuscated, in-clear and undecodable classes.
  - MP link variants.
  - `quoter` argument forms and mismatches.
  - Typed throws.
- Shown to fail without the change:
  - With `actions(of:)` returning an empty set, the oracle test fails on 19 of 20 cases and the positive unit tests fail.
  - With legacy behaviour (whole row, `HFRURL.absolute` instead of the origin check), the body-link, off-origin (4 of 6) and obfuscated off-origin tests fail.
- `swift test` passes locally on the whole package (HFRParsing 267 tests). `check-lot-deps.py` and markdownlint pass. SwiftLint was checked by hand (LESSONS 21).
