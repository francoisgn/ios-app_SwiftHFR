## What and why
The FilteredPosts tests hit the 60 s suite limit at random on a loaded parallel simulator clone. Causes removed (tests only, `FilteredPostsModel` untouched, no race found there):
- `settle()` polled `isRunning || isAdvancing` with `Task.yield()` on the main actor → now `await model.lastRun?.value`, resumed by the run's own completion.
- `ReaderGate.untilArrived()` polled with `Task.yield()` → now a continuation resumed by `wait()` (shared helper; other users call it once per gate and keep working).
- `aRunCancelledWhileItsBatchIsBeingNormalizedNeverDelivers` blocked a pool thread and a GCD thread on two semaphores → `CancellingNormalizer` cancels the run's task during the first message; no thread waits. It now also fails if the per-message `Task.isCancelled` check in `result(of:)` is removed (the old test was hidden by the generation guard).
- Suite `.timeLimit` raised from 1 to 5 minutes: kept only as a backstop for a real hang (`untilArrived` waits forever if a regression skips the gated call); no verdict depends on it.

## How it was verified
Not buildable on Linux: SwiftLint clean; Swift 6 concurrency reviewed by Opus (APPROVE, round 1; its low/nit findings were folded into the second commit: comment rewording, one-waiter note). CI must run the suites 3 times in a row (issue acceptance).
Out of scope, noted by the reviewer: `ContentLoaderTests.swift` private `Gate` and a bounded yield loop in `AccountsModelTests.swift:129` use the same polling pattern.
