**Problem.** LOT-052 (MPStorage transport, critical path) needs to find the storage MP on the MP list and read the JSON document of its first message; nothing parsed those pages.

**Cause.** The parsing half of `MPStorage.m` (`findStorageMPFromPage:` `:692-736`, `parseMPStorage:` `:835-903` at `2440d79`) was not ported yet.

**Change** (`Packages/HFRKit/Sources/HFRParsing/MPStorage/`, spec `docs/lots/LOT-038-mpstorage-page.md`):
- `MPStoragePageParser.findThread(in:pageURL:)`: one MP list page through `PrivateMessageListParser` (LOT-023); the first row titled exactly the subject (with or without `[non lu] `) gives `.found(post:)`, else `.notOnPage(navigation:)` with the LOT-022 pager for the walk.
- `MPStoragePageParser.readDocument(in:)`: the thread's first page through `MessageBlockParser` (LOT-030). Gives `.storage(MPStorageMessage)` (`numreponse`, the text handed unchanged to `MPStorageCodec.decode`, the document) or `.notAddressed` for HFR's "Ce message ne vous est pas destiné" page; typed `MPStoragePageError` otherwise (`noMessage`, `notFirstPage`, `notStorageThread`, `missingParagraph`, `invalidDocument`, `unreadablePage`), never an empty document.
- `TopicPageMetadataParser.title(in:)` goes from `private` to `internal` to read the subject (no behaviour change).
- No change to `HFRModels` (`JSONValue`, `MPStorageCodec`, LOT-013 types untouched). No network, no cache, no alert: those are LOT-052's.

Why this wide: the issue's two parsers only. The GET URLs, the page walk and the writes stay in LOT-052.

**Tests** (`MPStoragePageParserTests`, 7 tests, 15 cases):
- `findMatchesOracle`: `S-find-R13u1`, `S-find-R13u1b` give post 3157083 = the oracle's `sPostId`; `S-find-R13a` gives `notOnPage` with no next page (the oracle's single GET).
- `readMatchesOracle`: on `R60a`, `numreponse` = `sNumRep` and the document = the oracle's `dData` through the codec.
- `readKeepsTextExact`: the text equals the raw `div#para…` paragraph of the capture with `&#034;` decoded, read with a regex independent of the parser.
- `notAddressed` (`RA5`), `otherPagesThrow` (`RA1`, `RU1`, `R20` → `notStorageThread`; `R22a` → `notFirstPage`; `R13a`, `R12` → `noMessage`), `subjectMatchIsExact`, `findsMarkedRow`.
- Shown to fail: six mutations (`contains` instead of equality, no `notAddressed`, no subject check, no continuation guard, `find` never found, `numreponse` off by one) each make a named test fail.
- Local: `swift test` green (all suites), SwiftLint clean on the new files, `check-lot-deps.py` 0 violations, markdownlint 0 errors.

**LEGACY FIXES** (CHANGELOG-LEGACY row 47): the page must be the storage thread's first page; `destiné` is looked for in the `.hop` notice of a page without messages only; malformed JSON is a typed error instead of turning the feature off for every account. The LOT-022 pager replaces the last child of `div.left` (LOT-004 RC4.6).

**PENDING CAPTURE**: a non-empty document (other clients' namespaces, pseudos, near 64 KiB, non-BMP, U+00A0 or smiley codes inside strings: #272); the storage row on page 2+ and the "destiné" answer for the storage thread itself (#333); a list page past the last one (#114); a storage message without a paragraph. Until #272 lands, U+00A0 removal and `invalidDocument` are not exercised on a page.
