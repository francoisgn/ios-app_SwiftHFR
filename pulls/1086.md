### Problem
Compact density, iPhone in landscape (side safe area): the section header text does not line up with the row text.

### Cause
Measured with a hosted probe (a list as the window's root, `additionalSafeAreaInsets` left 62 as the notch inset; 932x430 and 874x402):
- `contentMargins(.horizontal, 10, for: .scrollContent)` replaces the system's whole side inset, safe area included (regular: card at x = 82 = 62 + 20; compact before the fix: card at x = 10, under the notch), while the rows keep the safe area inside the card: row text 59 pt from the card instead of 7.
- `densityCardEdge` added `safeLeading` to the list frame's x, but the frame already starts past the safe area (x = 62): the header target was 134 instead of 72 and the header's 20 pt cap kept it at 92 pt from the card.
- Right side: the card also sat under a right-side inset (notch, Duo cover) at every revision, since the 10 pt margin replaced the trailing safe inset too (header and row text looked aligned because the leading inset is 0 there).

Evidence (offset from the card edge, expected 7 / 7; header, row):
| Code | 932x430, notch left | 874x402, notch left | 874x402, both sides |
|---|---|---|---|
| before #1048 (`1df2121a^`) | 69, 59 | 69, 59 | 69, 59 |
| head `74d863c1` (with #1048) | 92, 59 | 92, 59 | 88, 59 |
| this PR | 7, 7 | 7, 7 | 7, 7 |
So #1048 did not change the landscape case (it fixed the iPad/pushed-screen one); this was a separate defect. Notch on the right only: 7, 7 at every revision.

### Change
`DensityListMargins` (`App/SwiftHFR/Settings/ListDensity.swift`): leading and trailing scroll content margins add the measured safe inset (`safeAreaInsets.leading` / `.trailing`, so RTL follows; the trailing one is what keeps the card out of a right-side notch), and `densityCardEdge` is the frame's x plus the margin. Regular density is untouched (nil margins).
No wider: the header cap and `DensityHeaderMargin` are unchanged.

### Tests
`ListDensityMarginsTests.compactHeadersAlignInLandscape(size:regularClass:safeArea:)`, 6 cases (932x430 regular width, 874x402 compact width; insets left, right, both, right 84 = Duo cover): header and row 7 pt from the card (the cell's window frame) and the window gap right of the card = 10 + right inset. Round 1: the right-edge check fails (4 cases) with `+ safeTrailing` dropped, passes with it. `SectionProbe` now hosts the list as the root when nothing precedes it, so it extends under the side safe area as a screen's list does (an HStack parent put it inside the inset, where it read no inset); the `leading:` case keeps the HStack.
- Fails without the change: 6 issues (3 of the 5 cases, header and row), numbers in the table above; the existing portrait and iPad cases pass.
- Whole `SwiftHFRTests` target: 1917 tests, 246 suites, passed (xb.sh, iPhone 18 Pro iOS 27.0).

### Device check (operator, landscape)
Compact on and off, Favoris / Messages / Search / Settings lists, notch on the left and on the right (rotate both ways): card edge keeps about 10 pt from the safe-area edge on both sides (card edge out of the notch on the right too), header text and row text on one vertical line, nothing under the notch; LTR (RTL if a device is set). The probe sets the safe area with `additionalSafeAreaInsets`, not a real notch: unverified on a device.
