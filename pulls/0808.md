### Summary
As a member, I want Favoris, a category's topic list and the MP list to share legacy's list look (operator decision on #803, 2026-11-11): one rounded block per section, flat rows with thin separators, highlighted rows as rounded tinted pills inset inside the block, the sub-forum picker and filter at the top of the same block.

### Problem
Legacy's block is the system inset-grouped list, which the app already has. Two things differ: a highlighted row (favourite, flag, unread MP) is a full-width row background instead of a pill, and in a category the picker and the filter sit in their own section, apart from the topics.

### Cause
Highlights were `listRowBackground` over the card colour (#570); `ForumScopeBar` wrapped the filter in a `Section`.

### Change
- `Common/RowTint.swift`: `RowTint` is legacy's pill, corner 12, overflow top 4 (super favourite) or 2, sides 8, bottom 2 (`Common.swift:3677-3684`, `Favorites.swift:1134-1138`, `MainWindow.swift:736-740` @ 87fe0d1c). `rowPill(_:overflow:)` draws it behind the row; the overflow is capped by the density's `rowInsets`, so it composes with #804's compact margins (`ListDensity.swift` untouched). `RowTint.Base` is gone (a pill needs no base colour).
- `Navigation/TopicListView.swift` (category topics, Favoris, Drapeaux): rows use `rowPill`; the `tintBase` parameter is removed; legacy pointer comment.
- `Navigation/ForumScopeBar.swift`: the picker, filter and note are rows of the topic block, not a section.
- `Root/RootSidebarView.swift`: drops `tintBase: .clear`. The iPad sidebar rows now draw the same pill (iPad shots pending).
- `Messages/MessageListView.swift`: the unread MP tint is drawn as a pill at the call site (see the regression below); `MessageRowView.swift` no longer applies it itself.
- No `listStyle` change. Catégories root, profile and drafts untouched.
- No ledger entry: this matches legacy, and no GAP row describes list style.

### Open operator question
