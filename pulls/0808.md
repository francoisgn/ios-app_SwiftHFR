### Summary
As a member, I want Favoris, a category's topic list and the MP list to share legacy's list look (operator decision on #803, 2026-11-11): one rounded block per section, flat rows with thin separators, highlighted rows as rounded tinted pills inset inside the block, the sub-forum picker and filter at the top of the same block.

### Problem
Legacy's block is the system inset-grouped list, which the app already has. Two things differ: a highlighted row (favourite, flag, unread MP) is a full-width row background instead of a pill, and in a category the picker and the filter sit in their own section, apart from the topics.

### Cause
Highlights were `listRowBackground` over the card colour (#570); `ForumScopeBar` wrapped the filter in a `Section`.

### Change
- `Common/RowTint.swift`: `RowTint` is legacy's pill, corner 12 (`Common.swift:3677-3684`, `Favorites.swift:1134-1138`, `MainWindow.swift:736-740,771` @ 87fe0d1c). `rowPill(_:isSuperFavorite:)` draws it behind the row. `RowTint.overflow(isSuperFavorite:insets:)` measures it from the cell edge as legacy does: it stops `edgeGap` (4 pt) short of the block edge whatever the density (regular inset 16 gives 12 of overflow, #804's compact inset 7 gives 3), reaches 4 pt above and below a flagged row's content and 6 above a super favourite's, never past the cell. `RowTint.Base` is gone (a pill needs no base colour); `ListDensity.swift` is untouched.
- `Navigation/TopicListView.swift` (category topics, Favoris, Drapeaux): rows use `rowPill`; the `tintBase` parameter is removed. `flagPills(for:)`: Favoris and Drapeaux, legacy's one Favoris view (`vos_sujets` 0 or 1), pill only super favourites (`Favorites.swift:1134`); the category lists pill every flagged row. One switch, so the operator's answer on the Favoris flag pills is a one-line change.
- `Navigation/ForumScopeBar.swift`: the picker, filter and note are rows of the topic block (`densityRowMargin()`, #804), not a section.
