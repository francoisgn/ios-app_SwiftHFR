LOT-094

Claim: cloud/lot-094

**Problem.** `DeletePrivateMessageFlow` (#441) read the session's identity after the check, then posted in a second actor call. A login adopted in that hop sent this account's token with the new cookies. This was CodeRabbit's Low note on #441, accepted by the operator on 2026-11-02 and deferred to this follow-up.

**Change.**
- The seed (identity, token, `loginGeneration`) is still taken once, before the check.
- The POST now goes through `PostingSession.send(since:)` from #412, as the poll vote does. In one turn of the session actor it compares identity and generation with the seed, reads the token and builds the request.
- `.loginChanged` maps to the existing `sessionChanged` error (public API unchanged), `.missingToken` to `missingToken`, `.cancelled` to `network(.cancelled)`. Exchange failures are classified as before.
- No unfenced delete POST is left in HFRClient: `send(_:token:through:)` is removed, and the request and answer tests go through the fenced path.
- Spec: the fence covers construction of the request only. The cookies `URLSession` attaches afterwards are not fenced. HFR's answer to a token paired with another login's cookies is unknown until captured: `W64x` is a wrong token with the same account's cookies, not this case.

**Tests** (`swift test` after merging the latest `dev-swift`: 1403 tests, green; `swiftlint --strict` clean; `check-lot-deps.py` 0 violations)
- `loginChangedBeforeThePostSendsNothing`: seed taken, then a login of another account, a login of the same account or a logout lands before the POST. Result: `sessionChanged`, nothing sent (scripted session).
- `loginChangedOnHFRSession`: a real `HFRSession` restricted to loopback, so nothing can reach HFR. `advanceLoginGeneration()` after the seed gives `sessionChanged`. Without it, the delete passes the fence and is stopped by the host policy.
- Shown to fail without the fence: with the seed replaced by one re-read right before the send (the old hop), the new tests and the during-check tests fail.

**Review:** Fable APPROVE on c579baf (record below). A docs-only round for CodeRabbit followed; Fable re-review of the head is pending.

**Follow-up for cloud/orch:** move `FencedSend` and `send(since:)` from `Polls/PollVoteFlow.swift` to `Posting/` (Fable finding 3), since two flows now use them.
