**Problem.** `ComposerLayoutHostedTests.theCardAndTheFloatingButtonsAreTheSharedGapFromTheEdges()` hard-coded a 402 pt width and `ReaderUnderBarsTests.inANavigationStackTheTopBarAndTheBottomBarReachThePage()` assumed `insets.bottom >= 44`; both failed on the iPhone Duo simulator.

**Cause.** A test assumption, not an app bug. The Duo screen is 466 x 678 pt and its window safe area is `bottom 34, right 84` (read on the Duo, composer test with no bar at all): the system keeps 84 pt on the trailing side for its bars, and the reader's bottom-bar items move to the side there (`ReaderPageControls.swift` doc comment, changelog 96). The composer card respects that inset (8 pt gap from it); the page of the reader receives it (`EdgeInsets(top: 82, leading: 0, bottom: 34, trailing: 84)`).

**Change.** Both tests host in a window of `scene.effectiveGeometry.coordinateSpace.bounds`. The composer test measures the gaps against the window's safe-area range (`leadingEdge`/`trailingEdge`) instead of 0...402, and the half-width check uses its middle. Round 2 (Opus, Low): no fixed sleeps left; the reader test polls until the bar's inset is on the page (side band on the Duo, `bottom >= 44` elsewhere), the composer test until its frames are unchanged over four reads; a timeout ends red in the assertions. Round 1 (Opus): the reader test finds the bottom bar's own button (`_UIButtonBarButton`, below the top bar; the poll waits for it) and branches: where the window reserves >= 44 pt on a side (Duo) the page must get that side inset and the button must sit inside the band; elsewhere `insets.bottom >= 44` and the button overlaps the bottom band. Plus: the page gets at least the host's safe area on every edge. Both tests size the window from `scene.effectiveGeometry.coordinateSpace.bounds` (`UIScene.coordinateSpace` is deprecated in iOS 26). Nothing else touched.

**Tests.** Both red on the Duo before the change (`abs(402 - card.maxX - gap) < 0.5`, card maxX 310; `insets.bottom >= 44`, bottom 34). After: the two suites (8 tests) green on the Duo and the iPhone 18 Pro; whole `SwiftHFRTests` green on the iPhone 18 Pro (2033 tests, 262 suites).
