**Problem.** The head branches of merged PRs pile up on ezzz/HFRnow: 99 on 2026-11-10. Cloud sessions cannot delete branches, because the proxy refuses it.

**Change.** The operator decided on 2026-11-11 to turn on GitHub's "Automatically delete head branches". `scripts/configure-repo.sh` now sets `delete_branch_on_merge=true` next to `allow_auto_merge`, so the setting is recorded as code.
