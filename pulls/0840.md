## Problem
After PR 1 the arrival is the « Oh Oh Oh ! » alert alone and Noël soft is a red accent with falling snow. THEMES.md §4–§5 and the issue ask for Santa's sleigh pulled by its reindeer before the alert, Christmas objects crossing the screen every 20–90 min of foreground time, and snow piled on cards and the bottom edge; PR 1 also left flakes crossing large titles and falling while a list scrolls.

## Cause
None of it existed: no crossing scheduler, no art in the app, no piled snow (PR 1's first drift crossed row text), a fixed 56 pt top band, no scroll signal.

## Change
- **Crossing scheduler** (`NoelCrossingModel`, `@Observable @MainActor`, injected clock, random source and `HapticFeedback`): counts foreground time only (`foregroundChanged(active:)` from the scene phase), draws the gap uniformly in 20–90 min at launch and at each crossing, so two crossings are never less than 20 min of foreground time apart; hands one crossing at a time to one scene, only on a clear screen (lock, sheet / composer / alert, keyboard: the PR 1 `ScreenState`), with animations allowed and Noël soft on; stops it as soon as one of them comes up or the app leaves the foreground; one light haptic per crossing through `HapticFeedback` (the haptics setting applies; never without animations, so never with Reduce Motion).
- **Paths** (`CrossingPath`, pure): enter by a random edge fully outside, leave by the opposite one fully outside, at a random tilt (≤ 30°, ≤ 15° for the sleigh) and direction, ≤ 3 s. Art with a front (the sleigh) is mirrored as a whole when it goes right to left and turned along its line (`facing` = the travel direction); a star, gift, ornament or flake stays upright.
- **Arrival sleigh**: the arrival queues it when animations are allowed; the alert waits until it has crossed (`alert(for:screen:)`); a quarter of the window height, at most 240 pt, 3 s, one haptic; a tap closes it (THEMES.md §4 rule 3); without animations it is dropped and the alert comes alone. Small objects: 64 pt, 2.2–3 s, the small sleigh, a star, three gifts, an ornament, a flake.
- **Layer** (`NoelCrossingLayer`, one per scene, in `NoelBand`, clipped to it): asks the model every 15 s (every 0.5 s while the sleigh waits), never per frame; a `TimelineView` runs only while an object crosses. Decorations take no touch (except the sleigh's tap-to-close) and are `accessibilityHidden`.
- **Band** (`NoelBand`): 104 pt at the top (the navigation bar with its large title), 64 pt at the bottom; falling snow and crossings stay inside, so no large title, button, badge, tab bar, status bar or home indicator is crossed.
- **Piled snow** (static, kept with Reduce Motion): two caps on the top edge of the Catégories card, drawn as its section header (a list cell clips what leaves it), so the card covers their base and they never reach a row's text; two drifts in the bottom safe area, in the corners either side of a 160 pt clearance for the home indicator (none without a bottom safe area).
- **Pause while scrolling** (`SeasonalScrollActivity`): `onScrollPhaseChange` on the Catégories, topic and MP lists; the reader's web view reports its pan gesture and deceleration (its delegate is left to WebKit). The snow's timeline pauses while any of them moves.
- **Art**: the operator's validated renders only (`docs/assets/themes/renders/`, 2026-11-10): `noel_sleigh` (checked: two reindeer harnessed in front, drawn facing right), `noel_star`, `noel_gift`, `noel_gift_blue`, `noel_gift_gold`, `noel_ornament`, `noel_snowflake`, `noel_snowpile`; trimmed and scaled into `Assets.xcassets/Noel/` (transparent PNG, 372 KB in all, largest 807×285) by the committed `tooling/art/noel-crossings/derive.swift` (no pixel drawn). Credit: CREDITS.md « Seasonal art ».
- Spec `docs/lots/LOT-166-seasonal-noel.md` updated (behaviour, edge cases, art, what is left).
