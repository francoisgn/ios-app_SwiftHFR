## What and why
**Problem.** Launch with the current session expired: the home page loads logged out. "Se reconnecter" renews the **same** `HFRSession` (`AccountsModel.logIn`, `.existing`), so `currentSessionGeneration` does not move and `RootView` keeps its `RootModel`. `NavigationModel.isLoggedIn()` trusted `forums.loader.value?.isLoggedIn` first, and the forum list reloads only on pull-to-refresh: every list kept "Tous" forced, the "Connexion requise pour Favoris, Suivis et Lus…" scope bar and the Apple category hidden.

**Change.**
- `AccountsModel.currentSessionChanges` (observed): bumped when the **current** session changes in place: a re-login of that account (before the store write, which may fail after the session was renewed), synced credentials adopted into it, a check that finds it expired. Another account's change does not move it.
- `ForumListModel.sessionDidChange(expired:)`: marks the page as no longer answering for the login state and reloads it; `loginStatePage` is nil until a page applied by a load started after the change. An expiry the page already shows (read logged out, or loading) sends nothing and returns false.
- `NavigationModel.isLoggedIn()` reads `forums.loginStatePage?.isLoggedIn`, else the session. `NavigationModel.sessionDidChange()` marks every cached `TopicListModel` (a change counter vs the one its page was loaded under) and reloads the shown ones from page 1; a hidden list reloads page 1 at its next `onAppear`.
- `RootModel.currentSessionChanged(expired:)`: the home page once (shared by both tabs), then both navigations. `RootNavigationView` observes `accounts?.currentSessionChanges` (optional environment) and passes whether the current row is `.expired`.

Not here: two expiry cases the review found (a launch race on the session's cookie-based answer, a valid session that expires during a run is never re-checked) → #1235.
