### Problem
The default topic rows (Favoris, Drapeaux, category lists, search) showed the starter, a reply count, a dot and flag icons. Operator decision of 2026-11-16 (#817): parity with the current app. Separately (#975), the Favoris tab's title bar said « Drapeaux » on the `.flagged` source.

### Cause
`TopicRowView` was the dense row only; the last poster was parsed (`TopicSummary.lastPost`) but not shown. `TopicListView` titled the screen with `TopicSource.title`, which is « Drapeaux » for `.flagged`.

### Change
- `LegacyTopicRowView` (new default): pin (red) and lock before the title on one or two lines, unread-pages pill on the right, info line « ⚑ lue / total » (or « N pages ») left and « dernier posteur - date » right; no star, no reply count, no starter; VoiceOver says « Non lu », « Super favori » and the tinted flag (« Favori », « Drapeau », « Lu »). Ports `MainWindow.swift:455-504` and `Common.swift:3608-3650` @ 87fe0d1c.
- `TopicRowInfoLine`: pure formatting, legacy's date rules (`ObjCTopicListParser.m:420-454` @ 87fe0d1c): « il y a 13 min » / « il y a 3 h » under 24 h, then « 06/11/26 », « HH:mm » for a date ahead of the clock, all in Paris time.
- `TopicRowView` is now a switch on `TopicRowStyle.current` (`EXPERIMENTAL(flagIconRows, #905)`, default `.legacy`); today's row is `DenseTopicRowView`, code unchanged, for #905 to wire.
- #975: `TopicSource.navigationTitle` is « Favoris » for `.flagged` and `.favorites` (legacy `MainWindow.swift:1821, 2406`, `Favorites.swift:758` @ 87fe0d1c); `TopicListView` uses it. `title` is unchanged and still names the lists in the tab's « Liste » picker.
- Not touched: reader files, row tint logic (`rowPill`, `TopicListView.rowTint`).

### Tests
`TopicRowInfoLineTests`: dates (including the Paris-zone day and the « HH:mm » case), « lue / total », last poster (never the starter, date alone without an author), no reply count / starter in the default row. `TopicSourceTitleTests`: the Favoris title on both sources. `TopicFlagSpeechTests`: the spoken flag names. Not run here (Linux); the macOS compile-check runs on each head.
