**Problem.** (#961) At the end of a page the bubble, refresh and reply merged into one pill. (#965) › and the refresh were drawn with a solid accent fill, and ‹ › were accent-tinted even when no move existed.

**Cause.** Adjacent toolbar items merge into one glass pill; `ReaderBottomBar.State` carried "promotes" flags that applied `.glassProminent`, which also made ‹ and › two pills at the end of the page.

**Change** (`ReaderPageControls.swift` and its tests only).
- `State` lists the pills (`leading` ‹ ›, `centre` bubble, `trailing` refresh + reply); the system bar and the tab-bar strip draw from those lists.
- `promotesNext` / `promotesRefresh` and `readerBarProminence` are removed: no pill is filled with the accent. ‹ and › stay two pills at the end of the page (`separatesArrows`, two toolbar items with a fixed spacer) and one shared pill mid-page, as #965 asks.
- `canGoPrevious` / `canGoNext` give `previousTint` / `nextTint`: accent when the move exists, grey otherwise; the arrow is disabled too, and grey also while a page loads or a composer is open. Button frames are the system's ≥ 44 pt controls, unchanged.

**Tests** (`ReaderBottomBarTests`): `thePageBubbleIsItsOwnCentredPill` (#961, with `separatesArrows`), `theArrowsAreGreyedAtTheFirstAndLastPage` mid-page and at the end, first / middle / last / single page (#965). They reference members that do not exist without the change.

**Device checks (operator)**
- iPhone portrait / landscape, iPad: three separate pills at the end of a page, no overlap; ‹ › one shared pill mid-page.
- Bubble centring: the system bar centres the bubble between its neighbours, not on the screen. At the end of a page the left side is two pills against one on the right, so **expect the bubble off-centre** there (and mid-page with VoiceOver, or logged out). The strip under a visible tab bar (#649) is exact. If it matters, the next try is `ToolbarItem(placement: .status)`.
- No solid accent fill anywhere; ‹ grey on page 1, › grey on the last page, mid-page and at the end; the 44 pt hit areas; what French VoiceOver says for a disabled arrow (« estompé » or « indisponible »: unverified).
- Page menu opens from the bubble (#924); « Tirer vers le haut pour actualiser » unchanged.
