## What and why
**Problem.** With « Trier par catégorie » off, nothing shows the categories for the moment; legacy 4.0.2 has a button for it (`Favorites.swift:808-846` @ 87fe0d1c). Operator chose option 1 on #1450.

**Cause.** The port only had the persistent toggle.

**Change.**

A menu item, not a toolbar button of its own (GAP-1450); the grouped sort keeps its « sections repliées » toggle.
