### Requirements for the form spec #633 (not guaranteed by §4)
- R1: server and proxy logs never contain the IP in any form (neither plain nor hashed).
- R2: no cookie at all; the token and the draft are in local storage.
- R3: the draft is erased after a successful submission.
- R4: the IP and token hashes are never exported; the salt is destroyed at closing; the rate-limit hash stays in memory.
- R5: `{date_effacement}` is computed as closing + 2 months, with no separate setting (a manual earlier erasure stays possible, hence « au plus tard »); the dates and the threshold come from the config and are shown by the page.
- R6: an automatic erasure at `{date_effacement}` of all answers, pseudos and the mapping table, plus a documented procedure for backups and analysis copies (the other developer's copy included).
- R7: a command to find, show, correct or erase the answers of one pseudo, which also gives its analysis identifier so that copies can be fixed.
- R8: the detailed page is served by the form and opens without losing the draft.
- R9: the analysis copy is pseudonymised: a random identifier (not the internal SQLite id), and a mapping table that stays on the server only.
- R10: free texts of the analysis copy can be edited before analysis without touching the stored answers, with a trace of the edits.
- R11: an aggregate-only export for publication (closed questions, no free text, identifier, time spent or card order), no figure for a question or group of fewer than 5 respondents; it is the only data kept after erasure.
- R12: no audience measurement, client-side or in the logs (no IP, user agent or referrer).
- R13: a « limitée » flag keeps an answer stored but out of stats, later analysis copies and the R11 export (restriction, objection).

R4 also keeps the salt and the hashes out of backups, and R5 forbids postponing the closing date after the first answer (CodeRabbit threads, commit e5d8350d).
