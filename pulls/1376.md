## What and why
- **Problem**: sending from the composer failed with « Le forum ne répond pas : rien n'a été envoyé » (operator screenshot, #1375), while legacy posted on the same network at the same moment.
- **Cause**: that message is `ComposerMessages.network(.timedOut)`, the « nothing was sent » branch. It comes from the **form fetch** that runs before the POST; a POST timeout would show « n'a pas confirmé l'envoi » instead. `PostingFlow.loadForm` fetched the form with the list loads' 10 s idle limit. Legacy fetched the same forms with a plain `URLRequest(url:)`, so it got URLSession's 60 s idle default (all @ `87fe0d1c`):
  - reply form: `HFRswift/Swift/ReplyService.swift:238-243`
  - quote form, and the edit form through `fetchQuoteTemplate` (`MessagesView.swift:4325-4334`): `:804-809`
  - delete form: `:1046-1047`
  - moderator-alert form: `:1463-1470`
- **Change**:
  - `PostingFlow.formTimeout = 60`. `loadForm` uses it by default, so every form fetched for a write gets it: reply, new topic, MP, edit, delete, quote, prefill and options reads, plus the moderator-alert form (`ModeratorAlertFlow.prepare`).
  - Background reads keep 10 s by passing `timeout:`: the MPStorage forms (legacy went through ASI's 10 s, `Classes/MPStorage.m:769-775`) and the group-roster re-read after a confirmed change.
  - Unchanged: the POST was already 60 s, and the list, topic-page and search limits stay at legacy's 10 s.
  - Docs: `HFR-PROTOCOL.md` (timeouts line) and LOT-048 step 1 give the limits.
