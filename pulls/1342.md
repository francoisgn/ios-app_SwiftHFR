**Problem.** The « Page numéro » sheet has one fixed 260 pt detent around unscrollable content (hint, field, two button rows) that grows with Dynamic Type: at large sizes rows are cut (HIG › Typography). Same as legacy (`Common.swift:3212` @ 87fe0d1c).

**Cause.** `.presentationDetents([.height(260)])` and a plain `VStack`.

**Change.** `ReaderPagePicker.detents = [.height(260), .medium]`: the sheet still opens at legacy's 260 pt (unchanged at the default size) and can be dragged taller; the content sits in a `ScrollView` with `.scrollBounceBehavior(.basedOnSize)` so nothing is cut when the sheet cannot grow further (keyboard up, landscape, AX sizes). Mostly re-indentation of the `VStack`. The `// Port of` header names the difference. No string touched; no overlap with #1334 or #571's PR (checked); #949 (reuse from the topic list) benefits from the same sheet. Not done: opening at `.medium` by itself at accessibility sizes (needs `presentationDetents(_:selection:)` and a measured behaviour).

**Tests.** `ReaderPageSheetDetentsTests` pins the detents only (it fails without the change because `ReaderPagePicker.detents` does not exist). That the sheet opens at 260 pt, scrolls and can be dragged taller is the Mac check below. Cloud session: not built here (no Xcode), compile-check requested.

**Mac check owed on #893** (`host: mac-helps`): « Page … » at AX3 and AX5, smallest iPhone, portrait and landscape, number pad up: hint, field and both rows visible or scrollable; at AX5 also look for sideways truncation of the four `-10 … +10` buttons, which a vertical scroll cannot fix.
