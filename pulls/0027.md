**Cause.** The legacy app spreads third-party calls across the code: AQ (`aq.super-h.fr`, including an RSS fetch at every launch with no user action), Chevereto rehost (`img3.super-h.fr`), the Giphy SDK, video player iframes, and images from any host a poster chose, private addresses included, loaded as soon as the page is shown.

- `docs/lots/LOT-071-third-party-services.md`: inventory T1–T8 (trigger, data sent, owner, lot, status), with legacy file:line citations @ `2440d79` and the image hosts seen in the topic captures; rules R1–R6 (no forum cookie or `Referer` to third parties, keys and base URLs as configuration, no analytics SDK, inventory = allow-list); first-use notices N1–N4; operator decisions of 2026-10-27: **D-071-1** images, option C (private/LAN hosts, numeric host spellings and non-image `*.hardware.fr` paths never loaded; third-party images on tap or per the FEAT-011 setting), applied by LOT-083; **D-071-2** video players: placeholder, player on tap; **D-071-3** AQ: badge and check as legacy, notice before the first creation. R3, R5, R6 and N1–N3 are proposed, applied unless the operator objects.
- `docs/HFR-PROTOCOL.md` §8: a pointer to the spec and a row for the video players.
- `docs/LOTS.md` capture backlog: one row for the `reho.st` Referer check (§7 E26) and an `http`-only image host over https.

Only these three files change: the services themselves are implemented by their own lots.

**Security Blast Radius**
- _inferred_ — A poster-controlled image destination can cause requests from readers’ devices when images are loaded. The relevant private-network scope is each participating reader’s reachable network, potentially across many readers. The supplied evidence does not establish a backend fetch, private-response disclosure or successful privileged action.

**Security Findings and Attack Paths**
- _inferred_ — The two retained findings overlap: the never-load list explicitly handles address literals and hostname shapes, but not ordinary dotted DNS names resolving to private addresses. Such a name can pass the stated syntactic restrictions and direct an allowed image request toward the reader’s LAN. This exposure predates the PR; the new specification does not fully close it. The server-side SSRF label is not supported by the documented device-origin request path.

**Trust Boundaries and Controls**
- _observed_ — The specified controls separate forum sessions from third-party requests through cookie-less transport, restrict Referer disclosure, reject listed private and numeric destinations before loading, and repeat blocking at redirects. Tap-to-load is an additional disclosure control, not permission to bypass the destination block. These requirements do not demonstrate DNS-aware runtime enforcement.

**Hardening Proposals**
- _proposed_ — Make the destination invariant explicit for DNS-resolved addresses and require enforceable protection across initial connections, redirects and retries, without a validation-to-connection race. Apply it independently of tap or automatic-loading consent, and validate the eventual reader transport against that contract.
