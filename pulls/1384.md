## What and why
The App Store visuals are due to ezzz before the general release, and the captures need the Mac. This PR adds what can be decided now, in `docs/assets/appstore/`:

- **Art direction (proposal)**: the language of the `:o` social preview of #1380 (three flat inks, Inter headline, monospace kicker, the whole capture in a plain dark slab), not the README hero's perspective scene: an App Store page is a row of small portrait cards. `README.md` holds the rules; `art-direction.jpg` shows the phone frames with placeholders.
- **`source/frame.html`**: one template for every screen and every size; it adapts to the capture's aspect ratio, and flags a caption line wider than 94 % of the column and a font that did not load.
- **`source/screens.json`**: the proposed seven screens with their French captions (the two-column screen only at the wide sizes), and the pixel sizes.
- **`source/render.js`**: writes opaque RGB PNGs (Apple refuses an alpha channel). A missing capture writes nothing; `--allow-placeholders` renders previews into the git-ignored `preview/`; images are staged and swapped in only when the whole run passed; `--sheet` writes the contact sheet.

**Sizes** come only from Apple's App Store Connect Help, « Screenshot specifications », read 2026-11-27: <https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/screenshot-specifications/> (1320 × 2868 and 1206 × 2622 for iPhone, the latter being the required iPhone row; 2064 × 2752 for iPad 13"; 1398 × 2034 and 2007 × 2853 for iPhone Duo). The README quotes the page's requirement lines; the reviewer fetched the page and checked every size and quote.

No capture and no final image is committed; no fake app screen is drawn. The root README map links the new README.
