# PR #804: [SwiftHFR] Lists: side margins follow the density setting (#799)

### Problem
On iPhone the inset-grouped lists have about 36 pt between the screen edge and the text (outer system margin ~17-20 pt + inner 16 pt), same as legacy (`Favorites.swift:429` @ `87fe0d1c`). The operator wants the compact density to give the text more width.

### Cause
The margins were fixed: the outer one is the system's, the inner one is `ListDensity.rowInsets` (12 pt in compact).

### Change
- `ListDensity.outerMargin` (regular: nil = system layout, unchanged; compact: 10 pt) and the inner margin (`rowInsets`: regular 16 unchanged; compact 12 -> 7 pt).
- `densityListMargins()`: `.contentMargins(.horizontal, outerMargin, for: .scrollContent)` with the optional overload (nil = system layout), no `if/else`, so the list keeps its identity (scroll position, row `@State`) on a density change. For `.insetGrouped` lists only (all of them today; #803 keeps the group style, so the outer margin stays; whichever of #803/#804 merges second re-measures).
- `densityRowMargin()`: `.listRowInsets(.horizontal, 7)` in compact, nil (system) in regular, for rows that keep the system's vertical insets. Rows that read `rowInsets` (`TopicRowView`, `MessageRowView`) already sit on it; `SearchResultRow` carries `.listRowInsets(density.rowInsets)` on the row itself.
- `densityHeaderMargin()`: a section header takes the system's inset (16 pt, 20 pt in wide columns), so the modifier measures where the header starts (in the list's coordinate space) and moves it onto the cards' left edge + 7 pt; no constant.
- Applied to: Favoris and a category's topic list (`TopicListView`: rows, section headers), the scope bar rows above it (`ForumScopeBar`: sub-forum picker, filter, note), Catégories (`ForumListView`), MP list (`MessageListView`), profile (`UserProfileView`: rows, section headers), archived drafts (`ArchivedDraftsView`), forum search (`ForumSearchView`: outer margin, result rows at 7 pt; the search form rows keep the system inset).
- Untouched: settings, about, offline, polls, accounts, other lists that do not use density. Row separators keep the system inset. Landscape safe areas: the header target adds the list's leading safe area to the outer margin; checked in portrait only.

### Tests
- `ListDensityMarginsTests` (hosted, `UIHostingController` in a window): values; header and row text sit 7 pt from the card in compact under both horizontal size classes (trait override); header and row align in regular under both; search result text sits 9 pt closer to the card in compact than in regular (pixel scan of a rendered row); row `@State` survives a density toggle; first card minX = system in regular, 10 in compact. Red shown with the modifiers made no-ops (6 expectations failed), green restored.
- Full app suite on a throwaway iPhone 18 Pro simulator (iOS 27.0), clean derived data: 1353 tests (xcresult `totalTestCount`), 1 failure `TopicRowActionsTests.aRemovalInFlightIsNotSentTwice` (a model-only test of the favourites list, untouched by this diff; it also failed alone on one rerun and passed on another: a flake).
- Screenshots (fixtures only; Favoris, MP, Favoris with section headers; regular vs compact, light/dark, iPhone, iPad) on the operator's Mac, `~/agent-work/2026-11-10-ezzz-hfrnow-m4-captures/lots/799/`.
- Not captured: a revealed swipe action; swipe actions, badges and selection highlight on device are for the operator to check. The `ForumScopeBar` rows are not covered by a hosted test (they need a `TopicListModel`); #808 moves them into the topic block, so whichever PR merges second re-checks.
