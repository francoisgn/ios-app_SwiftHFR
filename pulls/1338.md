**Problem.** A `// Port of … @ <sha>` pointer whose sha moved without its lines (AGENTS §2) is caught only by a later review (#1197: `AppIconSettingsView.swift:4`).

**Cause.** Nothing reads the cited lines.

**Change.** `tooling/docs/check_legacy_pointers.py` reads the leading `//` blocks of `App/**/*.swift`; for each `Port of <path> (<items>) @ <sha>` item of the shape `Symbol :a-b` / `Symbol a-b` / `<Symbol>, lines a-b` it checks the symbol is in lines a-b of `git show <sha>:<path>` (whole word; a range past the end is wrong). Everything else (no range, another file's range, unreadable sha, `///`) is counted unchecked and never fails. New step in **Docs consistency** (`--fetch` = `git fetch origin legacy/duo-inner-cover`); README section in `tooling/docs/README.md`.
