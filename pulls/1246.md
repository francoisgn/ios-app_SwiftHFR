# PR #1246: [SwiftHFR] App icon windows are Gregorian whatever the device calendar (#1199)

### Problem
On a device whose calendar is Islamic, Hebrew or Persian, 10 December 2026 is not in month 12 of that calendar: the seasonal Noël icons (window 01/12–26/12) were not offered while the Noël theme was, they were offered on days 1–26 of that calendar's 12th month, `AppIconSeasonModel.checkAtLaunch` asked to revert in mid-December, and the `<icon>:<year>` « already answered » token used that calendar's year.

### Cause
`IconWindow.contains` and `lastEndYear` read month, day and year with the calendar they are given, and the two models default to `Calendar.current` (their only construction sites pass none). The seasonal theme goes through `NoelCalendar.gregorian(in:)` ("season dates and years never follow a Buddhist or Japanese device calendar"); the icons did not. The icon tests build a Gregorian calendar everywhere, so `.current` was never exercised.

### Change
`IconWindow.contains` and `lastEndYear` convert through `NoelCalendar.gregorian(in: calendar.timeZone)`: the day and the year are Gregorian, the time zone is still the given calendar's. Both models, the picker's offered icons and the season check go through those two functions, so nothing else changes. No new API, no engine change.

### Tests
`AppIconCalendarTests` (written first): for Islamic Umm al-Qura, Hebrew, Persian, Buddhist and Japanese device calendars, the Noël window contains 10 Dec 2026 and not 10 Jun 2026; `lastEndYear` is 2026 on 3 Jan 2027 and on 27 Dec 2026 and 2025 on 10 Dec 2026; and the picker (`AppIconSettingsModel`, the real model) offers the Noël icons on 10 Dec and not on 10 Jun for the Islamic, Hebrew and Persian calendars. The Buddhist and Japanese rows share Gregorian month and day, so they pass before the change as a guard; the Islamic, Hebrew and Persian rows fail before it (the issue notes Foundation's month numbering for these calendars was not run, the test settles it). Not run here (Linux); `compile-check` runs on the head.
