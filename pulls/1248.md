**Problem.** The image panel's history (`ImageUploadView.swift:177`) and the composer's image chips (`ComposerImageChips.swift:64`) loaded their thumbnails with `AsyncImage`: `URLSession.shared`, its cookie store and cache, no policy check, any host, `http` included. They were the only image requests outside `ReaderImageFetcher`.

**Cause.** Ported from legacy's `AsyncImage` (`HFRswift/Swift/AnswerView.swift:2984` @ `87fe0d1c`).

**Change.**
- `RemoteThumbnail` (`Common/`): a grey square until the image is there, loaded with `.task(id: url)` through a `SmileyImageLoader` (shared decoded-image cache).
- `SmileyImageLoader.rehostThumbnails(host:)`: like `.gif()`, third parties allowed but a host filter (`http`/`https`, exact host, case-insensitive) asked about the URL and every redirect hop; `nil` host → nothing is requested.
- The host is `ImageUploadServices.thumbnailHost` = `hostName ?? configuredHost`: the configured `RehostBaseURL` host, read alone (`baseHost(from:)`) when the build has no key, so an imported or synced history keeps its thumbnails. Captures `U01`/`U03` upload to `img3.super-h.fr` and answer with image URLs on the same host. An entry on another host, or a build without `RehostBaseURL`, shows the grey square and sends nothing (documented in LOT-103).
- `ImageUploadModel.thumbnails` is built once in `init` (`services.thumbnails()`); `ReplyComposerView` passes that same loader to `ComposerEditorCard` → `ComposerImageChipsRow`, and the history rows read it: one loader, one transport per panel.
- No wider: no change to the fetcher, the policy, or other image paths.

**Tests** (`App/SwiftHFRTests/Composer/RehostThumbnailTests.swift`, app-hosted; not run here, Linux cannot build the app):
- `ImageLoadingConventionTests.appCodeLoadsNoImageOutsideTheImageClient`: no `AsyncImage(` / `URLSession.shared` outside comments in `App/SwiftHFR`. Red before the change: the same scan on the base lists `ComposerImageChips.swift:64` and `ImageUploadView.swift:177`.
- `anOffHostThumbnailSendsNoRequest` (other host, suffix trick, look-alike, `file:`): `MockTransport` records no request.
- `anOnHostThumbnailIsRequestedOverHTTPS`, `aRedirectOffTheHostIsNotFollowed`, `withoutAHostNothingIsRequested`.
- `theServicesLoaderAsksOnlyTheirHost`: the services' own loader asks the on-host thumbnail only.
- `aKeylessBuildUsesTheConfiguredBaseHost`: `baseHost(from:)` cases and a keyless services loader.
- The rehost tests did not compile before the change (`rehostThumbnails`, `thumbnails(fetcher:)`, `baseHost(from:)` absent).

SwiftLint 0.65.1 `--strict` clean on `App/SwiftHFR` and `App/SwiftHFRTests`.

Follow-up (round-3 residuals, Low): #1249 (loader built once per app rather than per `ReplyComposerView.init`; a test on `ImageUploadModel.thumbnails`).
