## What and why
**Problem.** A member's signature is member input. With `… </span>TEXT<span class="x">…` inside it, `anonymize` stopped at the stray `</span>`, so `TEXT` survived, and `check` reported nothing (it only looked inside the span).

**Cause.** `_find_para_blocks` ends a `span.signature` with a depth count of `<span>` tags, which the member's own markup can unbalance. `check` had no rule on what follows a signature.

**Change.**
- `anonymize`: a signature runs to the last `</span>` before the end of its `div#para…` and of its `td.messCase2`, when that is further than the depth count (`PERSONAL_CONTAINERS`, `_personal_block_end`). HFR's structure: all 336 corpus signatures are the last child of their para div (followed by `</div></td>`). A stray `</div>` is covered by the `td` container.
- `check` (`StructuralVerifier`, own walk, LESSONS 33-34): no element and no text may follow a signature in its parent; a signature or mood span carries one attribute, `class`, with exactly one token (`signature` or `MoodStatus`), so no duplicate or extra class; any `<svg>` or `<math>` is unexpected markup (html.parser reads `<style>`/`<script>` as raw text where a browser does not, #768).
- Issue point 2 (`title=`, empty `<a href>`, ``'&lt;img alt/src&gt;'``) was already refused by #771's walk; the new test pins it next to the class rule.

**Why no wider.** The mood status is left to the depth count: in the corpus it is followed by HFR's own markup in its cell (`R63a`, `R33b`), so the signature rule would over-redact. Follow-up: #793.
