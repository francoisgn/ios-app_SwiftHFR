## What and why
**Problem.** A member's signature (and mood) is member input. With `… </span>TEXT<span class="x">…` inside it, `anonymize` stopped at the stray `</span>`, so `TEXT` survived, and `check` reported nothing (it only looked inside the span).

**Cause.** The block's end came from a depth count of `<span>` tags, which the member's own markup can unbalance (a stray `</span>`, `</div>`, `</td>` ends it early, an unclosed `<span>` makes it run on into the next message). `check` had no rule on what follows a signature or mood.

**Change.**
- `anonymize` (`PERSONAL_BOUNDARIES`, `_personal_blocks`, `_personal_block_end`): a signature or mood runs to the last `</span>` before the next element HFR prints after it, which a member cannot write. From the whole corpus (2026-11-11): every signature is followed by `</span></div></td></tr></table>` then the next `table.messagetable`, the footer's `tr.cBackHeader…` or `<br /><script>`; every mood (470 of 470) by `div.avatar_center`, `span.TransactionsReportsLink` or `td.messCase2`. Every opening is redacted, even when a depth count ran past it. With no boundary in the page, the depth count stays.
- `check` (`StructuralVerifier`, own list `_V_AFTER_END`, LESSONS 33-34): between a signature or mood and that next element, only end tags, blanks, a bare `<br>` and the footer's numbers-only `<table>`; a signature or mood span carries only `class`, with exactly its own token; any `<svg>` or `<math>` is unexpected markup (html.parser reads `<style>`/`<script>` as raw text where a browser does not, #768); any `<[CDATA[` outside an XML document too (html.parser swallows it to `]]>`, a browser ends it at the first `>`; the AQ feed `T01` is XML and keeps its CDATA).
- Issue point 2 (`title=`, empty `<a href>`, ``'&lt;img alt/src&gt;'``) was already refused by #771's walk; the new test pins it next to the class rule.
- README: the new rules.
- Round-2 Low findings (forged boundary, no boundary, silent cut, three test gaps): follow-up #797.
