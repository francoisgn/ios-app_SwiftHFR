**Problem.** Four sheets closed on « Annuler » or a swipe-down with no question asked, and the typed text was lost:
- « Alerte Modération » (the reason); it only blocked the swipe while a send ran.
- « Créer un sondage » (a question and up to ten answers); no swipe guard at all.
- The AQ and bookmark title sheets (the review found them in the same class).

HIG › Sheets: "If people have unsaved changes in the sheet when they begin swiping to dismiss it, use an action sheet to let them confirm their action". HIG › Modality: "help people avoid data loss by getting confirmation before closing a modal view".

**Cause.** A port of legacy, which closed at once:
- `HFRswift/Swift/MessagesView.swift:7479-7484` (moderation sheet).
- `HFRswift/Swift/TopicPollDraft.swift:124-127` (poll sheet).
- `MessagePopupPromptSheet`, the AQ and bookmark prompts.

All @ `87fe0d1c`.

**Change.** The same behaviour as the main composer (#429, UXD-15).
- While there is something to lose, the swipe-down is refused (`interactiveDismissDisabled`).
- « Annuler » then asks « Abandonner cette alerte ? », « Abandonner ce sondage ? » or « Abandonner ce bookmark ? », with « Abandonner » (destructive) and « Continuer la saisie » (cancel).
- With nothing typed, « Annuler » closes at once, as today. Blank text (spaces, line breaks, blank answer rows) is nothing to lose.
- Shared code in `Common/DiscardConfirmation.swift`: `View.discardConfirmation(_:hasChanges:isAsking:discard:)` and `DiscardConfirmation.hasText(_:)`.
- Moderation sheet: `ModeratorAlertComposerModel.hasUnsavedReason` (a non-blank reason while the sheet has not ended). The swipe is still refused while a send runs. A sheet that ended still closes by itself.
- Poll sheet: `PollCreationSheet.hasChanges(_:from:)` compares the fields with the poll the sheet opened on, blank text left out. An unchanged edit of the composer's poll closes at once.
- SwiftUI gives a sheet no hook for a swipe attempt. UIKit's `presentationControllerDidAttemptToDismiss(_:)` belongs to the presentation's delegate, which SwiftUI owns. So the swipe is refused and the question comes on « Annuler », as in the composer. The swipe question itself, for these four sheets and the composer, is #1264.
- Docs updated: LOT-135 (the "swipe drops the reason" line was false), LOT-100 (its row and its UX evolutions), LOT-132, LOT-134.
- #1261 adds `AlertVerbs`; once both land, the two button titles can move there.

**Tests** (`App/SwiftHFRTests/Common/DiscardConfirmationTests.swift`, app-hosted; not run here, since Linux cannot build the app). All are red before the change: the predicates did not exist.
- `aModerationReasonAsksOnceTyped`, `anEndedModerationSheetHasNothingToLose`.
- `aPollAsksOnceAFieldDiffersFromWhatTheSheetOpenedOn`: question, answers, choices, visitors and closing date; blank-only text does not count; an unchanged edit does not ask.
- `aTitleAsksOnceTyped`: the AQ and bookmark rule.

The views' wiring (the poll sheet's `draft` with its closing-date toggle, the dialogs) has no unit seam. The device check covers it.

SwiftLint 0.65.1 `--strict` clean. `ledger.py check-body`: 6 blocks ok.

Device check (#893):
