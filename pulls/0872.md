**What was captured.** 30 read-only GETs as user1 on 2026-11-13, at least 60 s apart:
- `R830a`-`f`: the three global buckets, each next to its flags page (`owntopic=1/2/3`).
- `R830g`, `R830h`: `forum1.php…cat=13…&owntopic=1` and the flat `participated` bucket of cat 13.
- `R830r01`-`r20`: the flat `read` bucket of each of the 20 categories in the global `read`.
- `R830s`: the global `participated` bucket with no cookies.
- `R830t`: the same with a stale jar (user1's `md_user`/`md_id`, a wrong `md_passs`).

Following the issue, the values were compared raw on the Mac and only what is safe and new is published.
