# PR #806: [SwiftHFR] Reader: drop HFR's signature separator, one thin line (#755)

### Problem
With « Afficher les signatures » on, each signature is preceded by HFR's row of dashes (« --------------- ») on top of the reader's own border line (operator, device test 2026-11-11).

### Cause
HFR opens every `span.signature` with its own separator: a space, 15 dashes, a line break, then `<br />` (capture `R267a`, #267, merged in #764). LOT-034 kept it as signature text; legacy removed it (`LinkItem.m:74` @ `87fe0d1c`, every 15-dash string of the message). `.signature` in `reader.css` already drew a 1px dashed top border, so the two added up.

### Change
- `MessageContentNormalizer` (LOT-034): the signature HTML drops the separator at its start (leading spaces, exactly 15 dashes, the whitespace after). Nothing else: dashes in the body, or inside the signature after its first text, stay. This differs from legacy on purpose (the issue asks for it): legacy also deleted a member's own 15-dash line.
- `reader.css`: `.signature` keeps legacy's `1px dashed` line (`style-liste-light.css:79`, `style-liste-dark.css:45` @ `87fe0d1c`) in the theme's `var(--separator)` (THEMES.md §3). Only the dashes text goes away.
- Docs: LOT-034 (differences 8 and its mentions) and HFR-PROTOCOL updated: the separator is captured (R267a) and dropped only at the start of a signature.

### Tests (red first)
- `MessageSignatureSeparatorTests` (HFRKit, 4 tests): `R267a` (all 9 signatures lose the separator, keep "Signature removed."), a body line of dashes is kept, own dashes after the signature's first text are kept (both hand-built fragments, marked as such: the capture has none, #755 comment), a signature without separator is untouched. Seen failing without the change (3 of 4).
- `SignatureLineWebTests` (app, real `WKWebView` on `R267a`): no dashes in the signature, computed `border-top` is `dashed` / `1px`, as wide as the message text. Seen failing without the change (dashes present; the line was already dashed, so only the dashes check fails).
- `SignatureLineWebTests` also checks `borderTopColor` equals the theme separator in light and dark (fails with `lightgray`); 14 and 16 dash runs and a separator-only signature are tested (hand-built; the 16-dash test fails without the guard).
- Full app suite on one simulator: 1348 tests, all pass (round 2). HFRParsingTests: 522 tests, one timing test (`HFRBBCodeWriterTests` scan-time ratio) failed under machine load, unrelated.

Screenshots (fixture text only): before/after, light/dark, in `~/agent-work/.../lots/755/` (sig-after2-*.png after, sig-before-*.png before).
