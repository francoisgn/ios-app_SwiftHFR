A category's topic list shows « Page N » with ‹ › only. #980 (operator, 2026-11-16) wants what the MP list got in #968: a « page x / y » pill with a menu (first, previous, next, last, typed page), shown at the end of the list, hidden when the category has one page. ### Cause

### Change (no second pager: `PagerToolbar`, `PagerPageMenu` and `PagerJump` from #964/#968 are reused untouched)
- `TopicListContent.pageCount` / `pageCountIsExact`, computed as the MP list does (`MessageListContent.init(page:)`): HFR's last page, at least current + 1 when a next page exists; exact when HFR says so or on the last page.
- `TopicListModel`: `pageCount`, `canGoToLast`, `goToLastPage`, `goToPage` (clamped when exact, as typed otherwise), `perform(_ jump:)`.
- `TopicListView.PageBar`: `.pageOf` status, `last`, `jump`, spinner while loading; the bar is hidden on a one-page category (`page > 1 || hasNext`). End-of-list visibility is the existing `pagerBar` (#898). No long press.
- Flags and favourites stay without a pager (`source.isPaged`).
- The count is a lower bound when HFR's pager is a window: shown « y+ », and « Dernière page » stays disabled then (rule of `PagerPageMenu`).
