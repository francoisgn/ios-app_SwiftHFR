### Problem
On iPhone the reply composer covers the whole screen, so the message being answered is out of sight while writing (operator, 2026-11-08). The composer also lacks legacy's control row: no "Vider le texte", no undo or redo (GAP-086 "not planned"), the formatting bar is always on screen instead of behind **Aa**, and Smileys, image and GIF sit in the top bar next to text buttons "Annuler" / "Envoyer" instead of legacy's `xmark` and `arrow.up`.

### Cause
The composer was ported from `2440d79`, before legacy added the row's **Aa** toggle (`143ea5f0`) — the reference is now `87fe0d1c` (#655) — and it is a plain full-height `.sheet`.

### Change
- **Control row** (`ComposerControlRow`, port of `AnswerView.swift:940-1054`, `:1656-1705` @ 87fe0d1c): "Vider le texte", "Annuler", "Rétablir" | "Smileys", "GIF Giphy" (only with a Giphy key, as before), "Insérer image", **Aa** ("Afficher / Masquer les styles"), glass capsules, two rows when one does not fit (Dynamic Type XL), cells sized by `ComposerBarMetrics`. **Aa** shows the existing formatting bar above the row; hidden on each opening, hiding it closes the palette (legacy `:367`, `:1047-1051`). #599 / #602 / #605 bar behaviour unchanged.
- **Undo / redo / clear** (`ComposerHistory` + `ReplyComposerModel`): legacy rules (`:624-635`, `:1345-1374`): every change of the text is one step, insertions included (`update(text:)` is the one path of typing and of every insertion), at most 200, a new change empties redo; undo/redo put the caret at the end, clear empties without asking (one step) and puts it at the start. The text the sheet opens with (draft, quote, edit prefill) is no step. Each step is saved like typing (debounced autosave, LESSONS 48). Disabled with nothing to undo/redo/clear, and while sending or closing (`canEdit`).
- **Top bar** (`ComposerHeader`, round 3): legacy's own header (`composerHeader`, `:768-819`, button styles `:1704-1745` @ 87fe0d1c) instead of a navigation bar: "Fermer" (`xmark`, glass circle, same close choice as before) and "Brouillons" (`tray`, LOT-101, prominent when drafts are archived) on the left, the title in the middle, "Envoyer" (`arrow.up`, prominent glass when there is something to send, greyed while the text is empty, spinner while sending) on the right.
- **Docked card** (round 3, operator reference of 2026-11-10 on #653): the editor sits in a rounded glass card (legacy `composerEditorStyle`, `:1748-1760`) with the control row just under it, both moving with the keyboard. On an iPhone the sheet's detent is the card's measured height (`ComposerCardLayout`: header, banners, fields, image chips, editor, row); the editor is as tall as its text from 2 to 6 lines of the body font (Dynamic Type included), then scrolls inside the card. The system lifts the sheet over the keyboard (measured: a detent that also counted the keyboard inset was shown at full height). The full-screen toggle ("Plein écran" / "Réduire") sits in the editor card's top corner, so the top bar stays legacy's.
- **Image chips** (round 3): an image the panel uploaded and inserted shows as a 56 pt thumbnail (the host's mini rendition) at the top of the card with an ✕ ("Retirer l'image"). The snippet stays in the text where legacy puts it, so posting and drafts are unchanged; the chip shows while the snippet is in the text, and the ✕ removes it as one undoable step. A hand-typed link gets no chip (nothing loaded from an unknown host).
- **Height** (`ComposerHeight`, b·operator, GAP-653): on an iPhone (the device idiom: inside an iPad form sheet the size class is compact too, measured) every composer opens as the docked card (above): a reply (topic or MP) and an edit (operator, 2026-11-08), a new topic and a new MP too (operator, 2026-11-10), their fields part of the card. "Plein écran" / "Réduire" swaps it for `.large` and back. Each height is the sheet's only detent: measured on the iOS 27.1 simulator, a sheet that also offers `.large` is expanded to it as soon as the keyboard comes up, which hid the page again; so dragging does not change the height. Not remembered: every opening starts partial (operator, 2026-11-10), except at an accessibility text size, where it starts full with the toggle still there: the control row takes two tall rows and a partial card leaves no line for the text (round-2 screenshot 8; operator decision, confirmed 2026-11-11). In compact height (iPhone landscape) the system shows the sheet full whatever its detent (measured, round-2 screenshot 6), so there is no toggle there and the sheet is the full one (`ComposerHeight.shown`). The partial sheet gets an opaque `.background` (a sheet below full height is glass, and the dimmed page showed through the editor in dark mode). iPad: presentation unchanged (`.large` form sheet, no toggle), with legacy's top bar.
- **Page behind the partial sheet** (operator, 2026-11-10): over the reader (reply, edit, the author menu's MP) it is undimmed and scrolls (`presentationBackgroundInteraction(.enabled(upThrough:))`, partial only), and its taps are ignored while the composer is open: each presenter reports its sheet to `TopicReaderModel.composer(_:isOpen:)`; while one is open (`suspendsPageInteractions`) links, message menus, videos, pictures, image placeholders, selection quotes, the reply button, page changes, swipes, "Actualiser" and "Réessayer" (both disabled), "Sondage" (hidden) and "Marquer comme non lu" (hidden, no `W18` write) do nothing, the "…" overflow has no entry, the back button is hidden (going back would drop the sheet), and `ReaderWebController.ignoresPageTaps` drops the taps in the web view itself (a long-pressed link gets an empty menu of ours, never WebKit's previewing one).
