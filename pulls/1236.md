**Problem.** Réglages › Taille du texte below 100 % scaled row text under the 11 pt HIG minimum (7.7 pt for `caption2` at 70 %).

**Cause.** `TextScale.pointSize` multiplied the system size by the percentage with no floor. Legacy (`AppTextSizeScale.scaledUIFont`, `Common.swift:314-322` @ 87fe0d1c) never goes under 100 %, so it has no floor to port; the 70-130 % range is our own extension.

**Change.** One place, `TextScale.pointSize` (every `scaledFont` and `font` goes through it): when the factor is below 1, the size is `max(system * factor, min(system, 11))`. 100 % and above are untouched; a size the system already gives under 11 pt (small Dynamic Type) is not raised. Legacy behaviour unchanged, so no ledger entry. The reader's web view scales through `factor` in a script, not this function: not changed here, tracked in #1237. The 11 pt hierarchy collapse at 70 % (subheadline, caption, caption2 all 11 pt) is an accepted consequence, recorded in the LOT-169 spec.

**Tests** (`TextScaleTests`, iPhone 18 Pro simulator, iOS 27.2): `shrunkTextNeverGoesUnderElevenPoints` (15 issues) and `belowTheDefaultOnlyTheFloorChangesTheScaledSize` (2 issues) fail without the change, pass with it; `thePercentageMultipliesTheSystemSizeOfEveryStyle` adjusted for the floor; `seventyPercentIsSmallerThanTheDefault` skips styles already at 11 pt. `ListDensityLayoutTests`, `ReaderTextScaleTests`, `ComposerBarMetricsTests` green. No screenshots (a list needs a login; none typed). The device/list check at 70 % (Favoris, Messages, xSmall) goes to the next general review list on #893.
