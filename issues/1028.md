### Summary
As an iPad member with two app windows, I want two composers open on the same topic not to overwrite each other's draft, so that a reply I sent in one window does not come back as a draft in the other.

### Facts
- LOT-146 (#236, PR #1027) enables iPad multi-window. Every window shares one `DraftStore`, keyed by topic.
- Two composers open on the **same topic** in two windows autosave into the same draft, so the last write wins. A reply sent from window A deletes the draft. Window B's still-open composer can then write its text back at its next autosave.
- Legacy @ 2fe43da3 has the same single store: `ReplyDraftStore`, one app-wide UserDefaults entry keyed by topic (`HFRswift/Swift/AnswerView.swift:137-140`, `reply_active_drafts_by_topic_v1`), with no multi-window handling. So this is **not a parity gap**.

### Options (operator)
1. Keep legacy's behaviour, documented in the LOT-146 spec (what #1027 ships).
2. A per-window draft (a key per scene): a product change, an M5 / LOT-145 item.
3. A notice in window B when its draft changed under it.

Question also asked on #236 ([comment](https://github.com/ezzz/HFRnow/issues/236#issuecomment-6946106893)). Raised by the Opus review of #1027 (finding 4).
