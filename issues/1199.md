# Issue #1199: [SwiftHFR] settings: seasonal icon windows follow the device calendar while the Noël theme forces Gregorian

### Summary
As a member whose device uses a non-Gregorian calendar, I want the seasonal icons offered on the same days as the seasonal theme, so that the Noël icons appear in December and the "revenir à l'icône précédente ?" question is not asked in the middle of the season.

### Scenario
On a device whose calendar is Islamic, Hebrew or Persian, 10 December 2026 is not in month 12 of that calendar: the Noël icons (window 01/12–26/12) are not offered while the Noël theme is; they are offered on days 1–26 of that calendar's 12th month; `AppIconSeasonModel.checkAtLaunch` asks to revert in mid-December; the `<icon>:<year>` token uses that calendar's year.

### Evidence (`7a61018f`)
- `App/SwiftHFR/Settings/AppIconOption.swift:32-33` and `:42-43`: `calendar.dateComponents([.month, .day], from: date)` on the calendar it is given.
- `App/SwiftHFR/Settings/AppIconSettingsModel.swift:52` and `AppIconSeasonModel.swift:30`: `calendar: Calendar = .current`; the only construction sites (`AppIconSettingsView.swift:13`, `SwiftHFRApp.swift:31`) pass none.
- By contrast `App/SwiftHFR/Settings/Seasons/NoelCalendar.swift:31` and `SeasonalThemeModel.swift:198` go through `NoelCalendar.gregorian(in:)`: "season dates and years never follow a Buddhist or Japanese device calendar".
- `App/SwiftHFRTests/.../AppIconTestSupport.swift:28` builds a Gregorian calendar for every icon test, so `.current` is never exercised.

### Fix
`IconWindow.contains` and `lastEndYear` convert through `NoelCalendar.gregorian(in: calendar.timeZone)`, or the two models default to it.

### Test (fails before the fix)
`iconWindowsAreGregorianWhateverTheDeviceCalendar`: `IconWindow(12,1,12,26).contains(10 Dec 2026, calendar: Calendar(identifier: .islamicUmmAlQura))` is true, and `lastEndYear` on 3 Jan 2027 is 2026.

Foundation's month numbering for these calendars was not run here; the test settles it. Rework model: Sonnet 5.5, reviewed by Opus 5.5.
