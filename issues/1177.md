### Summary

As a user picking a smiley or a GIF, and above all with VoiceOver or Switch Control, I want the detail to open inside the picker with a visible way back, so that I am never three modal layers deep with a swipe as the only exit.

### Rule

- "Display only one sheet at a time from the main interface. […] If something people do within a sheet results in another sheet appearing, close the first sheet before displaying the new one." — [HIG › Sheets](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets) (Best practices; page updated 2026-03-24).
- "Offer alternatives to gestures. […] For example, if you use a swipe gesture to dismiss a view, also make a button available so people can tap or use an assistive device." — [HIG › Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility) (Mobility; updated 2025-06-09).
- "Make it easy to identify a modal view's task." — [HIG › Modality](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/modality).

### What the app does (dev-swift @ `7a61018f`)

- The composer is already a sheet or a full-screen cover (`App/SwiftHFR/Composer/ReaderComposerPresenter.swift:45-47`); it presents the smiley picker as a sheet (`Composer/ReplyComposerView.swift:117-119`).
- `App/SwiftHFR/Smileys/SmileyPickerView.swift:66-69`: the picker presents `.sheet(item: $model.presented) { SmileyDetailView(…).presentationDetents([.medium, .large]) }`. `SmileyDetailView` (`:283-300`) is a bare `VStack` (image, code, « Insérer », favourite button, keywords): no `NavigationStack`, toolbar, title or close button. The only ways out are a swipe down or « Insérer ».
- `App/SwiftHFR/Composer/Gif/GifPickerView.swift:36-43`: `.sheet(item: $model.selected) { GifConfirmationView… }` over the GIF sheet: the same third layer.

### Legacy at `87fe0d1c`

Two layers only: the panels are sheets over the composer (`HFRswift/Swift/AnswerView.swift:499-523`); the smiley detail is an in-panel card (`:2140`, `:2605`) with an `xmark` button labelled « Fermer » (`:2496-2509`). ### Proposed fix and check

- Check: at most two presentation layers when a detail is open; VoiceOver reaches a back or close element. Mac check on iPhone and iPad. Coordinate with #909 (the picker closing on the first tap), which touches the same flow.
