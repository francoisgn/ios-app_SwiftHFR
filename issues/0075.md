### Summary

As a member replying to a topic or an MP, I want to quote a whole message, a selection of it or several messages, so that my answer carries the right `[quotemsg]` blocks.

### Scope

HFRClient (ADR 0008), on top of LOT-048, LOT-031 and the LOT-042 session:
- **Quote template**: load a message's quote form (LOT-031 quote link) with `PostingFlow.loadForm` (LOT-048) through the selected account's session, and return the server's `content_form` prefill as served. Never rebuild `[quotemsg=…]` (HFR-PROTOCOL: always reuse the server's prefill).
- **Selection formatter**: a pure port of `ReplyQuoteSelectionFormatter`. One block: its body is replaced by the selection, or the selection is bolded. Several blocks: the first block holding the selection (case-insensitive) is used.
- **Multi-quote**: the composer GET carries the topic's `quotes…` cookie, kept by the LOT-042 session (`toggleQuote`, `clearQuotes`). After a quoting reply that LOT-048 confirms as `success`, this lot clears that topic's selection: the clean-up the LOT-048 spec leaves to this lot.
