# Issue #644: [SwiftHFR] Reader: secondary text under 4.5:1 on whitelist header (classic) and on a blocked quote inside a whitelisted message

### Summary
As a reader who whitelists or blocklists members, I want the grey secondary text on their tinted headers and quotes to stay readable, so that dates and quote headers keep WCAG AA (4.5:1). Three spots measure just under it in light mode.

Refs #577

### What the Mac check measured
Mac check of #577 on dev-swift f9901d9b, iPhone 18 Pro iOS 27.0 simulator, light mode, dark brightness read back at 100 %. Harness: an anonymized topic fixture in the real reader, whitelist and blocklist forced by a local-only seam. Colours from `getComputedStyle` in the real `WKWebView`; `--secondary` = 108,108,112.

| Where | Style | Background under the text | `--secondary` contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whitelisted author, header date (odd and even) | Classique | 255,230,245 (`--love`) | 4.45 |
| Folded blocked quote header inside a whitelisted author's message (odd) | Classique | 244,220,234 (5 % tint over `--love`) | 4.05 |
| Same, modern | Moderne | 244,234,240 (5 % tint over 40 % `--love`) | 4.45 |

Dark mode stays above 5.2 everywhere. These rules predate #577 (`reader.css` `.style-classic .message.whitelist .header`, `.content`, unchanged by it): #577 composes even-message tables over `--background`, but a quote inside a whitelisted message still sits on a tint over a tint.

Related: #639 (folded blocked quote on even messages, same ≥ 4.5 criterion on its header).

### Acceptance criteria
- `--secondary` (or the date and quote-header colour) reaches ≥ 4.5:1 on the classic whitelist header and on a folded blocked quote inside a whitelisted message, modern and classic, light and dark.
- A `ReaderSeparationWebTests`-style probe covers one of these cases.
