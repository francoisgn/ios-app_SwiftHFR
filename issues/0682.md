### Summary
As a reader who follows links between topics, I want to see how many readers are stacked behind the one on screen, so that I know how far back I am, as in the published app.

Milestone: M4 (operator, 2026-11-08). Found by the `2440d79..2fe43da3` sweep of #655 (row `602b900e`), opened by its catch-up pass.

### Legacy @ `2fe43da3` (`legacy/duo-inner-cover`, #372)
- A reader pushed from a link has `navigationDepth + 1`. When the depth is above 0, the title shows a small capsule (chevron and the depth, white on the action tint) before the topic title, accessibility label "N affichage de messages empilé" (`HFRswift/Swift/MessagesView.swift:3410-3440` @ `2fe43da3`, `602b900e`).
- At `2440d79` the same count was in a custom back button (`navigationDepthBackButton`, `MessagesView.swift:4238-4265` @ `2440d79`); `602b900e` moved it into the title and dropped the "back to root" button.

### Current SwiftHFR
A followed link pushes a new reader (`TopicReaderModel.linkedReader`, LOT-137); the reader's title is `model.title` (`App/SwiftHFR/Reader/TopicReaderView.swift:89`), with no depth count. LOT-137's spec does not list this as a difference.

### Scope
- The depth indicator next to the title of a pushed reader, same rule (shown when depth > 0), same accessibility label.
- Nothing else of the reader's title changes.

### Acceptance criteria
- Depth 0: no indicator. Depth 1, 2: the count, updated on back.
- A unit test on the depth given to each pushed reader; light, dark and Dynamic Type XL checked on a Mac.
