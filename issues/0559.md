### Summary

As a Pro Max user, I want the tab bar back on any list, so that a rotation never hides it.

Steps on iPhone 18 Pro Max, simulator: open a topic in Catégories, rotate to landscape, switch to Favoris, rotate to portrait: the bar stays hidden on Favoris; rotating again brings it back. The hidden preference of the Catégories reader reaches the selected tab when the size class changes.

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The bar is hidden only while the reader of the selected tab is on screen in a collapsed split.
- [ ] The steps above show the bar.
