### Summary

As a member replying from the floating composer, I want the smiley picker to open and stay open on the first tap, so that I can insert a smiley without the composer collapsing and sending me back to the conversation. (Operator feedback on the latest iPhone build, 2026-11-15.)

### Observed (operator, 2026-11-15, MP and topic)

1. In an MP or a topic, tap Reply: the **floating composer** opens (the modern composer of #888, which is not yet behind its switch).
2. Tap the smiley icon **the first time**:
   - the smiley view opens, then closes almost at once;
   - the user is back on the MP or topic, the keyboard hidden, and the composer **collapsed and stuck to the bottom of the screen**.
3. The **second tap works**: the picker opens and stays.

### Expected

The first tap opens the smiley picker, and it stays open until the user picks a smiley or dismisses it. The composer keeps its state (open, text, cursor), and the keyboard comes back when the picker closes.
