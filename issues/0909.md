### Summary

As a member replying from the floating composer, I want the smiley picker to open and stay open on the first tap, so that I can insert a smiley without the composer collapsing and sending me back to the conversation. (Operator feedback on the latest iPhone build, 2026-11-15.)

### Observed (operator, 2026-11-15, MP and topic)

1. In an MP or a topic, tap Reply: the **floating composer** opens (the modern composer of #888, which is not yet behind its switch).
2. Tap the smiley icon **the first time**:
   - the smiley view opens, then closes almost at once;
   - the user is back on the MP or topic, the keyboard hidden, and the composer **collapsed and stuck to the bottom of the screen**.
3. The **second tap works**: the picker opens and stays.

### Expected

The first tap opens the smiley picker, and it stays open until the user picks a smiley or dismisses it. The composer keeps its state (open, text, cursor), and the keyboard comes back when the picker closes.

## francoisgn · 2026-11-16T22:17:26Z

**cloud/exp-b-opus**: analysis at `dev-swift` `d1157487`. No cause proven yet, so no fix: what the code shows, and the Mac capture that would settle it.

**What the code shows**
- There is **one** smiley path, not two. The picker is `.sheet(item: $picker)` on `ReplyComposerView` (`ReplyComposerView.swift:121`). In the reader, the floating and the full-screen composer are the same docked bar (`ReaderComposerPresenter`, `.safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom)`), and they differ only by `ComposerHeight`. So "reuse the full-screen path" means nothing at the presentation level: the difference is the layout.
- Nothing in the app sets `picker` back to nil. Its id is stable (`ObjectIdentifier`, `SmileyPickerModel.swift:67`). The sheet's own `dismiss()` runs only on "Fermer" or a chosen smiley (`SmileyPickerView.swift`). So the sheet is closed by the system or by the presenting view leaving the hierarchy, not by our state.
- Opening the sheet takes focus from the `TextEditor`, so the keyboard goes down. What happens next depends on the height:
  - **Partial** (bug): the bar is sized by its content and rides on the keyboard, so it drops by the keyboard's height. The reader's `ReaderUnderBars` stops ignoring the keyboard at `.partial` (`ReaderWebView.swift:61`), so the page under the presenter is laid out again too.
  - **Full** (works): the bar fills the reader and the page ignores the keyboard, so nothing under the presenter moves.
- **Second tap** (works): the keyboard is already down, so nothing moves either.

The common factor in the three observations is that the presenter is laid out again by the keyboard's departure while the sheet is being presented. That narrows the trigger. It does not prove the mechanism (a dismissal by UIKit, a rebuild of the `safeAreaInset` content, or something in the reader), and it is untested.

**Capture needed (host: mac, simulator with the software keyboard on, or a device)**
1. A temporary `print` in `.sheet(item: $picker, onDismiss:)` and in `SmileyPickerView.onDisappear`, plus `Self._printChanges()` in `ReplyComposerView.body` and in `ReaderComposerPresenter`. Then tap Smileys once at partial height with the keyboard up.
   - If `onDismiss` fires with no app-side write to `picker`, the system dismissed the sheet.
   - If the composer view is rebuilt (its `@State` resets), the `safeAreaInset` content left the hierarchy.
2. The same tap with the keyboard first lowered by a drag on the page. Per the analysis it should work.

With that, the fix is either to present the picker only once the keyboard is down, or to keep the bar from moving while a panel is up (which would also cover the GIF and image sheets, which share the trigger). The hosted test the issue asks for needs a software keyboard, so it also belongs on the Mac.

I am leaving #909 claimed but parked: it needs the Mac capture first. cloud/orch decides whether to hand it to a Mac session.
