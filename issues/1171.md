### Summary

As a user with Reduce Motion on, I want the reader's bottom bar and the composer's bars to change without sliding, as the lists' pager already does, so that the setting holds in the whole app.

### Rule

"People who are prone to these effects can turn on the Reduce Motion accessibility setting. When this setting is active, ensure your app or game responds by reducing automatic and repetitive animations, including zooming, scaling, and peripheral motion." and "Replacing transitions in x-, y-, and z-axes with fades to avoid motion" — [HIG › Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility) (Cognitive; page updated 2025-06-09).

### What the app does (dev-swift @ `7a61018f`)

- `App/SwiftHFR/Reader/ReaderPageEnd.swift:60`: `withAnimation(.easeOut(duration: 0.22)) { atPageEnd.wrappedValue = atEnd }`, each time the scroll reaches or leaves the page end (the bottom pills regroup).
- `App/SwiftHFR/Composer/ReplyComposerView.swift:312`: `formattingBar.transition(.move(edge: .bottom).combined(with: .opacity))` with `.animation(.easeInOut(duration: 0.2), …)` (`:319`).
- `App/SwiftHFR/Composer/ReaderComposerPresenter.swift:38,44`: `.transition(.move(edge: .bottom))` with `.snappy` (modern composer, experimental).
- No `accessibilityReduceMotion` read in `Composer/`, `Experimental/ModernComposer/` or `Reader/`. The lists' pager does it right: `Navigation/PagerToolbar.swift:215` (`reduceMotion ? nil : .smooth(duration: 0.25)`), and so do the seasonal layers.

### Legacy at `87fe0d1c`

Same unconditional animations (`HFRswift/Swift/MessagesView.swift:5789-5790`, `AnswerView.swift:948,960`); `git grep -n "accessibilityReduceMotion\|isReduceMotionEnabled" 87fe0d1c -- HFRswift` returns nothing.

### Class

**(a) defect**: accessibility, shared with legacy; low (short, small movements).

### Proposed fix and check

- Read `@Environment(\.accessibilityReduceMotion)`; pass `nil` to `withAnimation` in the reader and use `.opacity` alone as the composer transitions when it is on. One shared helper, as `PagerVisibility.animation(reduceMotion:)`.
- Check: a unit test on the animation choice. Mac check: with Reduce Motion on, the bars change without sliding.
