# Issue #793: [SwiftHFR] Fixture anonymizer: an unbalanced </span> in a mood status would end its redaction early

### Summary
As a maintainer publishing captures, I want the mood status (`span.MoodStatus`) to be redacted to its real end even if it holds an unbalanced `</span>`, so that no member text survives next to it.

### Facts (found during #763)
- `anonymize` ends a `span.MoodStatus` with a depth count of `<span>` tags, as it did for signatures before #763. With `<span class="MoodStatus">Kilo</span>Lima Handle</span>`, "Lima Handle" would survive, and `check` would not notice.
- #763 fixed the signature by its structure (last child of `div#para…`). That rule does not carry over: in the corpus a mood is followed in `td.messCase1` by HFR's own markup (`<br/><span class="TransactionsReportsLink">…`, `<div class="avatar_center">`, `R63a`, `R33b`), so "last `</span>` of the cell" would over-redact.
- Unknown: whether HFR escapes a mood (plain text, so a `</span>` cannot appear) or renders member markup. Not in the legacy code at `87fe0d1c` (no `MoodStatus` hit) nor in `docs/HFR-PROTOCOL.md`.

### Scope
- Capture or source what a mood can hold (a mood with `<`, `&`, a BBCode tag), on the Mac if needed.
- If it can hold markup: end the mood at HFR's next known sibling, and have `check` refuse text between a mood and that sibling (own walk, LESSONS 33-34). If it cannot: record the fact in `docs/HFR-PROTOCOL.md` and close.
