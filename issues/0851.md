### Summary

As the operator, I want SwiftHFR's CI to follow the harness's new rules on slow and hung jobs, so that a hang fails fast and costs little runner time, and so that the docs job stays green at the next harness sync. Source: Belayline answer of 2026-11-12, harness epic francoisgn/belayline-agentic-harness#98 (merged on its `master`, not yet released).

### On hold

Held by the operator's decision of 2026-11-12: this starts at the sync that follows the next harness release (`/harness-update`). Until then there is no code, because of the quota economy. ([decision](https://github.com/ezzz/HFRnow/issues/368))

### Context

- Already done project-side:
  - #841 set a 45-minute timeout on the App test step and replaced fixed waits with events in the web-view tests;
  - #847 runs the app tests serially (`-parallel-testing-enabled NO`); the App job went green in 25 min 33 s.
- At the sync, the harness's `job-timeout` check fails every job that runs steps and has no `timeout-minutes`. Today most jobs have none (codeql, secrets, labels, pr-issue, mirror-from-ezzz, issue-triage, dependency-review, and most of ci.yml).

### Scope (after the sync)

1. **`timeout-minutes` on every job** that runs steps, sized from measured green runs at about 2× their p95 (harness CI-BUDGET §6, LESSONS L85). Also set it on the one step that can hang. For the App job: the green run took 25 min 33 s, and a red run can add up to 10 min of `simctl diagnose` (L72), so start near 60 min and measure. An exception needs a reason in `.harness/config.json` › `job_timeout`.
2. **Test-level limits** (L86–L87): `.timeLimit(.minutes(n))` on every Swift Testing suite that can hang (web-view, reader waits, `TopicReaderModelWaitTests`). Keep the `xcodebuild` allowances (`-test-timeouts-enabled YES`, `-default-…` and `-maximum-test-execution-time-allowance`) for XCTest.
3. **Measure once on the runner** whether the `xcodebuild` allowance fires on a Swift Testing test. The harness answer says the documentation does not settle it.
4. **Later, only if measurements justify it:** web-view tests in their own job, in parallel with the unit tests (L89). It costs one more macOS runner, so it is the operator's call.

### Never

- Skip, disable, quarantine or delete a test, or loosen an assertion (L90).
- Re-run PRs that are red on a job while `dev-swift` is red on that same job. Fix the base first. A re-run that turns green becomes a `type: bug` issue, not a pass (L91).

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every job that runs steps has `timeout-minutes`, or a documented exception; the harness `job-timeout` check is green.
- [ ] Every Swift Testing suite that can hang has a `.timeLimit`, and each limit is shown to fire on a forced hang.
- [ ] The measurement of point 3 is posted here.
- [ ] Two consecutive green App runs on `dev-swift`, with the job duration posted.
