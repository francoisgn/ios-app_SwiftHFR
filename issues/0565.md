### Summary
As an iPhone user, I want the page pager of a paged list (Catégories topic list, search results) to be reachable, so that I can change page. On iPhone 18 Pro the LOT-185 `PagerToolbar` bottom-bar items sit under the floating tab bar and cannot be used.

### Steps
1. dev-swift at 13bb2639 (contains LOT-185 / #550), iPhone 18 Pro simulator iOS 27.0, logged out.
2. Catégories > Hardware > Processeur (topic list), or Plus > Recherche, search "ryzen".
3. Look at the bottom of the list.

### Observed
The pager buttons ("Page précédente" y=803..841, "Page suivante", "Page 1") are laid out inside the tab bar's frame (tab bar y=791..874 of 874): only a ghost of "Page 1" shows through the glass tab bar. Same in light, dark, Dynamic Type XL, Reduce Transparency (opaque tab bar hides it entirely) and Increase Contrast. Also on the topic reader (where the pager is its own, same overlap) while #424 (#540) is unmerged.

### Expected
The pager is visible and tappable above the tab bar (bottom bar items placed in the toolbar above the tab bar, or the list gets a bottom inset). iPad is fine (no tab bar; pager hittable).

### Scope
App view only, `PagerToolbar` / its call sites (LOT-185 #550). Check the Favoris list too.
