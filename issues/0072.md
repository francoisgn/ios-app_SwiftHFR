### Summary

As a member, I want to search the whole forum and inside a topic, so that I can find a discussion or a message again.

### Scope

Two HFRClient flows (ADR 0008) over the LOT-041 request builders (`globalSearch`, `topicSearch`), the LOT-040 transport and the LOT-025 parsers:
- **Global search**: banned-word check first (Q-7: refuse with a warning, never substitute the query); POST `search.php` on a cookie-less session; read the refresh target of the waiting page (LOT-025); GET the results and the next pages; "no result" is an empty result, not an error.
- **Intra-topic search**: POST `transsearch.php` without following redirects (`RedirectPolicy.doNotFollow`); the `Location` is the result URL; no `Location` means no match.
