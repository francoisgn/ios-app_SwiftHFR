### Summary

As a reader, I want a smiley or inline image to sit **on the text baseline**, with its bottom level with the bottom of the text, rather than centred vertically on the line. A message full of smileys then reads like HFR and legacy. (Operator feedback, 2026-11-17, device screenshot: two large animated smileys centred on their text line, overhanging above and below.)
