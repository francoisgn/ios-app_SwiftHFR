# Issue #1378: [SwiftHFR] Modern composer: the six-line floor of the card's cap can make it taller than the space above the keyboard

### Summary
As a member writing a long reply with the modern composer at a large text size or on a small iPhone, I want the docked card never to be taller than the space above the keyboard, so that its bottom and its controls never go behind the keyboard.

### Observed (review of #1377, iPhone 18 Pro / 17e simulators, iOS 27.2)
- The editor's cap (`ComposerCardLayout.textCap`, #1136) never goes below six lines of the body font (`maximumLines`; test `aTightScreenNeverShrinksTheCapBelowSixLines`). When the room (`availableHeight`) is smaller than the card with six lines, the card is taller than the room.
- At 130 % on the iPhone 18 Pro: the card is 392 pt for 350 pt of room. It still fits under the navigation bar and eats the page strip.
- When the bar alone is taller than the whole space (no page under it), the card's bottom goes past the bottom edge: 15 / 35 / 55 pt in 340 / 300 / 260 pt windows (hosted probe over a page with no minimum height). An iPhone 17e at 130 % keeps 32 pt of margin, so the cases at risk are a smaller screen, a larger text size, or the iPhone Duo cover screen.
- #1377 fixed the other cause (the page's minimum height). This one is the bar's own height.

### Scope
- Bound the card by the real space, not only by the room minus the page strip. Either the six-line floor gives way to the space above the keyboard (the page strip first, then the floor), or the floor stays and the strip shrinks. Pick one and say why in the spec of the change.
- Modern composer only (Réglages › Expérimental); no change to the legacy composer.

### Acceptance criteria
- With a window or keyboard leaving less than six lines plus the chrome, the card's bottom stays on the keyboard and its buttons stay under the navigation bar, at 100 % and 130 %. A hosted test fails without the change.
- The #1136 behaviour is unchanged where the space allows it.
- Device check listed on #893.
