It reads the topic and shows **only the messages that pass at least one rule** (`Packages/HFRKit/Sources/HFRClient/FilteredPosts/FilteredPostsCriteria.swift:4-11, 44-53`, a port of legacy `ParseMessagesOperation.m:176-347`):

| Rule | Setting |
|---|---|
| The message was **quoted at least N times** (HFR's « Message cité N fois ») | **« Minimum de citations » = N** (3 by default; choices 3, 4, 5, 7, 10) |
| It is **one of your messages** | — |
| It **quotes you** (at any depth) | — |
| It **mentions your pseudo** | — |
| Its author is on your **liste blanche** | « Inclure les pseudos whitelistés » |

So a higher N keeps fewer « popular » messages; your own messages and those addressing you are always kept.
