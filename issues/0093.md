### Summary

As a reader, I want to save topics for reading without network, list them and remove them, so that I can read where there is no connection.

### Scope

An HFRStorage store of saved topics and pages: save, list, load and evict. Device-only and excluded from backup (ADR 0013). It reuses the LOT-066 cache directory, evictor and budget. The spec decides and justifies what is saved (parsed `TopicPage` models or raw HTML), the on-disk format and its version, the eviction policy, and what happens to a page saved by an older build. Fetching the pages is the app's job (LOT-139, #229): HFRStorage imports neither HFRNetwork nor HFRParsing. Importing the legacy archive is LOT-067 (#92), not this lot.
