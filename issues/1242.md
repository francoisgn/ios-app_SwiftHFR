# Issue #1242: [SwiftHFR] reader: a composer open during a root swap (Duo fold, iPad resize) can leave the page frozen, its close never reported

- State: open
- Author: francoisgn · created 2026-11-19T08:12:24Z · updated 2026-11-19T08:12:24Z
- Labels: type: bug, priority: medium, difficulty: easy, area: app, status: needs-triage, origin: audit, m4-review: quality
- Source: https://github.com/ezzz/HFRnow/issues/1242

### Summary
As a member who folds the iPhone Duo (or resizes the iPad window across regular width) while a composer is open over a topic, I want the page to stay usable afterwards. Today the reader can keep thinking a composer is open, so its taps are ignored, the back button is hidden and "Répondre" stays off.

### Scenario (read from the code, not run on a device)
A reply, edit or MP composer is open over a topic. The member folds the Duo or resizes the iPad window across regular width:
- `RootNavigationView` swaps the root, and `NavigationModel.reader(for:)` hands the same `TopicReaderModel` to a new `TopicReaderView`.
- The old view goes away without reporting its composer closed: `TopicReaderModel.openComposers` is fed only by the views' `onChange` (`ReaderComposerGuard`, `ReaderReplyFlow`, `ReaderAuthorMenuSheet`).
- So `suspendsPageInteractions` stays true on the shared reader until another topic is selected.

### Sources
- Found by the Fable review of #1241 (round 2), as a hole of `dev-swift`, not of that PR: https://github.com/ezzz/HFRnow/pull/1241
- `App/SwiftHFR/Reader/TopicReaderModel+Composer.swift` (`ReaderComposerGuard`, `suspendsPageInteractions`), `App/SwiftHFR/Reader/TopicReaderModel.swift` `composer(_:isOpen:)`.

### Fix idea (from the review)
Make each view's report authoritative when it appears: `.onChange(of: isEditing, initial: true)` in `ReaderComposerGuard`, and the same for the reply and the author menu's MP. A newly shown view then corrects a stale entry.

### Acceptance criteria
- A test: a reader with a stale `.edit` (or `.reply`) entry, a fresh report saying closed: the slot is free again.
- A check on the device (#893 list): fold the Duo with a composer open, unfold, the page responds.
