# Issue #654: [SwiftHFR] Floating "+" reply button on topic and MP pages, with its "Bouton +" setting (M4)

- State: open
- Author: francoisgn · created 2026-11-08T07:16:26Z · updated 2026-11-09T15:07:27Z
- Labels: type: bug, priority: high, difficulty: medium, area: app, status: needs-triage, beta-test finding, origin: legacy-evolution

### Summary
As a member, I want the floating "+" button at the bottom of a topic or MP page, and its setting, so that I reply in one tap the way the legacy app lets me.

Milestone: M4 (operator, 2026-11-08)

### Legacy behaviour (legacy `master` @ `21173e77`; not in `2440d79`, added after it)
- Setting **Réglages › Sujets › Bouton +** with three values (`AppSettingsView.swift:106, 481-482`; `Common.swift:243-262`, `AppReplyButtonBehavior`): **Réponse rapide** (default, `quick`), **Répondre comme le forum** (`forum`), **Pas d'icône** (`hidden`).
- The page shows a floating button at the bottom: icon `plus` for quick reply, `square.and.pencil` for the forum-style reply; no button when `hidden`, and none on an iPhone in landscape (compact height) (`MessagesView.swift:3065, 3180-3196`).
- Seen by the operator on a device on 2026-11-08, in the legacy app's settings.

### Current SwiftHFR
No floating reply button and no such setting (to be confirmed by the implementer with file:line).

### Scope
- Port the button and the setting with the same three values, key and default, on topics and MPs.
- Describe "Répondre comme le forum" from the legacy code before porting it: never guess what it opens.
- The per-post menu is a separate item (#594).

### Acceptance criteria
- The three values behave as in legacy on iPhone and iPad, light and dark; the setting persists.
- Tests on the setting and the button's presence per value and size class.
