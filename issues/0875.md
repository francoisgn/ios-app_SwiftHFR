As a member, I want the Tous / Favoris / Suivis / Lus filter in a sub-forum too, not only in a whole category, so that I find what I follow wherever I browse. Split from #593 (operator, 2026-11-13). Its items 3 and 4 at category level were delivered by #427 (PR #744).

### What is known
- HFR filters a sub-forum with the category list URL plus `subcat=<s>` and `owntopic=<n>`: `forum1.php?config=hfr.inc&cat=<c>&page=1&subcat=<s>&owntopic=<n>`. `R603d` (**verified**) shows that `subcat=<s>` answers 200 in place. The same 14 topics with `owntopic=1` is a run observation only (HFR-PROTOCOL §6c "Smiley page and filtered lists").
- The app knows a sub-forum only by its path (`/hfr/<cat>/<sub>/liste_sujet-1.htm`). `TopicSource.supportsFlagFilter` is false for `.subforum` on purpose, and the UI says the filter applies to the whole category.
- Where the numeric `<s>` comes from:
  - The REST sub-category lists (#285, PR #873, `R285a`-`R285c`) give each sub-category's `id` and `name`. That `id` is the HTML `subcat` (run observation in #873).
  - The topic rows of an HTML list carry `subcat=<s>` in their `forum2.php` links.
  - No capture yet ties a REST `name` to a home-page sub-forum path: match on names, or read `subcat` from the rows of the sub-forum's own page.
