# Issue #1249: [SwiftHFR] composer: build the image panel's thumbnail loader once per app, and test the model's loader

- State: closed

### Summary
As the maintainer, I want the image panel's thumbnail loader built once, not at each composer view init, and the loader the views read covered by a test, so that no URL sessions are churned and a wrong loader cannot pass the tests.

### Blocked by
#1248

### Scope
Residuals of the round-3 review of #1248 (#1193), left out because the review cap was reached:
1. `ReplyComposerView.swift:76`: `State(initialValue: ImageUploadModel(…))` is evaluated at each `ReplyComposerView.init`, and `ImageUploadModel.init` builds `services.thumbnails()`, i.e. a `ReaderImageFetcher()` with its `AnonymousTransport` and two `URLSession`s (`URLSessionTransport.swift:14-16`). Each re-evaluation of a presenting body while a composer is shown (`ReaderComposerPresenter.swift:36,45,47`, `MessageListView.swift:73`, `TopicListView.swift:108`) creates and discards a pair. Fix: build the loader once (stored in the live services, or lazily in the model).
2. `ImageUploadModel.thumbnails` (read by the history and the chips) is not tested: replacing it with `.gif()` keeps the suite green. Fix: assert that `model.thumbnails.hostFilter` refuses an off-host URL and accepts an on-host one.

### Acceptance criteria
- A test fails if the model's loader drops the rehost host filter.
- No `ReaderImageFetcher()` is built per `ReplyComposerView.init`.
