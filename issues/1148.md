# Issue #1148: [SwiftHFR] Decision: should the « Vidéos » setting (`embedded_videos`) sync across devices like « Afficher les signatures »?

- State: open

### Summary
As a member with an iPhone and an iPad, I may want my « Vidéos » choice (Intégrée or Lien, #691) to follow me, like « Afficher les signatures » does, so that the reader looks the same on both devices. Today ADR 0013 keeps `embedded_videos` device-only; this needs a product decision before any code.

### Context
- #691 (PR #1132) gives the key a reader and a writer. ADR 0013 (rows `Dropped, no reader` and the `embedded_videos` appendix row) still says it is dropped and never synced, and `SettingScopeTests` pins it `.device`.
- ADR 0013 §E syncs content-rendering preferences (`display_sig`, `theme_style`). « Vidéos » is of that kind, so the reviewer of #1132 expected either a sync or a recorded reason for staying device-only.
- Decision by cloud/orch on #1132: not in that PR.

### Options
1. Sync it (`.synced`): update `SettingKey`, the matrix test and the two ADR 0013 rows (an ADR amendment).
2. Keep it per device and record why (and fix the stale « dropped / no reader » text in ADR 0013 either way).

### Needs
The operator's call; then a small PR (the ADR change is merged by the core orchestrator or the maintainer).

#691, #1132.
