### Summary

As a member upgrading from the legacy app, I want my accounts imported from the legacy Keychain archive without the app instantiating arbitrary classes, so that the import stays safe without breaking. #1200 P4 tried that and had to be reverted: this issue carries it until a Mac check says which classes the archive really holds.

### What happened

- `LegacyAccountArchive.decode` (`Packages/HFRKit/Sources/HFRStorage/LegacyImport/KeychainLegacyAccountSource.swift`) reads the `HFR_COMPTES_KEY` item with `requiresSecureCoding = false`, as legacy's `unarchiveObjectWithData:` did (`MultisManager.m:43-47`).
- #1202 (`27f44e6`) switched to `requiresSecureCoding = true` with `decodeObject(of:)` limited to arrays and mutable arrays, dictionaries and mutable dictionaries, strings and mutable strings, numbers, data and mutable data, dates, URLs and `HTTPCookie`.
- **Measured**: on macOS, `LegacyAccountArchiveTests` "decodes every field of the legacy archive" failed with `sourceUnreadable` (run 44403024224, job 135150903639, the only failure). The replayed legacy archive (`MultisManager.m:49-80`: `NSKeyedArchiver` of an `NSArray` of `NSMutableDictionary` with `NSHTTPCookie`s) does not decode under secure coding with that list. Reverted in `7b2ca13`.
- **Risk today**: low. The archive comes only from the app's own Keychain item.
