### Summary

The reader web tests sometimes wait about 240 s for a scroll report that never comes, after a scroll back to the top, and then fail. CI goes red on PRs that did not touch the code.

### Observed

- `ReaderLandingWebTests.aDocumentLandsOnItsAnchor…` (`App/SwiftHFRTests/Reader/ReaderWebControllerTests.swift:201`): "reported: [2]" after 247 s, in job 133110414600 on #924 head 68d380ef. `reader.js` there is byte-identical to its merge base, and the same test passed in 3.8 s at 51da4c09 (job 133090341114). The log shows WebContent process trouble just before the failure.
- Probably the same cause: `ReaderPageEndWebTests.aTallPageReportsItsEndOnlyWhenItIsScrolledThere` on #924 (jobs 133066684120 and 133081541823), about 241 s waiting for the report after `window.scrollTo(0, 0)`. That test now moves to #820's own PR.

### Scope

- Find why a scroll report is lost on the simulator: a WebContent process restart, the `scroll` event not firing for a programmatic scroll with no visual change, or reporting that depends on `visualViewport`.
- Fix the cause in `reader.js` or in the test helper. Never quarantine, skip or loosen a test.

### Acceptance criteria
