# Issue #1454: [SwiftHFR] Topic lists: a final removal failure keeps another row's « Réessayer »

- State: open
- Author: francoisgn · created 2026-11-28T11:03:42Z · updated 2026-11-28T11:03:42Z
- Labels: type: bug, priority: low, difficulty: trivial, area: app, status: ready, origin: initial-port
- Source: https://github.com/ezzz/HFRnow/issues/1454

### Summary

As a reader removing flags from a topic list, I want the failure alert's « Réessayer » to retry the row the alert talks about, so that I never resend another row's removal by mistake.

### Observed (by reading, at `dev-swift` e672de7c)
In `App/SwiftHFR/Navigation/TopicListModel+RowActions.swift:62-63` (LOT-125), the `.failed` case of `removeFlag` sets `actionFailure` but does not clear `retryableRemoval`. Here is the scenario:
1. The removal of row A fails with a retryable error, so `retryableRemoval` is A.
2. Meanwhile, a removal of row B is in flight and then fails for good.
3. The alert shows B's reason, with a « Réessayer » that resends A's removal.

The Fable round-3 review of #1444 found this (finding 3). It belongs to the same class as the super-favourite case fixed there in 333f7bee.

### Fix
Set `retryableRemoval = nil` in the `.failed` case, with a model test that interleaves two removals as in the scenario.

### Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A final failure never shows « Réessayer ».
- [ ] A test covers it and is shown to fail without the fix.
