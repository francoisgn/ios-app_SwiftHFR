- Pure model: `ComposerCardLayout.cardHeight` is already bounded by `max(six lines, availableHeight)`, so "content taller than the room" cannot exceed the room in the model. A unit test on the model cannot go red for either issue, as the earlier analysis said.

**Candidate causes (none provable here)**
1. #1285: SwiftUI's keyboard safe area is not re-applied to the reader after the picker sheet, which has a `.searchable` field, hands the keyboard back. `onDismiss` sets `focused = .text`, a no-op if it is still `.text`.
2. #1286: `ComposerCardLayout.room` is an estimate (window − top inset − a hard-coded 44 pt nav bar − keyboard − 3 lines). The comment says the 44 pt is unmeasured on iOS 27. If the real container is smaller, the card overflows its container both ways, which matches "grows downwards".

**The only code fix I can see, and why I stopped**
Own the keyboard: `.ignoresSafeArea(.keyboard)` on the reader and a bottom padding on the bar fed by keyboard notifications. A hosted test posting `keyboardWillChangeFrameNotification` could then go red. It would, however, break the interactive drag-down of the keyboard that #853 relies on (`ComposerPlacement.swift` header), and I cannot check that on Linux. A blind change to the core layout of the modern composer is worse than the two bugs, so I did not make it.

## francoisgn · 2026-11-27T09:34:00Z

**mac/mac-mbp (w1285, Opus 5.5)**: measured on the iPhone 18 Pro simulator, iOS 27.2 (`dev-swift` @ 97a060bd), modern composer on, with the real software keyboard (328 pt, top at y = 546), in two hosted harnesses: (A) the docked presenter over a plain `ScrollView` page; (B) the **real `TopicReaderView`** (fixture page in a `TabView` › `NavigationStack`, its own reply path). No login, no network.

**Cause, same for #1285 and #1286: the reader's page has a minimum height, and the docked bar is a `safeAreaInset` of it.** `ReaderStateOverlay` stacks the bottom pull-to-refresh icon with `.padding(.bottom, 96)` (#1207, `de5ccd99`, merged 2026-11-19, the day before the report). That gives the page a minimum height of about 135 pt. Once the bar plus 135 pt is taller than the space between the navigation bar and the keyboard, the `safeAreaInset` stack overflows **both ways**, so the card grows upwards and its bottom slides behind the keyboard. Lowering the keyboard gives more space, so the card comes back.

| Lines at 100 % (B, real reader) | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 12 | 13+ (cap) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| card height (pt) | 190 | 210 | 230 | 251 | 271 | 312 | 315 |
| card bottom − keyboard top (pt) | −8 | −8 | **−5** | **+5** | **+15** | **+35** | **+37** |

At 130 %: +11 pt from 4 lines, +38 pt at the cap. In harness A (no minimum height), the same text keeps the card 8 pt above the keyboard at every line, so the cap and `availableHeight` are not the cause: the model's `cardHeight` stays ≤ `availableHeight` (379 pt).

**#1285 (smiley)**: the picker opening hides the keyboard (`willHide`, end y = 874). An insert brings it back (`willShow` 301 pt, then 328 pt, within 0.5 s, while the sheet is still closing). The bar comes back exactly where it was. With 3 lines, the card is 8 pt above the keyboard before and after (A and B). With 10 lines in the real reader, it is **15 pt behind the keyboard both before and after** the smiley. Nothing is specific to the picker: the operator's reply was most likely past the overflow line, on a screen smaller than 874 pt, where it starts sooner. The keyboard room (`ComposerKeyboardRoom`) is unchanged by the picker (379 pt before and after).

**Also measured**: the reader's inline navigation bar is **54 pt** (y 62 to 116), not the 44 pt `ComposerCardLayout.navigationBarHeight` assumes, so the room estimate was 10 pt too tall. The 3-line strip absorbed it. With 54 pt, the cap lands at about 12 lines at 100 % on this screen.

**Fix (draft PR #1377)**: the presenter gives the page exactly what the bar leaves it (`.frame(minHeight: 0, maxHeight: .infinity)` on the content), so no page minimum can push the bar (every docked composer: reply, edit, author menu MP; every panel). Whatever overflows is the page's, under the bars. Also `navigationBarHeight` = 54. A hosted test with a page that cannot shrink (0 / 135 / 300 pt) goes red without the fix: card bottom 42.7 pt and 125 pt past the edge.
