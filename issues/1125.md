### Summary
As a user of a gallery theme, I want the large title, the selected rows and the « Lignes denses » look to follow the theme as the rest of the list does. Three gaps came out of Fable's round-6 review on #1118 (#842, themed native lists). None of them is a regression of that PR.

### Scope
1. **Large-title row (medium, rendering not yet verified).** `ScreenLargeTitle` writes `listRowBackground(nil)` through `listLookTitleRow()` (`DenseListLook.swift:37`, `ScreenTitle.swift:144`), inside a `listLookRows()` Group that a theme paints `surface`.
   - Under a theme in the classic look, the title row therefore keeps the system card, or sits on `surface`.
   - Either way the `title` token is not on `background`, the only pair its contrast rule checks (`ThemeTokens.swift:155`), and THEMES.md §3 still says it is.
   - Fix: theme the title row: `background`, or no card under a theme. Make the rule match what is drawn, and pin it in a hosted test.
2. **Selection and headers under the list look are untested (low).** `ThemedListTests.theListLookRowsFollowTheThemeInBothLooks` covers neither:

   - a section header inside the themed Group.

   The themed selection in the nine #1107 lists rests on dev-swift's documented "a row's own background wins" rule. Fix: add a selected row and a `Section` with a header to that test.
3. **Dense look under a theme draws row text on `background` (low).** The text pairs `textPrimary`, `textSecondary`, `errorText` and `badgeOutline` are only checked on `surface` and `selection`. The dense look is experimental and off by default. Fix: add those pairs on `background` to `ThemeTokens.contrastRules` (each seen red first), or document the gap in THEMES.md §3.
