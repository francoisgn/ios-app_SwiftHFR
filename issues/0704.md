| Series | Setup | Runs | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `MessageTitleToneTests` alone | 20 | 🟢 20/20 pass (9 tests, 23 cases) |
| 2 | + `AppThemeTests` (its extension holds `AppThemeStabilityTests`), clean app builds looping in parallel, `-parallel-testing-enabled NO` | 20 | 🟢 20/20 pass, **but not CI-like**: that flag also turns off Swift Testing's in-process parallelism, so `AppThemeTests` (62–79 s) always ran entirely before `MessageTitleToneTests` |
| 2b | same suites and load, **CI-like**: parallel on, `-parallel-testing-worker-count 1` (CI log: one clone, all suites concurrent in one process); log shows both suites started together | 20 | 🟢 20/20 pass (19 tests in 2 suites) |

**Failures: 0 in 60 runs**, so no failing RGB to report. Local-only instrumentation (a `Comment` on the four `#expect` calls of `drawnColour(dark:)` and `rowDrawsTheTitleTone(dark:)`, plus a `print`, never committed) logged the samples on every run; they never moved:

| Case | Background | Read sampled = expected | Unread sampled = expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| light | `FFFFFF` | `636369` | `000000` |
| dark | `1C1C1E` | `98989F` | `FFFFFF` |

The other scheme's values, for reading a future failure: in light, `98989F`/`FFFFFF` would be the dark colours; a sample equal to `FFFFFF` (light) or `1C1C1E` (dark) for the read swatch would be the background.

**Hypotheses**
- *Shared appearance state (other scheme's colour)*: 🟠 not supported. `UITraitCollection.current` stayed `light` in all 232 logged samples, `dark=true` included, and while `AppThemeTests` flipped its windows concurrently (2b), yet every dark sample was right: the swatch resolves from its own `.environment(\.colorScheme)` through `ImageRenderer`, not from the host window.
- *Unrendered frame (background colour)*: 🟠 not reproduced either.
- Neither holds on this Mac. One fact from the failing CI log (run 42681255988 attempt 1): the failing and the passing `drawnColour(dark:)` cases and every neighbouring case report ~63.4 s, and `AppThemeTests` takes 62–79 s here; the 63 s is the main-actor wait behind `AppThemeTests`' window work, not the test itself. The CI process also runs the **whole** test target concurrently, which this measurement did not (two suites only).

**Suggestion for the fix (cloud/orch's call)**: keep a sampled-vs-expected `Comment` on those `#expect` calls in the committed test, so the next CI failure names the colour; then the hypothesis is decided from the CI log itself.

Logs and xcresults kept locally (`~/agent-work/2026-11-10-ezzz-hfrnow-i704/`) until the fix merges. No app change, no PR.
