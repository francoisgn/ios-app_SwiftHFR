### Summary
As the AQ write flow's author, I want the refusals the local AQ capture left out, so that the app does not guess them. Extends the method of #574; #591 already holds the addition after the flood limit, this issue holds the rest.

### Scope
From a **local** instance of `toyo@96c0aea` only, never the live service (same method as #574):
- a comment over the column length;
- a non-ASCII value in `nom`, `topic_titre` or `commentaire`;
- a topic or post number that is not numeric;
- an existing AQ id with a missing field.

The live host's own headers are not in this scope: no fixture holds them and no refusal may be sent to the live host to get them. A read's headers need the operator's go first.

`docs/HFR-PROTOCOL.md`, LOT-055 "Refusal answers of a local instance". `aq/api/addAlerte.php` @ `toyo@96c0aea`. The kit's `AQ local check` accepts the run's forms only, so a new value form is added to `tooling/fixtures/anonymize.py` with its test.
