### Summary

As a user confirming an action (deleting a message, alerting the moderators, reverting an icon, refusing the MP storage), I want the alert's buttons to name what they do, so that I cannot confirm an irreversible action by reading only « Oui ».

### Rule

"Create succinct, logical button titles. Aim for a one- or two-word title that describes the result of selecting the button. Prefer verbs and verb phrases that relate directly to the alert text […] In informational alerts only, you can use "OK" for acceptance, avoiding "Yes" and "No." Always use "Cancel" to title a button that cancels the alert's action." and "A specific button title like "Erase," "Convert," "Clear," or "Delete" helps people understand the action they're taking." — [HIG › Alerts](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/alerts) (Buttons; page updated 2024-02-02).
