### Summary
As a member who syncs the blocklist with MPStorage, I want a pseudo I unblock while a sync is merging to stay unblocked, so that a narrow race does not block it again on every device.

### Scope
These are two Low, non-blocking findings from the round-3 Fable review of #1020 (LOT-131), head `484be022`. They were left out of that PR because it had used its three review rounds.
1. `App/SwiftHFR/MPStorage/BlocklistSyncModel.swift` `reconcile`: the plan is built before the local writes and `refreshLists`, and its document side is applied after them. If the member unblocks a device-only pseudo inside that window, the stale plan writes the pseudo back to the forum, and the nested merge blocks it again on the device. Reproduction: copy `[Beta]`, forum `[Beta, Gamma]`, device `[Alpha, Beta]`, account `on`. Hold `refreshLists` during `start()`, unblock Alpha, then run `handle`. Result: `[Alpha, Beta, Gamma]` on both sides, where `[Beta, Gamma]` is expected. Fix: just before the `edit`, read the device list and `changes.pending` again, and build `adding` / `removing` from them. Test it with a gated `refreshLists`.
2. `BlocklistSyncQuestions` « Annuler » on the creation question records `declined` but keeps the held changes, and `.deviceOnly` in `handle` withdraws only its own change. A stale held removal can then outlive a re-block until the next sync after a later switch-on. Fix: withdraw every pending change whenever the account does not sync.
