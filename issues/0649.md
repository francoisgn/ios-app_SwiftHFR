### Summary
As an iPhone Pro Max user in landscape, I want the reader's page controls (‹ › and "N / M") to be visible and tappable, because the tab bar #540 keeps in regular width now floats over them.

Refs #540 (found in its Mac completion check). Related: #565 (list pager under the tab bar, iPhone portrait), LOT-186 (#549).

### Observed
dev-swift `2d851cf2`, iPhone 18 Pro Max simulator iOS 27.0, logged out, a public one-page topic opened from Catégories, list and reader side by side in landscape.
- The tab bar frame is y 376–440 (of 440). The reader's "Page précédente" / "Page suivante" (x 417–505) and "Page 1 sur 1" (x 637–694) sit at y 373–376: under the glass bar, only a ghost shows through. XCUITest reports them not hittable in landscape-left (hittable in landscape-right, though still drawn under the bar).
- The topic list's own pager in the left column ("Première page", "Page précédente", "Page 1", "Page suivante") is under the same bar: the landscape form of #565.
- Same result on `0822f99b`.
- Portrait (bar hidden by #540) and iPhone 18 Pro in both orientations (compact, bar hidden) are fine: the controls sit at the bottom and are hittable.

### Expected
In regular width with the tab bar shown, the reader's page controls and the list pager sit above the tab bar (bottom inset or toolbar placement), or the bar is handled so they stay reachable.

### Acceptance criteria
- Pro Max landscape, light and dark: reader ‹ › and "N / M" fully visible and hittable above the tab bar; list pager too (or tracked in #565).
- Portrait and compact iPhones unchanged.
