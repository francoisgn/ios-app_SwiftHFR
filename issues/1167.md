### Summary

As a reader using a large text size, I want the « Page numéro » sheet to show its field and both rows of buttons in full, so that I can still jump to a page.

### Rule

"Keep text truncation to a minimum as font size increases. In general, aim to display as much useful text at the largest accessibility font size as you do at the largest standard font size." and "Consider adjusting your layout at large font sizes." — [HIG › Typography](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography) (Supporting Dynamic Type; page updated 2025-12-16).

### What the app does (dev-swift @ `7a61018f`)

`App/SwiftHFR/Reader/ReaderPageSheet.swift:110`: `.presentationDetents([.height(260)])`. The content (`:73-92`) is a plain `VStack` with no `ScrollView`: a `.footnote` hint, a `.body` text field and two rows of text-style buttons (`minHeight: 36`), with `.padding(20)` under an inline navigation bar. All of it scales with Dynamic Type; the detent does not.

### Legacy at `87fe0d1c`

Same: `HFRswift/Swift/Common.swift:3212` `.presentationDetents([.height(260)])`.

### Class

**(a) defect** if the Mac check confirms clipping (Dynamic Type, shared with legacy).

### Proposed fix and check

- `[.height(260), .medium]` (or `.medium` at accessibility sizes), or wrap the content in a `ScrollView`.
- Mac check: open « Page … » at AX3 and AX5 on the smallest iPhone, portrait and landscape, number pad up: the hint, the field and both button rows are visible or scrollable.
