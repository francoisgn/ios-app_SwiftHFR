### Summary

As a user who lowers « Taille du texte » for a denser display, I want the smallest texts to stay legible, so that badges and info lines do not fall to 8 pt.

### Rule

"Follow the recommended default and minimum text sizes for each platform — for both custom and system fonts — to ensure your text is legible on all devices." with the table "iOS, iPadOS | 17 pt | 11 pt" (default / minimum) — [HIG › Typography](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography) (Best practices; page updated 2025-12-16). Same table in [HIG › Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility) (Vision).

### What the app does (dev-swift @ `7a61018f`)

- `App/SwiftHFR/Settings/TextScale.swift:21-24`: `UIFont.preferredFont(forTextStyle:…).pointSize * factor`, with no floor; `:30-32` builds `.system(size:)` from it.
- The range is `textSizePercentRange = 70...130` (`Packages/HFRKit/Sources/HFRStorage/Settings/SettingKey.swift:53`).
- At 70 % and the default Dynamic Type size, by arithmetic on Apple's size table: row info lines (`.caption`, `Navigation/LegacyTopicRowView.swift:48`) 12 → 8.4 pt; `BadgeCapsule` (`.caption2`, `Common/AccessibleColors.swift:83`) 11 → 7.7 pt; row titles (`.subheadline`) 15 → 10.5 pt; compact titles (`.footnote`) 13 → 9.1 pt.

### Legacy at `87fe0d1c`

`HFRswift/Swift/Common.swift:283-290`: `AppTextSizeScale` has only 100, 110, 120 and 130 %; it never goes below the system size. The 70–130 range is a recorded difference of LOT-169 (`TextScale.swift:4-6`).

### Class

**(a) defect** against the HIG minimum, introduced by a deliberate SwiftHFR difference (LOT-169, #295): the operator decides between a floor and the range.

### Proposed fix and check

- Floor the computed size at 11 pt in `TextScale.pointSize` (`max(11, …)`): the slider still densifies titles and body, and the small styles stop at the minimum. Or raise the lower bound of the range.
- Check: a unit test `TextScale(percent: 70).pointSize(.caption2, at: .large) >= 11`. Mac check: Favoris and Messages at 70 %, Dynamic Type at xSmall and at default.
