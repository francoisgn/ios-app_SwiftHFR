### Summary
As an iPad user, I want Bookmarks and Alertes Qualitay to list their topics in the middle column and open the topic on the right, as Favoris does, so that every list behaves the same in the split view, as in the published app.

Milestone: M4 (operator, 2026-11-08). Found by the `2440d79..2fe43da3` sweep of #655 (row `03e5e225`, "Complete HFR-006 iPad split navigation"), opened by its catch-up pass.

### Legacy @ `2fe43da3` (`legacy/duo-inner-cover`, #372)
- The sidebar destinations Catégories, Favoris, Messages, Bookmarks and Alertes Qualitay "use a topic list" (`IPadSidebarDestination.usesTopicList`, `HFRswift/Swift/MainWindow.swift:1307-1313` @ `2fe43da3`): a three-column split, the list in the content column and the topic in the detail column (`iPadSidebarRoot`, `:1796-1810`). Réglages, Rechercher, Crédits, Charte and Supprimer mon compte keep a two-column split (`:1811-1820`).
- Sidebar sections are titled "Forum", "Outils" and "Plus", sidebar title "Hardware.fr" (`:1824-1851`).

### Current SwiftHFR
Every Plus entry, Bookmarks and Alertes Qualitay included, is a two-column split with the entry in a stack in the detail column (`App/SwiftHFR/Root/RootSidebarView.swift:22-31`, LOT-170 "a Plus entry uses a two-column split", ported from `2440d79`), so a bookmark or an alert opens its topic in the same column as its list.

### Scope
- Bookmarks and Alertes Qualitay as three-column destinations (list in content, topic in detail), the other Plus entries unchanged.
- Check the section titles against the reference while there (LOT-170 names the sections, not their titles).
- Coordinate with #512 / #665 (detail column selection) and LOT-147 (#237, Duo inner screen).

### Acceptance criteria
- On an iPad in regular width, a bookmark or an alert opens on the right, the list stays in the middle; compact width unchanged.
- Tests on `RootModel` for the destination's column layout; Mac check in light, dark and Dynamic Type XL.
