# Issue #647: [SwiftHFR] iPad Catégories column: flag-tinted rows lose the list card colour (dark holes in dark mode)

### Summary
As an iPad reader, I want flagged rows in the Catégories column to look like the other rows with a colour accent, not like dark holes in the list, so that the flag tint from #590 reads the same as on iPhone.

Refs #590 (found in its Mac check; build `cb3da9ce` = `origin/dev-swift`).

### Observed
iPad Pro 13" simulator (iOS 27.0, landscape), logged in, Catégories > Apple > Tous les sujets (the `CategoriesSidebarColumn` list, `tintBase: .clear`). The list there **does** sit on a rounded card (white in light, (28, 28, 30) in dark). Plain rows show the card; flagged rows drop it and lay the tint over the column background instead. Sampled backgrounds:

| Appearance | Plain row | Blue flag | Red flag | Yellow favourite | Column background |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light | (255, 255, 255) | (227, 236, 247) | (243, 231, 236) | (243, 240, 232) | (242, 242, 247) |
| Dark | (28, 28, 30) | (0, 14, 24) | (24, 6, 7) | (24, 21, 0) | (0, 0, 0) |
| Dark + Increase Contrast | (36, 36, 38) | (13, 26, 37) | (37, 14, 15) | (37, 32, 10) | (0, 0, 0) |

In dark, tinted rows are darker than their untinted neighbours (they read as holes in the card); in light, the yellow favourite turns greyish. On iPhone (`.grouped`) the tint sits on the card and looks right.

The selected row is fine: it shows the system selection fill and no tint.

### Likely cause
#590 assumed this column "has no card" and passes `RowTint.Base.clear` (`RootSidebarView.swift:103`); the list style there still draws one.

### Acceptance
- In the iPad Catégories column, a flagged row's background is the card colour plus the tint, in light, dark and Increase Contrast (the base matching what the list draws).
- The selected row keeps the selection fill without tint.
