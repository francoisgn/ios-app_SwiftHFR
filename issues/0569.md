# Issue #569: [SwiftHFR] MP list: show the correspondent's avatar (avatar cache is never filled)

### Summary
As a member reading my MPs, I want each one-to-one conversation to show my correspondent's avatar, not a grey placeholder, so that I recognise people at a glance. Operator feedback, 2026-11-07, milestone M4.

### Cause
Only the read side of the avatar cache was ported.
- `MessageAvatarView.swift:10-47` (LOT-126) reads `AvatarCache` (LOT-066) and falls back to a grey circle.
- Nothing in the app or the engine ever calls `AvatarCache.store` (`git grep AvatarCache`). Every lookup misses, so every avatar is grey.
- Legacy filled the cache while parsing topic pages: `ParseMessagesOperation.m:176, 430-446` @ 2440d79, downloading when the avatar was missing or older than 24 h.

### Proposal
1. **Fill the cache from the pages read (parity).**
   - When a topic or MP page block has an `avatarURL` (`MessageBlockParser.swift:113-120`, `TopicPageSource.swift:84`), hand `(pseudo, url)` to an app-side `AvatarStore` actor.
   - The actor fetches with the cookie-less fetcher, from forum-images hosts only.
   - It honours the image policy (LOT-107), caps the file size, and skips the download when the cache is fresh (24 h).
   - MP rows reload when the store reports a new avatar: add the store's generation to the row's `.task(id:)`.
2. **Monogram placeholder.** Initials on a disc whose colour comes from a stable hash of the pseudo, with ≥4.5:1 text contrast. This replaces the grey disc; groups keep their icon.
3. **Later (needs a capture):** prefetch from profiles for correspondents never opened. This needs the `profilebdd.php?pseudo=` redirect captured (pending capture in HFR-PROTOCOL); open a `host: mac` capture issue then. No guessing.

### Acceptance
- Tests with a loopback-only session: no cookie is sent, and the policy is respected.
- A row refreshes after a store write.
- The monogram is deterministic.
- Mac check: light, dark, Increase Contrast, Dynamic Type XL.
- A GAP row records the monogram.
