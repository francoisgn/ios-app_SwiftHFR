### Summary
As a maintainer, I want `ReaderSettingsWatch` to stop reading settings once it is stopped, so that a reader that left the screen never applies a late change and the test stops flaking.

### Report
`ReaderSettingsWatchTests.aStartedWatchReadsNowThenFollowsEveryChangeUntilStopped` failed once on an iPad simulator under heavy load (PR #834 round 2, mac/M1, 2026-11-11). It passed 2/2 when rerun alone. The assertion after `stop()` failed: the watch read a settings change after it was stopped. `ReaderSettingsWatch` came with #786 (#750).

### Hypothesis to check, not assume
`stop()` cancels the task, but a change notification already delivered, or a hop to the main actor already queued, still runs the read. The fix is to check cancellation (or a stopped flag) before applying, and to cover the class: every `ReaderSettingsWatch` path.
