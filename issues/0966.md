### Summary

As a member with iCloud sync on, I want my recent rehost uploads to follow me to my other Apple devices, so that the composer's image picker shows them everywhere.

### Operator decision (2026-11-16)

- **Option A:** the rehost upload history syncs through the existing **iCloud key-value store** when iCloud sync is on. It also syncs **when sync is switched on**: the local history is pushed at activation.
- Only the **100 most recent** images are synced; the full history stays on the device.
- Sync off: nothing changes, device-only as today.
- This changes ADR 0013 rules 2 and 3 ("upload histories do not leave the device"). It needs an **ADR 0013 amendment** in the same PR, merged by the core orchestrator.

### Scope

- The store: one KVS key `{version, items:[{id, fullURL, mediumURL?, previewURL?, miniURL?, addedAt}]}`, capped at 100 newest, about 30 KiB. Check the appendix budget (< 200 KiB total).
- **Merge by id** (union of both devices' adds, newest first, cap 100). Do not use last-writer-wins on the whole list: adds made on two devices must both survive. Deletions, if the picker allows them: tombstones, or document the limit.
- **Activation:** when the member turns iCloud sync on, merge the local history into the cloud one.
- The Apple-ID change, quarantine and initial-sync rules of ADR 0013 (Amendments 1 and 2) apply as for the other lists.
- Tests: merge (two devices), cap, activation push, account change, and an older build ignoring the new key.

### Not in scope

- The update path from the current app: LOT-067 already imports `Documents/rehostImagesSwiftUI.json` at first launch. An end-to-end TestFlight check is not planned (operator, 2026-11-16).
- The old `rehostImages.plist` import: see its own issue.
