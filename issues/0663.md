# Issue #663: [Legacy 4.0.x] iPad / iPhone Duo split view: the right column stays on a pushed topic and ignores list selections

### Summary
On iPad in landscape (and now on the iPhone Duo inner display), in the split view with the topic list on the left and the topic on the right, the right column can get stuck on one topic. After that, tapping other topics on the left does not change the right column, which keeps the last topic that worked and stays usable. Each tapped row is still shown as read in the list.

This is the **legacy** app (`HFRswift`, 4.0.x). The SwiftHFR side is tracked in #512, which holds the first user video and the frame-by-frame steps of the second one.

### Report
- Store version **4.0.2**. Code is the same in **4.0.3 build 37** (tag `4.0.3` = local `master` `20a4b2f7`) and in `origin/master` `21173e77`.
- iPad Pro M5, iPadOS 26.7, landscape. Also seen on the iPhone Duo inner display (4.0.3 uses the same split root there, `RootTabView.usesSidebarRoot`).
- User's words: "j'ai déjà un thread affiché à droite (mode paysage sur iPad) et cliquer sur des threads à gauche ne rafraîchit pas le côté droit, cela ne fait rien et laisse affiché le dernier thread qui a fonctionné. Mais le fait de sélectionner des threads à gauche les comptabilise comme vus quand même."
- Videos: https://youtu.be/Iu3Zr8Qon_Q and https://youtu.be/Rcoag6OICHE (steps written in #512).

### Steps seen in the second video (from #512)
1. Catégories → a forum → long-press a topic → **Première page**: the topic opens on the right.
2. Tap a **link inside a post** to another page of the same topic: a second `MessagesView` is **pushed** in the right column (back chevron).
3. From that pushed reader, **edit** one of your own posts and **send**. The composer closes, the pushed reader reloads.
4. Tap other topics on the left, about one per second: rows highlight and turn read, **the right column stays on the pushed reader**.

The video does not show whether step 2 alone, or step 2 + step 3, is needed.

### Analysis (static, `4.0.3`, nothing changed)

**The selection path does run.** `TopicListRowView.openNavigationTarget` (`HFRswift/Swift/Common.swift:3460-3469`) calls `onSelectTarget(target)` then `onDidOpen(url)`.
- `onSelectTarget` → `RootTabView.selectTopic(_:for:)` (`MainWindow.swift:2142-2165`) sets the per-destination target (e.g. `favoritesTopicTarget`) and regenerates `detailNavigationResetToken`.
- `onDidOpen` is the list's own closure (e.g. `Favorites.swift:1179-1185`, `MainWindow.swift:729`, `MPListView.swift:32`): it sets `topic.isViewed = true` and `isLocallyViewedInApp = true` **locally, synchronously, on tap**.
- So "counted as read" does **not** prove the page was fetched. It only proves the tap reached `openNavigationTarget`. The right column is the part that does not follow.

**How the right column is supposed to follow.** `selectedIPadDetail` (`MainWindow.swift:1941-1993`) is a `NavigationStack { MessagesView(target) }` with `.id(detailNavigationResetToken)`. Replacing the token is the **only** thing that clears the stack: there is no `NavigationStack(path:)`, the path is implicit.

**What can survive the token change.** Pushes inside the right column are implicit too:
- `MessagesView.openInternalTopic` (`MessagesView.swift:3949-3961`) stores an `AnyView(MessagesView(…, navigationDepth + 1))` in `@State linkedTopicDestination` and sets `navigateToLinkedTopic = true`.
- It is presented by `.navigationDestination(isPresented: linkedTopicNavigationBinding)` (`:5929`), the same for search results (`:5936`).
- That `@State` belongs to the root `MessagesView`. When the token changes, SwiftUI drops the root `MessagesView` and its `navigationDestination` registration, and must also pop the pushed view controller from the column's `UINavigationController`. If that pop is lost, the pushed reader stays on top, and every later selection only replaces the root **under** it. That matches the video: stuck on the pushed reader, still usable, list rows still marked read.

**Why the edit may matter.** The composer is not a SwiftUI `fullScreenCover`. `coverVerticalFullScreen` (`MessagesView.swift:5358`, presenter `:5960-6045`) adds a `UIViewControllerRepresentable` anchor inside the pushed reader and calls `present(_:animated:)` with `.fullScreen` from UIKit.
- With `.fullScreen`, UIKit removes the presenting hierarchy (the whole split view) from the window while the composer is up.
- Sending reloads the pushed reader while it is off-window. SwiftUI's view of the detail stack and the real `UINavigationController` stack may diverge there; afterwards the `.id` reset no longer pops the pushed controller.
- SwiftHFR uses the same `.id` on a `NavigationStack` and does **not** reproduce with a push + composer opened and cancelled (#512). The difference that is left: a **sent** edit, and the UIKit full-screen presenter. This is a lead, not proven.

**4.0.3 specifics.** The Duo work (`FoldHandoffStore`, `prefersDetailOnlyTopic`) does not change this path. The Duo inner display is affected only because it now uses the same split root.

### Checks to run (Mac / device)

### Fix directions (to decide after the checks)
- **A, structural (recommended):** give the right column an explicit path owned by `RootTabView`: `NavigationStack(path: $detailPath)` with a `Hashable` route (`TopicNavigationTarget` / page) and `navigationDestination(for:)`. `selectTopic` sets the root and empties the path; `openInternalTopic` and the search-result push append to the path (through an environment action) instead of `navigationDestination(isPresented:)` + `AnyView`. The reset no longer depends on `.id` popping a controller.
- **B, if check 2 points at the composer:** present `AnswerView` with SwiftUI `.fullScreenCover` (its default transition is already cover vertical), or present from a context that does not remove the split view from the window.
- **C, safety net, small:** in `selectTopic`, clear any pushed state before the token change (e.g. a reset callback down to the root `MessagesView` that sets `navigateToLinkedTopic = false` / `navigateToSearchResult = false`). Does not help if UIKit and SwiftUI have already diverged.

## Reproduced on the simulator (2026-11-08)

**Build:** working copy at tag `4.0.3` (`HFRswift` scheme, Debug, unchanged code). **Device:** iPad Pro 13-inch (M5) simulator, portrait (the 13-inch keeps the 3-column split in portrait). Logged-in account.

### Minimum repro: a push is enough, no edit needed
1. Catégories → Jeux Video → tap « [TU] Star Citizen » (opens page 1 on the right).
2. In the first post, tap an internal link to the forum (`forum2.php … #t11730312`): a second reader is **pushed** in the right column (back chevron, page 345/1611).
3. Tap « [TU] World of Warcraft » on the left: the row is selected, Star Citizen turns grey (read), **the right column stays on Star Citizen page 345**.
4. Tap « Topic des ventes/Achats/Echanges… »: same, stuck; WoW turns grey too.

So step 3 of the video (edit sent from the pushed reader) is **not needed**. The composer lead (fix B) is dropped.

### Discriminating check: confirmed
5. Tap the back chevron of the pushed reader: the right column shows **« Topic des ventes… » page 1/67**, the last topic tapped. The root `MessagesView` had been replaced on every selection, under the pushed reader that survived.
6. With the stack empty again, tap « [Vds] assassin's creed… »: it opens on the right normally.

### Conclusion
- The `.id(detailNavigationResetToken)` reset replaces the root of the right column but does **not** pop a reader pushed by `navigationDestination(isPresented:)` from `MessagesView` (`MessagesView.swift:5929`). The pushed one stays on top until the user goes back.
- It is deterministic: one push in the right column is enough, every time.
- The list marks rows read on tap (`onDidOpen`), which is why the user sees « vus » while the right side does not change.
- Fix **A** (explicit `NavigationStack(path:)` owned by `RootTabView`, emptied by `selectTopic`, links and search results appended to it) is the direct fix. **C** (resetting `navigateToLinkedTopic` / `navigateToSearchResult` before changing the token) is the small alternative; to check on the simulator with the same steps.
- Not checked here: the in-topic search result push (same mechanism, `:5936`), landscape, the Duo inner display, a real device.
