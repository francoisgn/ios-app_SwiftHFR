### Summary

As a reader with low vision, I want the pull-to-refresh hint at the end of a page to be legible, at least when Increase Contrast is on, because it is the only visible cue for that gesture.

### Rule

"Strive to meet color contrast minimum standards." (table: text up to 17 pt, 4.5:1) and "If your app doesn't provide this minimum contrast by default, ensure it at least provides a higher contrast color scheme when the system setting Increase Contrast is turned on." — [HIG › Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility) (Vision; page updated 2025-06-09).

### What the app does (dev-swift @ `7a61018f`)

`App/SwiftHFR/Reader/Resources/reader.css:207`: `.pull-hint { … font-size: 0.75em; … color: var(--secondary); opacity: 0.75; }`. With `--secondary: #6c6c70` (`:6`) at 75 % on `#ffffff`, the blend computes to about 3.15:1 (about 2.96:1 on an even message's tinted surface); dark computes to about 4.8:1. The `prefers-contrast: more` block does not touch the hint. Computed from the CSS values, not measured. "Almost tone on tone" is an operator choice (#932, cited in the CSS comment).

### Legacy at `87fe0d1c`

**(a) defect** for the Increase Contrast case (accessibility, SwiftHFR-only); the default look is the operator's choice of #932.

- At least `opacity: 1` under `@media (prefers-contrast: more)` (plain `--secondary` is about 5.2:1 in light); the default stays as decided, or drops the opacity too.
