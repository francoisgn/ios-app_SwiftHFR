# Issue #1304: [SwiftHFR] Reader text floor: nested sub/sup and sub in a quote header grow at 100 % (#1295 follow-up)

### Summary
A sub or sup nested inside another sub/sup, or inside a quote header (`b.s1`), should keep its dev-swift size at 100 %, so that the reader text floor (#1237, PR #1295) changes nothing at 100 % and above.

### Scope
- `reader.css:218` (after #1295). Sub/sup and `b.s1` change the font size without setting `--context-100`. Results at 100 %:
  - `x<sup>2<sup>n</sup></sup>` in message text gives 11 px instead of 10.42 (15 / 1.44);
  - a sub inside a quote header gives 11 px instead of 10.63.
- Fix: `:is(sub, sup) :is(sub, sup) { font-size: max(calc(1em / 1.2), min(calc(var(--context-100, 1rem) / 1.44), var(--floor))); }`, plus a `--context-100` on `b.s1`. Follow the operator's rule on #1237: never smaller and never bigger than the 100 % size, with the floor at min(100 %, 11 px).

#1295
