### Time Profiler: hot paths
Two traces: the slow T3 cold open on the current build, and a T2 cold open on the pre-merge build. Both were taken with xctrace over all processes on the simulator. Tracing doubles the times (the traced T3 cold open took 51 s), so the traces are only used for where the time goes.
- **SwiftHFR main thread** (45 s window): 5.3 s of CPU in total.
  - **4.1 s** of it is XCUITest's own accessibility snapshots (`XCTElementSnapshotRequest`…`_accessibilityUserTestingSnapshot…`), i.e. the measurement harness.
  - The app's own main-thread work is about **1.1 s**, mostly SwiftUI graph updates (`AG::Graph::UpdateStack::update`, about 470 ms) and Core Animation commits (`CA::Transaction::flush`, about 350 ms).
  - The page parse runs **off the main thread**: `LiveTopicPageSource.parse` takes 97 ms on a worker thread.
  - Potential-hangs reported one "Severe Hang" of 4.9 s on the main thread, starting at about the moment of `appear`. It sits in the accessibility snapshot and run-loop work above, at the same time as heavy host paging.
- **WebContent process, main thread:** 5.0 s of CPU.
  - **4.2 s** is process launch: dyld's `JustInTimeLoader::loadDependents` / `mach_o::Header::forEachLoadCommand`, slow under memory pressure.
  - **3.8 s** is `WebProcess::unblockServicesRequiredByAccessibility`, which is WebKit turning accessibility on because XCUITest queries the tree.

  Both overlap the `show loads`→`ready accepted` gap. On a device without XCUITest, the second one does not happen.
- Nothing in SwiftHFR spins while stuck. In the stuck trace, the main thread uses 1.9 s of CPU over 45 s, mostly the harness again. The stuck state is a lifecycle state, not a busy loop.
- The **pre-merge** trace (a T2 cold open: 9.2 s to the first message while traced; `appear` 1.2 s, `load finish` 3.2 s, `ready accepted` 6.9 s after the tap) is of limited use. Its SwiftHFR frames came out unsymbolicated, and three other SwiftHFR processes from other agents' simulators ran in the same window. It does show the same host picture: the kernel used 97 s of CPU in 45 s. Nothing in it points to a cost that #705, #589 or #700 added.

### Cause hypotheses of #750: what the data supports
| hypothesis | verdict | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Request timing: redirects, two-hop redirect, `numreponse` | 🟠 minor | 1–2 × 301, a fresh TLS connection per hop: 0.5–1.8 s per fetch on this network. Worth fixing (reuse the connection, or ask for the canonical URL), but it is not what makes a load take 10 to 20 s here |
| Parsing on the main actor (LESSONS 4, 49) | 🟢 not supported | the parse takes 97 ms off the main thread; the app's main-thread work is about 1 s per 45 s |
| `WKWebView` load and the content-world script | 🟠 the largest share of a slow cold open, not settled | `show loads`→`ready accepted` takes 2.2–12.9 s (current) and 4.5–23 s (pre-merge).
