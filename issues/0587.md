# Issue #587: [SwiftHFR] Reader: refresh after pinch zoom lands several messages too far down

- State: open
- Author: francoisgn · created 2026-11-07T13:24:20Z · updated 2026-11-09T15:04:58Z
- Labels: type: bug, priority: medium, difficulty: easy, area: app, status: ready, host: mac, origin: swift-feedback
- Source: https://github.com/ezzz/HFRnow/issues/587

### Summary
As a reader, I want a refresh after a pinch zoom to reopen near the message I was reading, so that zooming does not make me lose my place.

### Found by
Mac check of LOT-186 (#552) on dev-swift dc38ffb9, iPhone 18 Pro simulator (iOS 27.0), logged out, public topic (page 1).

### Steps
1. Open a topic, scroll 3 screens down.
2. Pinch out (scale about 1.8). The same message stays at the top.
3. Tap Actualiser, wait 8 s.

### Expected
The page reopens at the same message (or very near).

### Actual
It reopens several messages further down (about 1 h 45 of topic time later). Without zoom the same refresh lands exactly (0 pt shift, 5 samples over 14 s, plus a second refresh). Likely cause: the reading top is saved in zoomed viewport points and restored with window.scrollTo in layout points, so the zoom factor multiplies the offset (ReaderWebController+Scroll.swift, reader.js unchanged).

### Suggested direction (not a fix)
Restore through a message: the topmost post plus its distance to the bar's edge, landed with landOnPost (as noted on #552).
