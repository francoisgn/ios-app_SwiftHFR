### Summary

As a user who switches apps during the Noël packing (27/12, Noël abusé over Legacy) while the reply composer or a sheet is up, I want the composer to keep its state when I come back, so that the once-a-year packing never resets what I was doing.

Follow-up of #1022 (LOT-166 PR 4, issue #256), from Fable's round-3 review (APPROVE, findings 1 and 3, not blocking).

### Scope

1. **`NoelPackingModel.sceneChanged(_:active: false)`** drops a **held** packing at once (`current = nil`). Scenario: elves cut past their first second by the reply composer (held: the theme stays drawn so no screen is rebuilt while input is up); the user switches app; `scenePhase` leaves `.active`, `drawnTheme` drops, `screenFill` flips `.theme` → `.system`, and the wrapped composer gets a new identity: its `@State` (image upload, selection, focus) resets; the text survives in `ReplyComposerModel`. Fix: on a phase change keep a held packing (the layer's existing 0.5 s poll drops it once its scene asks with a clear screen); drop at once only in `release(scene:)` (the scene is gone). Test: held, scene leaves → still drawn; scene active with the sheet still up → still drawn; clear → dropped.
2. **`SeasonalThemeModel.sceneDidBecomeActive`** drops a non-playing packing outside the packing days (`!packing.isPlaying`), which now includes a held one (two iPad windows across midnight 02/01 → 03/01). Either guard on `current?.scene == nil` (waiting only) or say « held ones too » in `docs/lots/LOT-166-seasonal-noel.md`.

Not here: the rebuild at the natural end of the elves, or after an inline keyboard closes (accepted in #1022, listed for the device review on #893).

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A held packing survives its scene leaving `.active` and is dropped only on a clear screen in that scene or by `release(scene:)`; tests in `NoelPackingTests`, shown to fail without the change.
- [ ] The behaviour outside the packing days matches the spec text.
- [ ] LOT-166 spec updated.
