### Summary
As a member scanning my favourites and flags, I want to see at a glance what is unread and how far behind I am. Legacy shows this with a capsule on the right holding the number of pages between my flag and the end of the topic, and with accented favourite rows. The Swift rows are less readable. Operator report after a device test, 2026-11-07. Milestone M4.

**Operator decision:** this overrides the GAP-026 "UX change (b)" and the GAP-027 "not planned" choices. The legacy reading cues come back.

### Fix
1. **Unread-pages capsule on the right.**
   - Value: `pageCount - currentPage` when the row has a flag and the value is above 0, otherwise hidden, as legacy does.
   - Style: the accent fill with white bold caption, clearly visible.
   - Accessibility: "N pages non lues".
   - Keep the dot for unread rows without a flag page.
2. **Footer "⚑ cur / max"** (with the poll mark) when the flag page is known, before the existing summary or in its place for flagged rows. Keep the relative date. Use the legacy wording.
3. **Row accent for favourites and flags.** Use the shared `RowTint` helper from #570 (PR #576) with the flag colour at low strength (yellow favourite, blue, red), and keep the super-favourite orange. Same light/dark/Increase Contrast rules as #570. It must not hide the iPad selection.
4. Update GAP-026 and GAP-027 (now ported), the pointer comment and a CHANGELOG-LEGACY row.
5. **Tests:**
   - capsule value and visibility (no flag; flag on the last page → hidden; 3 pages behind → "3");
   - footer text;
   - tint choice per flag.
