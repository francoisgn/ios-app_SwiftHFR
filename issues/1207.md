### Summary

As a reader, I want to refresh a topic list or a topic by pulling the top of the page down, **or the bottom of the page up**, with a refresh icon that grows as I pull, a haptic tap, and the refresh firing only once the icon reaches its full size, so that a refresh is deliberate and works from both ends of the page. (Operator feedback, 2026-11-18, with screenshots of a messaging app where pulling past the end of a conversation grows a round arrow icon until it triggers.)

### Today (dev-swift)

- **Topic lists:** the top pull uses the system `.refreshable` (`App/SwiftHFR/Navigation/TopicListView.swift:120`, also `MessageListView.swift:77`, `ForumListView.swift:42`, `BookmarksView.swift:57`, `QualityAlertsView.swift:65`, `OfflineTopicsView.swift:65`). There is no bottom pull.
- **Reader (topic and MP):** the page ends with a pull-to-refresh hint (#820, #932: visible at rest), `TopicHTMLBuilder.swift:40-42,101` and `ReaderPageEnd.swift`. Check, and state on the PR, what the top and bottom pulls do today in the `WKWebView`.
- Legacy @ 87fe0d1c: cite what legacy does at the top and bottom of a list and of the reader, if anything (file:line). Never guess.

### Scope

1. **Both ends**: a pull down at the top and a pull up at the bottom refresh, in the topic lists (Favoris, Drapeaux, categories, MP lists) and in the topic and MP reader.
2. **Progressive, deliberate trigger**:
   - The pull reveals a refresh icon that grows with the pull distance.
   - When it reaches full size: one haptic (`sensoryFeedback`, `.impact`), then the refresh fires.
   - Releasing before that cancels, with no refresh and no haptic.
   - The threshold comes from a named constant and is shown in the PR.
3. **Apple behaviour**: the system `.refreshable` (`UIRefreshControl`) only exists at the top, with a spinner. The bottom pull and the growing icon are a custom interaction asked for explicitly by the operator. Build it on scroll geometry (`onScrollGeometryChange`) and the system's own symbol `arrow.clockwise`, with no custom physics. Keep VoiceOver able to refresh through an accessibility action. With Reduce Motion, the icon appears without the growth animation.
4. While refreshing, the icon shows progress (spinner or the symbol's rotate effect) and disappears when done. On failure, the existing error banner shows.
5. The reader keeps its reading position after a refresh triggered from the bottom: no jump to the top. New messages appear below.

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A pure test on the trigger rule: pull distance → icon scale; a release below the threshold does nothing; reaching it fires exactly once per pull.
- [ ] Hosted tests: the top and bottom pulls refresh a topic list. The reader refresh from the bottom keeps the position.
- [ ] Device check on the next general review (#893): feel of the threshold, haptic, both ends, light and dark, Reduce Motion.
