### Summary
As a reader, I want the "page enregistrée hors ligne" banner to read clearly over the messages, so that the confirmation is not lost in the page text.

### Found by
Mac check of LOT-186 (#552) on dev-swift dc38ffb9, iPhone 18 Pro simulator (iOS 27.0), light mode, logged out, public topic.

### Steps
1. Open a topic, scroll down a little.
2. Top bar, overflow "Plus", "Enregistrer cette page hors ligne".

### Expected
The banner (OfflineSaveBanner, a LOT-185 safeAreaBar) has a glass or fill background like the other chrome.

### Actual
"1 page enregistrée hors ligne" and its close button are bare text under the navigation bar with no fill: the first lines of the message underneath ghost through. Readable, but it is the "bare text over the page" case that the follow-up note on #552 asked to turn into glass. The page does not move when it appears (0 pt shift).

### Suggested direction (not a fix)
glassEffect(.regular, in: .capsule) or a bar-like background, as the toast has.
