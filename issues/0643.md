# Issue #643: [SwiftHFR] MP reader (iPhone): the second conversation opened stays on "Chargement des messages"

- State: open
- Author: francoisgn · created 2026-11-07T23:02:09Z · updated 2026-11-09T15:06:55Z
- Labels: type: bug, priority: high, difficulty: medium, area: app, status: needs-triage, host: mac, beta-test finding, origin: swift-feedback
- Source: https://github.com/ezzz/HFRnow/issues/643

### Summary
As a member reading private messages on iPhone, I want every conversation I open to load, so that I can read more than one conversation without relaunching the app.

Refs #578 (found during its Mac check; the stall is older than #578, see below).

### Observed
iPhone 18 Pro simulator, iOS 27.0, compact width, logged in. Messages tab:
1. Open a read one-to-one conversation: it loads.
2. Back to the list, open a second read one-to-one conversation: the reader stays on "Chargement des messages" (waited 45 to 60 s; 7 waits out of 7 over 5 runs, three different conversations).
3. Actualiser does not recover it. Back, then the same row again: still stuck.

Reader log (`subsystem:SwiftHFR category:reader`) of the second conversation: the reader appears and disappears in the same millisecond, which cancels its load, and it never appears again:
```
appear model=B viewer=2 page=false loading=false error=false
load start model=B generation=1 page=1
disappear model=B viewer=2 current=true loading=true
load cancelled model=B generation=1
load finish model=B generation=1 current=false success=false
```
After Actualiser the page loads (`load finish … generation=3 current=true success=true`), but no `show loads` follows: the page never reaches the web view, and the pager shows "1 / 1" over the spinner.

Any of these conversations opened first in a fresh launch loads in 6 to 19 s. On iPad (regular width, split view) three conversations in a row all load.

### Not caused by #578
The same steps on 7880ba5d (the parent of #578's merge) give the same appear/disappear/cancel sequence on the second conversation. It looks like the compact `NavigationSplitView` in `MessagesRootView` / `MessageReaderColumn` (`.id(thread.id)`, `preferredCompactColumn`) rebuilding the detail when the selection changes, close to the #566 shape (topic reader) but on the MP path.

### Acceptance
- On a compact width, opening a second (and third) conversation after Back loads it.
- A reader stopped by a disappear before its first show loads again when it reappears, or when Actualiser is pressed.
- A UI or view-model test covers "open A, back, open B".

Same signature from a topic, found in the Mac check of #566 (after #575), on `dev-swift` @ f9901d9b. The iPhone 18 Pro simulator (iOS 27.0) ran a debug build, logged in.

**Steps:** Catégories → Apple → Tous les sujets → tap the topic. About 0.3 s later, while the push still runs, tap Back, then tap the same row again.

**Result:** stuck in **10 of 10** runs (5 with images "always", 5 with "never"). The spinner was still up after 90 s, and "Actualiser" had not recovered it 30 s later. On iPad, switching to Favoris and back during the load never sticks (10 of 10 ok): there the detail column empties instead.

The Mac was heavily loaded (load average above 500), so the timings prove nothing. The trace below shows the cause, and it does not depend on timing.

```
55:18.483 show loads token=1
55:18.726 disappear model=16035 viewer=1 current=true loading=false
55:18.727 stopLoading token=1 ready=false
55:19.636 show loads token=1
55:19.638 appear model=16035 viewer=2 page=true loading=false error=false
55:19.638 disappear model=16035 viewer=2 current=true loading=false
55:19.638 stopLoading token=1 ready=false
55:19.642 show skipped token=1: stopped
56:52.112 load start model=16035 generation=4 page=1      <- Actualiser
56:53.426 load finish model=16035 generation=4 current=true success=true
56:53.441 show skipped token=2: stopped
```
- In some runs viewer 2's disappear also cancels its load (`disappear … viewer=2 current=true loading=true`, then `load cancelled`). This is the sequence in this issue.
- The `show skipped …: stopped` line appears 21 times over the 10 runs.
- Code (f9901d9b): `ReaderWebController.stopLoading()` sets `stopped = true`, and only `resume()`, called from `onAppear`, clears it. When the visible reader's last lifecycle call is an `onDisappear`, every later `show()` returns early, including the one after `refresh()`. So the #575 rule "while stopped, `show()` does nothing" has no way out while the view is still on screen.
- Acceptance addition: on a compact width, open a topic, go Back at once and open it again, 5 times with images "always" and "never". The page always shows, and Actualiser always recovers it.
