### Summary
Follow-up of #714 part 3 (PR #790): three non-blocking findings of Fable's round-2 review of the MP avatar prefetch, so it never repeats useless requests and never stores the wrong member's avatar.

### Scope
1. **Permanent failures retried every 60 s.** `MemberAvatarPrefetcher` treats every failure as transient: a profile answering 404 or served at another URL (deleted or banned member, not captured, #115), or an avatar `AvatarStore` never keeps (over 256 KiB, not decodable), costs two GETs each time the row's task runs again after `liveRetryDelay`, all session. Cap the retries per pseudo or back off. A 4xx on the profile could map to `.none`, but only once a capture shows it (#115).
2. **No scheduled retry.** A list opened offline gets its avatars only when the row's `.task` runs again (the row reappears or another avatar is stored); rows left on screen keep the monogram. Either re-ask at `retryAt` while the row is shown, or correct the doc comment (`MemberAvatarPrefetcher.swift`, "recovers").
3. **Non-ASCII pseudo (pending capture, `host: mac` part).** `HFREndpoint.profileRedirect(pseudo:)` sends UTF-8 percent-encoding, which is not captured (HFR-PROTOCOL §6c "#714"). If HFR reads Latin-1, the lookup could name another member, whose avatar would be stored under this pseudo. Capture `profilebdd.php?config=hfr.inc&pseudo=<pseudo with é>` (UTF-8 and Latin-1 encodings), or skip non-ASCII pseudos until it is captured.

### Sources
- PR #790 review comments (Fable, round 2).
- `App/SwiftHFR/Messages/MemberAvatarPrefetcher.swift`, `Packages/HFRKit/Sources/HFRNetwork/Endpoints/HFREndpoint+Lookup.swift`.

### Acceptance criteria
- A pseudo whose lookup fails permanently is not looked up more than a bounded number of times per session (test).
- The behaviour of rows left on screen after a failure is either implemented or documented truthfully.
- The non-ASCII encoding is captured, or non-ASCII pseudos are skipped (test).
