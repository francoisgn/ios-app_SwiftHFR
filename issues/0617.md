### Summary
As a member using Increase Contrast in dark mode, I want the "non lu" capsule of the MP list to stay readable, so that the setting does not make it harder to read than the default.

Refs #576 (found in its Mac check).

### Observed
Build 4b1e77f5, iPhone 18 Pro and iPad Pro 13-inch (M5) simulators, iOS 27.0. `MessageRowView`: white `caption2` on `Color(.systemGray)`.

| Setting | Capsule fill, measured | White text contrast |
|---|---|---|
| Light or dark | 142,142,147 | 3.3:1 |
| Increase Contrast, light | 108,108,112 | 5.3:1 |
| Increase Contrast, dark | 174,174,178 | **2.2:1** |

`systemGray` gets lighter under Increase Contrast in dark while the text stays white, so the setting lowers the contrast. Seen on fixture rows and on a real list.

At half strength (the member has read the thread, legacy `MPListView.swift:435` @ 2440d79) the capsule is pale in every mode: about 1.7:1 in light. That half strength is legacy parity; say whether it stays.

### Acceptance criteria
- With Increase Contrast in dark, the full-strength capsule is at least as readable as in default dark.
- Decision recorded on the half-strength capsule (keep legacy, or raise it).
