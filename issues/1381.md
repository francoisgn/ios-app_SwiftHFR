## Art direction: proposal for the operator (draft PR #1384)

![The seven frames with placeholders](https://github.com/ezzz/HFRnow/blob/67778a120e0b92315bc1dd087fd634d873a54e22/docs/assets/appstore/art-direction.jpg?raw=true)

**Which language: B's (`:o` poster), not A's (the lens).** An App Store page is a row of small portrait cards: flat inks and a two-line headline stay readable at that size, a perspective scene does not, and the screenshot already is « the app on a phone ». It also ties the listing to the social preview.

- **Background**: three flat inks in turn, HFR orange, paper, ink.
- **Caption**: French, two short lines, Inter 900, the second line in the accent colour; a monospace kicker (`01/07 · CATÉGORIES`) and the `:o` signature above a rule.
- **Screenshot**: the whole capture, never cropped, in a plain dark slab (no island, button, lens or device name).
- **Template**: `docs/assets/appstore/source/frame.html` + `screens.json` + `render.js`. The hatched slots are placeholders: no fake app screen is drawn.

**Sizes**, from Apple's « Screenshot specifications » (<https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/screenshot-specifications/>, read 2026-11-27): iPhone 1320 × 2868 and 1206 × 2622 (the page's required iPhone row is the medium Dynamic Island display); iPad 13" 2064 × 2752, « Required if app runs on iPad »; iPhone Duo 1398 × 2034 and 2007 × 2853, required from April 2027 for apps on the iOS 27.1 SDK. **Question for the operator: does the listing ship iPad, and do we provide the Duo set now?**

**For the Mac session**: one PNG per screen and size in `docs/assets/appstore/source/captures/<size id>/<capture>.png` (names in `screens.json`; rules in `docs/assets/appstore/README.md`).
