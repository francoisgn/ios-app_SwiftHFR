### Summary

As a user opening the app when a season starts, I want to learn about the seasonal theme without a blocking alert whose title tells me nothing, so that the app does not interrupt me at launch for an information.

### Rule

"Avoid using an alert merely to provide information. People don't appreciate an interruption from an alert that's informative, but not actionable.", "Avoid showing an alert when your app starts. If you need to inform people about new or important information the moment they open your app, design a way to make the information easily discoverable." and "Write a title that clearly and succinctly describes the situation." — [HIG › Alerts](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/alerts) (Best practices, Content; page updated 2024-02-02).
