## ezzz · 2026-11-08T07:26:06Z

### Operator request (2026-11-07): show the members when reading a group MP

Submitted again as a new idea, and folded here rather than opened as a duplicate: **when I open an MP with several recipients, I want a way to see the list of its members**, from the thread itself, not only from the MP list.

What the captures already settle for this lot (`docs/HFR-PROTOCOL.md` §6c, lines 508-509; `docs/lots/LOT-058-mp-actions.md` lines 44-47):

- **Owner**: the roster is in the reply form's `newdest` (`R123c`), already read by `PostingForm.groupMembers` (LOT-058). It does not include the owner.
- **Member (not owner)**: neither the thread pages (`R123b`, `R123d`) nor the member's reply form (`R123e`) carry the roster. The only source is the MP list row: `<span title="owner, member,member">Interlocuteurs multiples</span>`, read as `PrivateMessageThread.interlocutors` (LOT-023), owner first (`R123a`, `R123f`).
- So the reader of a group MP should take the roster from the list row it was opened from (both roles), and may refresh it from `newdest` for the owner. A thread opened from a link or a notification, with no list row at hand, needs the MP list to be loaded (or the entry hidden), never a guessed roster.
- The `[non lu]` tooltip of the list row (`title="Ce message n'a pas été lu par : a, b"`, lower-cased names) could also mark which members have not opened the thread yet.
- A two-person thread (`R124`, after a removal) has no roster: no entry then.

UX ideas for the spec's "UX evolutions" section (still an optional evolution, legacy has no roster):

- A "Membres (n)" entry in the reader's toolbar or menu of a group MP, opening a sheet with the list (owner marked, tap on a pseudo → profile / new MP, per #595).
- For the owner, the same sheet is the natural place for the roster edits the engine already supports (add / remove through `newdest`, `W74`-`W77`), with the "Modération" system posts as confirmation.
