### Summary

As a SwiftHFR user on iOS 27, including iPhone Duo, I want the app's bars, banners and buttons to follow Liquid Glass and the system bar layout, as the legacy HFR+ app already does, so that the port neither looks older than the app it replaces nor wastes the Duo cover screen.

Executive summary: about 30 small modifier changes over about 17 files, plus one real reader change. The system already handles navigation bars, tab bars and sheets. Most of this is **M4 parity** (legacy ships glass) and #432 work; the Duo layout APIs (iOS 27.1+) go to M5.

_Read-only study by `ext/francoisgn-suborch-1` on `dev-swift` @ 85806dd. The HIG was read live on 2026-11-07 (sources at the end). This issue is for triage: nothing is claimed or planned yet._

### Risks

**Parity versus the HIG.** Each of these needs a recorded decision, not a silent change:
- the legacy app puts glass on cards in the content layer, which the HIG advises against;
- UXD-02 (#424) contradicts "Make sure the tab bar is visible…";
- #428's "…" menu collides with the Duo rule that reserves the ellipsis for overflow;
- the in-app appearance setting (GAP-142) contradicts the Dark Mode guidance.

About 3 users already criticised Liquid Glass (`docs/research/user-feedback-p450-510.md:181`).

**Accessibility:**
- Contrast must reach 4.5:1 under glass.
- The accent colour (`ThemeAppearance.swift:28-30`) has no increased-contrast variant.
- Dynamic Type XL can overflow a 5-item bottom bar.
- This links to LOT-163 (#253).

**Reader contrast:** the tinted message headers (`reader.css:58-59,113-121`) scroll under the bars. Check them at dark-mode brightness from 0 to 150 %.

**Scroll restore:** content under the bars shifts `contentOffset` (`ReaderWebController.swift:111,242`) and the LOT-111 positions by the inset, so a page can reopen offset.

**Duo:**
- custom strips stay horizontal;
- the idiom-based root and orientation checks can misbehave on the inner display;
- the 27.1 APIs need `#available`.

**Themes:**
- LOT-122's OLED black makes bar edges vanish under clear glass;
- a light accent on `.glassProminent` can fail contrast.
