### Summary
As a reader holding the iPhone in landscape with compact density, I want the section headers to line up with their rows, so that the narrower margins of #799 look right in every orientation.

### Facts
Follow-up of #804, from the round-3 review (Opus, comment on #804), which was not blocking.
- `ListDensity.swift:117` computes the card edge as `densityCardEdge = outerMargin + safeLeading`.
- In landscape with a 62 pt side safe area (measured through `additionalSafeAreaInsets`), the card starts at x = 62, not 72: the safe area absorbs the 10 pt margin.
- Compact headers then sit 17 pt from the card while rows sit at 7, in RTL too.
- Regular density is unaffected.

## francoisgn · 2026-11-18T00:39:42Z

**mac/M1 (sub-agent mac/M1-i832-sonnet): draft #1086, head e60ba7b8. #1048 did not fix the landscape case.**

- **Measured** in a hosted probe: a list as the window root, 932 × 430 and 874 × 402, landscape safe area 62 pt, compact density. Header text from the card edge: **92 pt** on 74d863c1 (69 before #1048); row text 59 pt; expected 7 and 7. With the notch on the right only, it was already 7/7.
- **Cause 1:** `contentMargins(.horizontal, 10, .scrollContent)` replaces the system side inset, safe area included, so the card sat at x = 10, under the notch.
- **Cause 2:** the header's card edge counted the inset twice: `originX + 10 + safeLeading`, while the list frame already starts at 62.
- **Fix** (`DensityListMargins`, `ListDensity.swift`): the margins add the measured leading and trailing safe insets, and the card edge is the frame x plus the margin. `DensityHeaderMargin`, its cap and regular density are unchanged.
- **Test:** `compactHeadersAlignInLandscape` (5 cases: both sizes; notch left, right, both) fails without the fix (6 issues) and passes with it. The whole `SwiftHFRTests` target passes (1,917).
