### Summary
As a reader holding the iPhone in landscape with compact density, I want the section headers to line up with their rows, so that the narrower margins of #799 look right in every orientation.

### Facts
Follow-up of #804, from the round-3 review (Opus, comment on #804), which was not blocking.
- `ListDensity.swift:117` computes the card edge as `densityCardEdge = outerMargin + safeLeading`.
- In landscape with a 62 pt side safe area (measured through `additionalSafeAreaInsets`), the card starts at x = 62, not 72: the safe area absorbs the 10 pt margin.
- Compact headers then sit 17 pt from the card while rows sit at 7, in RTL too.
- Regular density is unaffected.

### Scope
