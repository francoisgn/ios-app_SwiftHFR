### Summary

As a SwiftHFR member, I want long tasks I start (saving a topic offline, following a live topic) to stay visible on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and new MPs to reach me as a notification and a widget, so that I can leave the app without losing track. Live Activities are used only where Apple's guidelines allow them.

**The short answer on the MP idea:** an MP counter in the Dynamic Island is **not** a Live Activity under the HIG (🔴). The right answer for MPs is the LOT-140 notification plus a widget (see the widgets study issue).

_M5 study (UX evolutions, after the M4 parity). Read-only research by `ext/francoisgn-suborch-1` on `dev-swift`; the HIG was read live on 2026-11-07. This issue is for triage: nothing is claimed or planned. The HTML mockups were sent to the operator. They use fake data only._

### Context and constraints

**No push server, so no ActivityKit push updates.**
- Push updates need "a remote notification server" and APNs (ActivityKit push doc).
- A Live Activity "can't access the network". All its data comes from the app.
- The app can update an activity while it is in the foreground, or "while it runs in the background, for example by using BackgroundTasks".
- To *start* an activity, the app must be in the foreground, unless it uses a `LiveActivityIntent`.

What this allows:
- the member starts an activity, and the app feeds it while it runs or holds a background task;
- after that, only the `BGAppRefreshTask` runs the system chooses to grant;
- `staleDate` shows that the data is old.

**Limits:** 8 h active, at most 12 h on the Lock Screen, 4 KB of data, and the Lock Screen view is cut off above 160 pt.

**What exists today:**
- There is no widget extension and no App Group in `App/`.
- ADR 0012 already plans one synchronized folder per new target (`docs/adr/0012-app-project-file-strategy.md:27`).
- Nothing in `docs/` mentions ActivityKit, Live Activities or the Dynamic Island.
- On the engine side:
  - `MPUnreadPoll` reads `GET /` in a probe session and never writes cookies back (`Packages/HFRKit/Sources/HFRClient/MPPoll/MPUnreadPoll.swift:12-21`). A `nil` answer means "no information", never 0.
  - `NotificationLedger` has an `isDue(minimumInterval:)` check (`…/NotificationLedger.swift:81`).
  - LOT-140 (#230, M4) is not built yet, and it must record into the ledger (`docs/lots/LOT-091-notification-ledger.md:51`).

**Privacy:** MP senders and subjects never appear on the Lock Screen by default, as the HIG asks for "an innocuous summary". An activity names a topic, never the account's pseudo.

**Cookies:**
- Account sessions only contact `*.hardware.fr` (`docs/HFR-PROTOCOL.md:118`; LESSONS 30, 37, 56).
- Extensions and activity data hold counts and titles only, never cookies.

**iPhone Duo:**
- The outer camera "expands into the Dynamic Island for Live Activities" (HIG, Designing for iPhone Duo).
- The HIG update of 2026-10-13 adds limited-width layouts (a 28 pt circle, leading view only).

**1. Offline save.**
- **Start and end:** it starts when the member taps "Enregistrer hors ligne" (`App/SwiftHFR/Offline/ReaderOfflineSave.swift:31-35`). It ends on `finished`, `stopped` or `failed` (`OfflineSaveModel.swift:14-22`).
- **How:** `BGContinuedProcessingTask` (iOS 26+). The system "displays the progress of this task in a Live Activity and a person can cancel it". It can use the network, and no widget extension is needed.
- **What we provide:** the title "Hors ligne : <titre>" and the subtitle "Page 12 sur 40", from `.saving(done:total:)`.
- **Caveats:**
  - Today a save stops when the member leaves the topic (`OfflineSaveModel.swift:66-69`), so changing that needs a spec.
  - A batch is only 5 pages (`ReaderOfflineSave.swift:8`), so the feature is only worth it with an M5 "N pages / whole topic" option.

**2. MPs: notification + widget.**
- **Why not a Live Activity:** the HIG reserves Live Activities for "tasks and events that have a defined beginning and end", under 8 h, and says "Don't replicate notification layouts". An MP activity also could not update without push while the phone is locked, and it would put senders on the Lock Screen.
- **The alternative:**
  - (a) The LOT-140 notification shows the count by default, with senders as an opt-in.
  - (b) A small widget and a Lock Screen widget read `{account, count, lastCheck}`, which the app writes to the App Group after each check. The widget makes no network request and holds no cookie.
  - `nil` is shown as "inconnu", never 0.

**3. Live follow of a topic.**
- **Start and end:** on a *flagged* topic, the member picks "Suivre en direct · 1 h / 2 h / jusqu'à 23:00". The follow lasts at most 8 h.
- **Data:** `FlaggedTopicsPoll` makes one GET of the flags page, which never moves a flag (`…/FlaggedTopicsPoll.swift:16-21`). The activity shows the gain in `repliesCount` since the start. It never polls the topic page itself, because that moves the flag (E25).
- **Updates:** a polite timer while the app is in the foreground. In the background, only the runs the system grants, so the activity shows `staleDate` and "Mis à jour à 21:48".
- **Why 🟠:** the activity is stale exactly when the phone is locked.

**4. Sending a reply.**
- A POST takes seconds, and an `unconfirmed` end state left on the Lock Screen would act as a notification. `unconfirmed` means the write may have been posted, so it is never resent (`Packages/HFRKit/Sources/HFRClient/Posting/PostingError.swift:5-6`).
- **Instead:** `beginBackgroundTask` around the send, plus a local notification "Envoi non confirmé : vérifiez « Sujet de test »", only when the app is in the background and the send is unconfirmed or refused.

**5. Image upload and 6. Search.**
- Both take seconds.
- The upload API reports no progress (`ImageUploadServices.swift:9`).
- The search is one POST plus a 1 s waiting page (`GlobalSearchFlow.swift:8,53-54`).
- Both are rejected.

### Recommendation

1. **Proposal 2 first:** ship LOT-140 as planned, then an M5 lot "MP widget + App Group" (count only, no cookies in the extension).
2. **Then proposal 1:** the one clean Live Activity. The system draws it and no extension is needed. It comes with a "save N pages / whole topic" option and a spec change for `leave()`.
3. **Proposal 3** only if the operator accepts that it is mostly stale while the phone is locked.
4. Proposal 4 becomes a local notification; proposals 5 and 6 are dropped.

### Open questions for the operator

1. MP notification text: count only by default, with sender and subject as an opt-in?
2. Multi-account widget: one account chosen in the widget, or a total?
3. Offline save: may a save keep running after the member leaves the topic? Do we want a "whole topic" range?
4. Live follow: is an L effort worth it with no push server and an activity that is stale on the Lock Screen?
5. iPhone Duo: design the limited-width and outer-display variants now (LOT-147), or wait for a device capture?
