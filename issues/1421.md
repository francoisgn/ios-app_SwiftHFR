### Summary
As a reader at the end of a topic page, I want a refresh pulled from the bottom to keep me at the bottom, so that the page does not flash its top before coming back. Operator feedback, 2026-11-27.

### Observed (operator's iPhone, current dev-swift build)
- At the bottom of a topic page, refresh by pulling up from the bottom (the end-of-page refresh).
- **The top of the page shows very briefly**, then the reader goes back down to where it was (or to the new messages).

### To check
How the page is reloaded on that refresh: a full document replacement whose scroll position starts at 0 and is restored after layout would explain the flash (see #995, WebKit's late reset of a new document, and `WebKitResetWatch`). Compare with legacy's reload at the end of a page (cite `file:line` @ 87fe0d1c). Options to weigh: keep the current offset across the reload (restore before the first paint, e.g. hidden until the restore lands), or append the new messages to the current document instead of replacing it, if the reader already supports that.
