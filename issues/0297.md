### Summary

As a member, I want to keep my one-to-one MPs apart from my multi-participant MPs and read and answer the group ones like topics, so that group conversations stop drowning in my private exchanges.

Operator decision 2026-10-28: multi-participant MPs ("MP multiples") are like topics of their own, but private; their reading and participation experience comes close to public topics. A setting chooses "Tout en un" (DEFAULT: today's single list, unchanged UX) or "Deux listes"; with two lists, a switch at the top of the Messages screen: "MP" / "MP multiples".

### Scope

- **Setting** (Réglages, section Messages): "Affichage des MP": "Tout en un" (default) / "Deux listes". New key; absent = "Tout en un", so nothing changes for anyone who does not opt in.
- **Two lists:** a segmented control at the top of the MP list (LOT-126 (#217)): "MP" (one-to-one) and "MP multiples" (group). Each segment shows its own unread count; the tab / sidebar badge stays the total.
- **Group detection:** `PrivateMessageThread.isGroup` (HFRModels, LOT-023): HFR prints `<span title="<pseudos>">Interlocuteurs multiples</span>` in `sujetCase6` for group threads (HFR-PROTOCOL.md §6c, fixtures `R04`, `R08-2`, `R13u1`). Legacy does the same test (`MPListView.swift:376-377`, `Classes/HFRMPViewController.m:134`).
- **Paging:** no server-side filter of the MP list is known; the split is client-side over the pages loaded. A segment whose loaded pages hold no thread loads the next page (bounded, stops at the last page) rather than showing an empty list while more exist. Any server filter needs a capture first, never a guess.
- **Group rows look like topic rows:** title, participants (tooltip names, `interlocutors`), replies, last post, pages; unread marker as topics.
- **Group thread reading and participation like a public topic:** it opens in the topic reader with the topic features as their lots land (page navigator LOT-110 (#201), first-unread LOT-111 (#202), message actions LOT-087 (#179), quote and multi-quote LOT-086 (#178), quick reply LOT-153 (#243)), replying through LOT-128 (#219). List here which topic features apply to MPs and which do not (flags do not exist for MPs), with the legacy source for each.
