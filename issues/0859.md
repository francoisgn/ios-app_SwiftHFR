As a member, I want the "Stockage MP" settings to show one switch per thing it does, each with a short explanation, so that I understand what MPStorage is for and turn on only what I want (for example « MP multiples → dark topic »).

### Requested (operator, 2026-11-12)
- A clearer "Stockage MP" area, with **one switch per function** that MPStorage enables, including a switch **« MP multiples → dark topic »** (#858). « Dark topic » is the community's word for a non-public topic held in a group MP (operator, 2026-11-12): the switch makes group MPs behave as close as possible to a public topic.
- An **info bubble** on the settings to explain them.
