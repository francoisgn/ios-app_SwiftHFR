## francoisgn · 2026-11-13T21:54:51Z

Approach changed (operator decision, 2026-11-13), after the device tests of two sheet variants: iOS always moves a sheet to its largest detent when the keyboard rises, and with a single detent the page under it stays inert, so no sheet gives "half height + page scrollable with the keyboard up".

New plan (branch `fix/853-composer-inline-bar`, stacked on #854's branch):
- On iPhone the reader's composers (reply, MP of the author menu, edit of one's message) are no longer a sheet: a bar docked at the bottom of the reader, on the keyboard (`safeAreaInset`), as in Messages or the Claude app; the page above is the reader's own scroll view, so it scrolls with the keyboard up.
- Dragging the page down pulls the keyboard down with the finger (`keyboardDismissMode = .interactive` on the web view's scroll view); the bar stays with its text. Page taps stay ignored while writing.
- "Plein écran" fills the reader with the same composer (no state lost); iPad keeps its sheet; the page's bottom inset counts the bar and the keyboard.

The earlier attempt (`adaa2f89` and its device variants on `fix/853-composer-page-scroll`) is dropped.
