### Summary
As a member using the « Rétro 5250 » theme, I want the sections of grouped screens (Réglages and others) to be visibly separated, so that I can tell where a group starts and ends. Today the group card is the same black as the page: section headers, rows and footers run together.

### Observed (operator's iPhone Duo, 2026-11-28, Réglages under Rétro 5250)
- « Notifications MP », « Stockage MP », « Thème », « Sujets »: each section's rows sit on the page's black with only the green row separators; nothing marks the group's edges, and a section header reads like one more row.
- Likely everywhere a grouped list is drawn under a theme whose `surface` equals (or nearly equals) its `background`.

### Proposed (operator)
A subtle 1 pt outline (or separator) around each section group, in a mid tone of the theme (« gris moyen 1pt »; in Rétro, a dim tone that stays in the theme's palette rather than a foreign grey), drawn only when the group's fill does not stand out from the page.

### Scope
- A theme-aware group outline: drawn when `surface` vs `background` contrast is below a threshold (Rétro 5250, possibly Noir/OLED-like themes), 1 pt, colour from the theme (a new optional token, e.g. `groupOutline`, falling back to `separator` at reduced strength), in light and dark variants; THEMES.md rule.
- Every grouped screen (Réglages and sub-screens, Comptes, Profil, À propos…), insetGrouped lists; no change for themes whose cards already stand out.
- Hosted render test (outline present under Rétro, absent under Legacy); screenshots.

### Acceptance criteria
- Under Rétro 5250 the section groups of Réglages are visibly bounded; other themes unchanged; contrast of the outline ≥ 3:1 against the page (WCAG 1.4.11 for UI component boundaries) or justified.

Found by the operator on device. Related: LOT-192 (#733, Rétro 5250), #1370.
