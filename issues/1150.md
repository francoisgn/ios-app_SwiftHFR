### Summary

As a user, I want every sheet and the image viewer to close with the system close button at the same place, so that dismissing a panel works the same everywhere and as in other iOS 27 apps. Today nine sheets use a text button « Fermer », one of them on the trailing (confirm) side, where legacy used an `xmark` symbol.

### Rule

- "Use the standard Back and Close buttons. […] Prefer the standard symbols for each, and don't use a text label that says Back or Close." — [HIG › Toolbars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/toolbars) (Navigation; page updated 2025-12-16).
- "In iOS and iPadOS, for sheets with a single view, the Cancel button belongs on the leading edge of the top toolbar. When present, the Done button belongs on the trailing edge." — [HIG › Sheets](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets) (iOS, iPadOS; updated 2026-03-24).
- API: [`ButtonRole.close`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/buttonrole/close) (iOS 26.0+).
