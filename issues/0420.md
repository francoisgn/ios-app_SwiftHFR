### Summary

As a user, I want to choose my theme in a **Thème sub-menu with a live preview** of each theme, so that I see what I pick before I leave the screen. The **Legacy** theme keeps its accent hue slider; the other themes have fixed colours and no slider.

### Scope

Revised by the operator on 2026-11-11 (replaces the 2026-10-28 "10 presets on the slider" scope). Contract: `docs/THEMES.md` §1–§3.

- **Two kinds of theme.**
  - **Legacy** (today's app): keeps the continuous « Teinte d'accent » hue slider and its stored keys (`theme_day_color_action`, `theme_night_color_action`), shape unchanged (LOT-121).
  - **Themes with fixed colours** (those of the M4/M5 theme lots: Swift, Rétro 5250, OG clair and gris, Noël, Spooktober…): their accent comes from `theme.json`, **no slider is shown** for them.
- **Theme selection sub-menu with a preview.** « Thème » opens a sub-screen, built like the app-icon picker (LOT-123, LOT-189: grouped, one tile per choice, check mark on the active one), where each theme tile shows a preview in light and dark (list row with the accent glyph, a link, a toggle, a badge). The preview updates as the slider moves, when Legacy is the highlighted theme. Choosing a fixed theme hides the slider; choosing Legacy shows it with the stored hue.
- **Readable accent text** (decision 14, intentional difference from legacy): the raw tint stays for fills and toggles; text and glyphs drawn in the accent use a derived colour of the same hue that reaches 4.5:1 on the surface (the night tint is 3.5:1 on black at 240°). Applies to the Legacy slider; a fixed theme's accent is checked by LOT-188's contrast test. Rule in `docs/THEMES.md` §3.
- **Open for the operator**: the 10 preset dots of the 2026-10-28 scope (hues Rouge 0.00 … Rose 0.92) are **dropped from this scope** unless you want them on the Legacy slider only; say so on this issue.
- **Relation to LOT-188 (#729).** #729 owns the theme engine (`theme.json`, tokens, contrast test) and its gallery inline in « Thème ». This lot **depends on it** and turns that gallery into the sub-menu with a preview; whoever takes it adjusts #729's « Gallery » bullet by a comment, not a second gallery. Scope kept from the split of LOT-122 (#213): LOT-121 keeps the slider and its tints, LOT-122 the dark styles.
