# Issue #1264: [SwiftHFR] Sheets with typed text: answer a swipe-down attempt with the discard question, not only a refused swipe

- State: open
- Author: francoisgn · created 2026-11-19T14:37:05Z · updated 2026-11-19T14:37:05Z
- Labels: type: feature, priority: low, difficulty: medium, area: app, status: needs-triage, origin: deliberate-gap
- Source: https://github.com/ezzz/HFRnow/issues/1264

### Summary
As a member who swipes down a sheet holding typed text (the reply composer, « Alerte Modération », « Créer un sondage », the AQ and bookmark title sheets), I want the discard question to appear when I swipe, as HIG › Sheets asks, so the sheet does not just bounce back with no explanation.

### Rule
"If people have unsaved changes in the sheet when they begin swiping to dismiss it, use an action sheet to let them confirm their action." ([HIG › Sheets](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets))

### Today
- The composer (#429, `ComposerSheetSettings`, `ComposerHeight.swift`) and, with #1263 (#1169), the four other sheets refuse the swipe while there is text (`interactiveDismissDisabled`). The question only comes on « Annuler » (`View.discardConfirmation`, `Common/DiscardConfirmation.swift`).
- SwiftUI exposes no swipe-attempt callback. UIKit has `UIAdaptivePresentationControllerDelegate.presentationControllerDidAttemptToDismiss(_:)`, but SwiftUI owns the presentation controller's delegate. Replacing it may break SwiftUI's own reset of the presentation binding on dismissal (raised in #1263's review).

### Scope
- Find a supported way to learn of a swipe attempt on a SwiftUI sheet, for example forwarding to SwiftUI's delegate rather than replacing it. If none is reliable, record the decision to keep the refused swipe.
- Apply the result to all five sheets at once.
- Measure on a device: iOS 26 and 27, iPhone and iPad.

### Acceptance criteria
- A swipe-down on a sheet holding text shows the same question as « Annuler » (« Abandonner » / « Continuer la saisie »). « Continuer la saisie » keeps the sheet and its text.
- No regression of the presentation binding after a confirmed discard or after « Ajouter », « Envoyer » or « Créer ».

Origin: round 1 review of #1263 (#1169).
