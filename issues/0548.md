### Summary

As a SwiftHFR user on iOS 27, including iPhone Duo, I want the app's lists, banners, toasts and buttons outside the reader to follow Liquid Glass and use the system bars, so the app does not look older than the legacy HFR+ it replaces and its bars move to the side on the Duo cover screen.

Part of #545 (M4, operator decision 2026-11-07: the HIG wins over legacy; every divergence is recorded in GAP). The full blast-radius table and HIG sources are on #545.

### Scope (everything outside `App/SwiftHFR/Reader/`, except the image viewer)

- **Image viewer** (`Reader/Viewer/ImageViewerView.swift`):
  - remove the two `toolbarBackground` calls at `:24-25` and keep `.toolbarColorScheme(.dark)`;
  - the toast at `:77` becomes `.glassEffect(in: .capsule)`;
  - the button at `:46` becomes `.glassProminent` if it floats;
  - `ellipsis.circle` at `:67` becomes `ellipsis`.
- **List pagers** become a system `ToolbarItemGroup(placement: .bottomBar)` instead of `safeAreaInset` + `.background(.bar)`:
  - `Navigation/TopicListView.swift:60-62,291`;
  - `Messages/MessageListView.swift:62-64,170`;
  - `Search/ForumSearchView.swift:24,128`;
  - `FilteredPosts/FilteredPostsView.swift:115-117,196`.
  Legacy reference: list pagers `MainWindow.swift:977-981`, filtered "Résultats suivants" `MessagesView.swift:4728-4740` @ `2440d79`.
- **Top strips** with a `.bar` fill become `safeAreaBar(edge: .top)` without the fill:
  - `TopicListView.swift:53`;
  - `Offline/ReaderOfflineSave.swift:62`;
  - `FilteredPostsView.swift:72`;
  - `MessageListView.swift:191`;
  - `Smileys/SmileyPickerView.swift:167`.
- **Notice cards** (`regularMaterial`) **stay as materials**: HIG Materials says "Don't use Liquid Glass in the content layer". Only cards that float over scrolling content get glass, each one justified in the spec.
- **Buttons:** `.glass` / `.glassProminent` only on floating controls; content-layer buttons stay.
- **Circled toolbar symbols:** `Root/RootTabsView.swift:78`.
- If it simplifies the code, one small `View` extension can mirror the legacy `LiquidGlass.swift` helpers.
