### Summary
As a reader, I want the page bar of a list (« ⏮ ‹ Page N › ») to show only when I reach the bottom of the list, so that it does not take space at the top of a list where I almost never change page.

Milestone: **M4**. Source: operator UI comparison of 2026-11-14, capture 04 (Messages, logged in; pseudos and titles redacted).

### Observed (SwiftHFR @ `37c099b5`)
The pager (`App/SwiftHFR/Navigation/PagerToolbar.swift`, LOT-185) sits above the tab bar at all times, from the top of the list.

### Why (operator, 2026-11-14)
- Messages is opened because there are new messages, and they are always at the top: one rarely changes page.
- The same holds for every topic list: the page change belongs at the end of what one has read.
- The pager itself is kept (it is the retained SwiftHFR improvement of GAP-024, without the fake total); only when it shows changes.

### Scope
- **Every paged topic list, Catégories included, and the Messages list** (operator, 2026-11-14): the pager appears when the list is scrolled to its end, and stays hidden above that.
- A list shorter than the screen shows it at once (its end is visible).
- VoiceOver: the page actions stay reachable when the bar is hidden (for example as accessibility actions of the list), so that hiding is visual only.
- Ledger: `gap-edit` of GAP-024 and GAP-221 (glass pager) in the PR's `### Ledger` section.

### Acceptance criteria
- [ ] At the top of a long list the pager is hidden; at the end it shows; scrolling back up hides it again.
- [ ] Same behaviour in a category's topic list, Favoris lists that page, search results and Messages.
- [ ] VoiceOver can change page from anywhere in the list.

Related: #565 (pager under the tab bar, closed), #649, #865 (top bar title scrolls away), #896 (fifth tab item).

### Operator view (2026-11-14)

The pager meant here is the bar « ⏮ ‹ Page N › » of the lists (`PagerToolbar.swift`, #881). For the operator it **stays visible at the bottom of the screen at all times**, as it is today after #881. To settle between the operator and ezzz before coding: `status: needs-decision`. If ezzz's « only at the end of the list » is kept, it goes in Réglages › Expérimental (#903) rather than replacing today's behaviour.

### Common ground (operator, 2026-11-14), replacing the « Operator view » above

- While the list scrolls, the pager is **hidden**, which keeps the room ezzz wants.
- When the list **reaches its end**, the page bubble « ⏮ ‹ Page N › » **appears dynamically** with a short animation, at the bottom of the screen above the tab bar. It hides again when the user scrolls back up.
- A list shorter than the screen shows it at once, since its end is visible.
- VoiceOver keeps the page actions reachable at all times (accessibility actions of the list).
- No setting and no experimental option: this is the default behaviour. ezzz can still object on this issue.

### Additions (operator, 2026-11-15)

These go with the page bubble that appears at the end of the list:

1. **Refresh at the bottom too.** When the bubble appears at the end, a refresh button appears next to it. It is the **same** refresh button as the top bar's, reused (one component, one action), not a new one. The top bar keeps its own.
2. **Tighter spacing between the last row and the bottom controls.** The gap between the last row and the bubble and buttons is too large today. It reads as unused space, like the margin around the floating composer. Reduce it to the system's standard spacing above a floating bar, while keeping the last row fully readable and tappable above the controls (see #852's inset handling).
3. **Pull up past the end to refresh.** Once the list is at its end, pulling it further up (an overscroll at the bottom) triggers a refresh, like pull-to-refresh at the top.
   - Show a discreet hint below the last row: « Tirer vers le haut pour actualiser », almost tone on tone (secondary label colour, small).
   - While refreshing, show the system activity indicator.

### Acceptance criteria (added)
- [ ] At the end of a list, the bubble and a refresh button appear together. The button runs the same action as the top refresh.
- [ ] The gap between the last row and the bottom controls is the standard spacing. Measure before and after; screenshots go on the PR.
- [ ] Pulling up past the end shows the hint, then refreshes on release past the threshold, with a short haptic as at the top. Releasing earlier does nothing.
- [ ] VoiceOver: refresh is reachable without the gesture, through the button or an accessibility action.
