### Summary

As a reader about to lose the network (a flight, the metro), I want to download one or a few topics from my flag to the end, so that I can read them offline. The downloads expire by themselves and stay within a size budget, so the app never bloats. (Operator, 2026-11-12. Replaces the removal asked in #822. M5.)

### The problem in legacy (why it was removed there)

Legacy saved **one page at a time** and read it with a **second copy of the reader**. The online and offline readers are two separate controllers, so every reader fix had to be done twice and drifted. `filterTU` alone is applied in both, at `OnlineMessagesTableViewController.m:2437` and `OfflineMessagesTableViewController.m:2349` (#818).

Low use, and that duplicated reader, are why ezzz removed it (#822, 2026-11-11).

### What SwiftHFR does differently

- **One reader.** Offline pages go through the same reader as online ones: LOT-139 (#229) is built on LOT-083's reader, and LOT-068 (#93) stores the pages in HFRStorage. There is no second reader to maintain. Confirm while specifying: the offline path must not fork the reader.
- **The unit is a topic, not a page.** A download is "this topic, from my flag to the end", bounded by caps. It expires on its own, so storage cannot grow without limit.

### Behaviour (operator spec)

- **Choose** one or several topics, from the topic list, Favoris or the reader. The range is **from the flag (first unread) to the last page**, capped at **N pages per topic**.
- **Download** in the background with progress. A failure is reported per topic, and a partial download stays readable for the pages it got.
- **Expiry: 72 h** after download. Expired content is deleted automatically, at launch and in the background.
- **Caps:**
  - **N pages per topic.** A logged-in topic page of 40 posts is about 230 KB of HTML (captures `R21`, `P03m`: 230–231 KB; the 76 HTML captures average 70 KB, but many are not full topic pages). That is HTML only: images are not counted, and they can dominate.
  - **A global cap in MB** over all downloads.
  - **At most T topics offline at once.** A download beyond that is refused with a clear message, never a silent eviction.
- **Settings › Hors ligne** lists each download:
  - topic;
  - page range X → Y (flag → end, or capped);
  - download date;
  - expiry date;
  - size.
  Plus a total against the global cap, and delete one or all.

### Defaults proposed (to confirm when M5 opens)

| Setting | Proposal | Why |
|---|---|---|
| N pages per topic | 25 | about 6 MB of HTML; a long flight's worth |
| T topics at once | 3 (between the operator's 2 and 5) | keeps the list short and the cap meaningful |
| Global cap | 50 MB to start, **images included** | about 200 pages of HTML without images; measure an image-heavy topic before fixing the number |
| Images | **downloaded** (operator, 2026-11-12) | they count against both caps; a download stops at the cap and says so |

### Decided (operator, 2026-11-12)

- **Images are downloaded** with the pages: post images, smileys and avatars. They count against the per-topic and the global MB caps. When a cap is reached, the download stops and says which cap was hit; the pages already downloaded stay readable.
- **The page-level saves made by testers are purged.** The current "save this page offline" data (LOT-068/139, TestFlight builds only) is deleted at the first launch of the version that ships this feature, with no migration. This is a deliberate exception to the persistence rule: the data was never in a public release.
- **Until this ships**, the current page-level save stays as it is (option A). If it must go before M5, the only change allowed is hiding its menu entry: no other code for the interim (operator, 2026-11-12).
- **Still to measure:** the size of an image-heavy topic page, which sets the final global cap.
