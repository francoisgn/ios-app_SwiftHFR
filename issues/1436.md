### Scope

1. **Mid-grey OG gris (decision needed).** The engine's contrast rules (THEMES.md §3, #842) include the app's own error text (`AccessibleColors.errorText`, `#B00020` light / `#FF827A` dark) and badge grey on `background` and `surface`, and the system text of the appearance a single-variant theme shows in. No appearance gives all of them 4.5:1 on the forum's grey skin: `#B00020` needs a background and a row of at least about `#D0D0D0`; `#FF827A` of at most about `#202020`; black system text on `#666666` is 3.66:1. LOT-193 therefore ships a light grey (`#D5D5D5` page, `#DCDCDC` rows, `#E8E8E8` alternate). Options: (a) keep it; (b) an optional per-theme `errorText` and `badgeOutline` token (and the system text of the theme's appearance) so a theme can carry its own, with rules on them; the draft's `#666666`/`#AAAAAA` then needs the dark appearance with white row text, or black text on a `#808080` page: a product choice.
2. **`listStyle: ogTable`** (the dense rows that keep the app's list layout: header band, alternate cells, page bar, counters). The optional tokens are defined and checked but not drawn (THEMES.md §3, « Not drawn yet »).
3. **Screenshots** of a list and a topic page in both themes at Dynamic Type XL and AX5 (needs a logged-in session; the LOT-193 PR had none).
