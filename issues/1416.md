# Issue #1416: [SwiftHFR] Account sync: a refresh push can re-adopt a same-login copy whose cookies expire far later than the forum's

- State: open
- Author: francoisgn · created 2026-11-27T22:20:01Z · updated 2026-11-27T22:20:01Z
- Labels: type: bug, priority: low, difficulty: medium, area: storage, status: ready, origin: audit, m4-review: performance
- Source: https://github.com/ezzz/HFRnow/issues/1416

### Summary
As a user with the account sync on, I want the cookies the forum just confirmed never to be replaced by an older copy of the same login, so that a valid session check can never start a check → adopt → check loop against HFR.

### What happens (after #1406)
#1406 makes a same-login merge adopt the copy's account when the copy's login-cookie expiry is more than 7 days after this device's (`AccountSyncMerge.sameLoginAccounts`, `renewalMargin`). The push of a `.refresh` (`AccountSync.localChange`) runs that same merge. If the copy's expiry is more than 7 days past what the forum just sent, each valid check stores the renewed cookies, the push takes the copy's back and reports `replaced`, and the app checks the account again (`AccountsModel.checkSessions` → `persistSession` → `noteSyncChange(.refresh)` → `adoptSyncReport`). The adversarial review of #1406 (Fable round 3, finding 2) saw `replaced` on 20 of 20 checks on one device in an engine probe; the app side of the loop was read, not run.

**Not reachable today:** HFR sets the login cookies to +30 days on the login (`W03`) and on every logged-in answer (`R74`), and legacy-imported cookies keep the server's expiry, so no copy can be more than 7 days ahead of a fresh check. It becomes an unbounded request loop if that lifetime ever shortens by more than a week.

### Proposed fix
In the push of a `.refresh`, the just-confirmed account of that key is never replaced by a same-login copy; it is written to the copy when the copy lags more than the margin. Test (Linux, `HFRStorageTests`): a copy whose expiry is 40 days ahead, repeated refreshes with +30-day cookies → `replaced` empty on every push.

### Acceptance
- A `.refresh` push never reports its own key `replaced` because of a same-login copy.
- An idle device still adopts a copy far ahead at its reconcile (#1406 round 2 finding 1).

Refs #1390, #1406.
