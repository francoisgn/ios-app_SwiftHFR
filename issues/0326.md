### Why

Under the operator decision of 2026-10-29, outside contributors may have their adversarial review done by another of their own agents. The model matrix still applies: the reviewer is never the author model. A different person is not required. That keeps throughput up, but the author and the reviewer share one operator, so this issue adds a final independent audit.

**Outside contributor** means any claim or PR author other than the maintainer's own sessions: Bybifok (`moust4ki`), Ezzz's agents, or anyone else (`ext/<name>-…` ids). The steward session `ext/opus-helper1` acts for the maintainer and does not count.

### How

- A fresh **Fable** session gets the merged diff of each listed PR, its issue (acceptance criteria), AGENTS.md, LESSONS.md, ARCHITECTURE.md and HFR-PROTOCOL.md, and the current code around it. It does not get the PR discussion.
- It returns, per PR: OK, or numbered findings (severity, file:line, failing scenario, fix).
- High and Medium findings become fix issues. Security findings go to the maintainer privately (SECURITY.md).
