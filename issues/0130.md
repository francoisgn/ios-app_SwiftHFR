### Capture result
PR #527, `W130`, `R130a`-`R130c`: HFR stores a leading CRLF. The thread renders it, the edit form serves the text without it, the quote form keeps it (`[quotemsg=…]\r\nword`). So a browser that drops a leading newline corrupts nothing: an unchanged edit round trip loses the break whatever the client, a quote round trip keeps it.
