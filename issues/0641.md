# Issue #641: [SwiftHFR] Reader: the first post's header (and its "…") stays hidden under the top bar

- State: open
- Author: francoisgn · created 2026-11-07T22:11:07Z · updated 2026-11-07T22:11:07Z

### Summary
As a reader, I want to see and tap the header of the first post of a page (avatar, pseudo, date and "…"). Today it stays hidden under the status bar and the top bar, even after a pull to the top. Found during the Mac check of #594.

### Observed (mac/m2-lead repro, 2026-11-07)
- **Build** `f9901d9` (origin/dev-swift, after LOT-186 #552 and #575). **Devices**: iPhone 18 Pro simulator, iOS 27.0, portrait; iPad Pro 13-inch (M5) simulator, iOS 27.0, landscape. Logged out and logged in.
- Open any topic page. The first post's header sits at y ≈ 5–53 pt (accessibility frame of its "Actions du message de …" button). The top bar ends near 115 pt on iPhone, so the header is behind the status bar and the glass bar, and only its blurred shape shows through.
- A pull-down (swipe down at the top of the page) does not move it: the page is already at its top.
- A tap where the "…" sits opens nothing.
- On a **one-post page** no post menu can be reached at all. On other pages, only the first post is lost.
- Screenshots are kept locally (forum content, not published).

### Where to look (not verified)
- LOT-186 puts the web view under the bars: `contentInsetAdjustmentBehavior = .never` and `obscuredInsets`.
  - `ReaderWebController.swift:113`;
  - `ReaderWebController+Scroll.swift:17-24` (`applyObscuredInsets`, `scroll(toReadingTop:)`).
- Either the top inset does not reach the scroll view when the page is shown, or the first load scrolls to a reading top that sits under the bars.
- Refs #552 (LOT-186), #594.

### Acceptance criteria
- [ ] At the top of a page, the first post's header is fully below the top bar, on iPhone and iPad.
- [ ] Its "…" can be tapped on a one-post page.
- [ ] A test pins the first post's top at or below the bars' edge once the page is shown.
