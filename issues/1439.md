### Summary
As a reader who uses the largest Dynamic Type sizes, I want the Messages, topic list, Favoris and reader headers to stay readable at « Accessibilité – XL », so that a row is not one word per line and no label is clipped.

Found by the Mac pass of #693 (iPhone 18 Pro, iOS 27.2, dev-swift @ 9f7ec2b8, `xcrun simctl ui … content_size accessibility-extra-large`, light and dark alike). Not checked against legacy at that size: the legacy layout at the same size is the reference to read before fixing.

### What happens
1. **Messages row** (`MessageRowView`): the « non lu » capsule, the avatar and the unread dot leave a column of about 300 px for the title, which wraps one word per line (a title of three words takes six lines). One row is taller than the screen.
2. **Reader message header and body**: the body is indented by about 440 px of a 1206 px screen (avatar column), so the text runs at 3 to 5 words per line.
3. **Topic list under a category**: the « Sous-forum » label is clipped by the top edge of its card; the picker value wraps onto two lines.
4. **Favoris and topic rows**: the meta line truncates the last poster and drops the date (« name… », « name - il… »), and « 452 / 452 » wraps onto two lines.

### Acceptance criteria
- At accessibility sizes, each of the four places lays out on a stacked (vertical) arrangement instead of squeezing a column, with no clipped label and no truncated date.
- A hosted test renders each place at `accessibility-extra-large` and checks the title column width (item 1) and the label frame inside its card (item 3).
- Default size layouts do not change.
