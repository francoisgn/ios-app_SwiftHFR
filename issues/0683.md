### Summary
As a reader on a weak connection, I want a stalled page to give up after about ten seconds with a clear error, so that I can retry instead of waiting half a minute, as in the published app.

Milestone: M4 (operator, 2026-11-08). Found by the `2440d79..2fe43da3` sweep of #655 (row `ae196aa0`), opened by its catch-up pass.

### Legacy @ `2fe43da3` (`legacy/duo-inner-cover`, #372)
- `ae196aa0` "Improve topic loading feedback and timeouts": `kTimeoutContent 10`, and `kTimeoutMini` / `kTimeoutMaxi` both equal it (`Classes/Constants.h:70-72` @ `2fe43da3`; 30 and 60 at `2440d79`). Global search `TIME_OUT_INTERVAL_SEARCH 10` (`Classes/ObjCForumSearchWorker.m:8`, 15 at `2440d79`). The Swift layer uses `HFRNetworkTimeout.content = 10` (`HFRswift/Swift/Common.swift:1891-1893`) for topic pages, profiles and AQ (`MessagesView.swift:333`, `UserProfileView.swift:117`, `AQPlusView.swift:73, 278`). Background MP check unchanged: 5 s (`MPBackgroundService.swift:86`).
- These are `URLRequest.timeoutInterval` / ASIHTTPRequest timeouts: an **idle** limit (no data for that long), not a total one, except global search, which also sets `timeoutIntervalForResource` to 10 s, a cap on the whole transfer (`ObjCForumSearchWorker.m:131-132`).

### Current SwiftHFR
`RequestTimeout` keeps the `2440d79` values: standard 30 s, topic search 60 s, global search 15 s, background 5 s (`Packages/HFRKit/Sources/HFRNetwork/Transport/HTTPRequest.swift:18-27`, LOT-040). `HTTPRequest.timeout` is a **total** limit enforced by the transport (LOT-040: swift-corelibs ignores `timeoutInterval`).

### Scope
- Decide the new presets with the semantic difference in mind: a 10 s total limit is stricter than legacy's 10 s idle limit (a long topic page on a slow link can take more than 10 s while still receiving). Options: 10 s idle on Apple platforms plus a larger total cap, or a measured total value. Measure on a device (AGENTS.md §3.9), do not assume.
- Update LOT-040's table and `docs/HFR-PROTOCOL.md` §timeouts (the catch-up PR of #655 records the `2fe43da3` values there).

### Acceptance criteria
- The presets and their semantics are written in LOT-040 and tested on the loopback server (a stalled answer fails at the new limit, a slow but flowing one does not if the idle option is chosen).
- The reader's error state after a timeout says so and offers a retry.
