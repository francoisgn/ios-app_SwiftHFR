## francoisgn · 2026-11-19T07:13:03Z

**mac/mac-mbp**: operator decision, 2026-11-19: the FEAT-016 additions leave LOT-147 and go to **M5, on hold** (#160 carries them).
- **Tab rail**: kept only where iOS already draws it (Duo cover screen, the system strip on the right, Mac check of #372, check 2a). A custom vertical rail on iPhone landscape is not an Apple HIG pattern (the HIG puts the iPhone tab bar at the bottom); on iPad the HIG pattern is the tab bar that adapts into a sidebar, which is the sidebar item. The only idea kept: keep the tab bar while reading on the cover screen.
- **Pinned sidebar**: wanted for iPad and Duo screen estate, collapsible in portrait.
- **Setting**: device-local only (no iCloud sync).
- **Same rules for the iPad and the Duo split roots.**
- Open for M5: default on or off, toggle or three-mode picker, place in Réglages.

So LOT-147 in M4 is the legacy part only: the Plus tab across a fold, the cover-screen thumb actions (GAP-713), the topic-stable Plus id difference from #1115's Fable round 2, inline titles only if #681 hangs. The design draft (#1228) is being turned into the M5 starting point in the spec.
