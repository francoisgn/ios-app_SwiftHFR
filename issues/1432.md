### Summary
As a respondent, I read that "les journaux techniques du serveur ne contiennent pas votre adresse IP" (`tooling/ux-survey/web/donnees.html:49`). That is true of the survey service, but the Tailscale daemon on the same host writes its own log, and some of its lines carry the client's address. The deployment notes should make the host match the notice, or the notice should say what the host keeps.

Refs #635 (finding 3 of the UX-11 review). To settle before the public opening.

### What is known
- The service: measured on loopback at `ffa9418d`, no address, user agent, token, pseudo or free text in its log after a full run of requests (accepted, refused, rate limited).
- The relay: in Tailscale's published source, the serve and Funnel code logs the source address on its failure paths (tailscale v1.80.0, `ipn/ipnlocal/serve.go:418`, `:436`, `:503`: "getConn didn't complete from %v…", "failed to TCP proxy port … (from %v)"). This is a secondary source for one version: the version installed on the VPS must be checked on the host.
- The PO checklist (`docs/ux/questionnaire/verification-image.md`, point 5) greps the daemon's journal after successful requests only, so it would not see these lines, and it leaves the upload of the daemon's logs to Tailscale as a decision still to take.

### Scope
- `verification-image.md` point 5 and the README: decide and write down (a) the daemon's log upload switched off on the VPS, (b) a bounded retention for the daemon's journal on the host (or its unit logged at a level that drops these lines), and (c) a grep of the journal after a failed request too (a connection cut mid-request).
- If (a) and (b) are not done: reword the sentence of `docs/ux/questionnaire/donnees.md` and `web/donnees.html` so that it speaks of the survey service's logs only and names the relay's logs on the host, with their retention. The notice then needs a new `UX_MENTION_MAJ`.
- No code change in the service.

### Acceptance criteria
- The checklist names the setting chosen for the daemon's logs and how to verify it on the host.
- The notice sentence and the host's real behaviour agree; `go test ./...` still green (the notice concordance test).
