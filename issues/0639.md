# Issue #639: [SwiftHFR] Reader: folded blocked quote has the even message's own colour in light

### Summary
As a reader who has blocklisted someone, I want a folded quote of that member to stand out on every message, so that I can see at a glance that something was folded. On even (tinted) messages in light mode, the folded quote now has exactly the message's own colour.

Refs #577

### What the Mac check measured
Mac check of #577 on dev-swift f9901d9b, iPhone 18 Pro iOS 27.0 simulator. Harness: an anonymized topic fixture shown through the real reader, with the blocklist forced by a local-only seam. The colours come from `getComputedStyle` in the real `WKWebView`.

| Message | Folded `table.quote-blocked` background | Message background | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| even, light | 244,244,244 | 244,244,244 (`--surface-alt`) | 1.00 |
| even, light, Increase Contrast | 244,244,244 | 239,239,239 | 1.04 |
| even, dark (100 %) | 12,12,12 | 22,22,22 | 1.07 |
| odd, light | 244,244,244 | 255,255,255 | 1.10 |

### Cause
`reader.css`, `:where(article.message:nth-of-type(even)) table.quote-blocked`: the tint is `color-mix(var(--text) 5%, transparent)` over `--background`. In light, `--surface-alt` is also 5 % of `--text` over `--background`, so both colours are identical. Only the 3 px left bar and the ▼ tell the quote apart. Plain quotes on the same message step back to `--background` and stay distinct (1.10). The header contrast is fine (`--secondary` 4.74:1).

### Acceptance criteria
- On an even message, the folded blocked quote differs from `--surface-alt` in light, dark and Increase Contrast. For example: step back to `--background` like plain quotes, with the tint layered on top in the other direction, or use a stronger tint.
- `--secondary` on its header keeps ≥ 4.5:1.
- `ReaderSeparationWebTests` asserts that the even folded quote background differs from article 2's background.
