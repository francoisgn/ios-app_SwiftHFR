### Summary
As a member who splits the Messages list into « MP » and « MP multiples », I want a third choice, « Tous », that shows both, and the list to reopen on my last choice, so that I can still see every conversation at once, as the Favoris filters let me see every topic.

### Requested (operator, 2026-11-14)
- A third segment, **« Tous »**, which shows one-to-one and group MPs together.
- **« Tous » is the default on first use only**; after that the list opens on the **last choice**, remembered.
- Same principle as the topic filters Tous / Favoris / Suivis / Lus, which legacy remembers and SwiftHFR already keeps for a signed-in account (`TopicListModel.wantedFilter`, `App/SwiftHFR/Navigation/TopicListModel.swift:177-178`): reuse that mechanism, do not build a second one (#836).

### Scope
- `MPSegment` gains an `all` case, first in the switch; its unread count is the sum of the two others.
- The chosen segment is stored per account (new `SettingKey`, tolerant decoding: an unknown or missing value reads as « Tous »).
- The « Tout en un / Deux listes » setting is unchanged.
