As a reader replying on iPhone, I want the open composer to be compact, with less empty space around the bar and the card, and with icons of one consistent size, so that it takes no more room than it needs and reads as one clean control. ### What the operator saw (latest build, iPhone, keyboard up)

- **B. Too much empty space:** above the "Répondre" bar, between the bar and the text card, below the card down to the keyboard, and on the sides. The Claude iOS app's composer is the reference for density: the card sits close to the keyboard with thin margins.
