### Summary

As a reader replying on iPhone, I want the open composer to be compact, with less empty space around the bar and the card, and with icons of one consistent size, so that it takes no more room than it needs and reads as one clean control. (Operator feedback on the latest iPhone build, 2026-11-14; follow-up of #854 / #888.)

### What the operator saw (latest build, iPhone, keyboard up)

- **B. Too much empty space:** above the "Répondre" bar, between the bar and the text card, below the card down to the keyboard, and on the sides. The Claude iOS app's composer is the reference for density: the card sits close to the keyboard with thin margins.
- **C. Inconsistent icon sizes:** the bar's buttons (close ✕, drafts, send ↑) and the card's tools (emoji, GIF, image, Aa) are not the same size. **The card's tool size is the target**, and the bar's buttons should match it.
- **D. To try:** each icon in its own circle or pill, as in the Claude app (the `+` and the mic in round chips, the model in a pill). Keep the 44 pt hit areas (operator decision on #854/#888).

### Scope

- The iPhone composer chrome only (`ComposerView` and its bar, introduced by #888). iPad is unchanged unless the same spacing applies.
- Spacing tokens instead of fixed paddings, so that density follows the list density settings where it makes sense.
