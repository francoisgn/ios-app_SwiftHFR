### Summary
As an iPad reader, I want the « Masquer la liste » button legacy shows next to an open topic, so that I can read the topic wide in one tap and bring the list back the same way.

Refs #655 (inventory row 37).

### Legacy (reference `87fe0d1c`)
- Added by `03e5e225` (HFR-006 iPad split). `HFRswift/Swift/MainWindow.swift:2032-2043` @ `87fe0d1c`: in the detail column's toolbar, `ToolbarItem(placement: .topBarLeading)` with `Image(systemName: "rectangle.leadinghalf.inset.filled")`, `accessibilityLabel("Masquer la liste")`, shown only when the destination uses a topic list (`iPadSidebarDestination.usesTopicList`), a topic is open (`activeTopicTarget != nil`) and the list is not already hidden (`!isTopicListHidden`); the tap calls `toggleTopicListVisibility()` (the list column collapses, the split goes detail-only).
- `docs/ux/cadrage-points-ux.md:26` (A5) lists the button as shipped legacy behaviour.

### SwiftHFR today (`dev-swift` @ `e672de7c`)
- No such button: no match for « Masquer la liste » or `leadinghalf` under `App/`. `Root/RootSidebarView.swift:62` is a three-column `NavigationSplitView`; its only `detailOnly` use is the Duo portrait rule (`DuoSplitColumns`, `RootSidebarView.swift:245-272`). The system's own column controls may already collapse the list: check that first on an iPad (and say so here) before writing code.

### Scope
- The button, its conditions and its symbol as legacy; the list comes back through the system's sidebar / column control.
- Out of scope: persisting the column visibility and the sidebar modes « Automatique / Épinglée / À la demande » (GAP-655, FEAT-016, #160, M5 on hold).

### Acceptance criteria
- On an iPad in regular width, with a category's, Favoris' or Messages' list and an open topic, a leading « Masquer la liste » button collapses the list column; it is absent with no topic open and once the list is hidden.
- The Duo inner screen keeps its portrait rule (GAP-215, GAP-217).
- A pure test pins the visibility rule; a `// Port of HFRswift/Swift/MainWindow.swift (… :2032-2043) @ 87fe0d1c` header.
