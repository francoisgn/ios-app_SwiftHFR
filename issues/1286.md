# Issue #1286: [SwiftHFR] Modern composer: past its maximum height (~12 lines at 100 %) the card keeps growing and slides behind the keyboard

### Summary
As a member writing a long reply with the modern composer, I want the card to stop growing once it reaches its maximum height (about 12 lines at text size 100 %) and scroll its text inside, so that it never slides behind the keyboard.

### Observed (operator, 2026-11-20, device, `dev-swift` build, Réglages › Taille du texte 100 %)
- The docked card grows correctly with each new line up to about **12 lines** (the #1136 behaviour, #1223).
- Past that point, new lines keep growing the card, **not only upwards but downwards too**: its bottom slides **behind the keyboard**.
- Expected: at that height the card **freezes**; further lines scroll inside the editor.

### Source
- #1223 (`ced6568d`, #1136): « the docked card grows with the reply up to the room the screen leaves », with `ComposerKeyboardRoom` (no room until the keyboard has shown once; a hidden keyboard keeps the last room). The cap is reached (~12 lines) but not enforced as a hard maximum on the card's frame, or the card's bottom is not pinned to the keyboard's top once the cap is reached.
- Same area as #1285 (card behind the keyboard after a smiley is inserted): one fix may cover both; keep two issues so each acceptance criterion is checked.

### Scope
- The card's height is `min(content height, room left above the keyboard)`; its bottom stays pinned to the keyboard's top (or the safe area when the keyboard is down) at every height; past the maximum, the editor scrolls and keeps the caret visible.
- The maximum follows the text size (about 12 lines at 100 %, fewer lines at larger sizes: it is a height, the room the screen leaves, not a line count).
- No change to the legacy composer.

### Acceptance criteria
- Typing lines past the maximum: the card height stays constant, its bottom never goes below the keyboard's top, the caret stays visible.
- iPhone and iPad, portrait and landscape, text size 100 % and one larger size.
- A test that fails without the fix (the pure height/room model: content taller than the room → height equals the room, bottom anchored).
- Device check listed on #893.

Analysis from a cloud session (read of `dev-swift` @ `0e8bb39`, no device).

**Read**
- Pure model: once the cap binds, `cardHeight == availableHeight` (`textCap` takes the buttons, chips, controls, insets and `editorInsets` off the room), so the text-only symptom is not in the model.
- The docked card is a `safeAreaInset(.bottom)` of the reader: its bottom is placed by SwiftUI's keyboard safe area. A bottom that slides behind the keyboard looks like lost keyboard avoidance, as in #1285. Suspect, not a conclusion.
- **One real defect found**: `ReplyComposerView.editorCard` uses `measured.editorHeight`, but `measured.chips` is only set on the detent's copy (`ReplyComposerView.swift:316`). With an inserted image the cap ignores the chips row, so the card is one chips row too tall for the room. A separate PR fixes that (`Refs #1286`); the text-only symptom stays open here.
