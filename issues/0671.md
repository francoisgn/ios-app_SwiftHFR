### Summary

As a reader on the unfolded iPhone Duo, I want the everyday topic actions (reload the page, go back) at the bottom of the screen, on the right, so that I reach them with my thumb without shifting my grip on a large screen.

### Scope: a broader study, not a one-off fix

We already know about this problem, and it goes beyond the two icons the user names. On large screens held in one hand (Duo inner display, iPad, Pro Max), most of the reader's actions sit in the top corners, out of thumb reach. So this issue is a **study led by us** of where the reader's actions belong on these screens, not a patch that moves reload and back to the letter of the request. The user's remark is one input, alongside our own use and the UX survey (#631).

The study should cover:
- **Inventory**: every action of the topic reader and of the lists (back, reload, pages, reply, search, options menu, sidebar/list toggle), how often each is used, and where it is today on iPhone, Duo folded / unfolded and iPad.
- **Reach**: which zones are easy for the thumb, in portrait and landscape, with one hand and two.
- **Layout options**: a bottom action bar, a bottom-trailing group, moving the menu down, gestures; compared with the HIG for iOS 27 and Liquid Glass (#545).
- **Consistency**: one rule for Duo, iPad and big iPhones where possible, and the same answer for the legacy app and SwiftHFR.
- **Outcome**: a short recommendation, then the fix lots (legacy and SwiftHFR) that follow from it.

### Report

User feedback on the 4.0.4 Duo update (PR #372, iPad split view on the inner display), reported by the operator on 2026-11-08, paraphrased: the Duo update is very good, but the useful icons such as reload and back should be at the **bottom of the icon column on the right**, because that is easier to reach with the thumb.

### What the reader does today (legacy, `origin/legacy/duo-inner-cover`, `HFRswift/Swift/MessagesView.swift`)

- **Back**: the navigation bar's back chevron, top left; in the split, the sidebar/list toggle, top left too.
- **Topic actions** (poll, search, the options menu): `ToolbarItemGroup(placement: .topBarTrailing)` (around line 5455), top right.
- **Bottom bar** (`ToolbarItem(placement: .bottomBar)`, around lines 5560-5620): previous/next page buttons, and the **refresh** button only when `shouldShowBottomRefreshButton` is true, i.e. on the last page **and** scrolled to the bottom (`page >= currentMaxPage && isWebContentAtBottom`, line 4905). Anywhere else, refresh is only in the top bar / menu.
- On the inner display the Duo is held like a small tablet, often with one hand at the bottom: the top corners are the hardest to reach.

### First lead (one option for the study)

- On the expanded Duo root (`RootTabView.usesSidebarRoot`, `EnvironmentValues.hfrIsExpandedPhoneRoot`), keep a thumb group at the **bottom trailing** corner of the topic column: **Actualiser** always visible (not only at the end of the last page), **Retour** (pops the column's stack, or goes back to the list in `detailOnly` portrait), then the existing reply `+`.
- Keep the top bar as is for the other actions (no duplicate menu), so the iPad layout does not change unless we decide it should (iPad users may want the same; one setting or the same rule for both, to decide).
- Respect the bottom safe area and the Liquid Glass bottom bar style already used (`topicBottomBarCircularIconButtonStyle`, `bottomBarStableHitTarget`, from PR #372 / moust4ki #4).
