### Summary

As a reader, I want any forum link (in a post, the clipboard or a universal link) to open the right screen at the right page and message, so that I never land on page 1 by mistake.

### Scope

Pure function `HFRLinkRoute.route(for: URL) -> HFRLinkRoute`: topic (category, post, page, `#t` anchor) from `forum2.php` and canonical `/hfr/<cat>/[<sub>/]<slug>-sujet_<post>_<page>.htm` (slug → category through `ForumCatalogue` aliases), topic list (category, sub-category path, page, filter), profile, MP list/thread, search, unknown/external. Only `forum.hardware.fr` (LESSONS 37, 43).
