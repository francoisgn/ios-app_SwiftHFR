### Summary

As LOT-097 (#189, BBCode preview renderer, ADR 0014) and LOT-152 (#242), we need the BBCode rendering pairs the first batch (#172, done in #400: `W61`, `R95c`, `R95d`) and the LOT-001 corpus (quote prefills `R41a`, `R41b`, `R42` against `R20`) do not cover, so that the preview reproduces HFR's rendering from evidence and every remaining gap is a measured difference, never a guess (LESSONS 35-36).

### What to capture

**Read-only**: every pair is a `POST /apercu.php` (form `apercu_form` of any composer: `hash_check`, `apercu_contenu` = the source, `apercu_smiley`, `config=hfr.inc`), which renders without posting (`R95d`). One `apercu_contenu` per numbered item below (or a few items per POST, each on its own line, as `W61` did), the source recorded in the `.meta.json` as for `R95d`. Only item 10 posts something (one message on the test topic, so the thread page and its preview can be compared in public).

1. **Smileys** with `apercu_smiley=0` (smileys on): standard codes (`:)`, `:D`, `:o`, `:jap:`, `:??:`), a personal `[:user]` and `[:user:1]` of the capturing account, a code glued to a word (`bonjour:)`, `oui:p`), a code next to a quote or an ampersand (`"oui")`, `&)`), codes inside `[code]`, `[fixed]`, `[url]…[/url]`, `[img]…[/img]` and inside a bare URL in text (`https://example.invalid/:/x`); the same source with `apercu_smiley=1`.
2. **Colour**: the toolbar's own syntax `[#FF0000]rouge[/#FF0000]` (`R40a` inserts `'['+bgColor+']'`; `[#0055aa]` is rendered on `R20`), a named colour the palette offers, a 3-digit hex, and `[color=red]` for comparison (`[color=#FF0000]` was ignored in `R95d`).
3. **Email**: `[email]a@example.invalid[/email]` and the same address in plain text.
4. **Bullets**: `[*]` on three consecutive lines, with and then without `[list]…[/list]`, a `[*]` in the middle of a line, an empty `[*]` line.
5. **Syntax colours**: `[cpp]` with several keywords (`int`, `return`, `for`, `#include`), a string literal, a `//` comment, three lines; `[code]` with the same content (is `[code]` coloured too? `R27b` colours a `table.code` whose tag is unknown); whether `[c]`, `[java]`, `[php]` or `[html]` are tags.
6. **URLs**: `[url]` and `[img]` with `http://`, `ftp://`, `javascript:alert(1)`, `tel:0123`, `mailto:a@example.invalid`, `//example.invalid/x` (protocol-relative), `www.example.invalid` without scheme, an `[img]` of a non-image page URL, a URL holding `&` and `"`; a bare `https://forum.hardware.fr/` in plain text (auto-link or not).
7. **Whitespace**: runs of 1 to 6 spaces in plain text, in `[fixed]` and in `[code]`; a line starting with one space, in text and in `[code]`; a message starting with spaces; a tab; an empty line; three consecutive line breaks; trailing line breaks; a line break inside `[fixed]`.
8. **Nesting and malformed**: `[quote]` in `[quote]`, `[spoiler]` in `[quote]`, `[code]` in `[quote]`, `[b]` across a line break, `[b][quote]x[/quote][/b]`, `[quote][b]x[/quote][/b]`, `[b]` inside `[url=…]`, `[url]` inside `[b]`, `[quote]` never closed, `[/quote]` alone, `[b][/b]`, `[b][b]x[/b][/b]`, `[b]` inside `[code]`, `[quotemsg=1,1,1]x[/quotemsg]` (the prefill shape of `R41a`) and the same with a real `numreponse,ref,author id` of the test topic.
9. **Limits**: a message longer than HFR accepts (what the preview and the post do), and 30 nested `[quote]` (also wanted by #268).
10. **Thread vs preview**: one public post on the test topic holding items 1, 4 and 7, with its `/apercu.php` answer, both published, so the "whitespace aside, opening `<p>`" difference measured on the private raw pages of #400 is asserted by a test, smileys included (the "Vue Rapide" reviews substitute none, `R41a`).
