### Summary

As a reader, I want the message layout to match legacy at three points: the signature is indented like the text, the text has a right margin, and quote rules are no heavier than the separators between replies. That way a long topic reads as calmly as in HFR+. (Operator feedback, 2026-11-17, device screenshots of SwiftHFR and legacy on the same page.)

### Observed (SwiftHFR, dev-swift) vs legacy @ 87fe0d1c

| | SwiftHFR | Legacy |
|---|---|---|
| **(A) Signature** | Its text starts at the screen edge, left of the message text | Indented like the text. The dotted rule above it still starts at the edge |
| **(B) Right margin** | The text runs to the right edge (« se p|oser » is cut against the edge) | A right margin, from `padding-right` / `margin-right` in `SuperHFRplus/Assets/HTML/style-liste-*.css` (e.g. `style-liste-light.css:225, 263, 506`) |
| **(D) Quote rules** | A full-width rule above and below each quote block, as thick as or thicker than the separator between replies | No such rules: the quote shows its left bar only |

The signature rules are `span.signature` in `style-liste-dark.css:45-65` and `style-liste-light.css:79-99`; the light and oled sheets have the same blocks.

### Scope

- **(A)** Indent the signature text to the message text's left inset. The dotted rule above it keeps starting at the edge (operator).
- **(B)** Add the right margin from legacy's sheet: cite the exact value used, per theme sheet if they differ.
- **(D)** **Parity:** remove the rules above and below quotes (operator option 1).
  - If removing them hurts readability in a theme, keep a rule no thicker or more contrasted than the reply separator (operator option 2). Say which option was taken and why in the PR.
  - A rule inside a reply must never be heavier than a rule between replies.
- Every theme, light, dark and oled, and the density settings.

### Acceptance criteria

  - the dotted rule starts at 0;
  - the right padding has the cited value;
  - no quote rule, or one at most as thick as the separator.
- An app-hosted WebKit test measuring (A) and (B) in a real `WKWebView`.
- Tests fail without the change.
