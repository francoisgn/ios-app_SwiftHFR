# Issue #1220: [SwiftHFR] Reader: the default light accent (cyan) is under 4.5:1 on the light surfaces, Doux included

- State: open
- Author: francoisgn · created 2026-11-19T06:11:44Z · updated 2026-11-19T06:11:44Z
- Labels: type: bug, priority: low, difficulty: easy, area: app, status: needs-triage, origin: parity

### Summary

As a reader in the light theme, I want links and the accent to be readable on the page, so that the default cyan accent is not a pale blue on white (found by the review of #1219, the « Doux » light style).

### Observed

- The reader's link and accent colour in light is the day accent: `ThemeAppearance.dayTint` at the default hue 190°, `rgb(89, 227, 255)`, saturation 0.65, brightness 1 (`AppSettingsView.swift:328` @ 21173e77, legacy's formula).
- Contrast of that cyan: **1.52:1 on white and 1.38:1 on the « Doux » page (#F5F4EF)**, far under 4.5:1. The same holds for most hues (brightness 1 is a light colour); the dark side has the equivalent issue for 161 of 360 hues (#1216).
- No current test checks the light accent against the light surfaces: `ThemeAppearanceTests` checks the night accent on dark surfaces for the default hue only, and `ThemeDefinitionTests` covers the gallery themes' own tokens, not Legacy's day accent.
- Legacy has the same colour, so this matches the legacy app; it is a readability gap, not a regression of #1213 (« Doux » is 0.14 lower than white).

### Scope

- Sweep the day accent over every hue against the light surfaces (`white`, the « Doux » page) with `ThemeColor.contrast(with:)`.
- Decide with the operator whether the reader's link colour in light uses a darker derivative of the accent (a deliberate difference to record) or keeps legacy's value.
- Coordinate with LOT-180 (single accent) and #1216.

### Acceptance criteria

- A test that pins the decision (a lower bound, or the list of hues that stay under it).

- #1213 and #1219 (review round 1, finding 4), #1216.
