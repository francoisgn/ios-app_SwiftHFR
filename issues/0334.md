### Summary

Capture on the Mac, with the LOT-001 (#38) kit (`tooling/fixtures/`), what the multi-quote cookie holds. `W14`'s metadata records only its name, `quoteshardwarefr-prive-<post>`, not its value or expiry. LOT-049 needs to know whether HFR clears the selection itself after a post, or whether the client must.

### Scope

- The `Set-Cookie` of a multi-quote selection, as in `W14`: record the value **shape** (redacted to a placeholder that keeps its structure) and the expiry or `Max-Age`.
- After a reply posted with that selection, record whether HFR's answer expires or rewrites the cookie.
- Never commit a real cookie value. The meta records the names and the shape only (FIXTURES.md).
