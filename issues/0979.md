### Summary

As a member, I want a tap on a section title in Favoris (e.g. « Apple ») to open that category's topic list, as legacy does, instead of **freezing the app**.

### Observed (operator, iPhone, 2026-11-16, current dense rows plus « Mode compact » on)

- Favoris tab, sectioned by category. A tap on a section title such as « Apple » does **not** open the category's topic list, and the **app freezes**.
- Steps: Réglages › Mode compact on, Favoris triés par catégories on; Favoris tab; tap « Apple ».

### Code path (read, not reproduced)

- The header button calls `openCategory(source)` (`App/SwiftHFR/Navigation/TopicListView.swift:228-230`).
- That reaches `NavigationModel.openCategory` (`NavigationModel.swift:97-100`), which sets `topicFilter?.flag = .favorites`: it **also writes the global choice**, as legacy `Favorites.swift:357` @ 87fe0d1c does. It then sets `openedCategory` to push the list on the Favoris stack.
- Hypothesis to verify, not a conclusion: the global filter write makes the Favoris list re-evaluate or reload while the push is in flight. That gives a SwiftUI update loop or a main-thread wait, possibly only with compact density (`densityHeaderMargin`) or when the filter already equals `.favorites`.

### Expected

The category's topic list is pushed on the Favoris stack, with the Favoris filter, as legacy does. No freeze, in compact or regular density, sectioned or not. On a regular-width iPad, `RootModel.showCategoryFromFavorites` switches to Catégories.
