### Summary

As a user who picks the **light** theme (Réglages › Thème › Thème : Clair), I want the settings below it to be about the light theme: a choice between **white** and **soft**, warmer tones (an off-white, gentler than pure white). Today the screen keeps offering the dark theme's options (« Style du thème sombre » Standard / Plus sombre / Noir OLED, « Luminosité du thème sombre ») and shows a dark preview, which mean nothing in light mode. (Operator feedback, 2026-11-18, device screenshot of Réglages › Thème in light mode with the dark-style menu open.)

### Today (dev-swift)

- `ThemeSettingsView` always shows the accent hue, the dark-style picker (`DarkStyle`: standard, darker, OLED; `Packages/HFRKit/Sources/HFRModels/Settings/DarkStyle.swift`), the dark preview and the dark-brightness slider, whatever « Thème » is set to.
- There is no light-style setting: light surfaces are the system ones (`systemBackground`, `ThemeAppearance.readerBackground`, `ThemeAppearance.swift:177-184`) and the reader's light palette (`reader.css:7`, `--background: #ffffff`).

### Scope

1. **The screen follows « Thème »:**
   - **Clair**: show a « Style du thème clair » picker (Blanc, Doux), a light preview, and no dark-only controls.
   - **Sombre**: today's dark controls.
   - **Automatique / Système**: show both groups, each titled.
   - The accent hue stays in every case.
2. **New light style « Doux »**: warm, low-glare surfaces for the screens and the reader.
   - Page background around #F5F4EF, raised surfaces a step lighter, quotes a step darker. Text and secondary text are re-checked for 4.5:1 or more, links and the accent on the new surfaces too (`ThemeDefinitionTests` and the reader contrast tests).
   - The exact values are proposed in the PR with a screenshot, for the operator to validate.
   - « Blanc » is today's look, unchanged, and stays the default.
3. **Persistence**: a new device setting `theme_light_style` (Blanc = 0 default, Doux = 1), with tolerant decoding (ESSENTIEL persistence rule). It is scoped like `theme_dark_style` (ADR 0013: device). The Legacy theme only; a gallery theme with its own palette ignores it, like the dark style.
4. The reader, the native lists (via #842's `ThemedList` tokens, as a no-op for Blanc) and the settings preview follow « Doux ».
