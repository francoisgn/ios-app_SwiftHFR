### Summary

As a member, I want a third button at the top right of **Favoris**, next to refresh and « collapse/expand all categories », that switches between **« Non lus »** (only favourites with new messages) and **« Tous »**. With it I can see what is new at a glance. (Operator feedback, 2026-11-17.)

### Today and legacy

- dev-swift Favoris: the top bar has refresh plus collapse/expand, and no read-state filter. Presentation lives in `App/SwiftHFR/Navigation/FavoritesPresentation.swift`.
- Legacy @ 87fe0d1c `HFRswift/Swift/Favorites.swift:581-594` filters sections only by collapse state and the empty-sections setting (`shouldShowEmptyFavoriteSections`). There is **no unread/all toggle**, so this is a **deliberate gap** (operator).
- Related: #593 (closed), a consistent topic-list filter (favourite, flags, read state) in categories. Reuse its read-state notion and its look, and do not invent a second one.

### Scope

1. A toggle button in the Favoris top bar, trailing, beside refresh and collapse/expand, with two states:
   - « Tous »: today's view;
   - « Non lus »: only rows with unread messages. « Unread » is the same row state the list already shows (the unread count or marker). Cite the field used; do not guess what HFR marks as new.
2. In « Non lus », a section left with no unread row is hidden, unless the « empty sections » setting asks to show it. Super-favourites follow the same rule.
3. The choice persists across launches (an `AppStorage`/`SettingKey`, per device), default **« Tous »**.
4. The icon shows its state, for example `line.3.horizontal.decrease.circle` and `.fill`, with the accessibility label « Afficher : non lus / tous ». Keep it to one tap, no menu.
5. Layout: it must fit with the account button moving to the left (#1057), the title pill (#1012) and the Duo cover screen's bottom-bar refresh.
6. A ledger `gap-new` row: a deliberate difference from legacy.

### Acceptance criteria

- `FavoritesPresentation` tests: filtering keeps only unread rows and drops sections left empty, honours the empty-sections setting, and covers the super-favourites.
- A setting round-trip test: the default is « Tous ».
- A view test that the button exists and toggles.
