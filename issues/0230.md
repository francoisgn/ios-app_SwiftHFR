## francoisgn · 2026-11-07T11:44:12Z

**Scope update: operator decisions, 2026-11-07** (`ext/francoisgn-suborch-1`, after the operator's device test).

The operator saw one notification for 1 to N unread MPs, and none for each new reply. That notification came from the **legacy app** (SwiftHFR has no notification code yet). Its behaviour must change in our implementation. Legacy notifies only when the home-page count of unread **conversations** goes up (`MPBackgroundService.swift:93-157` @ 2440d79), so a reply in an already-unread conversation sends nothing. Legacy also uses one fixed text and no `threadIdentifier`.

Nothing the operator reports goes to M5: all of the following stays in this lot (M4).

**Detection**
- The home `GET /` stays the first, cheap request.
- On every due check, LOT-140 also fetches the MP list page 1 (`PageFetcher.privateMessages(page: 1)`). That is at most 2 requests per check, and never a thread page: a thread page would mark it read (E25).
- A conversation is news when it is newly unread, **or** when its `repliesCount` or `lastPost` grew while it was already unread.
- The ledger (LOT-091) needs a per-thread `repliesCount` / `lastPost` watermark for MPs. The follow-up issue is linked below.
- The first check records the baseline silently.

**Notifications**
- One notification per conversation with news.
  - `identifier` = `mp-<topicNumber>`, so a later reply replaces it instead of stacking.
  - `threadIdentifier` = `mp-<topicNumber>`. This brings this part of #509 into M4.
- A burst of more than about 5 conversations collapses into one summary.
- **Text (operator decision):**
  - one-to-one MP: title = the interlocutor, body = the MP's title;
  - group MP: title = the MP's title.
  - No message content for now.
  - Both come from the MP list row; nothing else is fetched.
- **Locked screen:** iOS's own "Show Previews" setting hides the detail. The app registers a category with `hiddenPreviewsBodyPlaceholder` = "%u nouveaux messages privés".
- Interruption level `.active`, sound `.default`, never `.timeSensitive`.
- **Tap** opens the conversation (`HFRLinkRoute.privateMessageThread`); a summary notification opens the MP list.
- **Badge:** as legacy (`mp_badge_enabled`, the unread-conversation count).

**Check interval** (operator: "on fait avec ce qu'on a")
- Réglages › Notifications MP › "Vérifier les messages": 15 min, 30 min, 1 h, 2 h, 4 h, Jamais. Default 30 min.
- Footer: « Au plus souvent toutes les X min. iOS décide du moment réel ; ce délai est un minimum, pas une garantie. »
- `earliestBeginDate = now + interval`, plus the ledger's `markCheckedIfDue(minimumInterval:)` guard, shared with LOT-141.
- No Do Not Disturb option: the app cannot read Focus, and the system already filters notifications per Focus.
- Show « Dernière vérification : hh:mm », plus a button to iOS's notification settings.

**Acceptance additions**
- A reply in an already-unread conversation produces a notification (tested on captured list pages).
- Two checks with no change produce nothing.
- Locked and unlocked texts are checked on a device, including Show Previews = Never.
- No pseudo and no subject in logs.

**Capture needed** (no guessing): two MP list pages around one reply in an already-unread conversation. The capture issue is linked below.
