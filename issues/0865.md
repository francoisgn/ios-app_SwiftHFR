# Issue #865: [SwiftHFR] Top bar: the title scrolls away when scrolling down (buttons stay floating) and comes back when scrolling up, on every list screen

### Summary

As a reader scrolling a list or a topic, I want the top bar's title to get out of the way when I scroll down, leaving only its buttons floating, and to come back when I scroll up, so that the content gets the whole screen as in most iOS apps. (Operator feedback, 2026-11-13, device screenshot of Favoris › Drapeaux. It applies to every scrolling screen of the app, not only Drapeaux.)

### Observed

The title area ("Drapeaux", with refresh and "⋯" to its right) stays fixed at the top while the list scrolls, and takes space from the content.

### Expected

- **Scrolling down:** the title area goes away and the content takes its place. Only the buttons stay, floating (Liquid Glass).
- **Scrolling up:** the title comes back.
- Same behaviour on every screen that scrolls content: categories, topic lists, Favoris, Drapeaux, MPs, search, settings lists. The reader is to check separately, against #820 and #641 (group R).

### Platform reference (sources; HIG page wording not yet read)

- WWDC25 "Build a UIKit app with the new design" (https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/284/): under the iOS 26 design, large titles sit at the top of the content scroll view and scroll with the content under the bar, while the bar's buttons stay.
- WWDC25 "Get to know the new design system" (https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/356/): Liquid Glass with scroll edge effects instead of hard dividers.
- The tab bar side exists already: `tabBarMinimizeBehavior(.onScrollDown)` behind the legacy setting `tabbar_minimize_on_scroll` (`App/SwiftHFR/Root/RootTabsView.swift:39`). The top bar has no equivalent.
- UIKit's `hidesBarsOnSwipe` (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uinavigationcontroller/barhideonswipegesturerecognizer) hides the whole bar, buttons included. It is not what is asked.
- **To do before coding:** read the HIG pages "Navigation bars" and "Toolbars" and cite their wording in the PR.

### Likely cause, to verify

About 26 screens force `.navigationBarTitleDisplayMode(.inline)` / `.toolbarTitleDisplayMode(.inline…)`, which pins a small title in the bar. The standard large title, `.automatic` or `.large`, scrolls away with the content and leaves the buttons. Check the legacy look at `87fe0d1c` and the M4 Liquid Glass work (#545, LOT-185/186) before changing it, so that parity and the reader chrome are not broken.
