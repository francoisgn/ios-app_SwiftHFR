### Summary

As a user of Noël soft, I want the seasonal images trimmed exactly, so that none of them loses its bottom outline or keeps a transparent row at the top. `tooling/art/noel-crossings/derive.swift` crops one row too low (found in the review of PR #1438 for LOT-194).

### Scope

- `tooling/art/noel-crossings/derive.swift:40-41` flips the bounding box as if the bitmap context's rows ran bottom-up (`y: height - 1 - maxY`), but the rows of that buffer already run top to bottom like the `CGImage`'s. The crop is shifted by `height - 1 - maxY - minY` rows: it keeps transparent rows at the top and cuts the bottom outline of each subject (measured on the Marianne render: 9 rows, 171 opaque pixels in the last row).
- Fix: `y: minY`, re-run `swift tooling/art/noel-crossings/derive.swift` from the repository root, check the bottom outline of the sleigh, the snow pile, the elf, the tree and the garland (the imagesets of `App/SwiftHFR/Assets.xcassets/Noel/`).
- The 14 July images (LOT-194, `tooling/art/fete-nationale/derive.swift`) are fixed in PR #1438; do not touch them here.
