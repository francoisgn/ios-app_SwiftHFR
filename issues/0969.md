As a reader, I want the menu that opens from a tap on a member's avatar or pseudo to be anchored on that pseudo, like the message's « + » menu (#924), so that it appears where I tapped and not in the top-left corner of the screen.

### Observed (operator, iPhone, 2026-11-16)

- A tap on the avatar or pseudo shows the author menu « Post de <pseudo> » (Profil, MP, Blacklist, Whitelist) as a panel in the **top-left corner**, over the title bar, far from the tapped post.
- The « + » menu of the same post opens **anchored at the tapped spot**, as the system's horizontal callout (Citer, Citer □, Link, Alerter).
- Screenshots kept locally by the operator (they show member pseudos, so they are not attached).

### Wanted

- The author menu is anchored on the tapped pseudo or avatar (`ReaderMessageMenuAnchor`, as the « + » menu does), in the same visual family.
- A vertical list may stay if the system's callout cannot hold four items with icons. It must sit next to the pseudo.

If the operator's build already includes cd049542 and the menu still shows for a normal post, that is a second bug, to check in the same PR.
