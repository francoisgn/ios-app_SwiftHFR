### Summary

As a reader paging through a list, I want the « page x / y » pill to be as easy to hit as the ‹ › buttons next to it, and to look like one control of the bar, so that the pager is one consistent Liquid Glass group.

### Rule

- "As a general rule, a button needs a hit region of at least 44x44 pt" — [HIG › Buttons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/buttons) (Best practices; page updated 2025-12-16).
- "Prefer using standard components in a toolbar. By default, standard buttons, text fields, headers, and footers have corner radii that are concentric with bar corners." — [HIG › Toolbars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/toolbars) (updated 2025-12-16).
- "If you apply Liquid Glass effects to a custom control, do so sparingly." — [HIG › Materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) (Liquid Glass; updated 2025-09-09).

### What the app does (dev-swift @ `7a61018f`)

- `App/SwiftHFR/Navigation/PagerPageMenu.swift:96-97`: the pill is `.padding(.horizontal, 12).padding(.vertical, 6).glassEffect(.regular, in: .capsule)`, the label of a `Menu` with `.buttonStyle(.plain)` (`:79`): body text plus 12 pt of padding, with no `minHeight` or `contentShape`.
