### Summary

As a reader in light mode with the default accent, I want the links in a message body to be legible, so that I can see and tap them: the accent colour is 1.5:1 on white (WCAG AA asks 4.5:1 for text).

### Measured

Real reader (`WKWebView`, the theme's `readerScript` installed with `ThemeAppearance.dayTint(hue:)` / `nightTint(hue:)`), contrast of a body link (`.content a`, colour `--action`) on the page, « Blanc » light style, modern and classic alike (found while handling the review of #1366, which first quoted 4.0:1 from the CSS fallback `#007aff`, a value the app never paints):

| Accent hue | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| 0° | 3.08 | 3.71 |
| 60° | 1.07 | 11.19 |
| 120° | 1.32 | 8.97 |
| 190° (default) | **1.52** | 7.76 |
| 240° | 4.89 | **2.24** |
| 300° | 2.58 | 4.46 |

So with the default hue the links of every message are nearly invisible in light mode, and a blue accent does the same in dark.

### Scope

Text drawn in `--action` across the reader page (body links, `a { color: var(--action) }` in `reader.css`), not the secondary texts of the allowlist surfaces (#1362, #1366). The accent is the user's choice (legacy hue slider, `ThemeAppearance.dayTint`: S 0.65, B 1): a fix has to keep the hue recognisable, for instance a text-only variant of the accent darkened (light) or lightened (dark) until it reaches 4.5:1 on the page, while the accent keeps painting fills and the app tint. Gallery themes already hold their `accentText` to 4.5:1 (`THEMES.md` §3). The operator decides how far the look may move from legacy.

### Acceptance criteria

- A body link reaches 4.5:1 on the page in light and dark, modern and classic, « Blanc » and « Doux », at every hue of the slider (parametrised hosted test, as `ReaderContrastAccentAndNestingWebTests` in #1366).
- Links in tinted surfaces (allowlist, moderation, own message, quotes) too.
- Legacy look decision recorded on the PR.
