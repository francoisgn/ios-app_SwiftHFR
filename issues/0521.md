### Summary

As a contributor, I want every app test to wait on a condition, not on time, so that a slow simulator never turns a CI run red (the class of #467 and #514).

### Scope

The Opus review of #517 (finding 5) lists suites that still spin or sleep:
- `App/SwiftHFRTests/.../ContentLoaderTests.swift:12`: an unbounded `Task.yield` spin;
- `PseudoListsSnapshotTests.swift:118`;
- `ReplyComposerDraftTests.swift:99, 215` and `ReplyComposerSendTests.swift:133`: 100 ms sleeps.

Replace each one with a signal (`CheckGate`, `App/SwiftHFRTests/Accounts/AccountTestSupport.swift`) and keep every assertion. A time limit stays only as a suite-level backstop against a hang (#366, #468, #517).

### Acceptance criteria
- [ ] No `Task.sleep` and no `yield` loop used to wait in `App/SwiftHFRTests` (grep listed in the PR).
- [ ] App job green.
