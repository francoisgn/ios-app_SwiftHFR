# Issue #308: HFRClient: never overwrite a fresher hash_check after a concurrent login (TopicFlagFlow, PostingFlow)

- State: closed
- Author: francoisgn · created 2026-10-28T11:02:18Z · updated 2026-11-09T14:59:12Z
- Labels: type: bug, priority: medium, difficulty: easy, area: client, origin: initial-port

### Summary
A flow that records the page's `hash_check` after its GET can overwrite a newer token if LOT-043's `adoptLogin` replaced the session's cookies and token meanwhile. The next POST then gets the bad-token answer (W17b). Found by the Fable review of LOT-051 (PR #263).

Scope
- `TopicFlagFlow.swift:98` and `PostingFlow.swift:130`: compare the session identity (`userKey()`) before and after the request, and skip `updateHashCheck` if it changed. The alternative is a documented app-level rule of one flow at a time per session; pick one and record it in ARCHITECTURE or the LOT-043 spec.
- Also, in `TopicFlagError.rejected`, replace "nothing changed" with "by LOT-037's shape rule": no addflag/delflag refusal is captured.

Acceptance criteria: a test with a stub transport where a login is adopted between the GET and the token update shows that the fresher token is kept, and that the test fails without the change.
