### Summary
As ezzz or a parity checker, I want a CI check that a legacy pointer lands on the symbol it names, so that a pointer whose sha was moved without its lines (AGENTS §2) is caught when it is written, not by a later review. Split from #1197 (the comments it listed are fixed by its PR); the class fix is the checker.

### What exists
About 30 pointers were spot-checked in the #775 review; one was wrong (`AppIconSettingsView.swift:4`, `2440d79` lines under `@ 87fe0d1c`), and nothing in CI would catch the next one.

### Scope
`tooling/docs/check_legacy_pointers.py`, run in the **Docs consistency** job: for each pointer of the form `<path> (<Symbol> … :a-b) @ <sha>` (the formats of AGENTS §4 and the `// Port of …` comments of `App/`) check that the named symbol appears in lines a-b of `git show <sha>:<path>`. Start with the one format of the `// Port of` header comments, report the others as unchecked, and never fail on a pointer it cannot parse.

### Acceptance
- The checker's own test, on the `AppIconSettingsView` pointer as it was before #1197 (`lines 360-365, applyIconSelection 213-224 @ 87fe0d1c`): red; green with the corrected pointer (411-416, 221-231).
- Runs in the Docs consistency job without a full legacy checkout (`git fetch origin legacy/duo-inner-cover` or a shallow fetch of the cited shas).
