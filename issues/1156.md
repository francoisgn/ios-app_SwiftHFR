### Summary

As a user writing a reply, I want every composer button to have a comfortable hit region, so that I do not miss the small ✕ of an image chip or a formatting cell.

### Rule

"As a general rule, a button needs a hit region of at least 44x44 pt — in visionOS, 60x60 pt — to ensure that people can select it easily" — [HIG › Buttons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/buttons) (Best practices; page updated 2025-12-16). Default / minimum control size on iOS: "44x44 pt | 28x28 pt" — [HIG › Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility) (Mobility; updated 2025-06-09).

### What the app does (dev-swift @ `7a61018f`)

- `App/SwiftHFR/Composer/ComposerImageChips.swift:70-79`: the chip's ✕ is `Image(systemName: "xmark.circle.fill")….font(.system(size: 18))` in a `.buttonStyle(.plain)` button with no frame or `contentShape` (`.padding(2)` is outside the button): an 18 pt target, and a fixed size that ignores Dynamic Type.
- `App/SwiftHFR/Composer/PollCreationSheet.swift:91-98`: `Image(systemName: "minus.circle.fill")` with `.buttonStyle(.plain)` and no frame.
- `App/SwiftHFR/Composer/FormattingToolbar.swift:12`: `cellSize = (density == .compact ? 40 : 48) * dynamicType * scale.factor`; `ComposerControlRow.swift:97-99` clamps to 44 pt only inside the modern card (`inCard ? max(cell, ComposerHeader.hitSize) : cell`). The default composer and every iPad get 40 pt in compact density, and 33.6 pt (regular) or 28 pt (compact) at the 70 % text size.

### Legacy at `87fe0d1c`

- Image chip: no counterpart (new with #653).
- Poll: same bare `minus.circle.fill` plain button, `HFRswift/Swift/TopicPollDraft.swift:84-92`.
- Control row: a fixed 34 pt cell (`HFRswift/Swift/AnswerView.swift:1684-1688`): legacy is smaller.

### Class

**(a) defect**: touch targets are an accessibility matter, whatever legacy does. Not a regression for the control row.

### Proposed fix and check

- `.frame(minWidth: 44, minHeight: 44).contentShape(.rect)` on the two icon buttons, as `ComposerEditorCard`'s toggle already does; size the chip glyph with a text style or `@ScaledMetric`.
- Apply the `max(cell, 44)` clamp to both chromes (drop the `inCard` condition); the glyph can stay scaled.
- Check: extend the `cellSize` unit test (compact, 70 %, `inCard: false`); a hosted layout test on the two button frames. Mac check: the two capsules of the control row still fit one line on the narrowest iPhone.
