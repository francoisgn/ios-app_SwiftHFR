### Summary

As a reader, I want the top bar (back button, title pill and trailing button group, on every screen) to sit on a light, translucent, slightly blurred band, the way Liquid Glass apps do (the operator's reference is the Claude iOS app). Today it sits on a band that is almost black, so the bar reads as a heavy dark strip over the list. (Operator feedback, 2026-11-18, device screenshots: the Apple category topic list in dark mode, with the dark band marked in yellow left and right of the « Apple » pill, next to the Claude app's top bar.)

### Observed (dev-swift build of 2026-11-18, iPhone, dark mode)

- On the category topic list (and, per the operator, everywhere), the area behind the floating glass controls is a dense dark gradient. The list content under it (the « Tous / Favoris / Suivis / Lus » segments) is barely visible.
- In the reference app, the content under the bar stays visible: dimmed and blurred, not blacked out.

### Analysis so far (code read on dev-swift, not measured)

- No hand-made bar background was found: no `toolbarBackground`, `UINavigationBarAppearance`, `scrollEdgeEffectStyle` or material behind the title in `App/SwiftHFR` (`ScreenTitle.swift`, `TopicListView.swift:61-90`). The band is therefore most likely the **system scroll-edge effect** of the navigation bar, over our black list background and with the large-title and inset layout, or a stacked effect (a `safeAreaBar(edge: .top)` at `TopicListView.swift:66` adds its own edge effect even when it is empty).
- To find on the Mac: which layer draws the band (view debugger), and whether the empty `safeAreaBar` and the `safeAreaInset(edge: .top)` double it.

### Scope

- The top band follows **Apple's default Liquid Glass behaviour**: the soft scroll-edge effect, with content visible and blurred under the bar. Do not add a custom blur or material: stay within Apple behaviour (operator rule, #1070).
- On every main screen: Catégories and category lists, Favoris, Messages, Recherche, Plus, the reader and Réglages; light and dark.
- Remove whatever darkens the band beyond the system default, for example an always-present empty `safeAreaBar`, a stacked edge effect, or an opaque background colour reaching under the bar.
- Legacy theme pixel tests (LOT-188) stay green. A theme colour may tint the band only through the system effect.
