# Issue #912: [SwiftHFR] Reader: a picture inside a link opens the linked full-size image, or warns before opening an external page

### Summary

As a reader, when I tap a picture that is a link, I want the app to follow what the link means. A link to a larger image (a lighter preview shown in the post to keep the forum fluid) opens that image at full resolution in the viewer. A link to anything else warns me before leaving and opens it in the browser I chose. (Operator feedback, 2026-11-15.)

### Today (dev-swift)

- `App/SwiftHFR/Reader/Resources/reader.js:79-91`: a tap on a loaded `img.hfrplusimg` posts its own `src` (`data-viewer-src`) to the viewer (LOT-109). The `<a href>` that wraps it is **ignored**.
- `ImageHostTable.fullSizeURL` (LOT-056, `Packages/HFRKit/Sources/HFRNetwork/ImageHosts/ImageHostTable.swift:64`) upgrades a known thumbnail to its full image (captured shapes: `img3.super-h.fr` thumbnails, `reho.st` `/preview/` → `/self/`, with the `Referer` of `T30`–`T33`). This works only for the hosts in the table, whatever the link says.
- A picture linking to a page opens the picture, never the page. The link is reachable only through the long-press menu (#615).
- No warning before leaving the app. The « Navigateur par défaut » setting exists (LOT-137, `SettingKey.opensLinksInDefaultBrowser`, legacy key `open_links_in_default_browser`).

### Requested (operator, 2026-11-15)

1. **Picture inside a link to an image:** the tap opens the **link target** in the viewer, at full resolution.
   - The target counts as an image when:
     - (a) it is an identified rehost shape (`ImageHostTable`), or
     - (b) its path ends in a known image extension.
   - It is fetched through policy C and the cookie-less client like every viewer image (consent, `Referer` table).
   - When the target fails, the viewer falls back to the preview.
2. **Picture inside a link to anything else:** the tap shows a short confirmation:
   - the message « Ce lien ouvre une page externe » with the domain;
   - the buttons **Ouvrir** and **Annuler**.

   Ouvrir opens the link per « Navigateur par défaut » (in-app Safari view, or the default browser). A forum link keeps LOT-137's in-app route, with no warning.
3. **Picture not inside a link:** unchanged (today's viewer).
