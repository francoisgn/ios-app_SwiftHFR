# Issue #1184: [SwiftHFR] storage: `PseudoListStore`, `ReadingPositionStore` and `DraftStore` re-implement `SmallStoreState` and miss its guards (version probe, empty account, account key)

### Summary
As the maintainer, I want the three older stores to follow the same load rules as `SmallStoreState`, so that "update the app" is told apart from "file damaged", an empty account is refused, and one account has one key whatever its spelling. The duplication itself is for #836; the diverging behaviour is the finding.

### Scenarios
1. A v2 pseudo-list or reading-positions file whose shape changed is reported as `loadFailed(<decoding error>)` instead of `unsupportedVersion(2)`.
2. `block("x", account: "")` stores a blocklist under the key `""`.
3. Positions recorded under "Alice" are not found under "alice" (the app already works around it).

### Evidence (`7a61018f`)
- `Packages/HFRKit/Sources/HFRStorage/SmallStores/Core/FileSmallStorePersistence.swift:24-27`: reads the version first ("a newer format may not decode as this one, and must still be reported as newer") and refuses `version < 1`.
- `PseudoLists/FilePseudoListPersistence.swift:17-20` and `ReadingPositions/FileReadingPositionPersistence.swift:17-20` decode the whole document directly.
- `PseudoLists/PseudoListStore.swift:70`: `document.blocklists[AccountKey.make(account), default: []]` with no empty-account check (small stores use `AccountKey.forWriting`, which throws `.emptyAccount`).
- `ReadingPositions/ReadingPositionStore.swift:38,43,82,100,180`: `document.accounts[account]` by the raw string; `App/SwiftHFR/Accounts/AccountDataEraser.swift:99-106` removes "every spelling of the account's key".

### Fix
Move the three stores onto `SmallStoreState` + `FileSmallStorePersistence` (file names, encoders and date strategies kept; positions keys migrated on read to `AccountKey`).

### Tests (fail before the fix)
- `FilePseudoListPersistenceTests/newerVersionWithAnotherShapeIsReportedAsNewer`: `{"version":2,"lists":{}}` gives `.unsupportedVersion(2)` (today `loadFailed`).
- `ReadingPositionStoreTests/positionIsFoundWhateverTheAccountCase`.
