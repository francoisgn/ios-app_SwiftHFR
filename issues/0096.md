### Summary

As a maintainer, I want one pseudo normaliser in HFRStorage instead of three private copies, so that the blocklist, drafts and accounts compare pseudos by one stated rule.

### Scope

Replace the three copies in `Packages/HFRKit/Sources/HFRStorage/` by one normaliser, and state the rule each caller keeps:
- `PseudoLists/PseudoNormalizer.swift` (public): U+200B removed, then lower-cased (legacy `BlackList.m`);
- `Drafts/DraftNameNormalizer.swift` (internal): the same rule;
- `AccountBook.normalized` in `Accounts/AccountBook.swift:124-130` (internal): every format-category scalar removed and white space trimmed, no lower-casing (`samePseudo` lower-cases when comparing).

**Constraint from ADR 0013 §7**: the per-account key form is frozen (U+200B removed, then lower-cased, nothing else). LOT-172 keeps its own `accountKey` function: this lot leaves it alone and changes no stored key. HFRStorage cannot import HFRParsing, so `HFRText.normalizedPseudo` cannot be reused.
