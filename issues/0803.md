**Operator decision, 2026-11-11 (supersedes B1): the legacy group style everywhere.** The operator compared with legacy @ `87fe0d1c` on his device (Favoris, a category's topic list, the MP list). Legacy uses one style on all three:

- Unread badge on the right; the MP list has avatars on the left and a « non lu » chip.

The goal is consistency through **one row look and one block style**, not by removing the frame. This is legacy's look, so there is no deliberate gap: the PR cites legacy file:line. The device screenshots are kept locally and not posted here, since they show real pseudos.
