### Summary

As a reader with a long topic title, and above all with VoiceOver or a large text size, I want the full title shown by the title pill to stay until I dismiss it and to be fully readable on an iPhone, so that the only way to read a truncated title does not disappear while I read it.

### Rule

- "Minimize use of time-boxed interface elements. Views and controls that auto-dismiss on a timer can be problematic for people who need longer to process information, and for people who use assistive technologies that require more time to traverse the interface. Prefer dismissing views with an explicit action." — [HIG › Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility) (Cognitive; page updated 2025-06-09).
- "Avoid displaying popovers in compact views. […] Reserve popovers for wide views; for compact views, use all available screen space by presenting information in a full-screen modal view like a sheet instead." — [HIG › Popovers](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/popovers) (iOS, iPadOS).
