### Summary

As a member, I want my super-favourites, collapsed favourites sections, favourite smileys, smiley search history and image upload history kept between launches, so that the app remembers my choices.

### Scope

Small HFRStorage stores, each with pure value logic, a persistence protocol, and file and in-memory implementations, placed in the tiers of ADR 0013:
- **Super-favourites** (topic ids), per HFR account (account key, ADR 0013 §7). This lot is the local copy; iCloud sync is LOT-172 (#311).
- **Favourites layout**: collapsed sections only, device-only. ADR 0013 drops the legacy visible/hidden category order (`arrayCategoriesVisibleOrder`/`HiddenOrder`, no HFRswift writer).
- **App-local favourite smileys** (`{code, image URL, date added}`), per HFR account.
- **Smiley search history** (JSON, at most 50 entries, device-only).
- **Rehost upload history** (JSON, device-only, personal data).

Importing the legacy data is LOT-067 (#92, ADR 0013 §5), not this lot.
