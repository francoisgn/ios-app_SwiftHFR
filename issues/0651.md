# Issue #651: [SwiftHFR] MP list: read titles in the secondary colour, unread in the primary, in every theme (legacy parity) (M4)

- State: closed
- Author: francoisgn · created 2026-11-08T06:26:41Z · updated 2026-11-08T13:15:40Z
- Labels: type: bug, priority: medium, difficulty: easy, area: app, beta-test finding
- Source: https://github.com/ezzz/HFRnow/issues/651

### Summary
As a member, I want my MP list to show at a glance which conversations I have read, as the legacy app did: unread ones stand out, read ones fade, whatever the theme.

Milestone: M4 (operator, 2026-11-08)

### Legacy behaviour (operator's description, checked in the code)
Three states, independent of each other:
1. **Unread by the correspondent(s):** a leading "non lu" capsule (`MPListView.swift:427-437` @ `2440d79`), at half strength once I have read the thread (`:436`). SwiftHFR already does this (#576, GAP-111).
2. **Unread by me:** the title is semibold in the primary colour, the strongest contrast of the theme: white on a dark theme, black on a light one (`MPListView.swift:469`; `Common.swift:3119-3120` @ `2440d79`, `.foregroundStyle(isVisited ? .secondary : .primary)`).
3. **Read by me:** the title is regular in the secondary colour, grey in black and white themes (same lines).

### What SwiftHFR does today (dev-swift, `App/SwiftHFR/Messages/MessageRowView.swift`)
- State 1: ported.
- State 2: semibold title, plus an accent dot and an accent row tint (GAP-227, #576).
- State 3: regular weight, but the title stays in the **primary** colour. The grey of a read title is missing, so read and unread rows differ only by weight, dot and tint.

### Scope
- A read thread's title uses the secondary colour, an unread one the primary colour, as in legacy. Keep the weight, the "non lu" capsule, and the #576 dot and tint unless the operator says otherwise.
- Every colour theme: light, dark, the LOT-122 dark styles (darker, OLED black), classic and modern, Increase Contrast. The secondary colour must keep text contrast at 4.5:1 or more on each background, including the tinted unread row and the iPad selection (#576, #617, #644).
- Check whether the topic lists follow the same rule: legacy's `TopicListRowView` is shared by topics and MPs (`Common.swift:3120`).

### Acceptance criteria
- On the MP list, an unread title is primary and semibold, a read title secondary and regular, in every theme above.
- Contrast of both title colours measured on each background (unit test on the theme palette, and a Mac check).
- A test fails if a read title is drawn in the primary colour.
