As the team, we want a short technical spec of the survey form, so that it is built without back-and-forth.

- Formats: single choice, card sort, reaction cards, free text; every question optional.
- End screen with live statistics; anti-duplicate without account, rate limiting, honeypot; CSV and JSON export.
