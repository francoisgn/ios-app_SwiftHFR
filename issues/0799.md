### Summary
As a reader on iPhone, I want the side margins of the card lists to follow the list density setting, so that the compact density gives the text more of the screen width.

### Context
Operator, device test 2026-11-11 (iPhone, Favoris): the side margins are very wide. Two margins add up, about 36 pt from the screen edge to the text:
- **Outer margin, card to screen edge (~20 pt):** the system margin of `.listStyle(.insetGrouped)`, larger on large iPhones.
- **Inner margin, card edge to text (16 pt regular / 12 pt compact):** `ListDensity.rowInsets` (`App/SwiftHFR/Settings/ListDensity.swift:18-23`), taken from legacy (`HFRswift/Swift/Favorites.swift:429` @ `87fe0d1c`). Legacy uses the same inset-grouped list, so it has the same wide margins.

### Decision (operator, 2026-11-11)
Tie the margins to the density setting:
- **Regular:** keep today's margins.
- **Compact:** both margins about halved:
  - outer margin about 10 pt, through `.contentMargins(.horizontal, …, for: .scrollContent)`;
  - inner margin about 6–8 pt.

This is a deliberate difference from legacy, so the PR carries a `gap-new` ledger row.

### Scope
- Every inset-grouped card list: Favoris (Drapeaux), Catégories, a category's topic list, MP list, profile, archived drafts, and any other list using `ListDensity`.
- One source of truth: the margins live in `ListDensity`, next to `rowInsets` and `sectionSpacing`, not per view.
- iPad: check it still reads well (wide columns); the compact values may apply to iPhone only if iPad looks wrong, measured on screenshots.

### Acceptance criteria
