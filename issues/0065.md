### Summary

As a member who uses MPStorage, I want the app to find my storage MP and read its JSON document, so that my bookmarks, synced blacklist and MP flags follow me across clients.

### Scope

Two pure parsers in HFRParsing, no network:
1. **Find**: on an MP list page, the row of the storage thread (subject `MP_NAME`, with or without the `[non lu] ` prefix) and its `post` id; when absent, enough pager information for the caller to try the next page.
2. **Read**: on the storage thread page, the first message's `numreponse` and the JSON text of its first `<p>` (U+00A0 removed), handed unchanged to `MPStorageCodec` (LOT-062). The "not for you" (`destiné`) page that legacy uses to drop a cached post id is a typed result.

The page walk, create and update writes are LOT-052. ★ Critical path: LOT-052 waits for this lot.
