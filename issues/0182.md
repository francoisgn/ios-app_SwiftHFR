## francoisgn · 2026-10-28T14:09:23Z

Operator decision 2026-10-28 (ADR 0013 draft, Q13): the HFR flag is the cross-platform reading position, because it lives on the server. This store keeps the FINE position (scroll offset, last message seen) on the device. Syncing that fine position between Apple devices through iCloud KVS is decided, but it belongs to the iCloud sync lot that ADR 0013 will open. Here, only keep the model Codable and small (one entry per topic, a capped number of entries) so it can sync later.
