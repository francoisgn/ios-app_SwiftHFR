### Summary
As a member on iPhone, I want a setting that chooses whether the composer opens full screen or as the small card docked on the keyboard, so that each member gets the layout they prefer. Default: full screen, as legacy.

### Requested (operator, 2026-11-12)
- Some members will prefer the full-screen composer, others the small docked card: let them choose.
- **Default = legacy: full screen** (`MessagesView.swift:5429-5443`, `coverVerticalFullScreen` @ 87fe0d1c).
- Whatever the setting, the existing control that switches the height stays: from full screen, it still shrinks the composer to the card floating above the keyboard, and back.

### Today
- `App/SwiftHFR/Composer/ComposerHeight.swift:3-12, 26-35`: on an iPhone in regular height every composer (reply, edit, new topic, new MP) opens at `.partial`, or `.full` at an accessibility text size; the height chosen with the toggle is not remembered (operator, 2026-11-10). This issue replaces "every opening starts at partial height" with "every opening starts at the height of the setting".
- Partial height is a (b) evolution (GAP-653).
