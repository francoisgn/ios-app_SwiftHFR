### Summary
As the SwiftHFR maintainer, I want a capture of what HFR answers to a profile request (`/hfr/profil-<id>.htm`) sent with a **refused or expired session**, so that the profile sheet shows the right state instead of guessing. Since 2026-11-26 HFR refuses profiles without cookies (403, `R1363a`, #1363); with a stale session it may answer the same 403, or the stale-session `.hop` « Identifiants invalides ! » that the profile edit pages answer (`R70a`–`R70d`, HFR-PROTOCOL §6c LOT-044).

### Scope
Read-only GETs from the Mac, with the fixture kit (`tooling/fixtures/flows.py`, kit account user1, its **own** profile, like `R1363a`/`R1363b`):
1. `GET /hfr/profil-<id>.htm` with user1's `md_user` and `md_id` and a wrong `md_passs` (the `R70` setup): status, redirect, body, `Set-Cookie`.
2. If a session can be invalidated without a write (only if the operator approves; otherwise skip): the same request after it.

Publish anonymized (`R1363c`…) with metas, MANIFEST and `corpus.d` rows, plus an HFR-PROTOCOL note under §6c LOT-027 « Logged-in members only ».
