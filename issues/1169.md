### Summary

As a user who typed a moderation reason or a poll (a question and up to ten answers), I want a confirmation before a swipe-down or « Annuler » throws it away, as the main composer already does, so that I do not lose it by accident.

### Rule

- "Support swiping to dismiss a sheet. […] If people have unsaved changes in the sheet when they begin swiping to dismiss it, use an action sheet to let them confirm their action." — [HIG › Sheets](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets) (iOS, iPadOS; page updated 2026-03-24).
- "When necessary, help people avoid data loss by getting confirmation before closing a modal view." — [HIG › Modality](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/modality).

### What the app does (dev-swift @ `7a61018f`)

- `App/SwiftHFR/Reader/Actions/ModeratorAlertComposerView.swift:42` `Button("Annuler") { dismiss() }` and `:49` `.interactiveDismissDisabled(model.isSending)` (only while sending); the reason lives in the model, dropped when the sheet closes (`ReaderAlertFlow.swift:18`).
- `App/SwiftHFR/Composer/PollCreationSheet.swift:68-79`: `Button("Annuler") { dismiss() }`, no `interactiveDismissDisabled`; the state reaches the composer only through `onSave`.
- The main composer handles it (`Composer/ReplyComposerView.swift:110-116`, `dismissDisabled: model.closeNeedsChoice`).

### Legacy at `87fe0d1c`

Same gap: `HFRswift/Swift/MessagesView.swift:7478-7484` (plain `dismiss()`, no dismiss guard), `HFRswift/Swift/TopicPollDraft.swift:124-127`.

### Proposed fix and check

- `.interactiveDismissDisabled(hasChanges)` and a `confirmationDialog` (« Abandonner », destructive; « Continuer la saisie », cancel) on a swipe attempt and on « Annuler » when the fields differ from their initial values.
- Check: a view-model test of the "has unsaved text" predicate for both sheets.
