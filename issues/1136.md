### Summary

As a writer in the floating (partial-height) composer, I want the text card to grow with my reply until it nearly reaches the top of the visible page, so that I can see what I write without switching to full height. Today it stops growing, whatever the number of lines. (Operator feedback, 2026-11-18, device screenshot: a long reply in the docked composer; the operator wants it to grow until the « Fermer » and « Brouillons » chips reach the first line of the page's last message.)

### Today (dev-swift)

- The editor is as tall as its text, between `minimumLines = 2` and `maximumLines = 6` lines of the body font, plus insets (`App/SwiftHFR/Experimental/ModernComposer/ComposerHeight.swift:51-85`, `ComposerCardLayout.editorHeight`). Past 6 lines it scrolls.
- The operator reports that it **no longer grows at all**. Check this first: either the 6-line cap is what they see, or the measured height (`text`) no longer follows the content (a regression, for example after #1129 or #895). Name which on the PR, with a test.

### Scope

1. **Grow with the text**, line by line. If a regression is found, fix it first, with its own red test.
2. **New cap**: replace the fixed 6 lines with the room that is actually available. The card grows until the top of the floating buttons (close, drafts, send) reaches a top limit that keeps a strip of the page visible: at least the header of the page's last message, as on the operator's screenshot, below the navigation bar and the safe area. Past that limit the editor scrolls inside the card. Pick the limit from geometry (safe area, keyboard frame, the measured parts), not a magic number, and state the value on an iPhone 18 Pro in the PR.
3. **Apple behaviour**: Messages and Mail grow the input with the text up to a cap, then scroll, and the HIG sets no line count. Stay within that: no custom drag handle. The full-height toggle stays as it is.
4. Only the partial height of the modern composer (`EXPERIMENTAL(modernComposer, #904)`, the default until #855) changes. The legacy full-screen composer and landscape (always full height) are unchanged.

**Design sketch for the new cap (to be confirmed when the branch exists):**
- The card is the sheet's own detent (`.height(cardHeight)`), lifted over the keyboard by the system, so the room is measured **outside** the sheet: window height minus the keyboard frame, minus the top safe area and navigation bar, minus the strip of the page to keep visible.
- `ComposerCardLayout` gets that room as an input (`availableHeight`) and `editorHeight` becomes `min(max(text, 2 × line), room − top − chips − controls − insets)` instead of `6 × line`, with `maximumLines` kept only as a floor (never below 6 lines when the room allows, never negative).
