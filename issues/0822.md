## francoisgn · 2026-11-12T04:39:42Z

Operator decision (2026-11-12): offline reading is **not** removed from SwiftHFR. In legacy, the maintenance cost came from a duplicated reader (`OfflineMessagesTableViewController.m`, 2360 lines next to the online one); SwiftHFR reads offline pages with its one reader. The feature is redesigned as offline reading **by topic** (flag to end, page and size caps, at most a few topics, 72 h expiry, Settings screen) in **#846** (M5, on hold). This removal request is superseded by #846; closing it is left to its author or the operator.
