# Issue #1394: [SwiftHFR] Reader: every topic page goes through `forum2.php` and pays 1 or 2 redirects; the reload after a reply costs 3 to 4 round trips

- State: closed

- Labels: type: bug, priority: medium, difficulty: medium, area: network, origin: audit, m4-review: performance

### Summary
As a reader, I want a page change inside a topic to cost one request, so that reading is as fast as in the legacy app and the forum sees a third of the traffic. Audit #1388, finding B3.

### What happens (read at `dev-swift` `1685bcc`)
- Every page load builds the id form `forum2.php?config=…&cat=…&post=…&page=N` (`Packages/HFRKit/Sources/HFRNetwork/Endpoints/HFREndpoint+Topics.swift:28-37`, called by `PageFetcher.swift:45-52` and `App/SwiftHFR/Reader/TopicPageSource.swift:44-49`). HFR answers 301 to the canonical path, a second 301 when the topic has a sub-category (HFR-PROTOCOL §6, R5, R25), then 200: 2 to 3 round trips per page.
- The canonical URL is known after the first load (`ReaderPage.pageURL`, `TopicPageSource.swift:114`), but a page change rebuilds the id form (`ReaderTarget.atPage`, `ReaderTarget.swift:78-80`).
- After a public reply the reader asks for page `max + 1` (`TopicReaderModel.swift:427`): 301, optional 301, 302 past the end (R24), 200.

### Counts
| Scenario | Round trips now | With the canonical path |
|---|---|---|
| Next page / refresh | 2 to 3 | 1 |
| 10 pages of a topic with a sub-category | 30 | 10 (+2 on the first open) |
| Reload after a reply | 3 to 4 | 2 |

Legacy rewrites `_N.htm` in the topic's own URL for a page change (`HFRswift/Swift/MessagesView.swift:3438-3444`, `HFRswift/Swift/Common.swift:2818-2834` @ `87fe0d1c`): one request when that URL is canonical.

### Proposed fix
Once a page of the topic has been served, build the other pages from its final URL (`…-sujet_<post>_<N>.htm`, the same slug and sub-category); keep `forum2.php` for the first open and for `numreponse` links. The transport's redirect policy and host check stay as they are (LESSONS 37, 43: the path comes from a response of the forum host).

### Test
Engine test with `MockTransport`: after a first page whose final URL is canonical, `page N` requests that path, not `/forum2.php`. Protocol facts: R5, R24, R25 captures only.

Refs #1388, #1375.
