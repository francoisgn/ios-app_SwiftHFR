# Issue #401: [SwiftHFR] Follow-up of capture #172: --private keeps MP text in the composer review and content_form

- Labels: type: bug, priority: medium, difficulty: easy, area: tooling

### Summary

The anonymizer's `--private` mode replaces MP bodies (`div#para…`) and subjects, but leaves two copies of MP text on composer and edit-form pages: the "Vue Rapide de la discussion" review (the cell after `td.messCase1bis`) and the `content_form` textarea (an edit's stored text, a quote prefill). Publishing such a page for a real conversation would leak private messages.

### Facts

- Capture run 9 (#337 batch 2, PR #400, `R95c`, `R95f`, `R95g`, and earlier `R47b`, `R47c`, `R43c`): every one is a page of the operator's fixture threads (`fixture-test-N`), so the corpus holds no leak today. On those pages the review keeps the rendered posts and `content_form` the source.
- `anonymize.py check --private` does not flag either position, so `publish.py` would accept a real thread's composer.
- The MPStorage edit form (R60b) already gets a dedicated check of `content_form` and of the review (`mpstorage_form_copies`): the same positions, handled for one thread only.

### Change

In `tooling/fixtures/anonymize.py`, in private mode: replace the review cell's content and `content_form` by the private placeholder unless the page's thread is a fixture thread (`fixture-test-N` in the hidden or visible `sujet`) or the MPStorage thread (existing check); add both positions to `check --private` (both verifiers). Synthetic tests in `tooling/fixtures/tests/` (a real-looking thread composer leaks without the change). Republishing the existing fixture-thread pages must leave them unchanged.
