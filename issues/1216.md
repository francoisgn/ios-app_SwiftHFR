# Issue #1216: [SwiftHFR] Reader: night accent and links under 4.5:1 on the legacy grey for many slider hues

### Summary

As a reader who picks an accent hue, I want links and the accent to stay readable on the standard dark page, so that a red or blue accent is not hard to read now that the page is legacy's grey (#2E2F33) and no longer black. (Found by the adversarial review of #1212, the fix of #1210.)

### Observed

- Contrast of the night accent (and links) on the reader page, recomputed in the review of #1212: on the #2E2F33 page, **161 of the 360 slider hues fall under 4.5:1** (hues 0–12° and 212–359°); on the old black page 37 did.
- Examples: hue 0° (red) 3.71:1 (5.82:1 on black); hue 220° 3.64:1 (5.72:1 on black).
- `ThemeAppearanceTests` checks the accent for the default hue (190°) only.
- Legacy uses the same grey and the same accent formula, so this matches legacy; it is a readability gap that #1210's move to the grey made wider.

### Scope

- Sweep every hue (0–359°) against `ThemeAppearance.readerSurfaces` for the standard dark style, with the contrast helper of `ThemeAppearanceTests`.
- Decide, with the operator, whether to lift the night accent's brightness in standard dark above legacy's formula (a deliberate difference to record), or to accept legacy's values and say so.
- LOT-180 (single accent) owns the accent's contrast; coordinate.

### Acceptance criteria

- A test that sweeps the hues and pins the decision (a lower bound, or the list of exceptions).
