### Summary
As a member whose pseudo contains a space, or another character that `md_user` URL-encodes, I want my own posts and the quotes of my posts highlighted, so that the reader shows them as it does for every other member.

### Scope
- `VisibilityPolicy.init(ownPseudo:)` (`App/SwiftHFR/Reader/VisibilityPolicy.swift:51-53`) builds `ownKey` from `PseudoNormalizer.comparisonForm(ownPseudo)`.
- The pseudo it receives is the account key, i.e. the raw `md_user` value: `AppEnvironment.make` passes `accountName` to `ReaderSettings.live(pseudo:)`, and `ReaderSettings.visibility` hands it to `VisibilityPolicy`.
- That value is URL-encoded (HFR-PROTOCOL §5, `W03`; `AccountsModelLoginTests` stores `user%20two` for "user two"). So "user two" never matches their own posts (`.own`, class `me`) nor the quotes of their posts (`.ofMe`).
- LOT-159 (#1290) fixed the same class in `PseudoListManager.isOwn` with `PseudoListManager.keyForms(of:)`, which compares the raw and the percent-decoded key, as `AccountBook.checkSessionCookie` does. Fix: reuse it in `VisibilityPolicy`, or pass the account's display name.
