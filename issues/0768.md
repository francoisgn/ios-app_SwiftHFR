Follow-up from the round-5 review of #771, Low and pre-existing: `anonymize.py` `StructuralVerifier` runs on `html.parser`, which treats `<style>` and `<script>` as raw text everywhere. A browser does not do that inside `<svg>` or `<math>`, so a signature span hidden in `<svg><style>…</style></svg>` escapes every structural check. The corpus has no `<svg>` today.
Fix: make `check` report any `<svg>` or `<math>` element as unexpected markup. Added to this issue's remaining scope, next to part 2.
