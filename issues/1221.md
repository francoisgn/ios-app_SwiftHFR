### Summary

As a user who picks the « Doux » light style, I want the native lists to be warm too, not only the screen behind them, so that the rows do not look like white cards on an off-white page. (Left out of #1219 on purpose; asked by #1213's scope item 4.)

### Today

- #1219 paints « Doux »'s page `#F5F4EF` behind every native screen (`ScreenFill.softLight`) and in the reader. List rows keep the system's `secondarySystemGroupedBackground` (white in light), so they read as « a step lighter » than the page.
- #1213's scope also asks the native lists to follow « Doux » through the `ThemedList` tokens of #842 (a no-op for « Blanc »).

### Scope

- Decide with the operator whether the rows should be tinted (and to which colour: a warm white such as `#FBFAF7`, 16.3:1 for text and 5.0:1 for the `#636366` secondary text) or stay white.
- If tinted: add the light style's row, selected-row and separator tokens to the themed-list path for Legacy only, keeping « Blanc » on the system colours (the LOT-188 pixel tests must still pass unchanged).
- Check every list screen (favourites, topics, MP, settings) and the iPad split view.

### Acceptance criteria

- The decision, a test per token and a pixel check that « Blanc » is unchanged; screenshots on the next general review (#893).
